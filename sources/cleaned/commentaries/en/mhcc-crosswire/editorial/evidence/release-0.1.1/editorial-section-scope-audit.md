# MHCC 0.1.1 — section scope and navigation: editorial conclusions

**Historical source-bound investigation**, not a competing release approval. This audit originally tested whether absent native verse addresses represented truly missing prose or already existing authorial sections indexed too narrowly. Its many dated draft checkpoints are superseded by the [approved MHCC 0.1.1 release](../../releases/MHCC-0.1.1.md); the complete exploratory tables remain recoverable from Git history and [issue #274](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/issues/274).

## Final decisions

- **17 existing authorial commentary sections** had independently reviewed coverage beyond their original addressability; **24 native SWORD aliases** now reach those same sections. The source section body is neither duplicated nor split into fictitious verse-by-verse prose.
- Chapter-introduction and chapter-only commentary units are **not automatically a missing verse-level commentary**. Preserve authorial scope, including independent I/K/TOP identities and ambiguous sections, and use reader-level fallback when appropriate.
- Differences between original CrossWire and a separately digitized CCEL version do not automatically justify replacing CrossWire wording, paragraphs or reference anchors.
- Corrected the malformed **Ezekiel 3** chapter-overview citation and target only with independently recorded original evidence: `3:12–2` → `3:12–21`. The approved release also includes the separately documented **1 Chronicles 7** `Dn` → `Dan` transcription correction; neither change is an unrestricted editorial license.
- Source paragraph/title topology and literal cross-reference targets remain content-sensitive. A semantically plausible destination cannot be created from a plain-text citation without attested source evidence.

# MHCC 0.1.1 — editorial decision record

**Accepted result:** [MHCC 0.1.1 release](../../releases/MHCC-0.1.1.md). Earlier private-candidate ZIPs and pre-release approval questions recorded in this file's history are **superseded**, not current blockers.

## Decisions and reasons

| Subject | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passage navigation | Link **24 additional native verse keys** to **17 pre-existing authorial sections** | Independently attested section scope; no fabricated or repeated exposition |
| CrossWire / CCEL differences | Do **not** harmonize their separate electronic editions | Different provenance, attribution and textual witnesses |
| Chapter-level comments | Preserve a single original unit | Indexing convenience is not permission to split prose into verse commentary |
| Ezekiel 3 | Change displayed citation and target `3:12–2` → `3:12–21` | Independently documented malformed original digital reference |
| 1 Chronicles 7 | Change `Zebulun or Dn` → `Zebulun or Dan` | Attested transcription error, not stylistic modernization |
| Other isolated spelling variants | Leave unchanged unless independently proven incorrect in the identified digital edition | Another edition's spelling does not override this source |

Corrections are **exactly two** and source-bound, not a general permission to rewrite the author. Their affected native shared keys, reviewed transforms and immutable compiled hashes belong to [the rebuild manifest](../../../../../../../../config/commentary-rebuilds/mhcc-crosswire.json) and [native audit](../../../../../../../../modules/mhcc-crosswire/SOURCE_AUDIT.json).

## Reader and release boundary

The original indexed stock and earlier `BRMHCC` 0.1.0 remain separately identifiable. The final corrected version uses SWORD ID `MHCC`, which collides with upstream CrossWire's own `MHCC` name. Do not infer transparent updates or deletion of an installed `BRMHCC`.

Real Xiphos spot checks and native indexed-link proofs are **bounded samples**; a commentary linked across a chapter may repeat in `diatheke` or be displayed more than once by some clients. This is an application/reader presentation question, not evidence of duplicate authored prose.

Exact provenance, license scope, approvals and public release conditions: [MHCC 0.1.1 release](../../releases/MHCC-0.1.1.md), [rights acceptance](mhcc-0.1.1-editorial-rights-acceptance.md) and [audit issue #274](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/issues/274).

## Why the former rights hold was closed

The original research identified that CCEL has separate terms for some digitally prepared material and that public-domain historical prose does not necessarily confer copyright clearance over all modern transcription layers. After considering this, the owner chose the official **CrossWire source declaration** as the project's rights basis, rather than blending in separately edited CCEL data.

This is a **project-level licensing decision**, not an independently obtained legal opinion certifying every editorial layer or international jurisdiction. The originally prepared inquiries were **never sent** and have no operative status.

## Practical boundary

Maintain the original source, module metadata license and attribution; publish only the explicitly reviewed CrossWire-derived resource and attested corrections. The owner authorized publication of the resulting [MHC 0.1.0 release](../releases/MHC-0.1.0.md) separately from this rights decision. New third-party prose, imagery or translations need their own rights review.

## Why these candidate decisions still matter

- The exact official CrossWire MHC 2.2 authored OSIS is the maintained source witness, not a mixture of alternative CCEL transcriptions. Authored exposition can span native keys, but original divisions and prose must not be merged merely to simplify indexing.
- Six overlap groups required **source-attested navigation reindexing** without deleting any of the **4,256 original commentary bodies**.
- The **2 Chronicles 1** original chapter preface was initially read from verse context. Later independent native comparison showed that the genuine original preface must be classified as a separate chapter introduction, while verse 1 commentary remains its own section. The reviewed `title type="chapter"` treatment corrects original scope; ordinary Xiphos verse copying need not show the introduction. [Native proof](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/actions/runs/44501242523) and [Xiphos introduction-menu proof](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/actions/runs/44539128176).

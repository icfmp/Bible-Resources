## Limits that must not be overstated

The historical checks **did not certify** every clicked GUI hyperlink, every visual paragraph/note or full Android/AndBible presentation. The Psalm 60 superscription destination was a particular click-test concern. This is a reader QA boundary, not permission to change the source. See [reader acceptance scope](reader-acceptance-scope-2026-11-19.md).

The owner accepted CrossWire's official `Public Domain — Copy Freely` declaration as the rights basis on 2026-11-19. Unsent inquiry drafts and earlier rights-hold language were **superseded**. Preserve CrossWire/CCEL/Logos attribution; accepting the provider claim is not independent legal certification of every third-party addition. [Rights decision](rights-review-2026-11-19.md).

# Bible J. N. Darby (BPC 2024 rev. 2.0) — Bible Resources 0.1.0

First independently curated French J. N. Darby SWORD release based on the **official 2024 rev. 2.0** edition of Bibles et Publications Chrétiennes, published as **Public Domain** ([publisher rights and edition](https://editeurbpc.com/telechargements)). This is **not** the differently licensed revised JND translation.

The public-domain authorial Bible wording, punctuation, 66-book canon and **6,340** original footnotes are preserved. The separate Bible Resources derivative corrects **eight** demonstrably wrong OSIS machine link targets, three deceptively clickable labels in notes explaining **intentionally absent** verses, and two schema-invalid measurement tags, through separately reviewed, reversible and exact-source-hash-pinned technical overlays. Original printed note prose is unchanged, and no intentionally omitted verse is inserted.

**Provenance:** Original publisher 2024 rev. 2.0 OSIS SHA-256: `2204629e3efb3024dceb392780ef5e61f4296ea832c90e3dd2a5c3224179c1d3`. Final privately audited corrected OSIS SHA-256: `1f0d439b20b763e3548a05bb766d9f59447918f973ea92f8695d7578e096178a`. Compilation uses pinned CrossWire SWORD `osis2mod` r3918 and stable SWORD reading libraries. SWORD module `BRJND2024`, version `0.1.0`, `zText` with `Luther` versification.

**Evidence:** [editorial review and owner's scoped approval](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/issues/133), [end-to-end import and full-corpus audit](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/issues/62), [stable Android note and missing-verse behavior PR #167](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/pull/167). Real Xiphos and AndBible stable reader testing includes original notes, correct Colossians navigation, original missing-verse explanations rendered as plain text, and restart persistence. The separate GNOME Bible alpha was not used as a release criterion.

**Known limitations:** The original edition's whitespace and line spacing cannot be guaranteed byte-identical after standard SWORD/XML serialization. Marked source paragraphs, headings and poetry and the original prose/notes are preserved in audited structural positions. Visual layouts differ by reader. This release does not claim typographic facsimile fidelity, exhaustive GUI review of every note, or an official BPC software endorsement. The revised 2026 JND is not part of this package.

The release includes `BRJND2024-0.1.0.zip` containing standard SWORD `mods.d/` and `modules/` directories, plus `SHA256SUMS.txt` and an exact source/native audit receipt. Verify the archive SHA-256 against the manifest and the independent catalogue before installing. To install, use your reader's SWORD ZIP importer or extract into a dedicated SWORD module home. Do not overwrite the eBible or publisher source modules. Report translation-specific issues with verse, reader version and screenshots in [Bible Resources issues](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/issues).

## Limits and publication boundary

This is a **representative** two-reader review, not exhaustive inspection of every verse, note popup, font/justification setting, OS or automatic upgrade. A source/native hash comparison is the complete corpus fidelity gate; the screenshots confirm only these visible reading situations. Android's safe automation flags intentionally distinguish a successful UI control sequence from independent visual inspection.

The existing published `0.1.0` archive and live catalogue remain unchanged. **This QA evidence does not authorize a new public `0.1.1` release.** Publication requires protected merge of the appropriate release candidate, exact public asset/catalogue integrity and explicit owner authorization. The final publicly delivered ZIP must be independently verified against its own manifest after release.

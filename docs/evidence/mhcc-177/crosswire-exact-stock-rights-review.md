## Positive distribution evidence

1. The [official CrossWire MHCC listing](https://crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=MHCC) describes the **specific named module** with the explicit distribution statement **Public Domain** and also says it may be copied freely. The statement is corroborated by the independently retrieved original `.conf`, not inferred solely from Matthew Henry's death or the printed book's age.
2. [CrossWire's SWORD module metadata documentation](https://crosswire.org/sword/develop/swordmodule/) distinguishes a `Public Domain` `DistributionLicense` from other states such as copyrighted material or permission restricted to CrossWire. The original MHCC metadata is in the former category.
3. [CrossWire's general end-user FAQ](https://wiki.crosswire.org/EnduserFAQ) cautions that **other modules** may only be licensed to CrossWire for distribution. The explicit MHCC-specific Public Domain declaration, not the general fact of CrossWire hosting, is therefore the relevant positive evidence.
4. The intended **byte-preserving upstream reuse** does not incorporate the separately downloaded CCEL XML, CCEL headings, a new translation, unverified authorial source text or third-party additions into the candidate SWORD data.

## What this does not prove

- CrossWire's own declaration is primary source **distribution evidence**, not an independent opinion on every potentially protected digital correction or ancillary note in every jurisdiction.
- CrossWire attributes the text to a printed edition and an older corrected electronic copy; the exact original digital source and all amendments have not been independently reconstructed. **Do not** claim editorial equivalence to the print copy beyond the upstream attribution.
- The original `.conf` identifies a CCEL `TextSource` URL. [CCEL's copyright policy](https://www.ccel.org/about/copyright.html) explicitly cautions about protected editorial material and requests permission for CCEL edition republication. This review does **not** approve republishing the independently obtained CCEL XML or creating new derived editions from it.
- [PR #212](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/pull/212) identified an original Jude-versus-Judges annotation mismatch in the **separate CCEL witness**. That is not grounds to rewrite the compiled CrossWire original, or to infer compiled corruption from a false cross-edition join.
- [PR #213](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/pull/213) proved a private actual Xiphos startup/window for the pinned original module. It did not prove correct commentary display, link interaction, or any AndBible Android frontend result.

## Scope and production choice

- **Chosen upstream witness:** CrossWire `MHCC` 2.0 (2021-02-14), the *concise*, not the complete, Matthew Henry commentary. The proposed production track is **unmodified SWORD stock reuse**, not reconstruction from the independent CCEL transcription.
- **Pinned original:** ZIP SHA-256 `5599108faf01753864d51136a87f120d022e70867cc9df70206a0af3c3a5ef7d`; `.conf` SHA-256 `2762a04757ec3ee14bcf5d442fedcd47d2b938d573de4e9100a31aa073bc5863`; full 29,975-key ordered native raw-corpus SHA-256 `8abd8bedb4eb0ff425999a9435e18d3c302d3a4f47863e3af5b1de36dfd538e6`.
- **Scope of permitted technical transformation:** the private `BRMHCCReview` candidate changes only reviewed SWORD module identity/path metadata; all six original compressed data files and all original occupied raw SWORD entries remain identical. An eventual *public* module identity and its final package/profile must be separately reviewed. The private candidate is not a release.
- **Non-equivalence:** the original CCEL ThML is a distinct digital witness, not an authenticated exact source for CrossWire's original compiled payload. Its original five Jude-heading/`Judg`-marker contradictions must not be used to fabricate a CrossWire defect. Of the ten initially unresolved non-reference prose comparisons, five were invalid cross-book joins and five remain valid **different-edition** comparisons (three small; two substantial). These are scholarly review items, **not five proven CrossWire corrections**. No source word, citation target, punctuation or compiled binary was altered. See [#225–#231](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/issues/177).

For the **exact-stock reuse** track, full original-to-private-candidate binary/raw-key equality proves absence of *our own* payload loss or edits. It does **not** prove the compiled digital witness reproduces the claimed Moody Press printing in all details. Requiring forced CCEL↔CrossWire lexical equality would be an unjustified gate; source and edition limitations instead belong in the editorial decision and reader profile.

### Latest Android caveat — stop treating instrumentation as content approval

[#248](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/pull/248), merged as `422055d8b2d23f8646c299f29fa28b168be2a6f8`, passed exact-head Validate and real official Android CI. All **29,975** original native occupied keys were reread; **28,288** substantive numbered entries were eligible for original 16-consecutive-word matching. The real selected Android reader exposed two WebViews, but accessibility and a successful read-only app-owned Chrome DevTools DOM inspection each yielded **zero matching authentic MHCC phrases**. One DOM page was read; it may represent the Bible pane rather than the commentary pane, but this is unproven. **Android authorial text and clicked reference navigation are therefore unverified**, not a demonstration of broken source. Do not continue speculative DOM probing without a specific positive/negative decision criterion.

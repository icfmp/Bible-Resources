## Directly observable editions differ within the confirmed exposition

The two historically connected witnesses agree in substance, argument sequence and paragraph/section transition but do **not** agree in every lexical detail. For example, the public CCEL text within exposition II says `must reckon upon it`, whereas printed p. 216 in the visually inspected reprint says `must count upon it`. The reprint also has `he never did them mischief` on p. 215 where the CCEL public rendering says `he never did those mischief`. The underlying exact CCEL XML original payload and full facsimile typography have **not** been aligned character by character. These are **edition/digital preparation variants**, not automatic corrections to either witness.

The printed witness therefore independently corroborates **historical authorship of the substance of the alleged missing exposition**, making the simple explanation “CCEL invents the whole paragraph” untenable. It **does not** show the printed witness and CCEL share exact 569-token contents, punctuation, internal links, headings, edition rights or the same intended editor's lineation. In particular, **transcribing the printed paragraph** and **copying CCEL’s prepared XML with its links** are separate rights/provenance decisions.

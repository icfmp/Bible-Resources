## Short description

**Matthew Henry's Concise Commentary on the Whole Bible**

An English-language historic Protestant Bible commentary accompanying
all 66 books of the Protestant canon, based on the CrossWire MHCC 2.0

source errors. The original authorial paragraphs and section boundaries
remain unchanged.

## Long description

### About this work

*Matthew Henry's Concise Commentary on the Whole Bible* is the shorter
form of the commentary associated with Matthew Henry, an English
Nonconformist minister and biblical expositor. It is an aid to the study
of Scripture, not a Bible translation.

### Changes in Bible Resources edition 0.1.1

This edition is **not byte-identical** to the original CrossWire module
or to the earlier Bible Resources 0.1.0 publication.

- **Navigation:** twenty-four previously missing verse addresses now
  link to 17 independently reviewed original commentary sections,
  without inventing new commentary or changing their boundaries.
- **Ezekiel 3:** in the chapter synopsis, one malformed citation and
  its internal SWORD reference target read `3:12–2`; both are corrected
  to `3:12–21`. The single original commentary body is shared across
  11 existing native verse addresses.
- **1 Chronicles 7:** the digital source typo `Zebulun or Dn` is
  corrected to `Zebulun or Dan` in one original commentary entry.

Both editorial corrections are narrowly documented and byte-for-byte
reversible. The original commentary prose elsewhere, headings,
paragraphs, introductions, annotations and existing native section
links are preserved by complete source/native SWORD comparison.

### Edition and limitations

- **Language:** English; commentary on the Protestant 66-book Bible.
- **Format:** standard SWORD `zCom` with KJV verse indexing; rebuilt
  using chapter-based compression blocks.
- **Original source:** CrossWire `MHCC` 2.0 (2021-02-14).
- **Curated edition:** Bible Resources `BRMHCC` 0.1.1.
- **Rights:** CrossWire declares its specific digital module
  `Public Domain — Copy Freely`.

CrossWire attributes the historic source to a Moody Press 28th
printing (ISBN 0-8024-5190-X). This is the original provider's
attribution, **not an independently verified claim of textual
identity with the printed edition**.

The distinct CCEL digital witness was used as comparison evidence
only; its separately digitized text and markup were not incorporated.
Whole-corpus SWORD checks verify the exact bounded edition changes,
and representative passages have been read in Xiphos. This is not
a claim of perfect presentation in every third-party Bible reader.

**This document is proposed release copy for future editorial/owner review. There is no public 0.1.1 archive, release URL, catalogue update or signed release authorization. Do not link it as an installed module.**

## Corrected navigation

Version **0.1.1** would make existing Matthew Henry Concise Commentary sections reachable from **24 additional Bible verses** across **17 independently corroborated source sections**. It is an **index-only correction** to the existing CrossWire MHCC 2.0-derived English resource (published by Bible Resources as `BRMHCC 0.1.0`), not a new translation, commentary rewrite, modernization or import of CCEL wording.

- Sixteen original comments explicitly covering two verses lacked their second navigable verse key. The missing links are now assigned to their **existing authorial commentary**, with source-verse scope independently checked against CCEL leading exposition. Sections affected: Ecclesiastes 4:7–8 and 9:11–12; Song of Songs 1:7–8, 6:2–3 and 8:13–14; Isaiah 21:11–12, 26:20–21, 50:10–11, 56:1–2, 57:1–2, 58:1–2, 58:13–14 and 61:10–11; Jeremiah 4:1–2 and 4:3–4; Lamentations 4:21–22.
- The existing commentary on **Matthew 13:24–30 and 36–43** is one distinct section spanning two nonadjacent passages. The correction links **36–43** to its original commentary; the separate **31–35** commentary stays intact and distinct. No incorrect continuous 24–43 section is created.

No original commentary body has been edited or deliberately duplicated; the new verse keys share the appropriate original SWORD record.

## Source and preservation

- Source: CrossWire `MHCC` **2.0**, originally compiled `zCom`, KJV versification. Original archived ZIP SHA-256: `5599108faf01753864d51136a87f120d022e70867cc9df70206a0af3c3a5ef7d`.
- Original occupied records retained: **28,718 numbered entries**, **1,255 book/chapter introduction entries** and **2 opaque TOP positions**. The 24 new aliases give **28,742 total numbered keys** in the corrected *private* compilation.
- Tested against exact source word order, whitespace-normalized visible text including punctuation, original non-wrapper element and attribute structure, reference destinations and original technical entry positions. Standard OSIS compilation may introduce wrapper nodes; **raw original XML byte-for-byte identity is not claimed**.
- The new standard compiled data use **`BlockType=CHAPTER`** in the corresponding curated SWORD `.conf`; the old original compressed stock used `BlockType=BOOK`. This is a documented *compression/packaging change*, not an alteration of commentary wording or its scripture reference targets.
- CrossWire declares the upstream content **Public Domain — Copy Freely**. This revised module would retain that exact source attribution and license declaration; no additional CCEL digital text or independently copyrighted annotations are imported or relicensed.

## Known limitations and intentionally unchanged material

- **25** cross-edition CCEL-vs-CrossWire reference-target annotation occurrences in **20** first-verse-only chapter entries remain unchanged: they are not proven CrossWire errors, and independently importing digital annotations is not part of this release.
- **NEW SOURCE ERRATUM / EDITORIAL DECISION PENDING — Ezekiel 3 chapter overview.** The original CrossWire `Ezek.3.1` entry contains the **only reversed** scripture range detected among **1,204 original K/I distinct-body `reference` annotations**. CCEL's separate chapter page visibly reads `Ezek. 3:12-2` in its overview yet labels the actual following section `3:12-21`. The source likely lost a terminal digit; the specified historical printed edition has not been independently checked. See [source audit #43705786140](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/actions/runs/43705786140), [erratum #277](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible-Resources/issues/277). This **index-only draft reproduces the source typo unchanged**. Before publishing, the owner must explicitly choose to retain and disclose it, or authorize a separately reviewed original-label-and-target correction with fresh full parity, copyright, checksum and reader evidence. Do not silently reword the original as part of the 24-alias update.
- **118** first-verse-only chapter entries and **2,285 potential whole-chapter navigation aliases** are **not** automatically expanded. Those source divisions and user-experience consequences require a separate decision. Original intro-only cases (Lev 19, Ps 108) remain untouched.
- A reader displaying all commentary addresses for a chapter may repeat the **same linked original prose** (confirmed in command-line `diatheke`). This reflects **reader rendering of linked entries**, not duplicate independent source text. Whole-chapter deduplication in Xiphos has **not** been demonstrated and is not claimed here.
- The work does not establish exact edition-wide correspondence to Matthew Henry's historical printed wording.

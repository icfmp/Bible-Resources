## Long description

### About this work

*Matthew Henry's Concise Commentary on the Whole Bible* presents a
shorter form of the commentary associated with Matthew Henry, an English
Nonconformist pastor and writer. It introduces and explains Bible
passages from a historic Protestant Christian perspective.

This is the **Concise Commentary (MHCC)**, not Henry's much longer
*Complete Commentary (MHC)*. It is a study companion and does not replace
a Bible translation.

### What is in this digital edition?

The published edition is curated from the exact **CrossWire MHCC 2.0**
compiled SWORD module, dated **2021-02-14**. Its original compressed
commentary data, headings, introductions, notes and Scripture-reference
markup are retained without editorial rewording. Bible Resources changes
only the installation module identity, matching data path and curated
release version.

Bible Resources does not combine or silently harmonize those

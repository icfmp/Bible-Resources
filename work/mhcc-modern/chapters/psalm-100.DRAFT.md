## An invitation to praise the Lord

This song of praise should be read as a prophecy. We may also use it as a prayer.

It points forward to the day when all people will know that the Lord is God, worship him, and become the sheep of his pasture.

We have every reason to worship God joyfully.

When we wandered like lost sheep, he brought us back into his fold. Surely that gives us abundant reason to bless his name!

The subject of our praise, and the reasons for it, matter deeply.

Know who God is in himself and what he is to you. Think about these things and make them your own.

Then your worship will become more constant and more heartfelt, more serious and more deeply engaged.

The covenant of grace, revealed in the Old and New Testaments, is full of rich promises. They strengthen the faith of even the weakest believer.

These promises give us a sure foundation for praising God and finding joy in him.

When we look at ourselves, our hearts may be heavy with sadness. But when we look to God's goodness and mercy, and to the comfort he promises in his word, we have good reason to praise him.

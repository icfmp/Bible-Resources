## All nations are called to praise the Lord

Here is a solemn invitation to all nations to praise the Lord, along with good reasons for that praise.

We quickly grow tired of doing good unless we keep alive the reverent and devoted affections that should kindle the spiritual sacrifice of praise.

This is a gospel psalm.

Paul quotes it in Romans 15:11 as evidence that the gospel would be preached to the Gentile nations and that they would receive it.

For many centuries, God was known and praised in Judah alone. This invitation had not yet been given to the Gentiles.

But Christ's gospel is to be preached to every nation. Through him, those who were once far away are brought near.

We are among the people to whom the Holy Spirit speaks in this psalm. He calls us to join God's ancient people in praising the Lord.

In this way, grace has overflowed to millions of sinners who were perishing.

Let us respond to God's gracious invitation.

Let us pray for the day when all the nations of the earth will declare his praise.

And let us bless God for the inexhaustible riches of gospel grace.

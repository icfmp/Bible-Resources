## A prayer for deliverance from malicious speech (verses 1–4)

The psalmist suffered deeply because of deceitful speech.

May every good person be delivered from lying lips! People had made false accusations against him.

In his distress, he prayed earnestly to God.

God is able to restrain lying tongues, and he graciously answered the psalmist's prayer.

Surely sinners would not behave as they do if they understood what their actions would lead to and seriously considered their end.

The terrors of the Lord are his arrows. His wrath is compared to burning juniper coals, whose fierce heat lasts a long time.

This is the portion of those who speak falsely. All who love lies and live by them will receive their portion in the lake that burns forever.

## The pain of living among hostile people (verses 5–7)

It is painful for a godly person to be forced to live among the wicked, especially when he longs to be separated from them forever.

But notice what characterizes a good person: he wants to live peaceably with everyone.

Let us follow David, who foreshadowed Christ. When distressed, let us cry out to the Lord, trusting that he will hear us.

Let us pursue peace and holiness, seeking to overcome evil with good.

## God's unfailing care for his people

We must not place our confidence in people, methods or secondary causes.

Should we trust the strength of the hills? Or princes and other powerful people?

No. Our confidence belongs to God alone.

Or the psalm may be telling us to lift our eyes beyond the hills, toward the God who gives everything on earth its place and purpose.

All our help comes from him. We must look for it in his way and in his time.

When difficulties and dangers are at their greatest, this psalm teaches us to find comfort in the Lord.

Almighty wisdom plans the safety of those under God's protection, and almighty power carries that plan out.

God is a watchful Keeper. He never grows weary. He does not sleep or even slumber.

In his shade, his people can rest with delight and assurance.

He is always close, ready to protect and refresh them.

The right hand is the hand of action. Let God's people attend to their duty, and they will find him ready to grant success.

He will take care that they do not fall.

Neither open attacks nor hidden schemes of their enemies can ultimately harm them.

The Lord can prevent the trouble they fear. When they do suffer, he can sanctify their suffering, remove it or make its burden lighter.

He preserves their souls from being stained by sin or overwhelmed by trouble. Above all, he keeps them from eternal destruction.

He watches over them through life and death: as they go out to work in the morning of their days, and as they return home to rest in the evening of old age.

His care extends over their whole lives.

The Spirit, their Preserver and Comforter, will remain with them forever.

Let us be found faithfully at our work, confident that the blessings promised in this psalm belong to us.

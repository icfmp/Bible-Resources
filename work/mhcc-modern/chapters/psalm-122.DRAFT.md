## Joyful love for Jerusalem (verses 1–5)

The joy and spiritual benefit we receive through the means of grace should outweigh the trouble and fatigue of attending them.

We should also encourage one another to do what is good.

When our Christian friends are undertaking good work, we should ask them to invite us and take us along.

How eagerly we should look forward to the heavenly Jerusalem!

With hope of a crown of glory, we can bear our cross cheerfully and even welcome death.

Jerusalem was called the beautiful city. It was a type of the gospel church.

That church is joined together in holy love and Christian fellowship, becoming like one united city.

If all Christ's disciples shared one mind and kept the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace, their enemies would lose their greatest advantage against them.

But Satan has always sought to divide so that he can conquer. Too few Christians recognize his designs.

## Pray and work for the church's peace (verses 6–9)

Even those who can do nothing else for Jerusalem's peace can pray for it.

Let us regard everyone who seeks the Redeemer's glory as our brothers and sisters and fellow travelers, whatever differences do not affect our eternal welfare.

Blessed Spirit of peace and love, you who lived in the soul of the holy Jesus, descend upon his church.

Fill his people with the spirit of heaven. Bring bitter quarrels to an end, and make us united in mind.

Love for fellow believers and love for God should stir us to follow the Lord Jesus.

Let us pray fervently and work tirelessly for people's salvation and for God's glory.

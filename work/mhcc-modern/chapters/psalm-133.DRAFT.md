# Psalm 133 — Modern English working draft

## The beauty of brotherly love

We cannot speak too often about the importance of living together in peace. It would be wonderful if we could say enough to persuade everyone to do so.

Unity is good for us. It brings honor and comfort, and gives lasting delight to those who live in it.

The psalm compares the pleasure of brotherly love to the holy anointing oil.

Such love is the fruit of the Spirit. It shows our union with Christ and brings beauty to his gospel.

Brotherly love is useful as well as pleasant. Its blessings are as countless as drops of dew.

Dew cools the heated air and refreshes the earth. In the same way, love quiets the heat of human passions.

It also softens the heart, preparing it to receive the good seed of God's word and bear fruit.

And here is the proof of its excellence: where brothers and sisters live together in unity, the Lord commands the blessing.

God commands the blessing. We can only ask for it.

Those who live in love and peace have the God of love and peace with them now. Soon they will be with him forever in a world of endless love and peace.

May everyone who loves the Lord bear with others and forgive them, just as God has forgiven us for Christ's sake.

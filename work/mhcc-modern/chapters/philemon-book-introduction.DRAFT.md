# Philemon — Book introduction (Modern English working draft)

Philemon lived in Colossae. He was a man of some standing and wealth, and he had become a Christian through Paul's ministry.

Onesimus was enslaved to Philemon. He had run away from his master and gone to Rome, where he heard the gospel through Paul and became a Christian.

Paul kept him with him for a time, until Onesimus's conduct showed that his conversion was sincere.

Onesimus wanted to make amends for the wrong he had done to his master. But he feared receiving the punishment his offense deserved, so he asked Paul to write to Philemon on his behalf.

Nowhere, perhaps, does Paul reason more beautifully or plead more persuasively than in this letter.

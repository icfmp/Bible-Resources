## An invitation to bless the Lord

Let us stir ourselves to give glory to God.

Let us also encourage ourselves to hope for his mercy and grace.

One excellent habit is to fill our spare moments with holy reflection, prayer and praise.

Then time need never feel like a burden. We would not waste our hours on empty conversation, useless entertainments or the indulgence of sinful desires.

We need nothing more for true happiness than the Lord's blessing.

And we should ask for spiritual blessings not only for ourselves but for others too.

Our prayer should not simply be, “Lord, bless me,” but also, “Lord, bless them.”

In that way we show that we believe God has blessings enough for others as well as ourselves.

And we show sincere goodwill toward our neighbors.

# Psalm 150 — Modern English working draft

## A final call to praise the Lord

This psalm calls us to praise God.

Praise him for his sanctuary and for the privilege of having it among us. Praise him for his power and the glory displayed in the heavens.

Those who praise the Lord in heaven see wonders of his power and glory that we cannot yet imagine.

Yet the greatest of his mighty works is known in his sanctuary on earth.

God's holiness and love shine more brightly in the redemption of humanity than in all his other works.

Let us praise our God and Savior for this.

We need not be concerned with identifying every musical instrument named in the psalm. The point is that we should spare no effort or expense in serving God.

Praise him with strong faith.

Praise him with holy love and delight.

Praise him with complete confidence in Christ.

Praise him with the triumph of faith over the powers of darkness.

Praise him by respecting all his commands and gladly accepting all his dealings with us.

Praise him by rejoicing in his love and finding comfort in his goodness.

Praise him by advancing the interests of his kingdom of grace.

Praise him with lively hope and expectation of his kingdom of glory.

Our last breath will come soon. As long as we have breath, let us use it to praise the Lord. Then we may breathe our last in comfort.

Let everything that breathes praise the Lord.

Praise the Lord!

What a fitting conclusion to a book inspired by God's Spirit and written to help us praise him.

For more than three thousand years, this book has given the church songs to sing.

Christ and his apostles quote it more often than any other book. It gives us the highest thoughts of God and his rule.

It speaks to every condition of human life and every stage of spiritual experience.

Its simplicity and clarity bear the marks of its divine origin.

## The Lord delivers his people (verses 1–5)

At times God allows the enemies of his people to gain great power over them.

When he delivers his people, his power shines all the more clearly.

How blessed are those whose God is the Lord, the all-sufficient God!

We can apply this to the deliverances God has given his people, both in ancient times and in our own day.

But we must also keep in view the greatest deliverance of all: the redemption accomplished by Jesus Christ, through which believers have been rescued from Satan.

## Give thanks for deliverance (verses 6–8)

God is the source of every deliverance. All the glory belongs to him.

The enemies of his people set traps to lead them into sin and trouble, and to keep them there.

For a time they may seem to succeed.

Yet let us put our trust in the Lord. We will not finally be put to shame.

Believers give all the honor for their salvation to God's power, mercy and truth.

They look back with wonder and thanksgiving on the way the Lord has led them.

And they rejoice that their help for the future comes from the One who made heaven and earth.

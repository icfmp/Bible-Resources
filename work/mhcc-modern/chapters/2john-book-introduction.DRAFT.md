# 2 John — Book introduction (Modern English working draft)

This second letter reads almost like a brief summary of the first. In just a few words, it addresses many of the same subjects.

The elect lady is commended for raising her children in virtue and faith. She is urged to remain in Christ's teaching, to persevere in the truth, and to guard against the deception of false teachers.

Above all, the apostle calls her to obey the great command of Christian love and charity.

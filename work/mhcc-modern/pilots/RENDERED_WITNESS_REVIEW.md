# MHCC Modern English pilot — preliminary rendered-witness comparison

**2026-11-14 — Preliminary editorial check only. NOT a validation receipt.**

Authoritative edition remains pinned published `BRMHCC` 0.1.0. These observations compare **working drafts** to the visible STEP Bible presentation of CrossWire MHCC, not to source SHA-attested `BRMHCC` native OSIS entries. The editor who drafted the pilots also inspected the displayed witness; this is **not an independent semantic audit**.

## Ruth 1

Reading witness: https://www.stepbible.org/?q=version%3DMHCC@reference%3DRut.1

Visible sections: 1–5, 6–14, 15–18, 19–22. The STEP presentation includes a **separate Ruth-wide introduction** before Chapter 1, not incorporated into the chapter working draft. Confirm its native source attribution, preserve/modernize separately, and do not silently drop it from the final module.

A preliminary comparison found that the original statement about young people exposed to bad company could be misread in the first draft: the people doing the exposing *consider the young people* to have firm principles. Corrected the pronoun reference explicitly without changing the argument. Terms around public ordinances, the deliberate religious profession, genuine repentance, Ruth's grace, and the “great change” require exact-source audit.

Status: **DRAFT / UNVALIDATED**.

## Romans 3

Reading witness: https://www.stepbible.org/?q=version%3DMHCC@reference%3DRom.3

Visible sections: 1–8, 9–18, 19–20, 21–26, 27–31. The draft retains these five groupings but changes headings to aid contemporary reading.

Two preliminary semantic corrections were made after seeing the rendered CrossWire witness:

1. Restore Henry's explicit assertion that the name *Jesus Christ* signifies an anointed Savior; a previous draft described Christ as anointed but omitted the statement about the **name**.
2. Retain the unusually direct assertion that **faith itself is a law** rather than smoothing it into “a governing principle,” which risks deleting a distinct rhetorical claim. Its meaning must still be checked in the context of the final argument about law and faith.

Source-specific precision still required around justification, the Surety's payment, the believer/Christ relationship, covenant and the law's continuing role. No modern theological correction is authorized.

Status: **DRAFT / UNVALIDATED**.

## Hebrews 7

Reading witness: https://www.stepbible.org/?q=version%3DMHCC@reference%3DHeb.7

Visible sections: 1–3, 4–10, 11–25, 26–28. Important fidelity check: Melchizedek is a **type** of Christ; the silence of the record about his birth/death is not a literal assertion that Melchizedek is eternal. The comparison explicitly distinguishes the better covenant from **Sinai's covenant**, *not* from the covenant of works; do not flatten this distinction.

Preserve the stronger repeated claims concerning Christ's sinlessness, uninterrupted priesthood, eternal life and deliverance under prosperity as well as under suffering. All remain pending exact-source key and markup comparison.

Status: **DRAFT / UNVALIDATED**.

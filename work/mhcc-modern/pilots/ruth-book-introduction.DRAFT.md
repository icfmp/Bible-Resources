# Ruth — Book introduction (Modern English draft)

> **Not a validated translation.** This book-level introduction is visibly
> distinct from Ruth chapter 1 in STEP's rendering of the original CrossWire
> MHCC. The corrected 0.1.1 source has no authorized content edit in Ruth,
> but the exact SWORD native introduction ownership, key, markup, punctuation
> and references must still be read from the pinned published ZIP. Do not
> silently merge this into Ruth 1's first commentary section.

Ruth gives us fine examples of faith, devotion, patience, humility, diligence and loving-kindness in the ordinary events of life.

The book also shows us how carefully God's providence attends to even our smallest concerns. That gives us every reason to trust him fully.

We can read Ruth as a beautiful, natural picture of human life, a carefully told account of important events, and a part of God's plan of redemption.

## Separate second-pass source-meaning review (readable CrossWire witness)

Source arguments to preserve, in the original order:

1. Exemplars of **faith, piety, patience, humility, industry, loving-kindness** amid ordinary life — all six retained, with `piety` → `devotion` and `industry` → `diligence` as modern equivalents.
2. God's **special providential care for smallest concerns** — preserved; do not reframe providence as chance or merely human attentiveness.
3. **Full trust in God** as the conclusion of that providence — preserved.
4. The book is both a **beautiful and natural picture of human life** and **a careful record of important facts** — retained as distinct but coordinated perspectives.
5. The book is **part of the plan of redemption** — preserved without adding interpretive details not supplied by Henry.

# 2 Timothy — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

The first purpose of this letter **appears** to have been to tell Timothy what had happened during Paul's imprisonment and to ask him to come to **Rome**.

Paul was uncertain whether he would live to see Timothy again, so he also provided a wide range of counsel and encouragement to help him discharge his ministry faithfully.

Because this was a **personal letter** to one of Paul's closest friends, written amid the distress of imprisonment with death approaching, it offers a striking glimpse of the apostle's character.

It also gives compelling evidence that **Paul sincerely believed the doctrines he preached**.

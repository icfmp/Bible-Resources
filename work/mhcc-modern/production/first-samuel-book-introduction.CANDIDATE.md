# 1 Samuel — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

This book tells us about Eli and the wickedness of his sons, and about Samuel's character and actions.

It then describes Saul's rise to become king of Israel and his misconduct, ending with his death and preparing the way for David's succession.

David was an outstanding picture of Jesus Christ.

His patience, humility, perseverance, and suffering under persecution from both open enemies and false friends provide an example to the church and to every one of its members.

Many events in this book encourage suffering believers to remain faithful, hopeful and patient.

The book also contains useful cautions and solemn warnings.

# 2 Corinthians — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Paul's second letter to the Corinthians was written **probably about a year after the first**.

Its contents are closely connected with that earlier letter.

Paul considered how his earlier message had been received. The response gave him reason to thank God, who enabled him to fulfill his responsibility toward the believers.

Many Corinthians showed **signs of repentance and improved their conduct**.

Others, however, continued following false teachers.

Paul delayed visiting the church because he did not want to discipline them severely before giving them an opportunity to change.

Some then accused him of **unreliability and inconsistency**.

They also attacked him as proud, self-glorifying and harsh, speaking about him contemptuously.

Nevertheless, this letter shows the same **deep affection for the Corinthian believers** that appeared in the first, the same concern for the gospel's honor and the same courage in correcting their sins.

The **first six chapters are mainly practical**.

The remaining chapters address the Corinthian church's particular circumstances more directly, while still providing instruction useful to Christians everywhere.

# 2 Thessalonians — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Paul wrote this second letter to the Thessalonians **soon after the first**.

He had learned that some believers had understood his earlier letter as predicting that **Christ's return and the day of judgment would occur during their own lifetime**.

Some responded by neglecting their **ordinary earthly responsibilities**.

Paul wrote again to **correct this misunderstanding**, which was also interfering with the spread of the gospel.

His earlier teaching agreed with the prophets of the Old Testament.

There were still purposes in God's eternal counsel to be fulfilled **before the Lord's day**.

He had spoken of Christ's return as near because **its certainty** makes it ever relevant, not because its date had been revealed.

This teaching led Paul to foretell **remarkable events in the later history of the Christian church**, demonstrating the prophetic gift with which God had equipped him.

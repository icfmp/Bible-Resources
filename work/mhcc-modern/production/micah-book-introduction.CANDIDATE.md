# Micah — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

God raised up Micah to **support Isaiah and confirm his prophecies**.

Micah called the people to repentance through **warnings of coming judgment** and **promises of mercy**.

**Micah chapter 5** contains an especially remarkable summary of prophecies concerning **the Messiah**.

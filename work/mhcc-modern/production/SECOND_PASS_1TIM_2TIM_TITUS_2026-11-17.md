## Eight precise, reversible sentence-level editorial changes

These revisions occurred **only in the unshipped modern derivative**, with individual manuscript editorial notes pointing to the relevant witness. The published SWORD original is untouched.

1. **1 Tim. 2:1–7:** a narrowed “political party” paraphrase was replaced with unrestricted nation, religious group, social rank or party.
2. **1 Tim. 2:8–15:** “faith and holiness” were removed as an invented gloss in Henry's specific childbearing sentence; his own condition, continuing in sobriety, was restored.
3. **1 Tim. 3:14–16:** an editorially qualified Jew/Gentile contrast was replaced by Henry's original unqualified expression; this remains Henry's claim, not a new historical judgment.
4. **1 Tim. 4:1–5:** restored the explicit testimony of the Holy Spirit in **both Testaments**.
5. **1 Tim. 6:6–10:** restored the explicit **nature-with-little versus grace-with-less** exhortation.
6. **1 Tim. 6:17–21:** restored the distinct image of those lifting their eyes in torment, not merely a generic judgment summary.
7. **2 Tim. 2:1–7:** restored Henry's separate warning that doing good must be done in such a manner that good is not discredited.
8. **Titus 2:11–15:** restored the explicit instruction to regard God in Christ as object of **hope and worship**.

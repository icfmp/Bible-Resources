# Ecclesiastes — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

The title **Ecclesiastes** means **“The Preacher.”**

Here God's wisdom preaches to us through **Solomon**, whom Henry identifies as the author.

Henry presents the book as a record written by Solomon near the end of his life, when he had come to understand his own sin and foolishness.

Solomon described his experience for the benefit of others; Henry calls the book **the record of his repentance**.

His conclusion concerning earthly goods was that they are all **vanity and vexation of spirit**.

This book teaches the emptiness of the world and its inability to make us happy. It also exposes sin's evil and the misery toward which sin inevitably leads.

No created good can finally satisfy the soul.

**True happiness is found in God alone.**

Through the teaching of the blessed Holy Spirit, this truth must lead the heart to **Jesus Christ**.

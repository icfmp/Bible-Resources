# Jonah — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Jonah came from **Galilee** (**2 Kings 14:25**).

His miraculous rescue from the fish made him **a type of our blessed Lord Jesus Christ**. Jesus himself referred to the event, thereby affirming that the narrative is true.

For **the almighty Creator and Sustainer of life**, everything described in the book was possible.

The example of the people of **Nineveh** shows the abundance of God's patience and forbearance toward sinners.

The book also presents a sharp contrast between **God's mercy and goodness** and **his servant's disobedience, impatience and irritability**.

Those who know their own hearts best will understand its lessons most deeply.

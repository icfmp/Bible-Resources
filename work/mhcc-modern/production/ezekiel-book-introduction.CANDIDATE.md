# Ezekiel — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

**Ezekiel** was a priest who was taken captive to **Chaldea with Jehoiachin**.

Henry believes all Ezekiel's prophecies were delivered there, at a place **north of Babylon**.

Their chief purpose was to **comfort the prophet's fellow exiles**.

Ezekiel was called to warn of terrible disasters coming upon **Judah**, especially judgment against **false prophets**, as well as against **neighboring nations**.

He also announced the future restoration of **Israel and Judah** from their places of dispersion and their blessed condition in later days **under the Messiah**.

Much teaching about **Christ** appears in this book, particularly toward its end.

## Six separately documented source-fidelity interventions

1. 2 Peter 1:16–21 — from hell rather than vague deserved judgment.
2. 2 Peter 2:1–9 — judgment alongside the devil and his angels.
3. 2 Peter 2:17–22 — original mist-of-darkness image explicitly in the world to come.
4. 1 John 4:7–13 — full strict justice / hopeless misery wording restored in its conditional context.
5. 1 John 5:6–8 — independent **modern-editor** label concerning “received text tradition” removed from Henry's own commentary text and documented in editorial review notes, without altering any Bible verse or Henry's recorded Trinitarian exposition.
6. Jude 1:8–16 — original perpetual blackness-of-darkness conclusion explicitly restored.

Every modified modern manuscript carries its own before/after justification and direct CCEL secondary-witness link. No Bible verse was invented or rewritten. Original published MHCC remains unchanged.

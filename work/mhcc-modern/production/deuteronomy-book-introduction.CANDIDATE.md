# Deuteronomy — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

Deuteronomy repeats much of the history and many of the laws found in the preceding three books.

Moses delivered this teaching to Israel shortly before his death.

He spoke it aloud so that it would deeply affect the people, and he wrote it down so that it would remain with them.

The generation to whom the law was first given had died.

A new generation had taken its place, and God wanted Moses himself to repeat the law as they prepared to possess Canaan.

The book shows God's extraordinary love for his church.

For his own mercy's sake, he continually preserved his people and intended that his name should still be invoked among them.

The whole book also displays Moses' love for Israel and makes him a remarkable picture of the Lord Jesus Christ.

May we apply its appeals and encouragements to our own consciences, so that we are moved to grateful, believing obedience to God's commands.

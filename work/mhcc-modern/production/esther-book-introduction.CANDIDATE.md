# Esther — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

The book of Esther shows God's care for the Jews who had been scattered among the peoples of a foreign empire.

He preserved them wonderfully when they faced destruction.

Although **God's name does not appear in the book**, his providential hand can be seen in the smallest events that contributed to the deliverance of his people.

The events belong historically between **Ezra chapters 6 and 7**.

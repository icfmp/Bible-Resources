## Editorial rules
- Modernize genuinely archaic vocabulary, idioms, syntax and punctuation, but retain effective original phrasing; prefer closer wording when equally intelligible.
- Preserve **all** arguments, images, examples, doctrinal positions, cautions/certainties, exhortations, intentional repetition and biblical references. No new explanation, new doctrine, loss or interpretation correction.
- Keep Henry's pastoral and literary voice. Never convert theology into generic, casual prose.
- Improve paragraphing and add **only justified**, source-faithful headings without editorial reinterpretation.
- Distinguish direct Scripture quotations, biblical paraphrase and allusions. Retain their original argumentative role; never silently substitute a newer Bible translation or mechanically strip quoted Scripture.
- Preserve exact original SWORD entry keys, annotations/ranges, links and genuine introduction content. Chapter tracking is an editorial workflow unit, **not a licence to merge or alter distinct source keys**.

## Per-chapter mandatory acceptance
1. **A — Draft:** load and record **all** source content relevant to the biblical chapter from the exact pinned published SWORD edition, including independently identified introductions and overlapping/ranged native entries; characterize structure; modernize in separate source file(s).
2. **B — Independent audit:** compare to the original at entry/span level, checking every point, argument, illustration, allusion, doctrine, emphasis, scripture link/target, quote, boundary, heading and paragraph. Confirm contemporary natural English without excessive rewriting. A different review pass must actively identify errors, not auto-approve.
3. **Technical checks:** nonempty expected units; immutable canonical references/keys; original-to-derivative mapping and zero unsupported additions/omissions; encoding/markup validity, duplicates, abnormal length changes, SWORD-compatible source shape. Technical green checks do not prove semantic fidelity.
4. **C — Correct/retest/validate:** when all checks pass, store provenance and audit evidence, commit **exactly one validated chapter per commit** (e.g. `feat(mhcc-modern): modernize Genesis 1`), push to dedicated branch and **only then** tick its checkbox here.
5. If blocked after bounded fixes, preserve an honest draft/evidence, leave checkbox **unchecked**, record precise unresolved objections and proceed to the next chapter without pretending validation.

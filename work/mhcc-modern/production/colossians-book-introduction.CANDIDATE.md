# Colossians — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

This letter was sent because difficulties had arisen among the Colossians, **probably through the influence of false teachers**. Those difficulties led them to seek Paul's guidance.

The letter's purpose is to show that **all hope of human redemption rests on Jesus Christ**, in whom alone are **complete fullness, perfection and sufficiency**.

Paul warned the Colossians against both **teachers who wanted to impose Jewish observances** and notions arising from **worldly wisdom, human inventions and traditions**. All of these could undermine complete dependence upon Christ.

The **first two chapters** teach the beliefs Christians must hold. The **last two chapters** teach the conduct those beliefs require: **the doctrine of faith and the duties of Christian life** for salvation.

# Ruth — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

This book gives excellent examples of faith, devotion to God, patience, humility, diligence and loving-kindness in the ordinary events of life.

It also shows how carefully God's providence attends to even our smallest concerns.

That should encourage us to place our full trust in him.

We may read Ruth as a beautiful and natural picture of human life, as a detailed record of important events, and as part of God's plan of redemption.

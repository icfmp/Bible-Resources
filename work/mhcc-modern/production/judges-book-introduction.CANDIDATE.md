# Judges — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

The book of Judges tells Israel's history during the time of the judges.

These were deliverers whom God raised up at particular times to rescue Israel from oppressors, reform the nation's religious life and administer justice to the people.

In this book, God's people appear less prosperous and less devoted to him than we might have expected.

Nevertheless, many believers remained among them, and the worship of the tabernacle continued.

This history demonstrates the repeated warnings and predictions Moses had given.

We should pay close attention to it, for it is full of important instruction.

# Jude — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Jude's letter addresses everyone who believes the gospel.

It appears intended to guard Christians against **false teachers** who had begun entering the church and spreading dangerous beliefs.

These teachers reduced Christianity to a **nominal confession and outward profession**, denying that believers must live holy lives.

They encouraged sinful conduct while flattering people with **the hope of eternal life**.

The apostle exposes the deceivers' character and announces their judgment, then closes with **warnings, encouragement and counsel for believers**.

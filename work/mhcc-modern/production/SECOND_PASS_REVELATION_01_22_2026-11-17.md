## Seven exact restored passages (Git commits and source witnesses in manuscript review notes)

1. **Rev 1:4–8:** reintroduced Henry’s vivid warning that apostasy can *wound and crucify* Christ, absent in shortened modern prose.
2. **Rev 2:8–11:** reintroduced Henry’s description of the permanent second death as dying and *always dying* rather than only saying “everlasting.”
3. **Rev 3:14–22:** restored the original statement of people in **hell** who once thought themselves near heaven, replacing softened “perished.”
4. **Rev 14:6–13:** restored the specific eternal misery of **both soul and body** in Henry’s judgment warning.
5. **Rev 16:8–11:** restored **through all eternity** in Henry’s claim about guilt and misery.
6. **Rev 18:9–19:** restored Henry’s precise **coffin, worm and unquenchable fire** image.
7. **Rev 19:11–21:** restored the image of the **burning lake** as the final destination of those blindly following the majority into sin.

All are in the **unreleased modern draft manuscript files**, annotated within each source file. No changes to released source MHCC, Scripture text, versification, or original authorial verse/group keys.

## Source-authorial interpretive and structural distinctions retained

- Henry’s historicist identification of **the pagan Roman Empire, papal Rome, Turkish power, Reformation and 1260-year witnesses** remains **attributed to Henry**, with his uncertainty or speculative status preserved where he expresses it. The rewritten text **does not** promote those interpretations into categorical Scripture exegesis or endorse anti-Catholic or anti-Turkish hostility in the reader’s voice.
- **Revelation 7:** printed CCEL outline separates 1–3 and 4–8, but the actual CCEL authorial exposition groups 1–8. The modern edition keeps the authorial **1–8** passage in one section, while preserving the printed outline separately.
- **Revelation 8:** printed CCEL outline has several sub-parts but the actual two expositions cover **1–6** and **7–13**. They remain distinct from the outline.
- **Revelation 11, 12, 13, 14, 17, 22:** printed outlines and actual prose divisions are not conflated. **Revelation 22** has one authored closing unit covering verses **20 and 21** with distinct source references; no new separated commentary is invented.
- The [separate complete Revelation introduction candidate](revelation-book-introduction.CANDIDATE.md) preserves the author’s two principal divisions (*things which are* and *things which shall be hereafter*), the continuing pastoral value of the seven church letters, and the purpose of strengthening the persecuted. Original native I/K ownership/placement of the preface is still **unknown**.

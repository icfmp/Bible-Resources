# Leviticus — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

God established different kinds of offerings and sacrifices to assure his people that their offenses would be forgiven when they brought those offerings with true faith and obedience.

He also appointed the priests and Levites, giving instructions about their clothing, responsibilities, conduct and provision for their needs.

He specified the festivals they must celebrate and when they were to observe them.

Through all these sacrifices and ceremonies, God showed that the wages of sin is death.

He also taught that there can be no forgiveness of sins apart from the blood of Christ, the innocent Lamb of God.

# 1 John — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

This letter explains **Christianity's fundamental doctrines and their practical consequences**.

It appears to have been written especially to confront false, ungodly beliefs and behavior.

Above all, it refutes attempts to diminish **Christ's full deity**, or to deny the reality and saving power of his sufferings and death as **an atoning sacrifice**.

It also opposes the claim that people saved by grace no longer need to obey God's commands.

Everyone who professes to know and enjoy fellowship with God is called **to walk in holiness rather than sin**.

An outward profession is empty when a person's conduct does not show the evidence of holy life.

The letter therefore encourages genuine Christians to remain in fellowship with **the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ**, persevere in true faith and grow in purity.

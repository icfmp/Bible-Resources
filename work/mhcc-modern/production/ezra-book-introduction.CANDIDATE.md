# Ezra — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

This book records the fulfillment of **Jeremiah's prophecy** concerning the return of the Jews from Babylon.

Its history especially teaches that every good work will meet opposition from enemies and may be damaged by the wrongdoing of friends.

Yet God will make his cause prevail, despite all obstacles and adversaries.

The restoration of the Jewish people was an event of extraordinary importance.

It helped preserve true religion in the world and prepared the way for the coming of **the great Deliverer, Jesus Christ our Lord**.

# Exodus — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

The book of Exodus tells how the children of Israel became a church and a nation.

Until now, we have seen true religion expressed mainly in family life.

Here we begin to see how it shapes the affairs of kingdoms and nations.

The name *Exodus* means “departure.”

The central event in the book is Israel's departure from Egypt and the bondage it suffered there.

That deliverance shows the fulfillment of several promises and prophecies God had given Abraham concerning his descendants.

It also foreshadows the church's journey through the wilderness of this world toward the heavenly Canaan, where it will find eternal rest.

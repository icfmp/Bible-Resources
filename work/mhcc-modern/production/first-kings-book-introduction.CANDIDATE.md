# 1 Kings — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

The history in this book describes the kingdoms of **Judah and Israel**.

But its special concern is the way **God's kingdom** was present among the people.

It is therefore sacred history.

The events it records occurred in an earlier period than the events described in ordinary histories.

This account also teaches us far more and is of greater interest than ordinary historical works.

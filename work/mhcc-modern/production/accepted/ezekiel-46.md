# Ezekiel 46 — Modern English

## Original source structure

**No separate authorial heading; one continuous commentary.**

## Worship and Christian freedom

This chapter describes the appointed worship of **the prince and the people** and the gifts the prince may give **to his sons and servants**.

Our Lord has commanded us to perform many duties.

But he has also left many matters to our choice.

Those who delight in his commandments may do **more than the minimum** for his glory, without **binding their own consciences** unnecessarily or imposing unsuitable rules on others.

Yet this freedom must never become an excuse to **neglect daily worship**.

Nor may we fail to apply **the sacrifice of the Lamb of God** to our own souls for **pardon, peace and salvation**.

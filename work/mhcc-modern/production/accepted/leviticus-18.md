# Leviticus 18 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Unlawful marriages and fleshly lusts.**

## Prohibitions against corrupt practices

This chapter forbids God's people to imitate the corrupt customs of the surrounding nations.

Its commands prohibit incest, degrading sexual acts and cruel idolatry.

The ruin of the Canaanites is given as a warning reinforcing these prohibitions.

God is giving moral commands here.

The most effective protection against gross sin is to keep closely and consistently to what God has appointed.

Only God's grace can preserve us from falling.

We should seek that grace through the means by which he has promised to give it.

God does not abandon people to their heart's sinful desires until they have first abandoned him and his service.

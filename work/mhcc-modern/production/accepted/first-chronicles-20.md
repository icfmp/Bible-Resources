# 1 Chronicles 20 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's wars.**

## David's wars

The Lord may severely correct the sins of his believing people, but he will not abandon them to their enemies.

His help will overcome every advantage in numbers and strength held by those who oppose his Israel.

Everyone who trusts in Christ will become **more than a conqueror** through the One who loves them.

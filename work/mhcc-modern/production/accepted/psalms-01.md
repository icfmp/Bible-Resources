# Psalm 1 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The holiness and happiness of a godly man. | 1–3 |
| The sinfulness and misery of a wicked man, The ground and reason of both. | 4–6 |

## The godly person and God's word (verses 1–3)

To **meditate on God's word** means to speak with ourselves about the great truths it contains, applying our minds closely and thinking about them steadily.

God's word should guide our actions and provide our comfort.

We should keep it continually in our minds **both day and night**.

No time is unsuitable for meditating upon it.

## The ungodly person and final judgment (verses 4–6)

The ungodly differ from the righteous both in **character** and in **condition**.

Instead of delighting in God's law, they accept wicked advice, walk in sinful ways and finally settle into the position of those who mock what is holy.

They produce no good fruit.

The righteous resemble useful, fruitful trees.

But the ungodly resemble **chaff blown away by wind**—the worthless material a farmer wants removed from the threshing floor.

They have no spiritual value before God, however highly they regard themselves.

Every new temptation can blow them in a different direction.

Chaff may remain mixed with wheat for a time, but **the One who holds the winnowing fan** will completely cleanse his threshing floor.

Those who have made themselves like chaff through sin and folly will be found to be so when God's judgment comes like a storm and fire.

The final outcome of the ungodly is fixed if they remain in their sin.

Yet someone who recognizes guilt and misery may enter the company of the righteous through **Jesus Christ, the living way**.

In him that sinner becomes **a new creation**.

Desires, pleasures, hopes, fears, sorrows, friendships, occupations, thoughts, words and deeds are all changed.

Through divine grace, the person takes on a new life and character, as the soul is increasingly transformed into the Redeemer's image.

How different the characters and final outcomes of the righteous and ungodly are!

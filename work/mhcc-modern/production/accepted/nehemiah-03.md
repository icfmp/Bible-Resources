# Nehemiah 3 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem.**

## Rebuilding Jerusalem's walls

The work was divided so that everyone knew the particular task assigned to them and could attend to it with a desire to do it well.

They competed only in **doing as much good as possible for the community**, not in pursuing conflicting interests or quarrelling with one another.

Every Israelite should lend a hand in building up Jerusalem.

People in high positions should never consider a task beneath their dignity when it will benefit their country. **Even some women joined in the work.**

Some worked on sections opposite their own houses, and one worked opposite his room.

When a great common task needs doing, everyone should begin with the part within their own reach.

If everyone swept the space outside their own door, the whole street would be clean. If everyone repaired one part, the entire wall would be repaired.

Some who had finished their own assignments helped their neighbors finish theirs.

The broken walls and piles of debris around Jerusalem picture the desperate condition of the world around us.

The number and malice of the people hindering the construction give us some small idea of the enemies Christians meet while doing God's work.

Everyone must begin **at home**. By advancing God's work in our own souls, we can contribute most effectively to the good of Christ's church.

May the Lord stir up his people's hearts to set aside petty disputes and consider worldly interests insignificant compared with rebuilding Jerusalem's walls and defending truth and godliness against open enemies.

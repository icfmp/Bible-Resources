## Rehoboam is forbidden to fight Israel (verses 1–12)

A few kind words might have prevented Rehoboam's subjects from rebelling, but all the military power of his kingdom could not bring them back.

When God has made his purpose known to us, it is useless to fight against it.

Even people without genuine faith may sometimes show enough regard for God's word to be restrained from wrong actions they would otherwise be inclined to commit.

## Priests and Levites find refuge in Judah (verses 13–23)

When the priests and Levites came to Jerusalem, faithful Israelites followed them.

People whose hearts were set upon seeking the Lord God of Israel left their ancestral land and moved to Jerusalem.

They wanted free access to God's altar and freedom from the temptation to worship the golden calves.

**What is best for our souls is best for us.**

In every decision, we should give spiritual advantages a higher priority than outward convenience.

God's faithful people should seek fellowship with his faithful ministers.

When we demonstrate willingness to give up worldly interests as far as Christ and his gospel require, we have evidence that we truly are his disciples.

It also benefits a nation to protect true religion and people devoted to it.

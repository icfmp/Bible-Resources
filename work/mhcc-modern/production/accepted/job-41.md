# Job 41 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Concerning Leviathan.**

## God illustrates his power through Leviathan

The Lord continued showing Job his own weakness and God's unlimited power through a description of **Leviathan**.

Henry reports that readers debated whether the creature was **a whale or a crocodile**.

He does not settle the question.

God demonstrated that Job was unable to subdue this mighty animal, then displayed something of the divine power that had made it.

If the language used about Leviathan shows such frightening strength, what language could fully express **the power of God's wrath**?

Let us recognize our own sinfulness and weakness and honor the majesty of God.

Let us take up the place and responsibilities he has assigned to us, cease depending proudly on our own wisdom, and give every part of the glory to **our gracious God and Savior**.

Every good gift comes from him and has been entrusted to us for a purpose.

Remembering both the Giver and his purpose, may we **walk humbly with the Lord**.

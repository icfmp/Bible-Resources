# Isaiah 32 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Times of peace and happiness. | 1–8 |
| An interval of trouble, yet comfort and blessings in the end. | 9–20 |

## Christ the righteous King and shelter (verses 1–8)

**Jesus Christ is the righteous King** promised here, and his faithful disciples share in the benefits of his reign.

The consolation and grace of his Spirit resemble **rivers flowing through a dry land**.

A traveler crossing a desert finds refreshing shade beneath a great overhanging rock.

Likewise, Christ's **power, truth and love** provide believers with their only lasting protection and refreshment as they journey toward heaven.

**Christ bore the storm himself to keep it from falling upon us.**

Let frightened sinners flee to him, for he alone can shelter and renew them in every trial.

Notice how much effort wicked people put into wrongdoing.

They work hard at it, direct their hearts toward it and develop clever plans to accomplish it.

Yet our comfort is that they cannot do **more harm than God permits**.

Let us pray to be freed from selfishness.

A truly generous soul thinks of generous things concerning God.

Such a person asks him for wisdom and discernment, the comfort of his presence, the influence of his Spirit and, in due time, **the full enjoyment of his glory**.

## Trouble overcome through the outpouring of the Spirit (verses 9–20)

When a people have greatly provoked the holy Lord, difficult times should not surprise them.

Many careless people refuse generosity while using their possessions for self-indulgence.

We deserve to lose earthly provisions when we use them chiefly to nourish sinful desires.

Let those who live in that way be afraid and awaken.

A genuinely blessed age arrives **through the outpouring of the Spirit from above**.

Only then will lasting good times come.

Henry understands the present condition of the Jewish people as continuing **until a fuller outpouring of the Holy Spirit**, which he still expects.

**Peace and quietness grow from righteousness**.

Lasting satisfaction exists only in true religion.

Real holiness is real happiness even now. In heaven, where holiness becomes perfect, happiness will also be perfect forever.

The good seed of **God's word** will be scattered widely.

Divine grace will water it, and patient, hardworking servants will be sent into the Lord's field.

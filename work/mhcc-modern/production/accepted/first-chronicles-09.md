# 1 Chronicles 9 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Genealogies.**

## The people who returned from Babylon

One purpose of preserving these genealogies was to guide the Jews returning from captivity.

The records helped them identify **which people to join** and **where to live**.

The chapter also describes the good order restored to the work of worship after the return from Babylon.

Everyone knew the duty entrusted to them.

Work is more likely to be done well when people know the responsibilities of their own position and take them seriously.

**God is a God of order.**

The earthly temple thus pictured **the heavenly sanctuary**, where worshipers never cease praising God day or night (Revelation 4:8).

Blessed be his name!

In heaven believers will praise him not in rotating shifts, but **all together and without interruption**, day and night.

May the Lord make each of us fit to share the inheritance of the saints in light.

# 2 Chronicles 27 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Jotham's reign in Judah.**

## Jotham's reign

The people continued acting corruptly.

Perhaps Jotham did not do all he could to reform the land.

Even a very good person may lack the courage and zeal to perform duties that are within reach.

The people's own conduct certainly deserved blame.

Jotham prospered and became powerful.

The more steadfastly we follow God, the stronger we become—both in resisting evil and in doing good.

The Lord often removes wise and devout rulers and gives a people others whose folly and wickedness punish them for having failed to appreciate earlier blessings.

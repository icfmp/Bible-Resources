# 1 Thessalonians 1 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The faith, love, and patience of the Thessalonians, are evident tokens of their election which was manifested in the power with which the gospel came to them. | 1–5 |
| Its powerful and exemplary effects upon their hearts and lives. | 6–10 |

## Faith, love and hope show God's powerful work (verses 1–5)

Every good gift comes from God, and sinners have no lasting good to expect except **from God through Christ**.

Because God has become **our Father through Jesus**, we can seek from him the very best blessings.

We should pray not only for ourselves but continually **remember other believers before God**.

Genuine faith **acts**. It changes both the heart and the conduct of the person who believes.

Faith works through **love for God and love for neighbors**.

Well-grounded hope of eternal life produces **patient endurance**.

Sincerity is shown when, in everything we do, we seek above all **God's approval**.

How can we recognize the evidence of God's electing grace?

Not merely through **religious words on our lips**, but through the gospel's **power within our hearts**.

That power puts sinful desires to death, loosens our attachment to the world and lifts our thoughts toward heavenly things.

Unless the Holy Spirit comes **with God's word**, its words remain a **dead letter** to the hearer.

The Thessalonians received the gospel through **the power of the Holy Spirit**.

They became so convinced of its truth that doubts and objections could not easily unsettle them.

They were willing to give up everything for Jesus and **entrust their souls and eternal destiny to the gospel's revelation**.

## Conversion gives visible testimony and hope in Christ's return (verses 6–10)

When careless, ignorant or immoral people turn from sinful habits and relationships to **trust and obey the Lord Jesus**, their changed lives become a powerful witness.

They begin living **soberly, righteously and devotedly toward God**.

Such a transformation speaks for itself.

Old Testament believers waited for **the Messiah's first coming**.

Christians now wait for **his return**.

He has not yet returned, but **God has raised him from the dead**.

This resurrection gives everyone an assurance that Christ **will come to judge the world**.

He came first **to purchase salvation**.

When he comes again, he will bring salvation to its full completion, granting **final deliverance from the wrath still to come**.

Let everyone flee without delay from that wrath and take refuge **in Jesus Christ and his salvation**.

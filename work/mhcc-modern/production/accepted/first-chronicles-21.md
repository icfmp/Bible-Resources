# 1 Chronicles 21 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's numbering the people.**

## David's census and the place of the temple

Chronicles does not recount David's sin concerning Uriah or the troubles that followed it; those events were not necessary to the particular subjects being addressed here.

But this book does recount David's sin in numbering the people.

The atonement made for that sin revealed **the place where the temple would be built**.

God's command that David build an altar was a blessed sign of reconciliation.

The Lord also showed that he accepted David's offerings on that altar.

This points to Christ, who was made sin and a curse for us.

The Lord was pleased to crush him so that through him God might be, not a consuming fire against us, but a God reconciled to us.

It is good to continue attending the ordinances in which we have already experienced signs of God's presence and discovered that he truly is with us.

We can say, “God graciously met me here; therefore I will continue to expect to meet him here.”

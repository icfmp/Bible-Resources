## Solomon appoints his officials (verses 1–19)

Solomon's wisdom surely showed itself in his choice of leading officials.

Several of them had also served during David's reign.

Solomon arranged the kingdom's contributions so that no single district was exhausted by the responsibility of supplying the royal household.

Every region gave its proper share.

## Solomon's kingdom enjoys peace (verses 20–28)

Israel's royal crown never shone more brightly than it did when Solomon wore it.

Peace surrounded his kingdom.

In this it pictured the kingdom of **the Messiah**, who would receive the nations as his inheritance and to whom princes would come in worship.

The spiritual peace, joy and holy security enjoyed by all faithful subjects of the Lord Jesus were pictured by Israel's earthly happiness.

But **God's kingdom is not food and drink**.

It consists of something infinitely better: **righteousness, peace and joy in the Holy Spirit**.

The enormous amount of food prepared for Solomon's household shows the crowds who served and visited him.

Christ is greater than Solomon.

He feeds all his people, not with perishable bread, but with the food that endures to eternal life.

## Solomon's God-given wisdom (verses 29–34)

Solomon's wisdom brought him more honor than his wealth.

He possessed what Scripture calls **largeness of heart**, meaning extraordinary mental capacity.

He received both wisdom and the ability to communicate it effectively.

Those with great gifts should have equally generous hearts, using their abilities for the benefit of others.

How much wisdom and knowledge is wasted when people do not share what they possess!

Yet the holy Scriptures contain every kind of knowledge necessary for salvation.

People came from faraway places because they valued knowledge more highly than their neighbors.

Solomon pictured **Jesus Christ**, in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.

Those riches are hidden **for us**: Christ has been made our wisdom by God.

His fame will spread throughout the earth.

People of every nation will come to him, learn from him, take his gentle yoke upon themselves and find rest for their souls.

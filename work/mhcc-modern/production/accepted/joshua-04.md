# Joshua 4 — Modern English

## Memorial stones taken from the river (verses 1–9)

The Lord's works are so worthy of remembrance, and human hearts so prone to forget them, that we need frequent reminders.

Such reminders honor God.

They benefit us and our children.

God therefore commanded Israel to prepare a memorial of the Jordan crossing.

## The crossing is completed (verses 10–19)

The priests carrying the ark did not move until the Lord directed them.

No one should grow weary of waiting while the signs of God's presence remain with them, even in the depths of hardship.

God also honored Joshua.

People are respected in the best way and for the best reason when others recognize that the Lord is with them and that they live before him.

## The memorial at Gilgal (verses 20–24)

Parents should teach their children about God's words and works from an early age, training them to follow his ways.

In everything we teach children, we should lead them to fear and honor the Lord.

Sincere faith is the best education.

We are just as obligated as Israel to praise our God's kindness.

Should we not establish memorials of gratitude toward the Lord, who has brought us through danger and distress in so many remarkable ways?

He has helped us just as truly as he helped the faithful people of earlier generations.

How ungrateful and unthinking we are when we fail to recognize his hand in our repeated deliverances!

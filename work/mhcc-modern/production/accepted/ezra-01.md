# Ezra 1 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The proclamation of Cyrus for the rebuilding of the temple. | 1–4 |
| The people provide for their return. | 5–11 |

## Cyrus announces the return (verses 1–4)

**The Lord stirred the spirit of Cyrus**.

The hearts of kings are in God's hands.

God governs the world by influencing human hearts, and whenever people do good, he is the One who moves them to do it.

During the Jewish people's captivity, God used them especially to draw the nations' attention toward himself.

Cyrus expected that those Jews who were able would willingly contribute offerings toward God's temple.

He also directed that they receive material support from his kingdom.

Those who say they wish the temple well should also act for its good.

## The people prepare to return (verses 5–11)

The same God who moved Cyrus to proclaim freedom moved the captives to take advantage of that freedom.

Some were tempted to stay in Babylon.

Others had the courage to return, because the Lord stirred their hearts by his Spirit and grace.

**Whatever good we do comes through God's grace**.

By nature, our affections tend downward toward earthly things.

When they rise toward what is good, God is the One who raises them.

The gospel's invitations resemble Cyrus's proclamation.

Those enslaved by sin may be set free through **Jesus Christ**.

Anyone willing to return to God through repentance and faith finds that Christ has opened the way out of bondage into **the glorious freedom of God's children**.

Many who hear the good news still choose to stay in Babylon, loving their sins and refusing the risks of a holy life.

But others overcome obstacles and are willing to follow, because God has lifted their spirits above the world and sinful desires.

Thus the heavenly Canaan will be populated even though many perish in their Babylon.

The gospel's invitation will not be made in vain.

The return from exile therefore represents **the redemption of sinners through Jesus Christ**.

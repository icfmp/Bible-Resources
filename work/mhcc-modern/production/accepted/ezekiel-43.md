# Ezekiel 43 — Modern English

## Original source structure

**No separate authorial heading; one continuous commentary.**

## The glory of God and the sacrifice of Christ

After Ezekiel had surveyed God's temple, he saw **the glory of God**.

When the **Holy Spirit** shows us **Christ crucified** and the gifts God freely gives us through him, we become ashamed of our sins.

That humility prepares us to understand more deeply the **mysteries of redeeming love**.

All of Scripture should be opened and applied so that people recognize their sins and repent.

We are **not now to offer sacrifices to atone for sin**.

Through **one offering**, Christ has **perfected forever those who are being sanctified** (**Hebrews 10:14**).

Nevertheless, we still need the **sprinkling of his blood** whenever we approach God the Father.

Even our best acts of worship and service are acceptable to him **only through the blood that cleanses us from every sin**.

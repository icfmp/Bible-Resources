# Psalm 70 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The speedy destruction of the wicked, and the preservation of the godly.**

## Prayer in urgent trouble

This psalm repeats almost the same words as **the last five verses of Psalm 40**.

Here we see **Jesus Christ** portrayed in poverty and distress.

At the same time the words announce righteous and fearsome judgment on his enemies—**Jewish, pagan and antichristian opponents** in Henry's historical and theological language.

The psalm also appeals for the joy and happiness of Christ's friends, to the honor of his Father.

Let us apply its teaching to our own troubles, remembering them and their **sinful causes** with faith rather than carelessness or resentment.

**Urgent trials should awaken earnest prayer**.

# Psalm 114 — Modern English

## Original heading

**An exhortation to fear God.**

## God's presence in redemption and renewal

We should recognize **God's power and goodness** in delivering Israel.

Those events point toward the far greater wonder of **our redemption through Christ**.

They should encourage us and others to trust God in the greatest difficulties.

When Christ comes to save his people, he releases them from **sin and Satan's power**.

He separates them from a godless world, forms them into his own people and becomes their King.

No sea or river Jordan is too wide or deep for the Lord.

When the appointed time comes, **he can divide it and drive its waters back**.

The same power established **the Christian church in the world**.

Satan and the powers of idolatry trembled before it.

But the most personal application concerns **the Spirit's work within a heart**.

What turns the current of a regenerated person's affections?

Why do sinful desires and corrupt habits retreat?

How are prejudices removed and an entire person made new?

**It happens through the presence of God's Spirit**.

At the Lord's presence even mountains and the whole earth may tremble, for creation has been placed under a curse through human sin.

God both protected and provided for Israel through miracles.

One was the stream of water flowing from a hard rock.

**That rock pointed to Christ**.

The Son of God, **the Rock of Ages**, gave himself in death to open a fountain that washes away sins and supplies believers with **water of life and consolation**.

No promised blessing is too great for them to expect from his love.

But sinners should fear their righteous and holy Judge.

Let us prepare now to meet God so we may stand before him with confidence when he comes.

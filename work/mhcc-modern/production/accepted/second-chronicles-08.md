# 2 Chronicles 8 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Solomon's buildings and trade.**

## Solomon's works, worship and trade

Governing a family in the fear of God sometimes requires more wisdom and determination than governing a kingdom with honor.

That task becomes harder when one's spouse is **a hindrance rather than a fitting helper**.

Solomon maintained the appointed sacrifices according to Moses' law.

Building the altar and seeing fire descend from heaven would have been useless if sacrifices were not then continually brought.

We, too, are required to bring **spiritual sacrifices every day and every week**.

A settled pattern of devotion is good.

When temple service had been properly organized, the house of the Lord was said to be **complete**.

The work of worship, not merely the building, was the essential matter.

The temple was not truly finished until those duties were established.

Canaan was a rich land, yet its people needed to obtain **gold from Ophir**.

Israel was a wise nation, yet relied on the **king of Tyre's sailors** for expertise at sea.

**Grace is better wealth than gold**, and knowledge of God and his law is better than worldly expertise.

Let the children of this world struggle over its toys.

As God's children, let us store treasure in heaven, because our hearts will follow what we treasure.

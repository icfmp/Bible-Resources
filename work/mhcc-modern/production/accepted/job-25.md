# Job 25 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Bildad shows that man cannot be justified before God.**

## Bildad considers human impurity before God

Bildad no longer discussed the question of why wicked people sometimes prosper.

Instead, he spoke of the immense distance between **God's glory** and **human beings' weakness**.

He reminded Job of truths Job had not considered sufficiently.

Even human righteousness and holiness at their best are nothing compared with God's holiness (**Psalm 89:6**).

How can guilty and polluted people stand before a God so glorious and great?

We must be **born again by water and the Holy Spirit**.

We must continually turn to **the blood of Jesus Christ**, the fountain opened to cleanse sin (**Zechariah 13:1**).

Let us humble ourselves as weak and sinful creatures and abandon every confidence in our own merit.

Yet awareness of our unworthiness makes **Christ's condescension and love** shine even more brightly.

Every sinner he redeems will eternally glorify the riches of his mercy and the power of his grace.

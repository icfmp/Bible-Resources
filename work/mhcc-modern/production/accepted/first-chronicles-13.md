## David consults about the ark (verses 1–5)

David did not ask what magnificent or pleasurable thing he should now do. He asked what **godly** thing he should do, so that he might enjoy the comfort and benefit of God's sacred oracle.

He wanted the ark brought among the people to be a blessing to them.

Those who honor God also do what truly benefits themselves.

People beginning their lives in the world act wisely when they bring God's ark with them.

Those who begin with reverence for God are well placed to continue enjoying his favor.

## The removal of the ark and Uzza's death (verses 6–14)

Uzza's sin warns us to avoid presumption, rashness and irreverence when approaching holy things.

**A good intention never makes a wrong action right.**

His punishment teaches us not to treat God lightly when we approach him.

Yet through Jesus Christ we may come boldly to the throne of grace.

The ark brought death to Uzza; similarly, for some the gospel is an aroma leading from death to death.

But when we welcome the gospel with love for its truth, it becomes an aroma leading from life to life.

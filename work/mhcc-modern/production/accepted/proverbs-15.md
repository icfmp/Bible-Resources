# Proverbs 15 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — Gentle words calm anger

A just cause is better defended with gentleness than with a hot temper. Nothing provokes anger more quickly than **harsh words**.

## Verse 2 — Share knowledge for others' benefit

Those who possess knowledge ought to use it wisely **for the good of other people**.

## Verse 3 — Nothing is hidden from God

God sees every **secret sin, secret act of service and secret sorrow**. His knowledge comforts the faithful and warns the ungodly.

## Verse 4 — The healing power of speech

Kind and truthful speech brings healing to wounded consciences by comfort, convinces spiritually sick souls of their need, and reconciles people who have quarreled.

## Verse 5 — Do not let others walk toward ruin

When people reject instruction, we should continue to correct them faithfully rather than allow them to proceed undisturbed toward destruction.

## Verse 6 — Worldly wealth can multiply fears

The riches of those devoted to the world may increase their suspicion, stir their passions and make the fear of death still more distressing.

## Verse 7 — Spread good knowledge

We use knowledge well when we share it with others. Foolish people's hearts have little that is good to offer.

## Verses 8 and 9 — Christ's atonement and sincere obedience

The wicked try to place other things **in the place of Christ's atonement or of holy obedience**. The grace to pray rightly is **God's gift and the Spirit's work**, which is pleasing to him.

## Verse 10 — Do not reject reproof

People who hate correction and refuse to part with their sins will perish in them.

## Verse 11 — Even human thoughts are open

Nothing can be hidden from God—not even the deepest thoughts of the heart.

## Verse 12 — The scorner avoids self-examination

Someone who mocks holiness cannot endure seriously examining the condition of the heart.

## Verse 13 — The misery of a gloomy spirit

A continually ungrateful, impatient and darkened spirit—often nourished by pride and excessive attachment to worldly things—makes life difficult for oneself and others.

## Verse 14 — Continue growing in wisdom

Wise people seek further wisdom, growing in **grace and knowledge of Christ**. Worldly minds rest content while flattering themselves.

## Verse 15 — Comfort the suffering and rejoice reverently

People weighed down by suffering and sorrow deserve **pity, prayer and comfort**. Others serve God joyfully and find that joy strengthens obedience, yet they should still rejoice **with reverence**.

## Verses 16 and 17 — Enough with the Lord

God's people may possess enough while worldly observers think they have almost nothing. **The Lord's presence** is better than the worries, temptations and troubles that accompany ungodly wealth.

## Verse 18 — Be slow to anger

Patient people not only avoid starting quarrels, but can also **calm conflicts once they begin**.

## Verse 19 — Laziness invents obstacles

Those unwilling to work imagine excessive hardship or danger in their duties. Many also remain uncertain about their spiritual condition because they keep neglecting some known duty.

## Verse 20 — Honor aging parents

Contempt for an elderly father or mother reveals a person's own foolishness.

## Verse 21 — Wisdom shapes the whole life

Truly wise people seek to keep **thoughts, words and actions** orderly, sincere and holy.

## Verse 22 — Think before acting

People unwilling to take time and effort to consult and deliberate are unlikely to accomplish their plans.

## Verse 23 — Speak appropriately

Wisdom is needed to **fit our words to the occasion**.

## Verse 24 — Set the heart on heaven

A godly person sets affections upon the things above, and life's path leads toward them.

## Verse 25 — God opposes pride and supports the afflicted

Pride has destroyed multitudes, but **God upholds those who are suffering**.

## Verse 26 — God knows sinful thoughts

Wicked thoughts offend the Lord, who knows what is within the heart.

## Verse 27 — Greed troubles the family

Covetous people deprive their own households of peace and enjoyment. Greed often entices them into reckless projects that end in ruin.

## Verse 28 — Wisdom governs speech

A godly person's wise control over the tongue is evidence of genuine wisdom.

## Verse 29 — Rebellion creates distance

God withdraws his favor from people who openly defy him.

## Verse 30 — The joyful news of salvation

How wonderful it is for a humbled soul to hear **the good news of salvation through the Lord Jesus Christ**!

## Verse 31 — A kind rebuke is a gift

Faithful and friendly correction supports spiritual life and leads toward eternal life.

## Verse 32 — Do not sacrifice the soul for the body

Sinners undervalue their own souls and therefore prefer bodily pleasure even when it harms the soul.

## Verse 33 — Reverence, Scripture and grace

The fear of God moves us to search **the Scriptures reverently** and follow **the Holy Spirit's leading**. As we humbly depend on God's grace, we are exalted through **Christ's righteousness**.

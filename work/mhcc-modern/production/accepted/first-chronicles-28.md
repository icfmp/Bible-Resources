# 1 Chronicles 28 — Modern English

## David calls Israel and Solomon to know and serve God (verses 1–10)

During David's last illness, many of the leading priests and Levites were in Jerusalem.

When he found himself able to speak, David recalled his desire to build the temple, God's refusal of that plan, and the gracious purpose God had for Solomon.

He called them to remain steadfast in their duty and faithfulness to the Lord.

We cannot do our work properly without **resolution** and strength received from God's grace.

True religion has **two distinct parts**: **knowing God** and **worshiping God**.

David called Solomon to know the God of his father and serve him with a whole heart and willing mind.

God makes himself known through his works and his word.

Only divine revelation fully reveals his character: his providence, holy law, judgment on sinners, blessed gospel and the Spirit's work in all genuine believers.

A person governed only by natural human thinking cannot receive this knowledge.

But through God's revelation, we understand the worth of the Savior's **atonement** and the Spirit's **sanctifying work**, which move us to obey his commandments.

The sinner then takes the proper place **at the foot of the cross**, acknowledging guilt, helplessness and deserved judgment while expecting all necessary gifts from the free mercy and grace of God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Having been forgiven much, the repentant sinner learns to love much.

## David gives Solomon the temple plan (verses 11–21)

The temple was sacred and pointed toward **Christ**.

Its form had to be directed by God.

**Christ is the true temple**, **the church is the gospel temple**, and **heaven is the everlasting temple**.

All are established according to God's wise and eternal purpose for his glory and our good.

David gave Solomon the design so that he would follow God's rule.

He also prepared costly materials and utensils.

Then David encouraged Solomon to look to God first for help.

Do not be discouraged: the Lord will help you.

The God who cared for our fathers and enabled them to complete the service entrusted to their generation will not leave us while he still has work to accomplish **in us or through us**.

Good work prospers when everyone involved sincerely supports it.

Let us hope in God's mercy: if we seek him, he will be found by us.

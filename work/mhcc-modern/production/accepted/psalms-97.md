# Psalm 97 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The Lord Jesus reigns in power that cannot be resisted. | 1–7 |
| His care of his people, and his provision for them. | 8–12 |

## The irresistible reign of Christ (verses 1–7)

Many have already found happiness in Christ, yet **there is still room for others**.

All have good reason to rejoice in his reign.

We cannot fully search the depth of his purposes, but **righteousness and justice are the foundation of his throne**.

Christ's rule could be a cause of joy for everyone.

Yet for some it will become a cause of terror.

That is their own fault, because they reject him.

Even the most determined and fearless opposition will fail before the Lord.

**Jesus Christ will come and bring every form of idolatry to an end.**

## The Lord's care for those who love him (verses 8–12)

Faithful servants of God rightly rejoice when he is glorified.

Anything that honors him gives pleasure to his people.

God cares for their safety.

But the protection promised goes far beyond preserving their earthly lives.

The Lord will guard his people's **souls from sin, apostasy and despair**, even under their most severe trials.

He will rescue them from the power of the evil one and bring them safely into his heavenly kingdom.

Those who rejoice in Jesus Christ and his exaltation have **sources of joy prepared for them**.

People who sow in tears will reap with joy.

Joy is assured to those whose hearts are upright, while a hypocrite's joy lasts only briefly.

Sinners tremble at God's holiness, but his saints rejoice in it.

God hates sin, yet **freely loves the repentant sinner who believes in Christ**.

He will finally separate the person he loves from the sin he hates, sanctifying his people **completely in body, soul and spirit**.

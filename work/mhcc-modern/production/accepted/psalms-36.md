# Psalm 36 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The bad state of the wicked. | 1–4 |
| The goodness of God. | 5–12 |

## The condition of wicked people (verses 1–4)

This psalm should deepen our hatred of sin and our desire for **God's loving-kindness**.

The root of wickedness is contempt for God and the refusal to show him proper reverence. Another root is **self-deception**.

We should ask God every day to protect us from flattering ourselves.

Sin does terrible harm to the person practicing it, and for that reason should be hated. Yet sinners too easily regard it as harmless.

If people deceive themselves, it is not surprising when they deceive others. A person false to their own soul can scarcely be trusted by anybody.

It is wrong to do evil; it is worse to **plan evil deliberately**, organizing it with care.

When we willingly drive holy thoughts from our minds during quiet hours, Satan is ready to replace them with sinful imaginings.

Hardened sinners become so stubborn that they defend their actions as though they could justify them before God himself.

## The goodness of God (verses 5–12)

Human beings may refuse compassion, but **God's mercy remains available**. That is great comfort to believers.

God acts wisely and well. We do not now understand everything he does, but we will know enough at the proper time.

His loving-kindness is precious to those who trust him. They place themselves beneath his protection and find security and peace.

A gracious person always wants **more of God**, but never wants anything **greater than God**.

God's providential gifts allow believers to be content with what they possess.

His appointed ordinances are sweet and strengthen spiritual life, yet complete satisfaction is reserved for the world to come.

God prepares his people to desire those pleasures and fills them by his Spirit with **joy and peace in believing**.

He gives life to whom he wills. Whoever is willing may come and **take freely from the water of life**.

May we know, love and sincerely serve the Lord. Then no proud enemy on earth or power from hell can separate us from his love.

Faith looks ahead as though the promised things had already occurred. It shows us the Lord upon his throne of judgment and **the dominion of sin overthrown forever**.

# Psalm 12 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The psalmist begs help of God, because there were none among men whom he durst trust.**

## Seeking God amid moral decline

This Psalm provides wise thoughts and prayers for difficult times.

What makes a time truly bad?

Ask worldly-minded people, and they will mention **lack of money, declining trade or the destruction caused by war**.

But Scripture points to deeper causes.

**Perilous times arrive when sin becomes widespread** (**2 Timothy 3:1**).

When godliness declines, the times are genuinely evil.

The One who made the human mouth will judge how it is used—whether for **proud, profane, deceitful or even useless words**.

When poor and needy people are oppressed, times are very bad.

God hears their sighs and sees their suffering.

When wickedness becomes widespread and **those holding authority encourage it**, the situation is especially grave.

Yet God provides **three consolations for such times**.

**First**, we can approach the Lord to ask him to put right every wrong.

**Second**, he will punish and restrain those who persist in pride and falsehood.

**Third**, he will deliver his oppressed people in the most suitable time.

Human beings may lie, but **God remains faithful**.

People may fail our trust, but **God can be trusted**.

His word is as precious and reliable as **silver refined repeatedly to the highest purity**.

Its truth and power have been demonstrated again and again.

However dark the age becomes, God will preserve **his chosen remnant**.

Until the end of the world, there will continue to be proud and wicked generations.

But God's people have been entrusted to **Jesus Christ our Savior**.

No one can snatch them from his hands.

They are built upon **Christ the Rock**, safe even against the fiercest persecution and temptation.

# Ezekiel 13 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Heavy judgments against lying prophets. | 1–9 |
| The insufficiency of their work. | 10–16 |
| Woes against false prophetesses. | 17–23 |

## False prophets speak their own inventions (verses 1–9)

When God appoints someone to a task, he also gives wisdom for it.

The false prophets did not speak what they had received from God.

By contrast, the ministers of Christ must proclaim **what God has revealed**.

These false prophets did not pray or maintain fellowship with heaven.

They devised words designed **to please people rather than to do them good**.

They did not resist sin or call people to repentance.

Instead, they flattered people into empty expectations.

Such teachers **widen the breach** between sinners and God.

They persuade people they are entitled to eternal life while **God's wrath still rests upon them**.

## Whitewashed walls cannot stand (verses 10–16)

One false prophet built the wall by announcing that Jerusalem would succeed and escape danger.

That promise made him popular.

Other prophets made the prediction appear still more convincing by coating the wall with smooth plaster.

But God's righteous judgment would come like a **storm** and destroy the poorly constructed wall when **the Chaldeans devastated the land**.

Hopes of peace and happiness that **God's word does not authorize** are deceptive.

They resemble a wall with a beautiful finish but **no sound foundation**.

## False prophetesses mislead the people (verses 17–23)

It is a terrible thing to prefer **pleasant lies to painful truths**.

The false prophetesses tried to persuade the people that they were safe.

Henry understands their acts as imagery of giving people **ease** and encouraging **pride through elaborate decorations**.

Their schemes would fail, and God would free his people from their influence.

Christians must remain close to **God's word** and seek **the Holy Spirit's teaching in everything**.

Let us trust his promises in such a way that we also **obey his commandments**.

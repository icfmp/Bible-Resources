# Isaiah 46 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The idols could not save themselves, but God saves his people. | 1–4 |
| The folly of worshipping idols. | 5–13 |

## Idols fall; the Lord bears his people (verses 1–4)

The surrounding pagan peoples mocked the Jews as though **Bel and Nebo**, their gods, were stronger than Jehovah.

But their worshipers could not save those idols.

Both the idols and the people worshiping them were carried away into captivity.

God's people therefore had no reason to fear either.

The things ungodly people expect to give them security and happiness will eventually prove unable to rescue them from **death and judgment**.

The true God will never fail his worshipers.

The story of every believer's life is, in a sense, **a brief reflection of Israel's history**.

God continually upholds our spiritual life through his grace, just as he upholds our natural life through Providence.

He will not abandon his people.

The One who **began** their well-being will **complete** it.

When age and weakness increase, they need his help as deeply as they did in childhood.

This promise to an Israel weakened and grown old as a nation also belongs to **every elderly follower of Christ**.

Infirmities may surround you.

People close to you may even grow weary of caring for you.

Yet God remains who he has always promised to be.

He will **bear you up**, carry you along your path and finally **bring you home**.

If we learn to love and trust him, we need not be anxious about the days or years that remain.

He will continue to care for us as **creatures of his power** and as **people renewed by his Spirit**.

## False gods cannot accomplish God's purposes (verses 5–13)

The prophet exposes the folly of making an idol and then praying to it.

The excessive expenses idolaters accept put to shame the stinginess of many professed worshipers of God, who want religion to **cost them nothing**.

Yet serving sin always costs a great deal.

God shows how senseless and powerless idols are.

His people should act as rational human beings and avoid such practices.

Many biblical prophecies given long ago have not yet been fulfilled.

But God's fulfillment of some is a pledge that the others will also come true.

Nothing brings deeper peace of mind than confidence that **God will accomplish everything he purposes**.

Even people who do not know or care about his **revealed will** may be called upon to carry out his **hidden counsel**.

Heaven and earth will pass away before even the smallest part of God's word fails.

The Lord calls stubborn sinners to listen.

They are far from right standing before him, yet they are summoned to heed his word.

A sinner's salvation begins with **a humble and contrite heart that trembles at God's word**.

Godly sorrow leads to genuine repentance and **faith in divine mercy through the obedience unto death of our divine Surety, Jesus Christ**.

At the appointed time Christ came as **God's righteousness and salvation** for his people.

His saving work continues in the church and remains available to all who believe.

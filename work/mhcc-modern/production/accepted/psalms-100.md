# Psalm 100 — Modern English

## Original heading

**An exhortation to praise God, and rejoice in him.**

## The whole earth called to joyful worship

This psalm of praise can be read as **a prophecy** and also offered as **a prayer** for the day when people everywhere will know that the Lord is God.

They will become his worshipers and **the sheep of his pasture**.

We have strong encouragement to worship him **cheerfully**.

We had wandered like sheep.

If God has brought us back into his fold, we have abundant reason to bless his name.

The reasons and themes of our praise are of the highest importance.

**Know God for who he is, and understand who he is to you**.

Consider these truths and apply them to your life.

Doing so will make your worship more faithful and regular, more heartfelt and earnest.

The **covenant of grace** is revealed throughout the Scriptures of both Old and New Testaments.

Its rich promises strengthen the faith of even the weakest believer.

They provide a firm foundation for our praise and joy.

When we look at ourselves, our spirits may be heavy and full of sorrow.

But when we look toward **God's goodness, mercy and the promises of his word**, we find many reasons to give thanks and receive comfort.

# Hosea 5 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The Divine judgments against Israel. | 1–7 |
| Approaching desolations threatened. | 8–15 |

## The Lord exposes Israel's hidden sins (verses 1–7)

God's searching eyes saw **Israel's secret attachment to sin**, the people's love for their wrong practices and the power those practices exercised over them.

**Pride** makes people stubborn in every other sin.

Judah was following the same path and would therefore fall with Israel.

People who deal falsely with the Lord are only **deceiving themselves**.

Those who seek God merely by bringing **flocks and herds**, while withholding **their hearts and souls**, cannot expect to find him.

Those who do not seek the Lord **while he may be found** will not succeed later.

It is therefore vital to seek him **now**, in the accepted time and day of salvation.

## Judgment approaches, but repentance remains possible (verses 8–15)

The destruction announced to unrepentant sinners is not merely a warning intended to frighten them.

It is **a sentence that will not be recalled** if they refuse to repent.

That we receive timely warning is itself **a mercy**, allowing us to flee from coming judgment.

Following human commands that contradict God's commands prepares a people for ruin.

Sometimes God's judgments consume sinful people **like a moth, dry rot or woodworm**.

The damage proceeds quietly.

People may imagine themselves secure and prosperous, only to discover later that everything has been **gradually decaying**.

God often allows this process to unfold slowly to give them **time to repent**.

A nation, like an individual, can waste away by degrees.

Lesser judgments are sent to warn sinners and prevent greater calamities if they will listen.

When Israel and Judah were threatened, they sought protection from **Assyria**.

This only made their injury worse.

Affliction would eventually drive them to seek **God himself**.

There is hope when people begin to grieve **more deeply over their sins than over their suffering**.

Under conviction and correction, we should seek to know the Lord.

Those led by severe trials to seek him genuinely will find him **an immediate helper and a secure refuge**.

With him there is abundant redemption for everyone who calls upon him.

True, lasting peace is found **only where God is**.

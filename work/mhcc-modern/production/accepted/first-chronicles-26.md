# 1 Chronicles 26 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The offices of the Levites.**

## The Levites' duties and the temple treasury

The temple's gatekeepers and treasurers needed **strength and courage**. They had to prevent unauthorized entry into the sanctuary and protect its sacred treasures.

Each day the altar consumed considerable supplies: **flour, wine, oil, salt and fuel**. The lamps also needed provisions.

Stores of these items were kept ready, together with the sacred clothing and utensils.

These were the treasures of God's house.

They pictured the abundance available in our heavenly Father's house, where there is more than enough for all.

From the unsearchable riches of Christ, pictured by those treasuries, our needs are supplied.

Having received from his fullness, we should give him all the glory and use our abilities and possessions according to his will.

The chapter also records officials and judges.

Civil government, like the ministry of God's word, is **an ordinance of God for the good of his church** and must not be neglected.

The Levites who served directly in the sanctuary, the singers and the gatekeepers were not also assigned this outward administrative work. Each responsibility already required their full attention.

Whatever our position, we need **wisdom, courage, strong faith, holy affections and steadfastness** in doing our duty.

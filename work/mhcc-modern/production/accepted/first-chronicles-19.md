# 1 Chronicles 19 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's wars.**

## David's wars

This chapter repeats the history recorded in **2 Samuel 10**.

A sinner's only safety is to submit to the Lord, seek peace with him and become his servant.

We should help one another in a good cause.

But let us be careful: we may be used as instruments of good for others and yet, through unbelief and sin, fail to receive salvation ourselves.

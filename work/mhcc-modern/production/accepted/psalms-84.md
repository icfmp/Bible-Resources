# Psalm 84 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The psalmist expresses his affection to the ordinances of God. | 1–7 |
| His desire towards the God of the ordinances. | 8–12 |

## Longing for the Lord's house (verses 1–7)

God's appointed ordinances comfort believers in this troubled world.

Through them they enjoy **the living God's presence**.

That explains their sorrow when they cannot join in public worship.

These ordinances are to their souls what **a nest is to a bird**.

Yet their blessings are only an advance taste of **the happiness of heaven**.

How can people sincerely desire heaven's holy dwelling while complaining that worship here is wearying and unpleasant?

Truly happy people continue in the practice of true religion through **the strength supplied by Jesus Christ**.

Every part of our ability comes from him.

Pilgrims traveling toward the heavenly city may cross many **valleys of tears and dry deserts**.

But God opens **wells of salvation** and sends consolation to support them.

Those who press forward in Christian duty receive grace added to grace.

As they grow in grace now, they will eventually be **made perfect in glory**.

## Seeking the God of worship (verses 8–12)

Whenever we approach God, we should ask him to look favorably upon **Christ, his Anointed One**, and receive us for Christ's sake.

Without the Mediator, we would not dare present ourselves before the Father.

But looking toward Christ in faith, we may confidently pray that God will regard us through him.

The psalmist loved God's ordinances.

We should regard **one day in God's courts as better than a thousand elsewhere**, and prefer the humblest duty in his service to the highest worldly promotion.

We live in darkness, but God is **our Sun**, giving light, life, guidance and direction.

We face dangers, but he is **our Shield**, protecting us from the fiery darts that surround us.

God has not promised riches and honors.

He has promised **grace and glory** to those who seek him through his appointed way.

Grace is **heaven begun on earth** through knowing, loving and serving God.

Glory is the completion of that blessedness: becoming like him and enjoying him fully forever.

Let us seek to walk uprightly and trust the Lord to provide everything that is truly good for us.

Even when we cannot go to the Lord's house, **we may approach the Lord of the house through faith**.

In him we can find genuine happiness and peace whatever our outward circumstances.

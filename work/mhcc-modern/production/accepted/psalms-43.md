# Psalm 43 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David endeavours to still his spirit, with hope and confidence in God.**

## Hope in God's light and truth

When David considered his own sin before God, he prayed that the Lord would not enter into strict judgment with him, knowing he would be condemned.

But regarding the conflict his enemies had brought upon him, he could rightly ask God to **judge his cause and act on his behalf**.

Even when we cannot experience spiritual joy in God, we may still **rely on him**.

Spiritual support remains available when spiritual delight seems absent.

God has never finally cast away anyone who trusts him, however frightened that person may be about personal spiritual condition.

We need nothing more for true happiness than the good that comes from God's favor and is included in his promises.

Those whom God leads, he directs toward **his holy hill**.

People who claim to follow the Spirit while turning their backs on **God's appointed worship** deceive themselves.

We should pray for the **Spirit of light and truth**, who supplies what is lacking now that Christ is no longer bodily present with his church.

He guides us along the road to heaven.

Whatever brings us joy or triumph, the Lord himself must remain the joy within it.

David returned to God as his **never-failing hope**.

May the Lord send out the truth of **his word** and the light of **his Spirit** to guide us in holiness, peace and salvation.

Like the suffering prophet, a Christian longs to be saved **from sin as well as from sorrow**.

May the heavenly wisdom shining through Jesus Christ teach us righteousness and lead us by that light and truth into **the New Jerusalem**.

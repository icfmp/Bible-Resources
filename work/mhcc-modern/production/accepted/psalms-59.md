# Psalm 59 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| David prays for deliverance from his enemies. | 1–7 |
| He foresees their destruction. | 8–17 |

## David prays for rescue (verses 1–7)

Here we hear **David's voice while confined within his own house**, surrounded by enemies.

We may also hear **Christ** amid merciless opponents, **the church** under the world's oppression, and **individual Christians** facing temptation, suffering and persecution.

That is how earnestly we should pray each day for protection from spiritual enemies—**Satan's temptations and the corruption within our own hearts**.

We should fear suffering **as evildoers**, but never be ashamed of hatred from those who do wrong.

People who persuade themselves that God pays no attention to what they say will often speak with no restraint or consideration.

Where reverence for God is absent, there is little to guarantee proper respect for other people.

## God's mercy as our defense (verses 8–17)

During danger and difficulty, it is wise and right to **wait upon the Lord**.

He is our defense and the place where we are safe.

Prayer becomes especially comforting when we remember him as **the God of our mercy**: the Author of every good thing within us and the Giver of every good thing we receive.

Wicked people can never be satisfied.

That discontent is a terrible misery, even when they possess much.

A contented person may not have everything desired but does not continually quarrel with Providence or fill the heart with resentment.

It is **not poverty itself, but discontent**, that makes someone miserable.

David would praise God because he had repeatedly found him to be a refuge in times of trouble.

The One who does so much for us deserves our deepest love, praise and service.

God's people's trials will end in **joy and thanksgiving**.

When the night of affliction passes, they will sing of divine power and mercy in the morning.

Let believers begin now to praise him with assured faith and hope for the blessings they will enjoy and praise forever.

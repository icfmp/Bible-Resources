# 2 Chronicles 6 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Solomon's prayer at the dedication of the temple.**

## Solomon prays at the dedication

Notice the **order** of Solomon's prayer.

First, and most importantly, he asks for **repentance and forgiveness**. This is the greatest blessing and the only secure foundation for other acts of mercy.

Only afterward does he pray for temporal benefits.

In this way Solomon teaches us what we should value and seek most earnestly in our prayers.

Jesus Christ teaches the same thing in his perfect pattern of prayer: only **one request concerns outward needs**, while the others concern spiritual blessings.

The temple pictured the **human nature of Christ**, in whom all the fullness of deity dwells bodily.

The ark pictured **his obedience and suffering**, through which repentant sinners receive access to God reconciled to them and enjoy fellowship with him.

In the person of **Emmanuel**, Jehovah has made our human nature his permanent dwelling place. Through him he dwells among his church of redeemed sinners and delights in them.

May our hearts become his dwelling place.

May Christ live in them by faith, making them temples dedicated to him and pouring his love into them.

May the Father look upon us through his Anointed One, remember us and bless us according to his mercy toward sinners through Jesus Christ.

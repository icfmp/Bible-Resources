# Esther 10 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Greatness of Ahasuerus—Mordecai's advancement.**

## Mordecai's greatness and service

Many more stories of Ahasuerus's greatness could have been told.

They were recorded in Persian chronicles that have long since disappeared.

But **Scripture will remain** as long as time endures.

The Holy Spirit considers the concerns of the Lord's despised worshipers more important than the exploits of even the most impressive earthly ruler.

Mordecai was truly great, and his higher position gave him **greater opportunities to do good**.

He did not deny his people, the Jews, and undoubtedly continued in the true faith.

He sought not his own wealth but his people's welfare.

Few have the opportunity to do as much good as Mordecai.

Yet all have the ability to do harm, and **everyone has some ability to do good**.

We are not required to do what lies beyond our abilities or outside the responsibilities of our position.

But we are all called to live according to the spirit displayed by the faithful people whose examples Scripture records.

If we live by **faith in Christ**, we will actively use whatever opportunities and abilities he gives us to promote his glory and other people's highest good.

**Genuine faith works through love.**

Wait upon God in faith and prayer.

The final outcome will be secure and glorious, because our salvation is certain through our Lord Jesus Christ.

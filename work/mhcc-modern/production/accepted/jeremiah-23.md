# Jeremiah 23 — Modern English

| Original outline | Verses |

## Christ, the righteous Branch and faithful Shepherd (verses 1–8)

Woe to people appointed to **feed God's flock** who refuse to care about the sheep's welfare!

Yet the neglected flock receives a promise of comfort.

Even if only **a remnant** remains, God will find his scattered sheep and bring them home.

**Jesus Christ** is the **Branch from David's family**.

He is righteous himself, and **through him all his people become righteous**.

He breaks **Satan's illegitimate power**.

The spiritual descendants of believing **Abraham** and praying **Jacob** will be rescued from **the guilt and dominion of sin**.

When Christ rules within a human soul, that soul lives in safety.

His name is **“The LORD Our Righteousness.”**

He is our righteousness in a way **no created being could ever be**.

**His obedience unto death is the righteousness that justifies believers and gives them the right to heavenly happiness**.

Our sanctification, which becomes the source of our own obedience, comes from **our union with Christ** and the Holy Spirit he supplies.

Every genuine believer can call upon Christ by this precious name.

We have nothing to plead except that **Christ died and rose again**, and that we have received him as our Lord.

The righteousness he achieved to satisfy **God's law and justice** is freely given to us.

Through the Holy Spirit, we are clothed in that righteousness, enabled to take hold of it and assured of our share in it.

**“The LORD Our Righteousness”** is a wonderfully comforting name for someone convicted of sin, conscious of guilt and aware of the need for a righteousness not their own.

This salvation surpasses every former rescue God gave his church.

May we be gathered to Christ and **found in him**.

## False prophets make people confident in sin (verses 9–22)

The false prophets of **Samaria** misled Israel into idolatry.

But the Lord judged the prophets of **Jerusalem** to have practiced even worse wickedness, making others bold in sin.

These teachers would suffer bitter judgment.

They first persuaded **themselves** that wrongdoing was harmless and acted on that belief.

Then they persuaded **others** to accept it.

People determined to persist in evil may be given over to **strong delusions**.

What divine revelation had these teachers actually received?

What had they understood of God's word?

Eventually they would look back on their folly and unbelief **with remorse**.

True prophets' instruction and conduct lead people toward **repentance, faith and righteousness**.

False prophets encourage people to rest in **religious forms and opinions** while remaining undisturbed in sin.

Let us be careful not to follow unrighteousness.

## False dreams are not God's word (verses 23–32)

No one can hide from the God who sees everything.

Why do false prophets fail to see the judgment their words are bringing upon themselves?

Consider the vast difference between their utterances and **the prophecies of God's true messengers**.

Their dreams must not be described as **divine revelations**.

Promises of peace invented by those teachers compare to God's promises **as chaff compares to wheat**.

The heart of a person who has not been humbled resembles **a rock**.

If the word of God does not melt it **like fire**, the word may break it **like a hammer**.

Who could remain safe or peaceful with **almighty God against them**?

His word is not a smooth, soothing or deceitful message intended to lull sinners to sleep.

Its **faithfulness to truth** distinguishes it from false doctrine.

## Mocking prophecy cannot cancel judgment (verses 33–40)

How miserable are people **forsaken and forgotten by God**!

Mocking the Lord's judgments does not make them fail.

God had once taken Israel as a people close to himself.

Now they would be **cast out of his presence**.

It is a sign of extreme irreverence to treat **God's own words as a joke**.

Every idle and profane word increases the guilt sinners must answer for at judgment.

If they remain unrepentant, **everlasting disgrace** awaits them.

# Zechariah 10 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Blessings to be sought from the Lord. | 1–5 |
| God will restore his people. | 6–12 |

## Seek blessings from God, not idols (verses 1–5)

Spiritual blessings were promised through figures drawn from **the abundance of earthly harvests**.

Rain arriving at the right time is a great mercy.

We may ask God for it when needed and expect him to hear.

Likewise, our prayers should seek every kind of mercy **at the appropriate time**.

The Lord makes clouds and sends showers.

Henry sees this as a possible invitation to seek **the influences of the Holy Spirit through faith and prayer**, through which the blessings of God's promises are received and enjoyed.

Turning to idols for help, as earlier generations had done, was foolish.

The Lord would visit the remnant of his flock in mercy and renew their courage and strength for conflict.

Everything created is to us what God makes it.

Anyone who supports a nation like **a cornerstone**, or unites people who differ like **nails joining beams**, must ultimately be given by the Lord.

Anyone called to overcome enemies will receive strength and success from him.

Henry also applies the picture to **Christ**, to whom we should look for leaders and servants who unite, sustain and defend his people.

The Lord will never say that we have sought him in vain.

## God gathers and strengthens his people (verses 6–12)

These precious promises concern **the Jews** and, in Henry's reading, also **the later days of the church**.

The preaching of the gospel is God's call for souls to come to **Jesus Christ**.

Those Christ redeemed by his blood, God gathers by his grace.

Difficulties will be overcome as decisively as the obstacles defeated during Israel's deliverance from **Egypt**.

God himself will be their strength and their song.

When we resist spiritual enemies and overcome them through his help, we have reason to rejoice.

If he strengthens us, then we should apply ourselves diligently to **all the duties of Christian life**.

We must be active in God's service and do everything **in the name of the Lord Jesus**.

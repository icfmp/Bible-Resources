# 1 Samuel 3 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The word of the Lord first revealed to Samuel. | 1–10 |
| God tells Samuel the destruction of Eli's house. | 11–18 |
| Samuel established to be a prophet. | 19–21 |

## God calls the young Samuel (verses 1–10)

When divine grace intends that a call should be effective, the Lord will repeat the call until the person responds.

Eli realized that Samuel was hearing the Lord's voice and taught him how to answer.

It was a humbling matter for Eli that God addressed Samuel rather than him.

Nevertheless, he helped the younger boy understand what to do.

Older believers should do everything they can to support and improve the abilities of younger people who are coming after them.

We should not withhold help even from people who may soon be given a higher position than our own (John 1:30).

Children should learn good words and responses while they are young, so that they become prepared to learn spiritual truth and take it seriously.

## Samuel hears judgment against Eli's house (verses 11–18)

How much guilt and corruption remains in us!

We are often aware of sins that our own hearts know well.

People who have the responsibility and power to restrain others from wrongdoing, yet refuse to act, **share in that guilt**.

They will be accountable as participants in the wrong.

Eli's response to the fearful sentence showed that he recognized God's right to act as he saw fit.

He trusted the Lord would do nothing unjust.

The gentleness, patience and humility of Eli's words show genuine repentance.

He accepted the consequences of his own failure.

## Samuel grows as God's prophet (verses 19–21)

Every increase in wisdom and grace comes from God being with us.

The Lord graciously continues his visits to those who receive them rightly.

Early faith is a young person's greatest honor.

Those who honor God will be honored by him.

Young people should consider Samuel's example and remember their Creator during their youth.

Even small children are capable of genuine faith.

Samuel shows that their worship and trust can please the Lord.

His gentle and beautiful qualities of character are the brightest adornment of youth and a sure source of true happiness.

# Psalm 5 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| God will certainly hear prayer: David gives to God the glory, and takes to himself the comfort. | 1–6 |
| He prayed for himself, that God would guide him, and for all the Lord's people, that God would give them joy, and keep them safe. | 7–12 |

## The God who hears prayer (verses 1–6)

God hears prayer.

He has always done so, and he remains as ready to receive prayer as ever.

One of our strongest reasons for confidence in prayer is that we approach him as **our King and our God**.

David prayed to the God who hates sin.

Sin is folly.

Those who persist in it are fools of their own making.

People who are determined to be wicked hate God and rightly face his judgment.

If they continue in that condition, their misery and destruction will be everlasting.

We should learn to value **truthfulness and sincerity in every part of life**.

Liars and murderers resemble the devil and show whose ways they follow.

The Lord abhors such wrongdoing.

David's enemies displayed these characteristics.

People who behave in the same way still oppose **Christ and his church**.

## God guides, protects and brings joy (verses 7–12)

David regularly prayed on his own.

Yet he was also faithful in attending **public worship**.

God's mercy must always be the foundation of our hope and joy in everything involving him.

Our prayers should reach beyond ourselves to other believers.

**Grace be with everyone who sincerely loves Jesus Christ**.

Every blessing comes through **Jesus Christ, the Righteous One**, just as God's favor was previously shown to Israel through David, whom he protected and established upon the throne.

Christ is the righteous Savior, Israel's King and **the source of blessing for every believer**.

His favor is the defense and protection of his church.

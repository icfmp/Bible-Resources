# Psalm 16 — Modern English

## Original heading

**This psalm begins with expressions of devotion, which may be applied to Christ; but ends with such confidence of a resurrection, as must be applied to Christ, and to him only.**

## David's devotion and the hope of Christ's resurrection

David sought God's protection with joyful confidence.

Everyone who has acknowledged **the Lord as their own Lord** should remember that commitment often, draw comfort from it and live consistently with it.

David gave himself to honoring God by serving God's people.

We must become saints on earth if we hope to join the saints in heaven.

Those renewed by divine grace and devoted to God's glory are saints already.

God's people in this world are excellent, though some are so poor that they need practical kindness.

David resolved not to share in works of spiritual darkness.

He renewed his deliberate choice of God as his **portion and happiness**, took comfort from it and gave God the glory.

Most people choose this world as their greatest good.

But the faithful person says: *However poor my circumstances may be, let me enjoy God's love and favor, be accepted by him and have the promised right to future life and happiness. That is enough.*

Heaven is our true inheritance, home, rest and everlasting good.

We should consider the present world merely **the country through which we travel toward our Father's house**.

Anyone who has God as the appointed portion possesses a beautiful inheritance.

Return to rest, my soul.

People ruled by grace continue desiring **more of God**, but not **more than God**.

They are satisfied with his loving kindness and do not envy others' merely physical pleasures.

Yet without his sustaining grace we are foolish enough to surrender real mercies for empty substitutes.

God had counseled David through **his word and Spirit**.

As he meditated during the night, his own thoughts reinforced the determination to live by faith for God.

The apostle Peter quoted **verses 8–11** in his Pentecost sermon (**Acts 2:25–31**).

Peter declared that David spoke of **Jesus Christ**, especially **his resurrection**.

As Christ is the Head of his body, the church, these verses also encourage believers who are guided and strengthened by Christ's Spirit.

Always place the Lord before you.

Then your heart and tongue may rejoice in him.

Death destroys ordinary earthly hopes, but **not the hope of a true Christian**.

Christ's resurrection is the pledge of the believer's own resurrection.

Sorrow belongs to the present world.

In heaven there is **fullness of joy**, and where earthly pleasures quickly vanish, the pleasures at God's right hand endure forever.

Lord, through your beloved Son and our Savior, show us the path of life.

**Justify our souls now**, and **raise our bodies on the last day**, when earthly sorrow becomes heavenly joy and pain becomes everlasting happiness.

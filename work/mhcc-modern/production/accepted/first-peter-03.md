# 1 Peter 3 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The duties of wives and husbands. | 1–7 |
| Christians exhorted to agree. | 8–13 |
| And encouraged to patience under persecutions for righteousness' sake, considering that Christ suffered patiently. | 14–22 |

## Wives and husbands should honor God in their lives (verses 1–7)

A believing wife should carry out her duties toward her husband even when he does not obey God's word.

People who reject religion often watch professing Christians' lives very closely. It is therefore important that our actions match our words.

Peter does not forbid clothing or personal care. He condemns **vanity, extravagance and costly self-display**.

Few of us know where to set appropriate limits on food and clothing. If poverty did not restrain them, many would continually desire more than they need, spending time and money on trifles.

The apostle commends adornment that cannot perish: **the Holy Spirit's graces beautifying the soul**.

A Christian's chief concern should be **governing the inner spirit**.

A gentle and peaceful heart will win more genuine respect and affection than elaborate clothing worn with a quarrelsome temper.

Christian duties should be performed willingly, out of love and obedience to God.

Wives are to show respect **not from terror or panic**, but from the desire to please God and live rightly.

A husband's duty includes **honoring his wife, protecting her, trusting her and supporting her rightful authority**.

Husband and wife are **heirs together of life's blessings and the inheritance to come**. They should live peacefully, treating one another with consideration.

Prayer sweetens their life together.

It is good for a family to pray together, but husbands and wives should also pray together **privately** and **with their children**.

To pray much, live holy lives. And to live holy lives, pray much.

## Show compassion, speak truth and seek peace (verses 8–13)

Christians will not always agree in every detail. Nevertheless, they should show compassion and love one another as brothers and sisters.

Anyone desiring a good life now or eternal life afterward must **control the tongue**, refusing malice, insults and deceit.

Turn away from evil, do all possible good and pursue peace with everyone.

**God sees and cares for the upright**, and no one can ultimately defeat those who follow Christ, the perfect example of doing good to others.

## Explain your hope gently and endure suffering for righteousness (verses 14–22)

We honor God before others when our conduct encourages them to glorify him.

Be ready to explain the reason for **Christian hope**, with **gentleness and reverent fear of God**.

When we truly fear the Lord, other fears lose their power.

A good conscience performs its function faithfully.

It is dreadful when **sin and suffering** meet, because wrongdoing makes affliction more painful, lonely and destructive.

It is better to suffer for doing what is right than to suffer for wrongdoing.

Jesus gives us the supreme example.

**The sinless One suffered in the place of sinners** so that he could reconcile us to God and bring us to eternal glory.

He died with respect to his human nature and was raised through the Holy Spirit's power.

If even Christ passed through suffering, believers should not expect to escape all suffering themselves.

God knows precisely what opportunities and warnings people have received.

Henry explains that **Christ by his Spirit preached through Noah** to the people of that generation, warning them before judgment.

God's patience endured for a long time, but finally came to an end.

Henry understands the spirits of those who rejected Noah's warning as having been committed after death to **the prison of hell**, from which, in his interpretation, **there is no redemption**.

Noah's rescue in the ark through the flood pictured the rescue of true believers through **the Holy Spirit's saving work**.

To avoid confusion, Peter distinguishes **baptism's outward washing** from the spiritual reality it signifies.

**Water by itself only cleanses bodily dirt**.

The saving reality is the Holy Spirit's regenerating grace, producing repentance, faith and the desire to live uprightly before God.

Do not rest on outward religious acts.

Many were baptized and faithfully attended worship yet never trusted Christ, and died without receiving his salvation.

Seek instead to be **cleansed by the Spirit and Christ's blood**.

**Christ's resurrection from the dead assures believers of purification and peace**.

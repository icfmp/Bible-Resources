# Psalm 132 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| David's care for the ark. | 1–10 |
| The promises of God. | 11–18 |

## David desires a dwelling place for the Lord (verses 1–10)

David committed himself to finding a place for **the ark, the sign of God's presence**.

When we have work to do for the Lord, it helps to **set aside a time to do it**.

Each morning we can plan the work of the day while submitting our plans to Providence, since we do not know what the day will bring.

Before anything else, let us seek **without delay** to have our hearts made **God's dwelling place through the Holy Spirit**.

David prayed that the Lord would inhabit the sanctuary prepared for him.

He also prayed that God would give the priests and other ministers grace to fulfill their duties.

David pleaded that he was **the Lord's anointed**, pointing forward to **Christ, the great Anointed One**.

We possess no merit of our own that would recommend us to God.

But **Christ possesses an abundance of merit**, and for his sake we ask to receive favor.

Every genuine believer is also anointed in Christ, receiving **the grace of the Holy One**.

Our prayer is that God will hear and answer **for his Son's sake**.

## God's promise and the reign of Messiah (verses 11–18)

The Lord does not turn away those who plead **his covenant through the Anointed Prophet, Priest and King**.

What astonishing love God shows toward his church!

He desires to dwell with his people, while we desire so little to dwell with him.

He remained in Zion until Israel's sins led him to hand the city over to enemies.

Lord, although we too are sinful, **do not abandon us**.

God's people enjoy a special blessing even on life's ordinary comforts.

That blessing gives them an added sweetness.

Zion's poor may be content with little earthly wealth because **far better treasures are prepared for them**.

The Lord richly nourishes **the new spiritual life**, satisfying those poor in spirit with **the bread of life**.

He gives more than we ask.

When he gives salvation, he also grants abundant joy.

God would frustrate every attempt to destroy David's royal house until **the King Messiah** arose from it to sit upon his Father's throne.

**All the promises are fulfilled in Christ**.

Those who refuse to acknowledge his reign will finally face everlasting shame and confusion.

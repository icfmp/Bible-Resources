# Malachi 2 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The priests reproved for neglecting their covenant. | 1–9 |
| The people reproved for their evil practices. | 10–17 |

## The priests have violated their covenant (verses 1–9)

What Scripture says about the covenant of the priesthood also teaches us about **the covenant of grace** made with everyone who believes, for Christians are spiritual priests.

It is a covenant of **life and peace**, promising true happiness both now and in the world to come.

Being chosen as God's messengers is an honor.

A priest's lips must **preserve knowledge for the people**, not withhold it from them.

All are concerned to understand God's will.

We should read Scripture ourselves, but we should also welcome instruction and counsel from God's faithful messengers regarding our souls.

Ministers should devote their best effort to **turning sinners back to God**.

Even among those who are called God's people, many still need to turn away from wrongdoing.

Those ministers most likely to help others repent are the ones who **teach sound doctrine and live holy lives in accordance with Scripture**.

Many priests abandoned that way and led the people into error instead.

Those who walk with God in peace and righteousness and lead others away from sin honor him.

He will honor them, while people who despise him will be treated lightly.

## Faithlessness toward God and marriage (verses 10–17)

Corrupt conduct grows out of corrupt beliefs and desires.

A person who acts falsely toward God will not act faithfully toward other people.

Some men in Judah despised the **marriage covenant established by God**.

They dismissed the wives from their own nation, probably so they could marry foreign women instead.

They made their wives' lives bitter while pretending before others to care for them.

But remember: **she is your wife**, the person most closely bound to you in this world.

A wife must be regarded **not as a servant but as her husband's companion**.

Marriage is bound by a solemn oath before God, and that oath must not be treated lightly.

Husband and wife should live together until death in **holy love and peace**.

God made **one Eve for one Adam**, although he had power to create more.

Why did he establish the union of one man and one woman?

Henry explains that it was for the raising of **children devoted to God**.

Parents must therefore live reverently before the Lord so that their children may also learn to serve him.

God declared his hatred of putting away a spouse.

Anyone wishing to avoid sin must guard **the inner spirit and desires**, where sin begins.

Cruelty and faithlessness in family life usually grow out of **selfishness**, which values personal cravings more than another person's welfare and happiness.

It is tiresome and offensive to God when people insist on justifying their wicked conduct.

Those who imagine God approves of sin are both insulting him and deceiving themselves.

Some skeptics asked, **“Where is the God of judgment?”**

But **the day of the Lord will come**.

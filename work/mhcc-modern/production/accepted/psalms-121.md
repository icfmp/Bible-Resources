# Psalm 121 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The safety of the godly.**

## Our help comes from the Lord

We must not put ultimate trust in **human beings, methods, tools or secondary causes**.

Should I depend upon the strength of the hills, on rulers or on powerful people?

No. My confidence is in **God alone**.

Or perhaps we are being called to look **beyond the hills** to the God who gives all earthly things their place and value.

All our help comes from him.

We should expect it from him in **his own way and time**.

This psalm teaches us to find comfort in the Lord especially when danger and difficulty are greatest.

His **almighty wisdom** plans the safety of those who place themselves under his care, and his **almighty power** accomplishes it.

He is a watchful Keeper.

He never tires, sleeps or even grows drowsy.

We may rest under his shelter with joy and confidence.

He is always near his people to protect and refresh them.

Our right hand represents our working hand.

When we turn toward the duties he has given us, we may find him ready to grant us success.

He guards his people from being finally overthrown.

Neither open attacks nor secret plots can cause them ultimate harm.

The Lord may **prevent the trouble they fear** or **sanctify, remove or lessen the suffering they experience**.

Above all, **he protects their souls** from sin's pollution, from being crushed under trials and from eternal destruction.

He watches over believers during life and death.

He is with them when they go out to work in the morning of life and when they return to rest in life's evening.

**The Holy Spirit, their Preserver and Comforter, stays with them forever**.

Let us therefore remain faithful in our work, confident in the blessing promised here.

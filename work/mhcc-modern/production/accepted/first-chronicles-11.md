## David becomes king over Israel (verses 1–9)

David came to occupy Israel's throne after reigning for seven years in Hebron over Judah alone.

God's purposes will be fulfilled at last, whatever obstacles stand in the way.

True greatness consists in being genuinely useful and dedicating all our abilities to the Lord.

## David's mighty men (verses 10–47)

This passage records the distinguished men who served David.

Yet David attributed his success not to the mighty men with him but to the mighty **God**, whose presence matters more than anything else.

By strengthening David, these men also strengthened themselves and advanced their own interests, because his advancement benefited them.

We likewise gain by doing what we can, in the places God gives us, to support the kingdom of the Son of David.

Those faithful to Christ will find their names recorded with far greater honor than these men's names in the records of earthly fame.

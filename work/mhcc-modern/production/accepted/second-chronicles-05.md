# 2 Chronicles 5 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The ark placed in the temple. | 1–10 |
| The temple filled with glory. | 11–14 |

## The ark is placed in the temple (verses 1–10)

The ark was a picture of **Christ** and therefore a sign of God's presence.

Jesus has promised to be with his followers always, even to the end of the world.

When we claim that promise through faith and prayer, its effect is as though the ark itself were brought into our worship gatherings.

We should seek that presence earnestly.

When Christ is formed within a person, God's law is written upon the heart.

The ark of the covenant has, in that sense, been established there.

The soul becomes **a temple of the Holy Spirit**, and finds genuine satisfaction.

## God's glory fills the temple (verses 11–14)

God took possession of the building by filling it with a cloud.

This showed that he accepted the temple as he had accepted Moses' tabernacle and assured his people that he would continue to be the same gracious God to them.

If we want God to dwell in our hearts, we must make room for him: everything else must yield.

**The Word became flesh**.

When he comes to his temple like a refiner's fire, who can endure the day of his coming?

May he prepare us for that day.

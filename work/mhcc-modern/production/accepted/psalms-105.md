# Psalm 105 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| A solemn call to praise and serve the Lord. | 1–7 |
| His gracious dealings with Israel. | 8–23 |
| Their deliverance from Egypt, and their settlement in Canaan. | 24–45 |

## Seeking the Lord's strength and favor (verses 1–7)

This psalm calls us to **stir ourselves up to praise God**.

Seek his strength—meaning **his grace**, and the power of the Holy Spirit to accomplish in us the good we cannot do by ourselves.

That strength comes from him, and we must keep asking for it.

Seek his favor for eternity.

Continue seeking him throughout this life.

God will not only be found by those who seek him; **he rewards those who diligently seek him**.

## God's covenant care and the story of Joseph (verses 8–23)

Remember the Redeemer's marvelous works, his wonders and the judgments he has spoken.

True believers may be few and live as **strangers and pilgrims on earth**.

Yet through God's covenant they have been promised **an inheritance far greater than Canaan**.

If the Holy Spirit has anointed them, nothing can ultimately harm them.

Even afflictions can be among their mercies.

They test faith and love, humble pride, loosen the hold of the world and make prayer more earnest.

Bread is called the staff of life because it supports the body.

When that support is taken away, the body weakens.

Likewise, **God's word sustains the soul** and is the bread of spiritual life.

Among the worst judgments is **a famine of hearing the Lord's word**.

Such a famine was severe in all lands when Christ appeared in the flesh.

Joseph's story pictures Christ's coming and its blessings.

At the appointed time, **Christ was exalted as Mediator**, holding all the treasures of grace and salvation.

Perishing sinners come to him and receive help.

## Israel's deliverance as a picture of salvation (verses 24–45)

Believers often grow most in their souls when they carry the cross.

Likewise, the church may grow in true holiness and increase in number during persecution.

Yet God raises up instruments of deliverance, and persecutors may receive judgment.

See how he provided for Israel in the wilderness.

Every blessing given to Israel as a nation pictured **spiritual blessings in Christ Jesus**.

Christ has redeemed us **by his blood**, restored our souls toward holiness and freed us from **Satan's bondage**.

He guides and guards his people throughout their journey.

He feeds them with **the bread of heaven** and gives them **the water of life from the Rock of salvation**.

He will bring them safely to heaven.

He redeems them from iniquity and purifies them as his own people, **eager to do good works**.

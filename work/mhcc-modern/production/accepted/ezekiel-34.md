# Ezekiel 34 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The rulers reproved. | 1–6 |
| The people are to be restored to their own land. | 7–16 |
| The kingdom of Christ. | 17–31 |

## Israel's shepherds neglect the flock (verses 1–6)

The people became like **sheep without a shepherd**.

Their enemies preyed upon them, and their land was left desolate.

No official position or high social rank places anyone beyond **the correction of God's word**.

Those who neglect the duties of their office and misuse the trust others have placed in them deserve rebuke.

## The Lord gathers and cares for his flock (verses 7–16)

God announced that he intended mercy toward **his scattered sheep**.

The promise first concerned **the restoration of the Jews to their own land**.

It also pictured **the Good Shepherd's tender care for his people's souls**.

He finds them in the darkness of ignorance and leads them into **his fold**.

He comes to their aid in **persecution and temptation**.

He guides them along righteous paths and gives them rest in **his love and faithfulness**.

Proud, self-sufficient people oppose the true gospel and those who trust it; we must guard against their influence.

The Shepherd provides **rest for troubled believers** but **terror for presumptuous sinners**.

## Christ's kingdom brings pasture and blessing (verses 17–31)

The whole nation appeared to be the Lord's flock.

But the people differed greatly in **their character**, and he knew how to distinguish them.

**Good pasture and deep water** picture **the pure word of God** and **the practice of justice**.

Henry understands **verses 23–31** as a prophecy of **Christ** and of the most glorious periods of his church on earth.

Under Christ, the Good Shepherd, the church becomes a blessing to those around it.

Though Christ was excellent in himself, he appeared **like a tender plant growing from dry ground**.

As **the Tree of Life**, bearing every fruit of salvation, he gives **spiritual nourishment** to his people.

We should constantly pray for **showers of blessing** wherever Christ's truth is preached and for everyone who professes the gospel to be filled with **the fruits of righteousness**.

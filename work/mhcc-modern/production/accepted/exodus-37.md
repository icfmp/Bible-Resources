# Exodus 37 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The making of the ark, and the furniture of the tabernacle.**

## The furnishings as pictures of worship and redemption

The furniture of the tabernacle pointed toward the spiritual worship that God accepts.

The **incense** represented the prayers of the saints.

The sacrifice offered on the **altar** pointed to the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world.

The golden jar of **manna**, the bread from heaven, pointed to the flesh of Jesus Christ, which he gave for the life of the world.

The **lampstand**, with its burning lights, represented the teaching and illumination of the Holy Spirit.

The **bread of the Presence** pictured the abundant provision God gives to people who hunger and thirst for righteousness.

That provision is supplied through the gospel, the appointed means of grace and the sacraments celebrated in God's house of prayer.

The craftsmen took great care to follow the rule God gave them.

We should imitate that obedience.

Let us seek the Holy Spirit's influence, so that we may rejoice in God and glorify him during our earthly lives.

And in the end, may we dwell with him forever.

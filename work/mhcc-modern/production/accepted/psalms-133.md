# Psalm 133 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The excellency of brotherly love.**

## The blessing of living together in peace

We cannot say too much to encourage people to **live in peace with one another**.

Such peace is good for our dignity and comfort, and it gives continued joy to those who enjoy genuine unity.

The beauty of this fellowship is compared with **the sacred anointing oil**.

Brotherly love is a fruit of **the Holy Spirit**, evidence of union with **Christ**, and an adornment to the gospel.

It is not only pleasant but beneficial.

Its blessings are as numerous as **drops of dew**.

As dew cools the air and refreshes dry ground, love cools **the burning heat of human anger**.

It softens the heart and prepares it to receive and bear fruit from **the seed of God's word**.

The excellence of this love is confirmed by God's promise: **where brothers and sisters live together in unity, the Lord commands his blessing**.

Human beings can only request a blessing.

**God commands it**.

Believers who live in love and peace have **the God of love and peace with them now**.

Soon they will be with him forever in a world of perfect and unending peace.

May everyone who loves the Lord **bear with and forgive one another**, as God has forgiven them **for Christ's sake**.

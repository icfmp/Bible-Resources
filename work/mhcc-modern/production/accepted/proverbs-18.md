# Proverbs 18 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — Seek every means of spiritual growth

Those who want knowledge and grace should make use of every appropriate way of growing in them.

## Verse 2 — Knowledge is not for display

People who study or profess religion only to have something impressive to show others have missed its true purpose.

## Verse 3 — Shame follows sin

As soon as sin entered the world, **shame** followed it.

## Verse 4 — The spring of wisdom

Wisdom stored in a believer's heart becomes a **continual spring of wise words**.

## Verse 5 — Judge the cause, not the person

What matters is the justice of a case, **not the social standing or identity of the person** involved.

## Verses 6 and 7 — The harm of uncontrolled speech

Wicked people cause extraordinary harm **to themselves** through words they refuse to govern.

## Verse 8 — Do not sow suspicion

Spreading quarrels and whispers is contemptible. Even small beginnings of jealousy may produce dreadful consequences.

## Verse 9 — Neglecting duty is also dangerous

Failure to perform known duties, including performing duties carelessly, may harm the soul just as surely as **committing active sins**.

## Verses 10 and 11 — God's strong tower versus wealth's illusion

The Lord's divine power, revealed through **Jesus Christ**, is a strong tower for those who trust him. Wealthy people who put ultimate confidence in earthly possessions imagine they live behind high and secure walls. Those defenses will fail when most needed, leaving them before the judgment of the One they rejected as Savior.

## Verse 12 — Pride and humility

Pride raises the heart before a fall. Genuine **honor follows humility**.

## Verse 13 — Listen before answering

Eagerness mixed with self-confidence exposes a person to shame when answering before understanding.

## Verse 14 — When the spirit is wounded

Firmness of character can sustain people through many bodily pains. But a conscience tortured by remorse overwhelms even human courage. **What, then, will hell be like?**

## Verse 15 — Knowledge must reach the heart

We should seek knowledge not merely to fill the mind but **to change the heart**.

## Verse 16 — Welcome at the throne of grace

Bless the Lord, who welcomes us to his throne **without money and without price**. May the gifts he bestows make room for **him** within our souls.

## Verse 17 — Hear what opponents say

Listening to criticism from our enemies can help us form a fairer judgment of ourselves.

## Verse 18 — The seriousness of drawing lots

Matters were sometimes entrusted to God by **casting lots with solemn prayer**. But the lot has been profaned when used for entertainment or to seek another person's possessions; Henry raises this as an objection to such practices now.

## Verse 19 — Avoid quarrels among relatives

Great care is needed to prevent conflict between relatives and others bound together by obligation. **Wisdom and grace make forgiveness easier; sinful corruption makes it harder**.

## Verse 20 — What fills the heart determines satisfaction

The word **belly** here stands for the heart. Our inward peace and contentment depend upon **what fills that heart**.

## Verse 21 — A tongue can destroy lives

Many people have caused **their own death or the death of others** through malicious or dishonest speech.

## Verse 22 — A good marriage is God's gift

A good wife is a great blessing to her husband and a sign of the Lord's favor.

## Verse 23 — Poverty and humble petitions

Poverty teaches that we cannot simply order others to serve our wishes. Before the throne of God's grace **we all come as needy petitioners**.

## Verse 24 — Christ, the faithful Friend

Jesus Christ never abandons those who love and trust him. For his sake, may we show that kind of friendship to others. Having loved his own in the world, he loved them to the end; we are his friends if we obey his commands (**John 15:14**).

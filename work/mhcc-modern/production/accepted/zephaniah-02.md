# Zephaniah 2 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| An exhortation to repentance. | 1–3 |
| Judgments upon other nations. | 4–15 |

## Seek the Lord before judgment (verses 1–3)

The prophet called his nation to repentance as the only means of preventing national destruction.

One description of this people could mean **a nation without desire**: people who did not desire God, his favor or his grace and were unwilling to repent or reform.

It could also mean **a nation without anything desirable**: people who had nothing in themselves to recommend them to God.

He might justly have told them to leave his presence.

Instead he called them to gather before him and **seek his face**.

We know what his decree brings upon people who persist in sin.

Therefore everyone has urgent reason to repent while the opportunity to receive grace remains.

Seek peace with God now, before the Holy Spirit ceases striving with you, before the day of grace or of earthly life is over, and before your everlasting condition has been determined.

Let the poor, despised and afflicted seek the Lord.

Let them seek to understand and obey his commands more faithfully and grow more humble over their sins.

The greatest hope of deliverance from national judgments lies in **prayer**.

## Judgment on neighboring nations (verses 4–15)

People are in a truly dreadful position when the word of the Lord announces judgment against them.

Not one of his words will fail.

God will restore his people to what rightfully belongs to them, even after they have been deprived of it for a long time.

In every age, people devoted to God have commonly faced mockery and abuse.

Yet God will be worshiped not only by Israel and the strangers who join it, but also by people from **the nations**.

Even distant peoples will be called to account for the wrongs they have done to his people.

When those who were arrogant and cruel in their prosperity suffer, others may offer them neither sympathy nor lament.

The destruction of once-flourishing nations will nonetheless prepare the way for the **overthrow of Satan's kingdom**.

Let us use the advantages God has given us well.

Let us expect him to fulfill every promise.

And let us pray that **our Father's name will be honored and made holy throughout the earth**.

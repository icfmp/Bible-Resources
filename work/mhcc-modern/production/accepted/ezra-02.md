## Families returning from exile (verses 1–35)

A record was kept of the families who returned from captivity.

See how sin can bring down a nation that righteousness would have raised up!

## Priests and Levites (verses 36–63)

Those who undervalue their relationship to the Lord during times of reproach, persecution or hardship will not enjoy its benefits when that relationship becomes honorable or advantageous.

People who possess no evidence of having become **spiritual priests to God through Jesus Christ by the new birth** have no right to claim the comfort and privileges belonging to Christians.

## Gifts for the temple (verses 64–70)

No one should complain about the necessary cost of their religion.

Seek first God's kingdom, favor and glory; other things will then be supplied.

Their offerings were small compared with the gifts given by the leaders in David's time.

Yet because they were **proportionate to the givers' abilities**, they were just as acceptable to God.

The Lord will carry his people through work undertaken according to his will, directed toward his glory and dependent on his help.

Those who hear the gospel, turn away from sin and return to the Lord will be guided and protected through the dangers of the journey.

They will reach safely the homes prepared in **God's holy city**.

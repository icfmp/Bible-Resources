# 1 Chronicles 4 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Genealogies.**

## Judah, Simeon, and the prayer of Jabez

This chapter gives additional family records for **Judah**, the largest and most famous of Israel's tribes, and also for **Simeon**.

The most remarkable person mentioned is **Jabez**.

Scripture does not tell us why he was more honorable than his brothers.

But it does show that **he was a man of prayer**.

True greatness comes through desiring to do God's will and praying earnestly.

Jabez prayed to the **living and true God**, the only One who can hear and answer.

In approaching God he recognized the Lord as the One who had made a covenant with his people.

Jabez did not state a promise of his own.

He left his commitment understood, unwilling to make assurances in his own strength, while sincerely desiring to dedicate himself entirely to God.

The attitude of his prayer can be expressed like this:

*Lord, if you bless and preserve me, you may do whatever you will with me. I will remain at your command and disposal forever.*

His prayer expressed a deep and affectionate longing for God's blessing.

It contained **four requests**.

**First, that God would truly bless him.** Spiritual blessings are best, because God's blessings are real and bring real results.

**Second, that God would enlarge his territory.** We should desire that the Lord enlarge our **hearts**, and thus increase our share in him and in the heavenly Canaan.

**Third, that God's hand would be with him.** God's hand guiding, protecting, strengthening and working in us and for us is entirely sufficient for all our needs.

**Fourth, that God would protect him from evil.** This includes the evil of sin, painful troubles and the harmful plans of enemies, so that such things would not injure him or make him truly a **Jabez—a man of sorrow**.

God granted what Jabez asked.

The Lord remains ready to hear prayer.

His ear is not too heavy to hear us now.

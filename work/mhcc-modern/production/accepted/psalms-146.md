# Psalm 146 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Why we should not trust in men. | 1–4 |
| Why we should trust in God. | 5–10 |

## Human help is short-lived (verses 1–4)

Those who delight in praising God now will praise him forever. What little value worldly pursuits have compared with that prospect!

There is **one Son of Man in whom help never fails, who is also the Son of God**. All other sons of men share fallen Adam's condition. Adam did not remain in his original honor; neither can any other human being guarantee lasting happiness.

God gave the earth to humanity, yet people constantly struggle over it. Eventually even the most powerful person owns nothing but the small piece of ground where the body is buried. On the day of death, **all earthly plans perish**. We should not make other people the ultimate ground of our hope.

## The Lord's eternal help and kingdom (verses 5–10)

For this life we hope in **God's providence**, and for the next we hope in **his grace**.

The God of heaven became human in order to save us. He died upon the cross for our sins and was laid in the grave; nevertheless, his purposes of love did not end there. **He rose again to fulfill them**.

Christ's earthly miracles picture his continuing works of salvation. He liberates prisoners from **sin and Satan**, opens the eyes of understanding, feeds people who hunger for salvation with **the bread of life**, and shows mercy to the poor in spirit, the helpless and the spiritually fatherless.

His kingdom continues forever. Let sinners turn to him and believers rejoice in him. Let us encourage one another to praise his name.

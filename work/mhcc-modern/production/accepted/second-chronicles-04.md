# 2 Chronicles 4 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The furniture of the temple.**

## The temple's furnishings

This chapter further describes the furnishings of God's house.

Both outside and inside, the objects pictured gospel grace and pointed toward good things still to come, whose substance is **Jesus Christ**.

The bronze altar appears here, though the account of its construction was not included in Kings.

Every sacrifice was offered upon it, and it sanctified the gift.

People worshiping in the courts could see the sacrifices burning.

That sight might lead them to consider the **great Sacrifice** who would come in the fullness of time to take away sin and end death—something the blood of bulls and goats could never accomplish.

As the smoke rose, they could direct their own hearts to heaven in holy longing for God and his favor.

In all our worship, the eye of faith should remain fixed on Christ.

The temple contained more furnishings than the tabernacle had done.

This pictured the future **enlargement of God's church and multiplication of worshipers**.

Blessed be God, there is enough in Christ for every one of them.

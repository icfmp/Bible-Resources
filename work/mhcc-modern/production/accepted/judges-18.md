# Judges 18 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The Danites seek to enlarge their inheritance, and rob Micah.**

## The stolen idols of Micah

The Danites decided to take Micah's gods with them.

How foolish they were!

How could they expect those gods to protect them when the gods could not even protect **themselves from being stolen**?

Taking the images for their own worship was a double crime.

It showed that they neither feared God nor respected other people.

They had lost both true religion and ordinary honesty.

Micah was equally foolish.

He called objects **his gods**, although he himself had made them.

The only One worthy of our worship as God is the One **who made us**.

Whatever we value as though our whole life depended upon it has taken God's place in our hearts.

If people devoted to idols are willing to live for those false gods, how much more should we love and serve the only true God!

# 2 Chronicles 10 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The ten tribes revolt from Rehoboam.**

## The division of the kingdom

Moderate and reasonable counsel is usually the wisest and best.

Gentleness can accomplish what force and harshness cannot.

Most people respond better to words spoken kindly.

Good words cost us only a little self-denial, yet they may accomplish a great deal.

To ruin people, it is often enough merely to leave them under the rule of **their own pride and anger**.

Yet whatever human beings plan, God continues accomplishing his purposes through everything and fulfilling the word he has spoken.

No one can pass on wisdom or prosperity as an inheritance to children by simply choosing to do so.

Nevertheless, our conduct—whether good or bad—usually affects those who come after us.

Therefore, let us pursue the good things that will belong to us forever.

Let us seek God's blessing for our children and their descendants **rather than wealth or worldly promotion**.

# 2 Chronicles 1 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Solomon's choice of wisdom, His strength and wealth.**

## Solomon seeks wisdom

Solomon began his reign by visiting God's altar publicly and reverently.

People who eagerly pursue present and earthly things are likely to be disappointed.

Those who entrust their circumstances to God's providence may not possess the most, but they enjoy the **greatest comfort** in what they have.

Those who make this world their ultimate goal fail to obtain the next world, and are disappointed with this one as well.

Those who aim instead for the world to come will receive it, with complete satisfaction; meanwhile they will also receive as much of this world's goods as is truly good for them along the way.

Let us therefore be content without the impressive things that people usually covet, since those things often become deadly traps for the soul.

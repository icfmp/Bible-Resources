# Hebrews 8 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The excellence of Christ's priesthood above that of Aaron is shown. | 1–6 |
| The great excellence of the new covenant above the former. | 7–13 |

## Christ is the High Priest we need (verses 1–6)

The central conclusion is that believers possess **exactly the High Priest they need**.

The Son took human nature, lived on earth and offered himself to God as a sacrifice for **his people's sins**.

We must never approach God or present any worship to him except **through Christ, depending on his merits and intercession**.

Only **in the Beloved** are we accepted.

All worship and obedience must stay close to **God's word, the complete and perfect standard**.

Jesus is the purpose and fulfillment of the law of righteousness.

The covenant contrasted here with Christ's superior covenant was made with **Israel as a nation**, with promises of temporal advantages.

The gospel promises, made certain through Christ, concern **spiritual blessings and eternal life** of infinitely greater worth.

Praise God for a priest suited perfectly to our helpless condition.

## God writes his law on believers' hearts (verses 7–13)

Christ's priesthood surpasses Aaron's through the **covenant of grace** that he mediates.

The law held sinners accountable but could neither remove their guilt nor quiet the conscience terrified by sin.

Through Christ's blood **full forgiveness** is provided, and God will remember believers' sins no more.

God once **wrote his law for his people**. Now he promises **to write it within them**.

He gives them understanding to know and believe it, memory to retain it, hearts to love it, courage to confess it and power to obey it.

When this foundation is laid, obedience becomes wiser, more sincere, willing, determined, steady and joyful.

The Holy Spirit's abundant work through gospel ministry brings an increase of Christian understanding across every sort of person.

May God fulfill this promise in our time and bless his ministers, so that many may believe and turn to him!

True knowledge of God comes together with **the pardon of sins**.

That forgiveness is **freely given, complete and unchangeable**.

Forgiveness opens the door to every other spiritual blessing, while unforgiven sin blocks mercy and brings judgment.

Let us examine whether the Holy Spirit has taught us to **know, love, fear, trust and obey Christ sincerely**.

All worldly vanity, external religious advantage and empty notions of faith will soon pass away. Those who trust in them rather than Christ will be left without hope.

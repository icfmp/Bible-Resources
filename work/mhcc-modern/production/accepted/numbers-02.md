# Numbers 2 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The order of the tribes in their tents.**

## The arrangement of Israel's camp

The tribes camped around the tabernacle, which stood in the middle of Israel's camp.

The tabernacle showed God's gracious presence among his people.

Yet the people had to place their tents at a respectful distance from the sanctuary.

The Israelites took their assigned positions without complaining or arguing.

Their orderly camp was both safer and more beautiful because of this obedience.

It is both our duty and our advantage to be satisfied with the position God has given us.

We should carry out our responsibilities well without envy, resentment, ambition or greed.

The church of the gospel should likewise be well joined together according to Scripture's pattern.

Each member should know and keep the appropriate place within the body.

When this happens, everyone who loves the church can rejoice at its good order (Colossians 2:5).

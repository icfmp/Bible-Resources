# 2 Chronicles 34 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Josiah's good reign in Judah.**

## Josiah seeks the Lord from his youth

Our earliest childhood years cannot usually be devoted to much useful service to others.

That makes it wise to dedicate **our earliest youth** to God, rather than waste the short life that remains.

Happy are those who seek the Lord and prepare to be useful while young, when others are chasing sinful pleasures, acquiring destructive habits and forming harmful relationships.

Who can calculate the sorrow prevented by early faith and the blessings it produces?

Careful self-examination and vigilance reveal the deceitfulness and wickedness of our hearts and the sins within our lives.

Josiah's example encourages us to humble ourselves before God and seek him.

Believers also learn not to fear death but to welcome it when it spares them from evils still to come.

Nothing hastens a nation's ruin more than rejecting earnest attempts at reform.

God will not be mocked.

Only he can turn the deepest flow of a person's affections, raising those who are dead in trespasses and sins.

His grace in the lives of those who seek the Savior while young has a special beauty.

Has **Jesus, the Day-spring from on high**, visited you?

Can you, like Josiah, remember first recognizing this light and life during your youth?

What extraordinary happiness comes from knowing Jesus from one's earliest years!

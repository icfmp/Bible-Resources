# Psalm 52 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The enemies of the truth and the church described, Their destruction. | 1–5 |
| The righteous rejoice. | 6–9 |

## Those who boast in wrongdoing (verses 1–5)

People who boast about sin are really boasting about their own shame.

Sinners abuse God's patience and restraint as an excuse to become hardened in wrongdoing.

But enemies of the church boast uselessly in their harmful plans, while believers may depend upon **God's mercy**.

A statement that contains some truth may still be a lie when its words are arranged to give an **intentionally false impression**.

The more cunning and careful the planning behind an evil deed, the more it reflects **Satan's character**.

When faithful people die, they are transplanted from earthly life into heaven, **the Lord's garden**, where they take root forever.

But wicked people who persist in sin are uprooted and perish.

Believers recognize that God will finally destroy people who refuse to make him their strength.

## The righteous trust God's mercy (verses 6–9)

Those who think power and wealth can sustain them without God deceive themselves.

The wicked person trusts the abundance of riches and expects wicked schemes to preserve them.

Such a person may acquire and retain possessions by any means, destroying anyone who stands in the way.

That seems to promise strength, but its end is ruin.

People who dwell through **faith and love** in God's house are like **green olive trees**.

To flourish in this way, we must live in holy confidence in God and his grace.

Praising him frequently gives beauty to our profession of faith and helps every grace become fruitful.

We can never run out of reasons to praise.

**His name alone is our refuge and strong tower**.

It is good to wait upon his saving name.

Nothing calms a troubled spirit more or keeps us from crooked methods of escape than **patiently hoping for the Lord's salvation**.

No one has followed his guidance and reached a bad end.

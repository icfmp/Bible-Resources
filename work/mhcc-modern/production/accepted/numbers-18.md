# Numbers 18 — Modern English

## The responsibilities of priests and Levites (verses 1–7)

The Israelites complained that approaching God was difficult and dangerous.

God therefore reminded them that the priests were appointed to come near to him on their behalf.

Aaron had no reason to be proud of his promotion.

His office carried heavy responsibilities and serious risks.

People entrusted with great authority or important work should remain humble and reverent toward God.

The greater the trust placed in us, the more danger there is that we might betray it.

This is one reason not to envy another person's honors or seek a high position merely for status.

## God's provision for the priests (verses 8–19)

All believers are spiritual priests, and God has promised to care for them.

Faithfulness to him carries promises for this life as well as the next.

From the provision made for Israel's priests, the apostle teaches that Christian congregations should support their ministers.

Poor or inadequate support can contribute to ministers' falling into disgraceful behavior.

Priests were to devote themselves fully to their sacred work.

They were not to be constantly distracted or disturbed by worries about earning a living through ordinary business.

Their lives should also demonstrate faith in God's providence and in the worship he had appointed.

The finest portions of firstfruits were to be offered to the Lord.

Those who try to save for themselves by giving God only unwanted leftovers deceive themselves.

God is not mocked.

## God's provision for the Levites (verses 20–32)

Just as Israel was distinct from other nations, the Levites were distinct from the other tribes.

People who have God himself as their permanent inheritance and portion should regard the world's possessions with a holy freedom from attachment.

The Levites also had to give God his due from their tithes, just as the other Israelites gave from their harvests.

Henry draws two lessons from verse 31 about enjoying earthly possessions without guilt.

**First**, what we own must have been obtained honestly and through rightful work.

Food is best enjoyed when earned through labor.

Anyone unwilling to work should not expect to eat (2 Thessalonians 3:10).

**Second**, God must receive the portion due to him.

We can enjoy our possessions properly when we have honored the Lord through them.

Giving the best to him prevents our possessions from becoming occasions of sin.

We should also give generously to those in need, so that what remains is enjoyed gratefully and with a clear conscience.

# Amos 1 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Judgments against the Syrians, Philistines, Tyrians, Edomites, and Ammonites.**

## God's warning to the surrounding nations

God chose **a shepherd and herdsman** to rebuke and warn his people.

People to whom God gives abilities for service must not be despised because of **their background or occupation**.

The prophet announced judgment against the surrounding nations that had oppressed God's people.

The expression concerning **three transgressions and four** does not specify an exact number. It means **many sins**: these nations had filled up the measure of their wrongdoing and were ripe for judgment.

God deals with these nations in ways that resemble one another, but each judgment also contains **something particular**.

In every age people have shown bitter hostility toward the Lord's people.

How dreadful are God's judgments when he calls his enemies to account!

# Psalm 67 — Modern English

## Original heading

**A prayer for the enlargement of Christ's kingdom.**

## A prayer for the nations

Every part of our happiness depends upon God's mercy. Therefore the first request is that God show mercy to sinners like us and forgive our sins.

Forgiveness comes through his blessing and is secured in it.

If we walk with him through faith, we may hope for **his face to shine upon us**.

The psalmist then prayed for **the conversion of the Gentiles**.

This shows that the saints of the Old Testament desired the privileges they enjoyed to be shared with others.

Many biblical promises and prophecies appear in the form of prayers.

God's answers to the prayers of the church are as certain as the fulfillment of his promises.

The joy desired for the nations is **holy joy**.

Let them rejoice that the Lord governs kingdoms by Providence and will bring even the kingdoms of this world under **the reign of Christ**.

That prospect offers every blessing.

The spread of the gospel brings benefits even to earthly society: **righteousness exalts a nation**.

God's blessing makes our created comforts genuinely sweet and useful.

The psalm looks forward to **the whole world worshiping the Lord**.

Once the gospel begins to spread, it continues onward until it reaches the ends of the earth.

It is good to join those whom God blesses.

Without Scripture's promises about the conversion of nations, efforts to evangelize them might appear hopeless.

But God has spoken so confidently that we may undertake **missionary work** trusting he will fulfill his word.

How could we refuse to share the knowledge of God and the salvation in which we rejoice?

Those who have never heard **cannot learn unless someone teaches them**.

Let us therefore go forward in God's strength and seek the **Holy Spirit's accompanying power** upon the proclaimed word.

Then Satan's kingdom will be overthrown, and **our Redeemer's kingdom** established.

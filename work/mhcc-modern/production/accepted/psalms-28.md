# Psalm 28 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| A prayer in distress. | 1–5 |
| Thanksgiving for deliverance. | 6–9 |

## Prayer in danger (verses 1–5)

David prayed earnestly in faith, trusting the Lord as **the Rock on whom his hope rested**.

Believers should continue praying until they receive some assurance that God has heard them.

David asked not to be numbered with wicked people.

He wanted deliverance from their traps and from the danger of being **infected by their sins**.

His request was that God would never allow him to adopt the deceitful and treacherous methods his enemies were using against him.

Even the best believers are conscious of how readily they may be drawn aside. We should therefore pray diligently for preserving grace.

Those careful not to share in wicked deeds have reason to hope they will escape the judgments pronounced upon them.

David spoke in **verse 4** of God's righteous judgments against evildoers.

Henry is explicit that this was **not the expression of revenge or uncontrolled passion**.

It was a prophetic statement that God will judge everyone who persists in wrongdoing.

The Lord will call sinners to account not only for the harm they have done, but also for the evil they **planned and attempted**.

Disregarding God's works is a source of sin and ultimately of ruin.

## Thanksgiving and intercession (verses 6–9)

When God answers prayer, we should bless him.

He is our strength, upholding us through every service and suffering.

A believing heart will eventually rejoice. We should expect **joy and peace through faith**, and give all the praise to God.

The saints rejoice not only in their own comfort but also in the blessings of others.

The more people share in sunlight—or in **God's favorable presence**—the less none of us receives.

David ended with a short but comprehensive prayer for God's people, who are **his precious inheritance**.

He asked God to save them, bless them with every good gift, and especially nourish their souls through **his appointed ordinances**.

He prayed that God would guide their conduct and direct events for their good, not merely for his present generation but for all who would follow.

Only in heaven will God's people be **lifted up forever**, never again to fall or be cast down.

Lord Jesus, save us from our sins. As the Son of Abraham, bless us with righteousness.

As the **good Shepherd of the sheep**, feed us, and as **the Resurrection and the Life**, lift us forever from the dust.

# Psalm 135 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| God to be praised for his mercy. | 1–4 |
| For his power and judgments. | 5–14 |
| The vanity of idols. | 15–21 |

## Praising the Lord's gracious election (verses 1–4)

Our praise should center on **the blessings of grace** flowing from God's everlasting love.

We should love and honor his name because he is our **covenant God and Father through Christ**, blessing us with every spiritual blessing in him.

The Lord chose a people to belong to him, so that **his name would be honored and praised** through them.

If they fail to thank him for this special favor, they show extraordinary ingratitude and unworthiness.

## God's power and faithfulness to his people (verses 5–14)

The Lord is, and will always be, **the same gracious, faithful God who works wonders** for his church.

And his church, in response, will remain **a thankful people who praise him**.

In this way his name endures through every generation.

He will return to his people with mercy and will delight in **doing them good**.

## Rejecting idols and witnessing through a holy life (verses 15–21)

The psalm teaches believers to resist idolatry and false worship by showing **what sort of gods the nations had been worshiping**.

The more tragic their condition, the more thankful we should be that we have received a greater knowledge of God.

We ought to **pity them, pray for them and seek their good**, not treat them with contempt.

May we glorify God's name and commend his truth not merely through speech, but also through **holy lives that imitate the goodness and faithfulness of Jesus Christ**.

# Jeremiah 41 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Ishmael murders Gedaliah. | 1–10 |
| Johanan recovers the captives, and purposes to retire to Egypt. | 11–18 |

## Ishmael murders Gedaliah (verses 1–10)

People who hate God's worshipers sometimes **pretend to be godly** in order to harm them more easily.

Death often arrives where it is least expected.

We should therefore continually examine whether our hearts and lives are in the condition we would wish to be found in when summoned before **our Judge**.

Sometimes a person's riches can obtain a temporary escape from deadly danger.

Yet those who imagine they can **bribe death** itself, saying, “Do not kill us; we have treasure hidden in the fields,” are terribly mistaken.

This sorrowful history warns us never to suppose that we are completely safe in this world.

**We cannot be certain of uninterrupted peace until we reach heaven.**

## Johanan rescues the captives but plans to flee (verses 11–18)

The success of wickedness is short-lived.

No one can truly prosper by hardening the heart against God.

Those who make excuses for sinful choices by pretending to fear a danger may then justly lose comfort because of **real dangers**.

A wise and peaceful ruler may be removed, only for a rash and ambitious person to rise in that ruler's place.

Such changes deeply affect the welfare of many people.

Only those who **fear the Lord and walk in his ways** can enjoy lasting steadiness and true happiness.

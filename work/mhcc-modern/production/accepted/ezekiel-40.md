# Ezekiel 40 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The Vision of the Temple.**

## The temple vision and its meaning

This chapter begins a vision that continues **through Ezekiel 48**, at the end of the book.

Henry rightly regards it as **one of the most difficult passages in Scripture**.

When we cannot resolve a difficult passage, we should thank God that our salvation does not depend upon understanding every detail of it.

Everything necessary for salvation has been made sufficiently clear.

We should patiently wait for God to reveal what we cannot yet understand.

This chapter describes **the two outer courts of the temple**.

Henry says it is **unclear whether the figure Ezekiel sees is the Son of God or a created angel**.

What is certain is that **Christ is both our Altar and our Sacrifice**.

We should look to him in faith whenever we approach God.

Christ is salvation **in the midst of the earth** (**Psalm 74:12**), and people may look to him from every direction.

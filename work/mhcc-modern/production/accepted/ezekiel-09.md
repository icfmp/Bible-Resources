# Ezekiel 9 — Modern English

## Original heading

**A vision denoting the destruction of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and the departure of the symbol of the Divine presence.**

## The faithful remnant is marked (verses 1–4)

It is a great comfort for believers that, even among destroyers and destruction, **there is a Mediator**.

He is **the great High Priest**, with access to heaven, and believers on earth have an interest in him.

God's glory appeared to move from above the ark toward the threshold.

This signified that the Lord was about to leave his **mercy seat** and announce judgment against the people.

Those marked for preservation were known by their sorrow over Jerusalem's sins.

They **sighed and cried to God in prayer** because of the abominations taking place in the city.

Those whom God keeps pure amid widespread wickedness he can keep safe amid widespread distress.

## The judgment begins at the sanctuary (verses 5–11)

The slaughter was to **begin in the sanctuary**.

This showed that God is especially displeased by sins committed by people **nearest to him and most privileged by his gifts**.

The person appointed to protect the faithful reported that his task was completed.

This points, in Henry's application, to **Christ's perfect faithfulness** to his Father's trust.

The Son was appointed to give eternal life to the people entrusted to him.

He declares that he has **lost none whom the Father gave him**.

If we are rescued while others perish, all the credit belongs to **God's mercy**.

We too have deserved his wrath.

We should continue **interceding for others**.

But wherever the Lord shows judgment rather than mercy, he acts with no injustice: he gives people what their ways deserve.

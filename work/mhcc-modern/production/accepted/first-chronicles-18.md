# 1 Chronicles 18 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's victories.**

## David's victories

This chapter repeats the events recorded in **2 Samuel 8**.

Our good fight of faith, under the **Captain of our salvation**, will end in everlasting triumph and peace.

The happiness of Israel through David's victories and his just government was only a faint picture of the happiness awaiting God's redeemed people in heaven.

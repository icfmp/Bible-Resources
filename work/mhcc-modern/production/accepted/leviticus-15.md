# Leviticus 15 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Laws concerning ceremonial uncleanness.**

## Ceremonial uncleanness and the need for purity

We do not need to speculate in great detail about these laws.

Rather, we have reason to give thanks that Christians are no longer obliged to undergo burdensome ceremonial purifications.

What truly defiles us is sin.

These regulations remind us that God sees everything, even things people never notice.

They point to the great duties of the gospel: faith and repentance.

They also illustrate its great privileges: Christ's blood applied to our souls for justification and his grace given for our sanctification.

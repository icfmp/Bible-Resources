## The leaders renew the temple work (verses 1 and 2)

Construction of the temple had been interrupted for about **fifteen years**.

Then God sent the people **two faithful ministers** to urge them to resume.

It is a sign that God intends mercy for a people when he raises up prophets to help them in the work and ways of the Lord—as guides, overseers and leaders.

The prophet **Haggai** shows us what mighty things God accomplishes through **his word** and through **the Spirit working with that word**.

## The elders answer the opposition (verses 3–17)

When we are engaged in God's work, his eye rests upon us for good and we receive his special protection.

That should encourage us to continue even when difficulties seem overwhelming.

The Jewish elders explained to their opponents what they were doing and why.

We should learn to be ready, with gentleness and reverence, to explain **the hope within us** and to give truthful reasons for our acts of service.

We must first understand our own responsibilities, then state them clearly.

While living in this world, we must acknowledge that **our sins provoke God's anger**.

Our troubles spring from human wrongdoing, while all our comforts come from his undeserved mercy.

Even when the work seems obstructed, **Jesus Christ** is carrying it forward.

His people are being built together as **a holy temple in the Lord, a dwelling place of God through the Spirit**.

# 1 Samuel 19 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Jonathan reconciles his father to David, Saul again tries to slay him. | 1–10 |
| David flees to Samuel. | 11–24 |

## Jonathan pleads for David, but Saul attacks him again (verses 1–10)

How powerful a truthful and well-reasoned appeal can be!

For a time, Jonathan convinced Saul that his hatred toward David was unreasonable.

Yet Saul soon returned to his murderous hostility.

This shows how deeply rooted the serpent's hatred is against the woman's offspring, and how deceptive and desperately corrupt the human heart remains without God's grace (**Jeremiah 17:9**).

A temporary conviction of wrongdoing is not the same as a lasting change of heart.

## David escapes and seeks counsel from Samuel (verses 11–24)

Michal's plan to gain time for David to escape was permissible, Henry says.

But the **falsehood** she told could not even be excused as necessary.

Her conduct showed that she did not possess the same spirit of godliness that had shaped Jonathan's words to Saul.

David fled to Samuel because he made **God his refuge**.

As a prophet, Samuel could offer the most reliable advice in a time of distress.

David had found little rest or satisfaction at Saul's court.

He now sought rest among the worshipers gathered around Samuel.

Those who enjoy fellowship with God may have few pleasures in this world, yet those pleasures are precious.

David returned to that fellowship in trouble.

Saul was so determined to kill David that repeated providential defeats of his plans did not convince him David was under God's special protection.

When God chose to protect David by an extraordinary means, **Saul himself prophesied**.

Many people possess remarkable spiritual gifts but lack saving grace.

They may even prophesy in Christ's name and still be rejected by him.

Let us therefore ask daily for renewing grace, which will become a spring of water welling up into eternal life.

Let us remain firmly devoted to **truth and holiness**.

In every danger and difficulty, seek God's protection, comfort and guidance through the means of worship and instruction he has appointed.

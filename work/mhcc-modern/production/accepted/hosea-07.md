# Hosea 7 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The manifold sins of Israel. | 1–7 |
| Their senselessness and hypocrisy. | 8–16 |

## Israel's many sins (verses 1–7)

Israel's wickedness arose from a practical refusal to believe that **God governs and sees human affairs**.

The people acted as though he neither saw their sins nor cared about them.

Their wrongdoing surrounded them.

Their hearts were inflamed by sinful desires **like a blazing oven**.

Even when the nation was in trouble, they never thought to seek God's help.

The visible acts of sin in a person's life are **only a small part of the evil within the heart**.

When a sinful desire is deliberately cherished inwardly, it eventually breaks out into actual wrongdoing.

Those who tempt others into drunkenness **cannot be true friends**.

They often intend the other person's ruin.

Thus people become instruments of judgment against one another.

Those who continue living **without prayer even during danger and distress** are not merely heated with sin; they have become **hardened in it**.

## Israel's instability and hypocrisy (verses 8–16)

Israel resembled **a cake not turned over**: burned on one side and still raw on the other, useless throughout.

The nation mixed **the worship of Jehovah with idolatry**.

Signs of approaching destruction had appeared, like **gray hair announcing age**, yet Israel did not notice them.

The pride that moves people to break God's law also encourages **self-flattery**.

God's mercy and grace are **the only safe refuge**, but stubborn sinners seldom think of fleeing there.

They may cry out in fear with words resembling prayer, but **their hearts do not truly turn to God**.

Even requests for earthly blessings may aim only to provide more fuel for sinful pleasures.

Changing from one religious group, opinion, outward form or particular vice to another still leaves people far from **Christ and genuine holiness**.

This is what we are like by nature, and what we will become if left to ourselves.

Let us therefore pray:

**Create a clean heart in us, O God, and renew a right spirit within us.**

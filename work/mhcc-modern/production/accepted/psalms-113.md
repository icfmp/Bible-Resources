# Psalm 113 — Modern English

## Original heading

**An exhortation to praise God.**

## Praise the God who raises the lowly

God receives praise from his own people.

They have special reason to praise him because they know him as his servants and experience so much of his kindness.

Speaking well of such a Master is a joyful and pleasant task.

His name ought to be praised **everywhere, from east to west**.

It should be praised throughout the world, though this is not yet the case.

One day all nations will come to worship him.

God is exalted above every blessing and praise we can offer.

We should ask with holy amazement: **Who is like the Lord our God?**

How astonishing that he stoops to observe the affairs of the earth!

How much more astonishing that **the Son of God came from heaven to earth and took our human nature** to seek and save the lost!

He loved guilty sinners so greatly that he became human to ransom their souls.

Sometimes the Lord displays his wisdom and power by choosing people whom nobody would have considered capable of important work—not even the people themselves.

He called **fishermen to become fishers of people**.

That is his customary way in the kingdom of grace.

He receives people who are naturally beggars and even traitors, makes them his friends and children, and gives them honor as **kings and priests**.

He counts them among the princes of his chosen people.

All our comforts come from him.

They can be especially welcome after a long delay, when we had given up expecting them.

Let us pray that countries still barren of gospel fruit will soon become fruitful and that many converts will join in praising the Lord.

# Judges 21 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The Israelites lament for the Benjamites.**

## Israel's rash oath and the cost of misguided zeal

The Israelites mourned for the tribe of Benjamin.

They were troubled by the oath they had made not to allow their daughters to marry Benjamite men.

People often care more about defending **their own authority and decisions** than about respecting God's authority.

They would have acted better if they had repented of their rash oath, brought the prescribed **sin offerings** and sought forgiveness in the way God appointed.

Instead, they tried to avoid being guilty of breaking their oath by doing things **just as wrong as breaking it**.

The fact that people may advise **treachery or violence** while believing they are fulfilling a moral duty shows how blind the human mind can become when left to itself.

It also reveals the disastrous consequences of a conscience misled by ignorance and error.

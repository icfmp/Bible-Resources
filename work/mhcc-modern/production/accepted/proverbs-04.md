# Proverbs 4 — Modern English

## Receiving the instruction of wisdom (verses 1–13)

We should regard faithful teachers **as fathers** and welcome their instruction, even when it includes correction and rebuke.

Solomon's parents instructed him **because they loved him**.

Wise and godly people in every age and social position agree that **true wisdom is expressed through obedience and joined to real happiness**.

Seek that wisdom diligently.

Especially seek mastery over **the sinful desires within your own heart**.

Work harder to obtain this spiritual wisdom than to acquire worldly riches.

Nothing is more necessary than **a saving share in Christ**.

A soul without wisdom and grace is **spiritually dead**.

How pitiful to possess wealth, status and power but die **without understanding, without Christ, without hope and without God**!

Listen to the One who has **the words of eternal life**.

Hold firmly to his instruction.

Then your path will be clear, and you can avoid being trapped or stumbling.

## Avoiding evil paths and guarding the heart (verses 14–27)

The path of wicked people can appear pleasant or like the quickest way to accomplish something.

But it is evil and will end in harm.

If you love God and care for your soul, **stay far away**.

Do not simply try to keep an adequate distance from temptation.

Go as far from it as you can.

**The path of the righteous is light**, for Christ is both **their Way and their Light**.

They will not become perfectly holy until heaven, but there they will shine like the sun.

**The path of sin is darkness**.

Wicked people stumble without knowing why, and often do not reflect on why God has brought hardship upon them or what the outcome may be.

Listening attentively to Scripture is evidence that grace has begun working in the heart and a means through which that work grows.

**God's word contains the right remedy for every disease of the soul**.

Guard your heart with all diligence.

Protect it from doing harm and being harmed, because **life flows from the heart**.

Above all, ask **the Lord Jesus for living water**—the Holy Spirit who sanctifies and gives life that lasts forever.

Then you can put away corrupt speech and dishonest lips.

Turn your eyes away from empty attractions and look straight ahead.

Follow the rule of God's word and **walk in the footsteps of the Lord and Master**.

Lord, forgive our past sins and help us follow you more faithfully in the future.

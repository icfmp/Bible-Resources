# Proverbs 7 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |

## Keep God's commands close to your heart (verses 1–5)

We must treasure God's commands and guard them carefully.

The instruction is more than **“Keep them, and you will live.”** We should keep them **as people who cannot live without them**.

Some people dismiss careful, conscientious obedience as unnecessarily strict.

But God's law is to be guarded like **the pupil of the eye**.

Indeed, when God's law is written on the heart, it serves as **the eye of the soul**.

Let the word of God dwell within us and be written where it will always be available for us to read and recall.

In this way we are protected against the destructive effects of our sinful desires and the traps of Satan.

Let God's word strengthen both **our fear of sin** and **our determination to avoid it**.

## Beware the ways of seducers (verses 6–27)

This passage gives a deeply moving example of the danger of **youthful lust**.

Whether understood as history or as a parable, it teaches a vital lesson.

After such a vivid account of the danger of approaching sexual temptation, who would dare to place themselves deliberately in its path?

That would resemble a person **dancing at the edge of a high cliff immediately after watching someone else fall over it**.

Those who bring ruin upon themselves begin by disregarding God's good commands.

We should pray every day that God will keep us from **running into temptation**.

Otherwise, we virtually invite the enemies of our souls to lay traps for us.

Keep well away from places and situations that encourage vice.

Beware especially of sins advertised as **pleasant**.

Their apparent attractiveness makes them more dangerous: they readily capture the heart and harden it against repentance.

**Consider how an action will end before you begin it**.

Even if someone lived as long as **Methuselah** and spent every day enjoying the greatest pleasures sin could offer, **one hour** of the anguish and trouble following such a life would outweigh them all.

# Psalm 111 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The Lord is to be praised for his works.**

## Praise for God's works and covenant faithfulness

The psalmist resolved to praise God himself.

Our personal example should agree with what we encourage other people to do.

God's works are the proper subject of praise, including his **providence toward the world, his church and individual people**.

Although the Lord's works are many, they can be spoken of as **one work**, because all the different events of Providence contribute to a single wise purpose.

When we examine his works humbly and diligently, we discover that they are **just and holy**.

Of all God's works, **his forgiveness of sin is the most wonderful**, and we should remember it for his glory.

God never forgets his covenant.

He has always remembered it and always will.

His works of Providence follow the truth of his promises and prophecies.

They are therefore true.

Because he has the right to govern the earth as he chooses, they are also righteous.

The same is true of **his work of grace in human hearts**.

Every divine command is sure.

All God's commandments were fulfilled by Christ, and they remain our standard of conduct in him.

The Lord redeemed his people from Egypt and delivered them repeatedly afterward.

These rescues pictured **the greater redemption accomplished by Jesus Christ in the fullness of time**.

In that redemption, everlasting righteousness shines together with boundless mercy.

No one is truly wise without **the fear of the Lord**.

That reverence leads to repentance, faith in Christ, vigilance and obedience.

Those who live this way possess real understanding, even if they are poor, lack formal learning or are despised by others.

# 1 Chronicles 3 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Genealogies.**

## David's descendants

Among all Israel's families, none was more distinguished than **David's family**.

This chapter gives a full account of that line.

**Jesus Christ**, as to his human ancestry, came from David's family.

Attentive readers will also notice how many advantages the children of the righteous enjoy.

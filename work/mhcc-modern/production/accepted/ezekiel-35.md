# Ezekiel 35 — Modern English

## Original heading

**A prophecy against Edom.**

## Edom's hatred will bring desolation (verses 1–9)

Anyone who has **God against them** also has **God's word against them**.

Those who cultivate unrelenting hatred toward God and his people display the attitude of **the sinful human mind**.

If they continue in it, they can expect only lasting desolation.

## Edom's boastful words are known to God (verses 10–15)

When we see the vanity of this world revealed through **other people's disappointments, losses and hardships**, we should become less attached to possessions, not more eager to seize them.

God knows **every word we speak**, even the most idle.

The boldest and most defiant expression is not beyond his rebuke.

Through the defeat of his church's enemies, the Lord intends **his own glory**.

We may be certain that he will accomplish his purpose.

Henry looks forward to the fullness of **Jews and Gentiles entering Christ's church**, when opposition characterized by **anti-Christian hostility** will finally be removed.

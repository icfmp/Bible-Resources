# Psalm 26 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David, in this psalm, appeals to God touching his integrity.**

## Integrity through grace

Under the Spirit of prophecy, David speaks of himself in a way that also makes him a **type of Christ**.

The claim of complete and spotless innocence is **fully and uniquely true of Christ**. It is through him that we are made complete.

Someone who walks sincerely while trusting wholly in God's grace is accepted according to the covenant mediated by Jesus, on the basis of **Christ's perfect obedience even to death**.

Such a believer asks God to examine and test the innermost soul.

Recognizing how deceitful the heart can be, the person seeks to discover and put to death every sin, to know whether faith is genuine and to obey God's holy commands.

Carefully avoiding evil company is both **evidence of integrity** and an important means of keeping it.

Hypocrites can attend God's appointed worship, so attendance alone cannot prove sincerity.

But coming in repentance, conscientious obedience and genuine devotion is a good sign of integrity.

David felt secure in God's grace. He delighted in blessing the Lord among congregations on earth and hoped soon to join **the great assembly in heaven**, praising God and the Lamb forever.

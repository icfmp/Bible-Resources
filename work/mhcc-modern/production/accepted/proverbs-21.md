# Proverbs 21 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — The Lord governs every heart

As a farmer directs water through the land, God directs human hearts as he chooses. Believers therefore ask him to turn **their own hearts and others' hearts** toward faith, reverence and love.

## Verse 2 — Do not trust self-assessment

We are partial and biased when judging our own motives and actions.

## Verse 3 — Outward worship cannot excuse injustice

Many deceive themselves by imagining that religious ceremonies make unjust behavior acceptable.

## Verse 4 — Sin becomes the wicked person's life

Pride, ambition, honor, joy and daily occupation can all become expressions of a wicked person's devotion to sin.

## Verse 5 — Diligence plans ahead

Truly diligent people combine steady work with thoughtful preparation.

## Verse 6 — Unlawful wealth brings death

Those seeking riches through forbidden practices are actually **pursuing their own ruin**.

## Verse 7 — Injustice returns upon its author

The injuries people inflict through injustice eventually rebound upon them, bringing destruction both here and hereafter.

## Verse 8 — The crookedness of fallen nature

Without divine grace, humanity's ways are perverse and estranged from God.

## Verse 9 — Seek relief from quarrels through prayer

It is wiser to avoid bitter conflict by pouring our hearts out before God. Through prudence, patience and constant prayer, God may remove the burden.

## Verse 10 — Evil desires corrupt conduct

The wicked heart's desires lead to degrading behavior.

## Verse 11 — Learn from warning and teaching

The inexperienced can learn wisdom from the punishment of evildoers and from instruction given to willing students.

## Verse 12 — Do not envy evil prosperity

Good people recognize the curse hidden in the apparent success of wicked people and do not envy them.

## Verse 13 — Listen to the poor

Those who oppress poor workers through low wages, refuse help when able, or neglect justice while in authority stop their ears to the needy. **Charity still requires wise discernment**, but not hardness of heart.

## Verse 14 — Restrain passions by better motives

If money can calm human fury, surely **reason, reverence for God and Christ's command** should be strong enough to restrain it.

## Verse 15 — The joy of true faith

Genuine joy is found in living according to true religion.

## Verse 16 — Even straying sinners may return

Those who turn deliberately toward spiritual darkness are in special danger. Yet **the all-sufficient Savior** still offers hope: let them flee to him without delay.

## Verse 17 — Worldly pleasure brings ruin

A life dedicated to sinful and worldly pleasures leads to destruction.

## Verse 18 — God's providence delivers the righteous

The righteous may be delivered from trouble while the wicked fall into it instead, appearing almost as a ransom in their place.

## Verse 19 — Unrestrained passions ruin relationships

Unchecked passions destroy peace and comfort within families and other close relationships.

## Verse 20 — Prudence preserves provision

Abundance gained through wisdom, steady work and thrift is valuable. Foolish people waste what they possess on selfish desires.

## Verse 21 — Faith produces a righteous life

True repentance and trust in **God's mercy through Jesus Christ** lead believers to pursue righteousness and mercy in their own conduct.

## Verse 22 — Wisdom may overcome strength

Wise people can accomplish great things even against opponents confident in their superior physical power.

## Verse 23 — Keep the soul from needless trouble

We should especially guard our souls against becoming trapped, agitated and disturbed.

## Verse 24 — Pride becomes habitual anger

Proud and arrogant people often become so passionate that anger seems to be their regular occupation.

## Verses 25 and 26 — The misery of laziness and greed

The lazy person's hands refuse honest work that could supply a living, while the heart never stops craving **wealth, pleasure and honor**. The righteous and industrious find their legitimate desires satisfied.

## Verse 27 — Hypocritical worship is hateful

It is especially detestable to pretend holiness while secretly intending wickedness.

## Verse 28 — False witnesses face judgment

Those who bear false testimony cannot escape its ultimate consequences.

## Verse 29 — Ask what God requires

Wicked people defy both God's law and Providence's warnings. Good people ask instead, **“What does God require of me?”**

## Verses 30 and 31 — Means are necessary, but salvation comes from God

We must make responsible use of available means, but our safety and salvation belong **to the Lord alone**. In spiritual warfare, put on **the whole armor of God**, relying for strength upon **his mighty power**.

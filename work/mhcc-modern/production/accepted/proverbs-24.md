# Proverbs 24 — Modern English

## Verses 1 and 2 — Do not envy sinners

Never long to cast away God's restraints in order to live like those who reject him.

## Verses 3–6 — Wisdom builds and strengthens

Godliness and prudence in outward affairs together form a wise person. Knowledge fills the soul with **the Spirit's graces and comforts** and strengthens us for spiritual service and warfare.

## Verses 7–9 — Do not surrender to ignorance or devise harm

The foolish imagine wisdom is beyond them and refuse to seek it. Doing evil is wrong, but **planning evil is worse**. Even sinful thoughts as they first arise require repentance. People who try to make others hateful often become hateful themselves.

## Verse 10 — Take heart in trouble

Trouble can tempt us to despair. Be courageous, and **God will strengthen your heart**.

## Verses 11 and 12 — Rescue those in danger

If someone is threatened by an unjust action, we must do everything within our power to help. How much more should we seek to rescue **immortal souls** through our words and example?

## Verses 13 and 14 — Taste the sweetness of wisdom

The pleasure and benefit of wisdom should move us to seek it. Most people readily enjoy sweet food, but too many have no taste for truth that is **sweet to a renewed soul** and makes us wise for salvation.

## Verses 15 and 16 — The righteous rise after falling

Sincere believers may stumble like travelers over a stone, but they get up and proceed with greater care. **Henry says this refers chiefly to falling into affliction rather than actual sin**.

## Verses 17 and 18 — Do not rejoice over an enemy's distress

We are forbidden to take sinful pleasure in the calamity of our enemies.

## Verses 19 and 20 — Do not envy the prosperity of the wicked

Whatever outward success evildoers enjoy, it does not amount to true happiness.

## Verses 21 and 22 — Faithfulness and social peace

Godly people promote peace in their communities. Better changes may sometimes be necessary, but avoid restless people who delight in upheaval.

## Verses 23–26 — Be fair and give an honest answer

God's wisdom equips people for the duties of their positions. A fair and well-suited answer earns the lasting gratitude of those it benefits.

## Verse 27 — Provide necessities before extras

Attend to necessary work and responsibilities before optional comforts. **Do not go into debt carelessly**.

## Verses 28 and 29 — The duties of a truthful witness

Henry says these verses identify **three failures of a witness**. We must guard testimony against falsehood, careless accusation and retaliation; do not repay others' wrongs with our own.

## Verses 30–34 — The neglected field and neglected soul

Farming is a blessing: without it the earth would be a wilderness. Even ordinary work is ruined by laziness and self-indulgence. **Fields full of thorns and broken walls** picture hearts left untended, where sinful desires grow as people fall asleep spiritually. Let that sight move us to **greater diligence in every good work**.

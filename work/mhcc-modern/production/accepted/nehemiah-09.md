# Nehemiah 9 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| A solemn fast. | 1–3 |
| Prayer and confession of sin. | 4–38 |

## The people fast and read God's word (verses 1–3)

Scripture directs and strengthens our prayers because the Spirit uses it to help us in our weakness.

Careful study of God's word increasingly shows us **our sinfulness and the abundance of his salvation**.

It calls us both to mourn our sin and to rejoice in him.

Every new discovery of divine truth should make us more diligent, not less diligent, in hearing God's word and taking part in his worship.

## Prayer, history and confession (verses 4–38)

Scripture preserves a summary of the prayers offered on this occasion. Undoubtedly much more was said.

Whatever ability God gives us for our duties should be used as fully as possible for his glory.

When confessing sin, it is good to remember God's mercies. His generosity makes our ingratitude more evident and deepens our humility and shame.

The Lord's treatment of Israel displayed his goodness and patience alongside the stubbornness of the people's hearts.

The prophets' testimony was **the Spirit's testimony through them**—the Spirit of Christ speaking through the prophets.

They spoke as they were moved by the Holy Spirit, so their words must be received accordingly.

The account moved the people to wonder at God's mercy and to recognize that their sins had brought them into their present condition.

Only unearned love could deliver them.

Is their history not a picture of our common human nature?

Let us examine **our nation's history and our own lives**.

Recall the advantages we had from childhood and ask how we first responded to them.

Do this often so we may remain humble, grateful and alert.

Pride and stubborn resistance to God destroy the soul.

Yet sometimes it is just as difficult to persuade a **brokenhearted sinner to hope** as it once was to persuade that sinner to fear.

Are you in that condition?

Consider the wonderful promise that God is **ready to forgive**.

Do not stay away from him because you feel unworthy.

Come boldly to his throne of grace, where mercy and timely help can be found.

**He is ready to forgive.**

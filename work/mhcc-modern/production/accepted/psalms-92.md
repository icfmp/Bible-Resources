# Psalm 92 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Praise is the business of the sabbath. | 1–6 |
| The wicked shall perish, but God's people shall be exalted. | 7–15 |

## Thanksgiving on the Sabbath and every day (verses 1–6)

It is a privilege to praise the Lord and hope that he will accept our worship **morning and evening**.

This is not only for the Sabbath, but for **every day**; not only in public gatherings, but also **alone and with our families**.

Each morning, thank him for the mercies of the night.

Each evening, thank him for the mercies of the day.

When going out or coming home, bless his name.

His work of **Providence toward us**, his work of **grace within us**, and above all his great work of **redemption** give us reason for joy.

Many people neither know nor care about his purposes.

Those who have learned to appreciate them through grace have even greater reason for gratitude.

If the ancient believers rejoiced in distant glimpses of the promised Deliverer, **how much more should we love and praise him!**

## Brief prosperity and lasting fruitfulness (verses 7–15)

God sometimes allows wicked people to prosper **as a judgment against them**.

Their flourishing lasts only briefly.

Let us therefore seek the salvation and grace of the gospel, so that **the Holy Spirit continually anoints us** and we may see and share in the Redeemer's glory.

Christ's grace, communicated through his word and Spirit, supplies everything that keeps believers spiritually alive and fruitful.

Ordinary trees cease bearing fruit as they grow old.

But in **God's trees**, spiritual strength need not fade as bodily strength diminishes.

The final days of faithful people may be their best, and their last work their finest.

**Perseverance is reliable evidence of sincerity**.

May every Sabbath, as we contemplate God's faithfulness, find us resting more firmly upon **the Lord our righteousness**.

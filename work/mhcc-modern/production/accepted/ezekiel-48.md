# Ezekiel 48 — Modern English

## Original source structure

**No separate authorial heading; one continuous commentary.**

## The land and the city where the Lord dwells

This chapter describes the **portion of land belonging to each tribe**.

In gospel times, **all things are made new**.

Much of the prophecy is expressed through **symbols and numbers**.

God has used this manner of speech to communicate mysteries whose meaning will not become fully clear until **the proper time**.

Yet access to **Christ's church**—both in its present struggle and its final triumph—is freely open through faith **from every side**.

Christ has opened the kingdom of heaven to all who believe.

Whoever desires may come and freely receive **the water of life and the fruit of the tree of life**.

**The Lord is there**, within his church, near to his people whenever they call upon him.

This is also true of every genuine Christian.

Wherever a soul has **a living principle of divine grace**, it can truly be said: **The Lord is there.**

May we be found among the citizens of that holy city.

May we live in a way worthy of that citizenship and enjoy **the Lord's presence** in life, in death and forever.

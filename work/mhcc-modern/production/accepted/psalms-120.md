# Psalm 120 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The psalmist prays to God to deliver him from false and malicious tongues. | 1–4 |
| He complains of wicked neighbours. | 5–7 |

## Rescued from deceitful accusations (verses 1–4)

The psalmist suffered greatly because of **lying tongues**.

May every godly person be rescued from malicious falsehoods.

His enemies had invented accusations against him.

When he was distressed, he turned to the Lord in earnest prayer.

**God can restrain the tongues of slanderers**.

The psalmist received a gracious answer.

Sinners would hardly dare to act as they do if they understood and seriously considered **the end of their wrongdoing**.

God's terrors are compared to **arrows**, and his wrath to **burning juniper coals** that give intense heat and retain it for a long time.

Such judgment awaits persistent liars.

All who love and practice falsehood will face the final judgment described as **the everlasting lake of fire**.

## Grieved by hostile neighbors, seeking peace (verses 5–7)

Living among wicked people can be a heavy burden for someone who loves God and hopes eventually to be separated from their company forever.

Here we see a mark of a godly person: **a desire to live peaceably with everyone**.

David also pointed forward to **Christ**.

Let us follow that example.

When distressed, call upon the Lord, and he will hear.

Pursue **peace and holiness**, making every effort to **overcome evil with good**.

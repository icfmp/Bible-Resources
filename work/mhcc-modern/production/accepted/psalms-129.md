# Psalm 129 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Thankfulness for former deliverances. | 1–4 |
| A believing prospect of the destruction of the enemies of Zion. | 5–8 |

## Remembering deliverance amid persecution (verses 1–4)

The enemies of God's people have often sought cruelly to wear down and destroy the saints of the Most High.

Yet the Lord has repeatedly delivered his church through grace.

**Christ built his church upon a rock**.

He has many ways of restraining evildoers and preventing them from accomplishing the harm they plan.

The Lord acts righteously when he refuses to allow his people to be completely destroyed, because he has promised **to preserve a people for himself**.

## The fate of those opposing Zion (verses 5–8)

God's people can flourish like a **fruit-bearing palm or green olive tree**.

Their enemies, however, are like **grass growing on rooftops**.

In the lands described here, roofs were flat and the thin layer of soil allowed grass to grow briefly but never mature.

That is the future of schemes directed against God's people.

No wise person asks God to bless people engaged in such destructive work.

Remember that **Jesus rose and now reigns**.

Remember how his people have survived like **the bush that burned but was not consumed**.

Therefore, do not be overcome by fear.

# Jeremiah 47 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The calamities of the Philistines.**

## The Philistines face judgment

The Philistines had long been enemies of Israel.

The **Chaldean army** would now sweep over their territory like a great flood.

When God determines to bring a people down, no one can prevent it.

When he intends to destroy the wicked, he can remove every source of assistance they rely upon.

The devastation caused by war is so dreadful that **peace should be highly valued**.

Yet we must submit to the Lord's appointments, because he orders everything with **perfect wisdom and justice**.

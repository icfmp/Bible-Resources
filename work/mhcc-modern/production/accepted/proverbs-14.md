# Proverbs 14 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — A household built or destroyed

A woman who has no fear of God and is willful, wasteful and given to ease can ruin her own household as surely as if she pulled down its walls.

## Verse 2 — Reverence or contempt

Here grace and sin show their true character. Whoever despises God's commands and promises also despises **God's power and mercy**.

## Verse 3 — Uproot pride

Pride grows from bitterness rooted in the heart. We must pull out **the root**, not merely cut off one visible branch. Wise and prudent words can help people escape difficulties.

## Verse 4 — Useful work involves some inconvenience

No real advantage is obtained without some difficulty or inconvenience—often something small that nevertheless frightens lazy people.

## Verse 5 — Witness truthfully

A conscientious witness will never knowingly describe events differently from what he or she actually knows.

## Verse 6 — Humility receives wisdom

A scoffer treats God's truth with contempt. Someone conscious of ignorance and unworthiness approaches **Scripture humbly**.

## Verse 7 — Recognize unholy conversation

A person's speech can reveal wickedness when no evidence of reverence for God can be found in it.

## Verse 8 — Know the road and its destination

We are travelers. Our concern is not to stare at extraordinary sights, but to learn **the rules for our journey and the destination toward which we walk**. The wicked person deceives himself and continues in error.

## Verse 9 — Do not mock sin or atonement

Careless people treat sin as something trivial, rather than something to grieve over. **Fools mock the sin offering**, and those who treat sin lightly also treat **Christ** lightly.

## Verse 10 — Hidden pain and hidden peace

We do not know what guilt or consuming passions torment an apparently prosperous sinner. Nor does the world understand **a sincere Christian's inward peace**, even amid poverty and sickness.

## Verse 11 — What upholds a household

Sin has ruined many important families, while righteousness often strengthens and raises households of humble standing.

## Verse 12 — Self-deception leads to destruction

Careless, worldly and sensual paths can seem right to those traveling them. Those who deceive themselves may destroy themselves. The joy promised by fleshly pleasure is vain.

## Verse 14 — The dread of returning to sin

Of all sinners, people who have knowingly turned away from their former faith can experience especially terrible fear when reflecting upon their course.

## Verse 15 — Do not believe everything

Readiness to believe anyone's claims can be deeply harmful; Henry recalls how the world first fell through misplaced trust. Spiritually wise people rely **on the Savior alone for acceptance** and guard themselves through God's word.

## Verse 16 — Holy fear protects

Reverent fear of God guards us against everything unholy.

## Verse 17 — Anger and revenge

Someone who loses their temper quickly deserves both rebuke and pity. Deliberate **revengefulness** is still more hateful.

## Verse 18 — Wisdom brings honor

Sin brings shame upon sinners; wisdom brings honor to the wise.

## Verse 19 — Goodness recognized even by enemies

Even wicked people sometimes have to acknowledge the excellence of God's faithful servants.

## Verse 20 — Friendship beyond self-interest

Worldly friendship often depends on personal advantage. How good it is to have **God as our Friend**: he will never abandon us.

## Verse 21 — Do not despise poverty

Looking down on someone because of occupation, clothing or outward appearance is sinful.

## Verse 22 — Plan to do good

Those who not only do good but actively **devise ways of doing good** wisely pursue their own best interests.

## Verse 23 — Real work versus empty talk

Both intellectual and manual labor can produce good results. But religion consisting **only of loud words without action** will achieve nothing.

## Verse 24 — Use wealth for good

The possessions of wise and godly people should enlarge their opportunities to be useful to others.

## Verse 25 — Courageous truthful testimony

An upright witness will risk even the displeasure of powerful people rather than conceal the truth.

## Verses 26 and 27 — Reverent confidence and the fountain of life

Those who fear the Lord so as to obey and serve him have a solid basis for confidence and will be kept in his care. Seek **this fountain of life** and escape death's traps.

## Verse 28 — Seek the growth of Christ's kingdom

Everyone who desires the good of Christ's kingdom should do what they can to bring more people into **his church**.

## Verse 29 — Patience resembles Christ

A gentle and patient person is learning from **Christ, who is Wisdom**. Uncontrolled passion reveals folly.

## Verse 30 — A peaceful heart helps health

Integrity, contentment and kindness contribute toward the well-being of the body.

## Verse 31 — The poor bear the Creator's image

To oppress poor people is to insult **their Creator**.

## Verse 32 — Death and a reliable hope

Wicked people die under sin's guilt and power, as though life were forcibly taken from them. Godly people may suffer and fear death to some degree, but they possess **God's true promise and a blessed hope**.

## Verse 33 — Wisdom in the heart

Wisdom dwells within the heart and therefore governs affections, attitudes and temper.

## Verse 34 — Godliness benefits society

True devotion and holiness promote industry, sobriety and honesty.

## Verse 35 — The King values humble service

The great King who rules heaven and earth rewards faithful servants who honor his gospel by fulfilling duties appropriate to their place. **He does not despise even the lowest service**.

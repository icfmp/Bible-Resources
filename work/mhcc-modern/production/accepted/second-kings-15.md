# 2 Kings 15 — Modern English

## Azariah, also called Uzziah (verses 1–7)

Uzziah generally did what was right.

It was a blessing for Judah that a good reign lasted a long time.

## Israel's later kings (verses 8–31)

This history shows how confused and troubled the kingdom of Israel had become.

Judah also suffered difficulties.

Yet compared with Israel, Judah's situation was a happy one.

The weaknesses that remain in genuine believers are very different from the **deliberately permitted wickedness** of people who do not fear God.

Such is our fallen human nature.

If our hearts were left entirely to themselves, they would prove deceitful above everything else and desperately wicked.

We have good reason to thank the Lord for restraints that have held us back from wrongdoing and for being kept away from temptation.

We should continually ask him to renew a **right spirit** within us.

## Jotham rules Judah (verses 32–38)

Jotham showed great respect for the temple.

Rulers may not be able to accomplish everything they wish in restraining vice and irreverence.

When that happens, they should work all the more diligently to **support and encourage godliness and moral virtue**.

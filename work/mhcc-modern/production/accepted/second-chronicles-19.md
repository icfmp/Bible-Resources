# 2 Chronicles 19 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Jehoshaphat visits his kingdom.**

## Jehoshaphat receives correction and reforms Judah

Whenever we return home safely, we should recognize that Providence preserved our coming and going.

After escaping unusual danger, we have special reason to give thanks.

Exceptional mercies create exceptional obligations.

A prophet plainly told Jehoshaphat that joining Ahab had been very wrong.

Jehoshaphat received the rebuke well.

Notice how it affected his conduct: he searched his kingdom carefully to identify what remained to be corrected.

The prophet's words also showed him that his earlier reforms had pleased God.

That encouraged him to complete what was unfinished.

It is good when commendation spurs us on toward greater faithfulness.

Different gifts and kinds of service come from **the same Spirit** and are given for the common good.

Everyone who has received a gift should use it to serve others.

We should thank God for **judges and ministers, scholars and officials, people who work with books and people who work in practical affairs**.

Jehoshaphat charged them to do everything in reverence for God, with sincere and upright hearts.

They were to work constantly to prevent sin, knowing that it offends God and can bring judgment upon the people.

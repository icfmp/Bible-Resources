# Judges 20 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The tribe of Benjamin nearly extirpated.**

## The near destruction of Benjamin

Israel was right to hate the terrible crime committed at Gibeah and to resolve that those guilty of it should be punished.

But the Israelites acted **too quickly and with too much confidence in themselves** when they made their plans.

The destruction of most of Benjamin was dreadful.

Yet the eternal destruction of human souls is more fearful still.

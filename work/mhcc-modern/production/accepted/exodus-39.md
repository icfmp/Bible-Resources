# Exodus 39 — Modern English

| Original outline | Verses |

| The priests' garments. | 1–31 |

## The priestly garments are completed (verses 1–31)

The priests' clothing was rich and beautiful.

In the early life of the church, these visible symbols taught people about greater blessings that would come later.

But the reality to which they pointed is **Christ and the grace of his gospel**.

Christ is our great High Priest.

When he undertook to redeem us, he clothed himself for service.

He received and displayed the gifts and graces of the Spirit.

He fastened himself to his purpose, resolved to complete the task.

He took responsibility for all God's spiritual Israel.

He kept his people close to his heart, engraved them on the palms of his hands and presented them before his Father.

He crowned himself with **holiness to the Lord**, dedicating all his saving work to the honor of his Father's holiness.

True believers are also spiritual priests.

The pure linen of their clothing for service represents the righteousness of the saints (Revelation 19:8).

## The tabernacle is completed (verses 32–43)

The tabernacle was a picture of Jesus Christ.

The Most High was visibly present in the sanctuary above the ark.

In the same way, God dwelt in the human nature of his beloved Son.

In Christ, all the fullness of deity lives bodily (Colossians 2:9).

The tabernacle also pictured every genuine Christian.

God the Father dwells in the soul of each true follower of the Savior.

He is the One that person worships and the source of every blessing enjoyed.

The tabernacle also represented the Redeemer's church.

The poorest and the most powerful are equally dear to the Father's love, freely given through faith in Christ.

Finally, the tabernacle was a picture of the heavenly temple (Revelation 21:3).

How magnificent will God's glory appear when the cloud is removed and his faithful worshipers see him as he truly is!

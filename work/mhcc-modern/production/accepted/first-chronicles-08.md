# 1 Chronicles 8 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Genealogies.**

## Benjamin's family record

This chapter gives a longer genealogical account of the tribe of **Benjamin**.

Many details that now seem difficult, sudden or confusing probably made good sense to the original readers and fully served the purpose for which these records were written.

At that time, many great and powerful nations existed and many distinguished people were famous.

Their names have now been completely forgotten.

Yet the names of many ordinary people who belonged to the **Israel of God** remain permanently recorded here.

**The memory of the righteous is blessed.**

# 2 Chronicles 13 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Abijah overcomes Jeroboam.**

## Abijah defeats Jeroboam

Jeroboam and his people had deserved severe punishment for their idolatry and apostasy.

Henry understands the victory God permitted Abijah to win as an act of judgment against them.

Yet Abijah himself was **not genuinely devoted to God**, as his character in **1 Kings 15:3** shows.

He drew confidence from his people's faith, not from a faithful life of his own.

People who deny the **power** of godliness often boast in its outward **form**.

Many who have very little genuine faith themselves still value it in others.

Nevertheless, Judah did include many sincere worshipers, and its cause was more righteous.

Surrounded by danger, where could they look for rescue but **upward**?

What an extraordinary comfort it is that the way to God remains open.

They cried out to the Lord.

Earnest prayer is truly a cry for help.

They added a **shout of faith** to their prayer, and became more than conquerors.

Jeroboam escaped Abijah's sword, but God struck him.

No one escapes God's judgment.

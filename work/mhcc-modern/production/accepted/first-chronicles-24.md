# 1 Chronicles 24 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The divisions of the priests and Levites.**

## The priests and Levites receive their duties

When everyone has a place, understands the work assigned to it and carries out that work faithfully, having more people involved is a blessing.

In **Christ's mystical body**, every member contributes something useful to the good of the whole.

Christ is the **High Priest over God's house**.

All believers have been made priests, yet they are to submit to him.

In Christ there is no difference in spiritual acceptance between **bond and free**, or between **older and younger**.

Younger brothers and sisters, if sincere and faithful, are no less acceptable to Christ than their elders.

May we all be children of the Lord, prepared to sing his praises forever in the heavenly temple.

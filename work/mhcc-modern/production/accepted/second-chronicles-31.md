# 2 Chronicles 31 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Hezekiah destroys idolatry.**

## Hezekiah's reforms continue

After celebrating the Passover, the Israelites worked energetically to destroy the monuments of idolatry.

Public worship should encourage us to cleanse **our hearts, homes and workplaces** from the pollution of sin and the idolatry of greed.

It should also move us to encourage others to do the same.

How we respond **after** a solemn act of worship is extremely important for our personal faith, our families and public religion.

The people had tasted the sweetness of the recent Passover and therefore contributed willingly to support temple worship.

Those who genuinely enjoy the benefits of a steady and faithful ministry will not resent the expense of supporting it.

Hezekiah was wholehearted in his purpose and sincere in his dependence upon God in everything he did for the Lord.

God granted him success.

Whether we have **many gifts or few**, may we work to use and develop them and encourage others to do likewise.

Work undertaken sincerely for God's glory will finally bring us honor and comfort.

# Song of Solomon 8 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Desire for communion with Christ. | 1–4 |
| The vehemence of this desire. | 5–7 |
| The church pleads for others. | 8–12 |
| And prays for Christ's coming. | 13,14 |

## Longing for closer fellowship with Christ (verses 1–4)

The church longs for the open affection and constant closeness that brothers and sisters share. By grace believers become participants in God's nature, and **Christ has become their Brother**.

Wherever we meet him, let us acknowledge our relationship to him and our love for him without fearing the contempt of others.

Do we truly desire to serve Christ more faithfully? What fruits of holiness have we prepared to express our love for him?

The church warns her children not to drive away Christ's gracious presence. When tempted, we should remind ourselves not to grieve the Holy Spirit.

## Love that survives every trial (verses 5–7)

The Jewish church came out of the wilderness through God's power and favor. Christ's grace also raised his church from a desolate condition.

A sinful life is a wilderness without true comfort. We cannot escape by relying on our own reasoning or righteousness. We must **lean upon Christ, the Lord our Righteousness, by faith**.

The church asks to remain secure in Christ's heart and under his protection, like a seal impressed upon his heart.

Those who love him fear anything that would draw them away—especially their own actions, which might interrupt the comfort of fellowship.

**No flood can drown Christ's love for his people.** Neither threats nor attractions of the world should lessen our love for him. His love strengthens believers against both persecution and prosperity's temptations.

## The church prays and works for others (verses 8–12)

Henry sees the church here interceding for **the Gentiles**, who had not yet received God's word and the ordinary means of grace.

People brought to Christ should seek ways to bring others to him. Stronger Christians should constantly pray for those who are young or weak in faith.

The images of a wall and door suggest a church founded securely on Christ, the **precious Foundation and Cornerstone**, and a lasting entrance through the removed barrier between peoples.

The Lord promises his care for those not yet called, and the church should likewise remain concerned for them.

Our hearts are vineyards to be guarded diligently. All their fruit belongs to **Christ's praise**. Serving him is no loss: those who labor for him ultimately receive a far greater gain.

## The prayer for Christ's return (verses 13 and 14)

At the close of this dialogue, Christ addresses the church dwelling in the gardens—among his people and in their appointed worship.

He calls her to persevere in prayer and praise, which delight him. She responds by asking him to **return quickly and bring her fully into his presence**.

Until that day, Christ is in heaven, pictured as mountains of sweet spices. At the appointed time, every eye will see him in the glory of the world to come.

Believers look forward to that day and seek to be ready for it. They should faithfully fulfill their present duties, so that others may see their good works and glorify the Father.

Continuing in prayer and thanksgiving, we may grow in joy and strength and look toward death and judgment without fear.

**Even so, come, Lord Jesus.**

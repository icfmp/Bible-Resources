## Ephraim quarrels with Jephthah (verses 1–7)

The people of Ephraim picked the same quarrel with Jephthah that they had previously picked with Gideon.

**Pride** lay at the bottom of it.

Pride is what so often produces strife.

It is wrong to attach insulting names to people or to entire countries, especially to those already facing outward disadvantages.

Such language can provoke quarrels with terrible consequences, as it did here.

Few disputes are more bitter than quarrels among brothers or between people competing for honor.

How carefully we need to watch and pray against sinful tempers!

May the Lord lead all his people to pursue everything that makes for peace.

## Ibzan, Elon and Abdon govern Israel (verses 8–15)

This passage briefly records three more judges of Israel.

The happiest lives and the happiest condition of a community are often those that produce the fewest dramatic events for historians.

What should a wise person truly desire?

To live honorably and quietly.

To be peacefully useful to those around us.

To enjoy a clear conscience.

But above all, without which nothing else is sufficient, to enjoy fellowship with **God our Savior** throughout life and die at peace with both God and other people.

## The visions of judgment upon Israel (verses 1–9)

God shows great patience, but he will not endure a people's stubborn rebellion forever.

Remembering the mercies we once received should make us humble when later blessings disappoint us, just as the produce of an earlier harvest should teach gratitude when the next harvest fails.

The Lord has many ways to humble a sinful nation.

Whatever trouble we experience, our most urgent prayer should be for **forgiveness of sin**.

Sin can quickly reduce a great nation to helplessness.

What would become of Israel if **the hand that should lift the people instead opposed them**?

See the power of prayer!

See what a blessing **people who pray** can be to an entire country.

God is ready and swift to show mercy; he waits to be gracious.

Israel was like a strong wall God himself had built to protect **his sanctuary**.

But the Lord stood upon that wall with **a plumb line**.

It had become a wall that **leaned and bulged**, failing the test of straightness.

God would measure Israel and expose its sin.

There comes a time when people who have often been spared **will be spared no longer**.

Yet even then God still called Israel **his people**.

The prophet's repeated prayers and their earlier successful answers should lead us to seek **the Savior**.

## Amaziah threatens Amos (verses 10–17)

Those who accuse their brothers and sisters in faith often portray them as **enemies of the king and nation**, traitors and disturbers of public peace, even when they are actually among the best friends of both.

People who use religious office for wealth and promotion tend to suppose everyone else is motivated by **the same interests**.

But servants who have received a commission from God, like Amos, should not fear **human opposition**.

Had God not strengthened Amos, he could not have set his face **like flint**.

The Lord frequently chooses **the weak and foolish things of this world** to shame the strong and wise.

Yet even earnest prayers and devoted service cannot always persuade proud sinners to accept **faithful warnings and rebukes**.

Everyone who despises or opposes God's word risks ruin unless they repent.

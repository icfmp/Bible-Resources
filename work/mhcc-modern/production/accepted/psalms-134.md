# Psalm 134 — Modern English

## Original heading

**An exhortation to bless the Lord.**

## Using every available moment for praise

We should encourage our souls to **give glory to God** and to hope for his mercy and grace.

It is an excellent practice to fill otherwise unused moments with **prayer, praise and godly meditation**.

Then spare time need not become a burden.

We will not waste our hours on empty conversation, idle entertainment or indulgence of sinful desires.

**The Lord's blessing is enough to make us happy**.

We should pray for spiritual blessings **not only for ourselves, but also for others**.

Let our prayer be more than **Lord, bless me**.

Let us also say, **Lord, bless you**.

In doing so we declare that **God has enough mercy for others as well as for us**.

We also demonstrate genuine love and goodwill toward them.

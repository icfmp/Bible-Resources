# Ezekiel 23 — Modern English

## Original heading

**A history of the apostacy of God's people from him, and the aggravation thereof.**

## Aholah and Aholibah picture Israel and Judah

The parable gives **Samaria and the northern kingdom of Israel** the name **Aholah**, meaning *“her own tabernacle.”*

Their places of worship came from **their own inventions** rather than God's command.

**Jerusalem and Judah** are called **Aholibah**, meaning *“my tabernacle is in her.”*

Their temple was the place **God himself had chosen** for his name to dwell.

The imagery and language of this passage belong to **the times and circumstances in which they were used**.

These humbling descriptions of fallen human nature should keep us in a spirit of **repentance and sorrow over sin**.

They should remove pride from our hearts and turn us away from **self-righteousness**.

They should also lead us to seek **God's grace continually**, praying that his Holy Spirit would enable us to **put sinful conduct to death** and live in holiness and godliness.

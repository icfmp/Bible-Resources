# Psalm 99 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The happy government God's people are under. | 1–5 |
| Its happy administration. | 6–9 |

## God's holy government (verses 1–5)

God governs the world through **his providence** and governs the church through **his grace**.

He governs both through **his Son**.

Earth's inhabitants have reason to tremble before him.

Yet the Redeemer still waits to show mercy.

Everyone who hears his warning should seek his grace.

The more we humble ourselves before the Lord, the more truly we **exalt him**.

We should worship with reverence because **he is holy**.

## God's servants call upon him (verses 6–9)

Israel's happiness is illustrated by the leaders who served the people best.

They made **God's word and law their standard** in everything, knowing that without obedience they could not expect their prayers to be heard.

They repeatedly prevailed in prayer.

The Lord performed miracles in answer to their requests.

They interceded for the people and received gracious answers bringing peace.

But our **Prophet and High Priest** is of infinitely greater dignity than **Moses, Aaron or Samuel**.

He has received and declared the Father's will to us.

Therefore, do not exalt God with your lips alone.

**Give him the throne in your heart**.

As we worship before his mercy seat, remember always that **he is holy**.

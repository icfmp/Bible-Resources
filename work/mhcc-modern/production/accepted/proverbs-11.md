# Proverbs 11 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — Honest measures

People may treat **false weights and measures** as a small offense, especially when the practice is widespread, but God regards such dishonesty as detestable.

## Verse 2 — The safety of humility

The humble enjoy greater security, peace and ease of mind than the proud. **Wisdom belongs with humility**.

## Verse 3 — Integrity guides life

An honest person's principles are settled, so the right path is clear.

## Verse 4 — Money cannot save us from death

Wealth offers no help when the day of death arrives.

## Verses 5 and 6 — Sin destroys its own followers

Wicked ways are dangerous. **Sin eventually becomes its own punishment**.

## Verse 7 — What death removes

When a godly person dies, **all fears vanish**. When an ungodly person dies, **all false hopes vanish**.

## Verse 8 — Providence preserves the upright

The righteous are often wonderfully kept from danger, while the wicked fall into the trouble they escaped.

## Verse 9 — The dangers of hypocritical speech

Hypocrites mislead others into wrongdoing by using **clever objections to God's word**.

## Verses 10 and 11 — A community relieved of wickedness

A nation prospers when wicked people lose their power to injure it.

## Verse 12 — Judge character, not success

A person of understanding does **not** judge someone else's worth merely by outward prosperity.

## Verse 13 — Keep confidences faithfully

A trustworthy person keeps confidential matters private, **unless God's honor or the genuine good of society requires disclosure**.

## Verse 14 — The value of counsel

Consulting others often brings important advantages. We should not assume our judgment needs no advice.

## Verse 15 — Consider the cost of guaranteeing debts

Do not endanger your family's welfare, your peace of mind or your ability to pay rightful debts by undertaking obligations you cannot meet. But remember the extraordinary grace of **Jesus Christ, who became Surety for his enemies**.

## Verse 16 — The honor of a wise woman

A godly, prudent woman preserves respect and esteem as firmly as strong people protect their wealth.

## Verse 17 — Cruelty harms the cruel

A cruel, ill-tempered person causes suffering to those who ought to be treated as his own flesh, and **punishes himself through that cruelty**.

## Verse 18 — Doing good has a sure reward

Those who make doing good their work have a reward as certain as God's eternal truth.

## Verse 19 — Holiness is happiness

**True holiness brings true happiness**. The more fiercely someone pursues sin, the faster destruction approaches.

## Verse 20 — God hates double dealing

Nothing is more offensive to God than hypocrisy and deceit. He delights in people whose intentions and conduct are sincere.

## Verse 21 — A crowd cannot protect sinners

Joining together with others to commit sin does not make anyone safe from judgment.

## Verse 22 — Beauty without discretion

Physical beauty is misused when separated from modesty and sound judgment. The same applies to other bodily advantages.

## Verse 23 — Evil desires recoil upon their owner

Wicked people desire harm for others, but their schemes return upon themselves.

## Verse 24 — Misers can lose what they withhold

People may become poor by refusing to pay just debts, help the poor or meet necessary expenses. Even careful hoarding can come to nothing whenever God so appoints.

## Verse 25 — Generosity and God's provision

In spiritual and earthly matters, God commonly treats his people according to the **measure of generosity** they show their neighbors.

## Verse 26 — Do not hoard God's gifts

We must not store up the blessings of God's bounty **only to advance our own interests**.

## Verse 27 — Seek good, not harm

Seeking harm is contrasted with seeking good. Those who refuse to do good may be doing harm **even to themselves**.

## Verse 28 — The living Vine

A believer is a branch of **the living Vine, Christ**. Those rooted only in worldly wealth will wither, but people grafted into Christ will bear fruit.

## Verse 29 — Troubling your household

Someone who brings suffering upon self and family through wickedness or carelessness will lose the ability to hold and enjoy possessions—just as **no one can grasp the wind or be satisfied by it**.

## Verse 30 — Righteousness nourishes others

The righteous are like **trees of life**. The influence of their lives nourishes and supports spiritual life in many others.

## Verse 31 — Seek the blessings Christ purchased

Even righteous people meet severe correction when they sin on earth. How much more will the wicked receive their deserved judgment! Seek the blessings **our Surety purchased through his suffering and death**, imitate his example and keep his commands.

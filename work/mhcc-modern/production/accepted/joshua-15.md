# Joshua 15 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The borders of the lot of Judah. | 1–12 |
| Caleb's portion, His daughter's blessing. | 13–19 |
| The cities of Judah. | 20–63 |

## Judah's boundaries (verses 1–12)

Before Israel left Gilgal, Joshua assigned the inheritances of Judah, Ephraim and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

Later the people moved to Shiloh, where another survey was made and the other tribes received their portions.

In due time, all God's people will be settled in the inheritance prepared for them.

## Caleb and his daughter Achsah (verses 13–19)

Achsah received land as a free gift from her father Caleb.

But it was land in the south, dry and easily parched.

She asked for more, and Caleb gave her both the upper and the lower springs.

Some understand the description as referring to a single field watered by rain from heaven and by springs rising from the ground.

This interpretation supports a familiar spiritual application.

When we pray, we may seek blessings for our souls and for heaven—the **upper springs**—and also blessings for our bodies and present lives—the **lower springs**.

Both kinds of blessing belong to God's children.

Through their relationship to Christ, they receive them freely from their Father as part of their inheritance.

## The cities of Judah (verses 20–63)

This passage lists Judah's cities.

But **Bethlehem** is not named, though it later became David's city and was honored as the birthplace of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Even at its greatest, Bethlehem was a little place among Judah's towns (Micah 5:2).

At this time it was apparently so small that it was not included among the cities listed.

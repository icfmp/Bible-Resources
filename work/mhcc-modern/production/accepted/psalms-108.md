# Psalm 108 — Modern English

## Original source heading

*No independent topical heading is supplied in the CCEL witness.*

## Praise, faith and confidence in God's help

We can help our worship by selecting passages from different psalms, as this psalm does from **Psalms 57 and 60**.

Such passages can strengthen devotion and awaken gratitude.

When the heart is steadfast in **faith and love**, the tongue brings honor to its owner by speaking and singing God's praise.

Every gift received from the Lord benefits and honors the person who receives it **when it is used in God's service and for his glory**.

Believers can pray with confident faith and hope for **every blessing of salvation**.

These blessings are guaranteed by God's **faithful covenant and promise**.

They may therefore expect God's help in every trouble and victory in every conflict.

Whatever we accomplish or obtain, **God must receive all the glory**.

Lord, visit all our souls with this salvation and with the favor you show your chosen people.

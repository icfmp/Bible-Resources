# 1 Chronicles 25 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The singers and musicians.**

## The musicians appointed to worship

David organized those appointed to sing and play music in the temple.

Here, **to prophesy** means to praise God with great earnestness and devout affection under the influence of the **Holy Spirit**.

Poetry and music were used to awaken these affections.

But unless the Spirit of God brings life and fervor into our worship, even the most carefully ordered services remain **lifeless and worthless forms**.

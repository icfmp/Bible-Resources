# 1 Kings 5 — Modern English

## Solomon makes an agreement with Hiram (verses 1–9)

Solomon intended to build the Lord's temple.

At this time Israel had no enemy, no **Satan**—that is how Henry explains the word.

There was no instrument of Satan hindering Solomon's plans or drawing him away from his purpose.

Satan constantly tries to obstruct work done for God's house.

When no active obstacle stands before us, we should be especially ready to do what is good.

God's promises should strengthen and quicken our efforts.

Every worldly talent, advantage and practical skill should be used in the interests of Christ's kingdom.

**Tyre supplied Israel with craftsmen, while Israel supplied Tyre with grain** (**Ezekiel 27:17**).

In the wisdom of divine Providence, one nation needs and benefits another.

This mutual dependence gives people opportunities to glorify God.

## Workers prepare the temple materials (verses 10–18)

The temple was largely built through the wealth and labor of **Gentiles**.

This pictured the future calling of the Gentile peoples into God's church.

Solomon commanded that expensive stones be prepared for the foundation.

**Jesus Christ is the chosen and precious Foundation Stone**.

We should make sure the foundation of our faith is secure.

The part of our religious lives that people cannot see deserves our greatest care and effort.

Blessed are those who become **living stones**, joined together into a spiritual house in which God dwells through his Spirit.

Who among us will help build the Lord's house?

## The danger of luxury and false security (verses 1–7)

People often believe that those who indulge their bodies and seek physical comfort are living well.

But Amos shows **the danger hidden in such ease**.

Here are the **pride, complacency and sensuality** for which God would call the people to account.

Careless sinners are in danger everywhere.

But those who feel completely safe **in Zion**, become indifferent to God's warnings and abuse their sacred privileges, are in the greatest danger.

Many imagine they belong to God while continuing in sin and conforming themselves to the world.

The downfall of other people should warn us not to be self-confident.

Those entirely absorbed by personal pleasures are often indifferent to **the suffering of others**.

This greatly offends God.

People who build their happiness upon sensual enjoyment will be **deprived of the very pleasures they love**.

Those who try to push **the day of judgment** far away will find it approaching them sooner than they expect.

## Judgment upon pride and hardened hearts (verses 8–14)

How terrible is the condition of those whose destruction God himself has declared!

He has power to fulfill his purpose, and no creature can overturn it.

Some people's hearts are so hardened that **even illness and death within their own families** do not move them to speak of God or worship him.

A field that refuses to be cultivated will be abandoned like **a barren rock**.

When we corrupt God's worship with sin, we should not be surprised if his providential dealings become bitter to us.

Let all people beware of hardening their hearts.

**God will bring down those who walk in pride.**

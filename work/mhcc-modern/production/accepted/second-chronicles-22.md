# 2 Chronicles 22 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The reign of Ahaziah, Athaliah destroys the royal family.**

## Ahaziah's reign and Athaliah's attempt to destroy David's line

The counsel of wicked people ruins many young people as they begin adult life.

Ahaziah allowed himself to be led by evil advisers.

People who encourage us to do wrong are advising us toward our destruction.

Though they pretend to be friends, they are our **worst enemies**.

Consider the harm caused by bad company.

Even when we escape being infected by its wickedness, we should still fear sharing its destruction (**Revelation 18:4**).

Here a **wicked woman** tried to destroy David's house, while a **good woman** preserved it.

Not one word of God's promise will fail.

At this point the prophecies that the **Messiah would descend from David**—and thus the salvation of the world—seemed to depend upon the fragile life of a **single infant**.

The ruling power wanted that child dead.

But God had made his purpose known, and the efforts of earth and hell could not defeat it.

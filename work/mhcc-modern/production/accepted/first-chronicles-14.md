# 1 Chronicles 14 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's victories.**

## David's victories

This chapter records three things: **David's kingdom being established**, **his family being built up**, and **his enemies being defeated**.

The account repeats events recorded in **2 Samuel 5**.

David's reputation and fame point toward the far greater exalted honor of **the Son of David, Jesus Christ**.

# Psalm 128 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The blessings of those who fear God.**

## The happiness of those who walk in God's ways

Only people who are truly devoted to God are **truly happy**.

We cannot reasonably claim to fear him if we do not strive conscientiously to walk in his ways.

Everyone who reverences the Lord can be blessed—whether **powerful or obscure, rich or poor**.

If we fear him and walk in obedience, life will be good under his care; death will bring something better, and eternity the greatest happiness.

With God's blessing, the faithful can earn **an honest living**.

Henry sees a **twofold promise** here.

They will have **worthwhile work to do**, because idleness is miserable and joyless.

They will also be given **the ability, bodily strength and mental capacity needed for the work**.

They need not live by exploiting the labor of others.

Quietly working and eating our own bread is both **a duty and a mercy**.

They and their families may enjoy the results of that labor.

Those who fear the Lord and follow his ways are blessed regardless of social position.

They will find abundant comfort in their family relationships and receive the good things God has promised and for which they pray.

Yet a good person gains little lasting consolation from seeing grandchildren if there is **no peace for Israel**.

Every genuine believer rejoices in the welfare and growth of God's church.

Beyond this life we will see far greater blessings: the **everlasting peace and rest reserved for God's Israel**.

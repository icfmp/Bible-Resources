## Solomon's succession and temple service (verses 1–23)

After giving instructions about the temple's construction, David organized its service and appointed the officers responsible.

When members of the same family work together in God's service, it should encourage them to love and assist one another.

## The Levites' work (verses 24–32)

The population of Israel had grown, and more workers were needed in the temple.

Every Israelite arriving with an offering should find a Levite ready to help.

When there is more work to do, it is fitting to have more workers.

The great difference between a true Christian and other people is **a new heart and a spiritual mind**.

A person with such a heart takes great delight in God's commandments and finds refreshment in his appointed worship.

For a spiritual person, every form of service brings satisfaction.

Such a person wants to abound continually in the Lord's work and is happiest serving so good a Master in so pleasant a task.

It does not matter whether the person is called to lead or to serve under someone else's leadership.

May we seek the Lord and serve him sincerely, trusting his word and leaving everything else to his wise disposal.

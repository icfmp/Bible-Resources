# Jeremiah 43 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The leaders carry the people to Egypt. | 1–7 |
| Jeremiah foretells the conquest of Egypt. | 8–13 |

## The leaders take the people into Egypt (verses 1–7)

**Pride produces conflict**, both with God and with other people.

The leaders preferred their own wisdom to **God's revealed will**.

People often deny that Scripture is God's word because they have already decided **not to submit to its commands**.

When someone is determined to continue in sin, that person may even assign **evil motives to the best actions**.

These Jews abandoned their own land and took themselves outside the protection God had promised there.

How foolish people can be: in attempting to improve their circumstances **through disobedience**, they bring disaster upon themselves.

## Jeremiah foretells Egypt's conquest (verses 8–13)

God can find his people **wherever they are**.

The Spirit of prophecy was **not confined to Israel's territory**.

Jeremiah announced that **Nebuchadnezzar** would conquer Egypt and take many Egyptians captive.

God sometimes uses **one wicked person or nation to judge another**.

He will also punish those who mislead people professing to belong to him or tempt them to rebel against his commands.

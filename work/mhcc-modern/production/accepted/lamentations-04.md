# Lamentations 4 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The deplorable state of the nation is contrasted with its ancient prosperity.**

## Jerusalem's former beauty is lost (verses 1–12)

What a change has taken place!

Sin dims the beauty of even the noblest abilities and the most excellent gifts.

But **the gold that Christ bestows and has tested in the fire** can never be taken away.

Its outward appearance may lose its shine, but its true value never changes.

Once more we hear of the horrors of Jerusalem's siege and destruction.

As we consider what sin brought upon God's people in earlier times, we should think seriously about **what the same causes might bring upon the church today**.

Lord, though we have rebelled against you, **turn to us and turn our hearts back to you**.

Help us to fear your name.

Bless us with grace that **awakens, converts, renews and strengthens**.

## The sins of leaders hasten the nation's ruin (verses 13–20)

Nothing brings a people toward ruin more quickly than **the sins of their priests and prophets**.

Even the king could not escape, for God's justice pursued him.

Yet **our anointed King** alone is the life of our souls.

We may dwell safely under **Christ's shadow** and rejoice in him even among enemies.

He is **the true God and eternal life**.

## Zion's suffering will end; Edom faces judgment (verses 21 and 22)

The prophecy announces that Zion's troubles will reach an end.

It does not mean that she will suffer the complete punishment she deserved, but that she will experience **everything God has determined to inflict**.

Edom's celebration will also end.

The troubles of God's church and each believer will soon be completed.

The judgment of their enemies is drawing near.

The Lord will expose their sins, and they will lie down in lasting sorrow.

In Henry's interpretation, **Edom here represents all the church's enemies**.

The prophet has shown how widespread Israel's corruption was.

That reality demonstrates God's justice in the judgments he sent.

It also reveals how urgently **the whole human race needs the grace of God in Jesus Christ**.

# 1 Chronicles 2 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Genealogies.**

## The families of Israel

We now come to the register of the children of Israel, the people set apart from the nations, who were to dwell as a distinct people and not be counted among other nations.

But in **Jesus Christ**, everyone who comes to him is welcome to his salvation.

All have equal privileges through **faith in him**, love for him and wholehearted devotion to him.

Everything truly valuable lies in **God's favor, peace and likeness**, and in a life dedicated to his glory by seeking the well-being of other people.

# Psalm 138 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The psalmist praises God for answering prayer. | 1–5 |
| The Lord's dealing with the humble and the proud. | 6–8 |

## Wholehearted thanks for answered prayer (verses 1–5)

When we can praise the Lord with all our hearts, we should not hesitate to let the whole world see our gratitude and joy.

Those who depend on God's **lovingkindness and truth through Jesus Christ** will always find him faithful to his word.

If God did not spare his own Son, will he not also freely give us all things with him?

The Lord may strengthen our souls even while outward affliction remains.

He gives us grace to carry burdens, withstand temptation and fulfill our duties.

He strengthens us to hold firmly to him by faith and wait patiently for the outcome.

Such grace deserves our sincere thanksgiving.

## His mercy to the humble and distance from the proud (verses 6–8)

Although the Lord is exalted, he regards **every humble sinner**.

The proud and unbelieving, however, will be excluded from his blessed presence.

God's consolations can revive us even in the middle of suffering.

He saves his people so that **the Holy Spirit, the giver of life and holiness**, can renew them.

When we give God glory for his mercy, we may receive the comfort of that mercy ourselves.

Confidence in his promises should **increase our prayers**, not make us stop praying.

Any good present in us is the Lord's work.

He works within his people both **to will and to act**.

He will complete the salvation of every genuine believer and will not abandon those **whom he has made new in Christ Jesus for good works**.

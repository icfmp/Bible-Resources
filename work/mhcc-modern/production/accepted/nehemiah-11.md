# Nehemiah 11 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The distribution of the people.**

## The people settle in Jerusalem

In every age, people have often preferred their personal comfort and advantage to the common good.

Even those who profess faith frequently seek their own interests rather than **the interests of Christ**.

Few have loved holy things and places enough to give up their comforts for them.

Yet our souls should delight to live where faithful people and opportunities for spiritual growth are plentiful.

If we have no love for **the city of our God**, or for anything that helps us enjoy fellowship with the Savior, how will we gladly depart this life to be with the Lord?

To a worldly-minded person, the perfect holiness of the **New Jerusalem** would be even more difficult to bear than the holiness of God's church on earth.

Let us seek first God's favor and glory.

Let us learn patience, contentment and usefulness in whatever place he has assigned us, looking forward with cheerful hope to entering the holy city of God.

# Ephesians 4 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Exhortations to mutual forbearance and union. | 1–6 |
| To a due use of spiritual gifts and graces. | 7–16 |
| To purity and holiness. | 17–24 |
| And to take heed of the sins practised among the heathen. | 25–32 |

## Walk together in humility and unity (verses 1–6)

Few duties are urged more strongly in Scripture than living in a manner worthy of **the calling to Christ's kingdom and glory**.

**Humility** opposes pride.

**Gentleness** makes us reluctant to provoke others and slow to become offended or angry.

We discover so many faults in ourselves—faults we sometimes find hard even to forgive ourselves—that we should not be surprised to find weaknesses in others that are difficult to overlook.

All believers hope in **one Christ** and look forward to **one heaven**.

They should therefore be **of one heart**.

They share one faith in its object, Author, nature and power, receiving the same great truths of religion.

They have been admitted into the church through **one baptism with water, in the name of the Father, Son and Holy Spirit, as a sign of regeneration**.

God the Father dwells in believers as within **his holy temple**, through his Spirit and special grace.

## Christ gives different gifts for the body's growth (verses 7–16)

Every true believer receives **some measure of grace** for the mutual good of God's people.

Christ distributes his gifts in the way that seems best to him.

He received gifts and graces to give them to his church, especially **the gift of the Holy Spirit**.

The knowledge required is more than merely understanding Christian ideas or acknowledging intellectually that Christ is the Son of God.

It produces **trust and obedience**.

There is fullness in Christ, and every believer receives a measure from that fullness according to God's wise decision.

Yet we will not reach complete maturity until heaven.

God's children continue growing throughout this life, and their growth brings **glory to Christ**.

The more someone uses their particular responsibilities and gifts **for the spiritual benefit of other people**, the stronger the evidence that **genuine Christian love** has taken root in the heart.

## Put off the old life and put on the new (verses 17–24)

Speaking by Christ's authority, Paul urged the Ephesians not to live like unbelieving Gentiles who followed **empty ideas and sinful desires**.

People everywhere still live under the influence of confused, worthless thoughts.

We therefore need to distinguish **genuine Christian life from a merely Christian name**.

Without saving knowledge, people remain in darkness and often **prefer the darkness to the light**.

They resist holiness.

Yet holiness is what God commands and approves, and it reflects something of **his purity, righteousness, truth and goodness**.

The gospel's truth becomes especially beautiful when seen in **Jesus himself**.

Our sinful nature is described as **an old person**, made up of many related desires and habits that reinforce one another.

Corrupt desires are **deceptive**.

They promise happiness but actually deepen misery and lead toward destruction unless they are resisted and put to death.

We must take them off like a **dirty, worn-out garment**.

But it is not enough to abandon corrupt principles.

We must receive **new and gracious principles** in their place.

The **new person** is a renewed nature, created through God's mighty power and governed by **regenerating grace**.

That grace enables a new life of genuine righteousness and holiness.

## Speak truth, control anger and work honestly (verses 25–28)

Paul gave practical examples of conduct that should adorn a Christian profession.

**Reject falsehood**.

Do not flatter or deceive people.

God's children should hate lying and refuse to practice it.

We must also guard against **uncontrolled anger**.

Sometimes wrong behavior must be confronted.

Yet even when displeasure is justified, we must express correction **without sinning**.

We give the devil an opportunity when early sinful impulses no longer grieve us, when we consent to them or when we repeat evil actions.

Resist sin and avoid even circumstances that invite wrongdoing.

**Idleness creates temptation**, including temptation to steal.

People should work diligently not only to provide for themselves but also **to have something with which to help those in need**.

What should we think of professing Christians who become rich through **fraud, exploitation and deceit**?

Charitable gifts cannot be acceptable when obtained through dishonesty or robbery.

God rejects the attempt to make **an offering out of stolen goods**.

## Use words that build up and forgive as God forgave (verses 29–32)

Corrupt speech comes from corruption in the speaker and harms the thoughts and conduct of listeners.

Christians should avoid such talk.

Instead, through their conversations they should encourage people to think seriously and should **strengthen, warn and comfort fellow believers**.

We are called to be **kind to one another**.

True kindness grows out of love within the heart and expresses itself through humble, courteous conduct.

Notice how **God's forgiveness becomes the reason for our forgiveness**.

We had no rightful cause to sin against him, yet he forgave us.

We therefore ought to forgive other people as he has forgiven us.

Lies, corrupt speech and conversation that arouses sinful desires **grieve God's Holy Spirit**.

So do bitterness, rage, uncontrolled anger, shouting, slander and malice.

Do not provoke the holy and blessed Spirit to withdraw his gracious influences and felt presence.

Our bodies will be redeemed from the grave at the resurrection.

Wherever the Holy Spirit lives as **the Sanctifier**, his presence is the **pledge of the joy and glory of that future redemption**.

How lost we would be if God withdrew his Holy Spirit from us!

## Editorial review — Ephesians 4

**Full CCEL original-English witness read:** [CCEL, Ephesians 4](https://ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhcc.xli.iv.html). Pinned published `MHCC 0.1.1` remains primary for exact native-SWORD mapping, annotations, independent editorial acceptance and compiled-reader testing.

- **1–6:** humility/gentleness, **one Christ/heaven/faith**, **water baptism as sign of regeneration**, Father indwelling by Spirit — retained.
- **7–16:** gift to every believer **at Christ's discretion**, growth toward heaven and love evidenced by **service to others** — retained.
- **17–24:** darkness of nominal faith, sinful desires as **deceptive**, old garment and **God-created new nature** — retained.
- **25–28:** truthfulness, just correction **without sinful anger**, resisting devil, work as defense against theft, **honest industry required for charity—not stolen offerings** — retained.
- **29–32:** words building up vs corrupting, **God's forgiveness causing ours**, specific passions that **grieve Holy Spirit**, Spirit's pledge of bodily resurrection — retained.

# 1 John 4 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Believers cautioned against giving heed to every one that pretends to the Spirit. | 1–6 |
| Brotherly love enforced. | 7–21 |

## Test teaching about Jesus Christ (verses 1–6)

Christians familiar with Scripture, humbly depending on God's instruction, can distinguish teaching consistent with the apostles from teaching opposed to it.

The center of revealed religion is **Jesus Christ's person and saving work**.

False teachers adapt their message to worldly standards, desires and tastes so that people ruled by sin will not be offended.

They often gain rapid approval and numerous followers because **the world loves what belongs to itself**.

But true teaching about the Savior's person leads human beings **away from the world and toward God**.

This marks the Spirit of truth in contrast to the spirit of error.

Teaching that is pure and holy, consistent with the apostles and Scripture, gives good grounds for recognizing divine truth.

It is no surprise that worldly people follow teachers whose message suits their own purposes.

## God's love begins with his free mercy (verses 7–13)

**The Spirit of God is the Spirit of love**.

Anyone unable to love God's likeness in his people has no saving knowledge of the Lord.

God's nature is kind, and he delights to give happiness.

His law requires love. Had all obeyed it, humanity would have enjoyed perfect happiness.

The gospel shows how forgiveness and salvation can be granted while upholding **God's glory and justice**.

Thus it reveals that **God is love**.

Some mysteries remain dark to our limited understanding.

God has shown his love so fully that **only unbelief and refusal to repent** prevent sinners from receiving eternal happiness, although **strict justice would condemn us to hopeless misery** because we have broken our Creator's laws.

We cannot adequately express the greatness of God's free, astonishing love for people who deserved judgment and who could neither increase nor diminish his greatness.

God could have made other worlds and other creatures by his word, yet **he chose to redeem sinners through Christ**.

Search the universe for love in its greatest splendor: it is found **in the person and cross of Jesus Christ**.

Love did not begin with our loving God.

**He loved us first**.

His love is not intended to remain fruitless in us.

It reaches its appropriate purpose as we become loving, just as faith finds its fulfillment in good works.

This is evidence that **God dwells within us through his regenerating Spirit**.

Love makes believers diligent in every good duty. It oils the wheels of their affections and moves them to help brothers and sisters willingly.

A person performing work with resentment does it badly.

The statement that God lives in us and we in him would be too magnificent for mortals to claim if **God himself had not revealed it**.

Those truly convinced they are his children cry **“Abba, Father”** and seek his will while hating sin.

Such a transformed heart bears the Holy Spirit's witness.

## Love casts out servile fear and produces obedience (verses 14–21)

**The Father sent the Son to be the Savior of the world**.

The apostles testify to this.

Confessing Jesus as God's Son involves **faith in the heart**, acknowledgment with the lips and a life that remains faithful despite worldly flattery or hostility.

A final judgment will come.

Blessed are those who await the Judge with **holy confidence**, recognizing him as Friend and Advocate.

Love for God assures believers of his love.

It enables them to suffer for Christ, confident they will share his glory (**2 Timothy 2:12**).

Distinguish **reverent fear of God** from **being terrified of him**.

Love-motivated obedience is unlike forced labor under an angry master. It is the willing service of a child to a beloved father, benefiting the whole household.

Many doubts and fears show that love has not yet grown toward maturity.

God invites sinners to share his salvation. Those changed by his love should rejoice, while giving him all the glory.

The Holy Spirit's adoption and God's love in the heart provide strong evidence of conversion.

Test this through the way you treat fellow believers.

Someone claiming to love God but deliberately nurturing anger, revenge or selfishness contradicts their own confession.

But when natural hostility becomes affection and gratitude, thank God for this pledge of eternal happiness.

**We cannot truly claim to love the unseen God while hating the brothers and sisters we can see**.

# Psalm 47 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The people exhorted to praise God.**

## Original source divisions

| Source commentary | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| First section | 1–4 |
| Second section | 5–9 |

## The Sovereign gathers the nations (verses 1–4)

The Lord with whom we have to do possesses **awesome majesty**.

The universal and absolute sovereignty of a holy God would be terrifying even to contemplate if it were not exercised through **his Son from a throne of mercy**.

Now it brings terror only to those who persist in wickedness.

God's people should encourage each other in joyful trust and obedience.

Sinners should submit to his authority and receive the salvation he offers.

**Jesus Christ will bring Gentile nations under his rule**, drawing them like sheep into the fold—not to kill them, but to preserve them.

He will transform their affections and make them **willing subjects in the day of his power**.

His kingdom also grants rest and security.

In its spiritual meaning the psalm teaches that **the Lord himself becomes his people's inheritance**.

This is the faith and submission of the saints:

**“The Lord will choose my inheritance for me; he knows what is best for me better than I do.”**

## Praise with understanding (verses 5–9)

We should praise God frequently and generously.

Yet one rule must always govern our worship: **sing praises with understanding**.

Know why you are praising God, what he has done and what the service means.

Worship is not acceptable when it is mindless and unreasonable.

We should never lose sight of the purpose of Messiah's exaltation.

The prophets continually return to the conversion of the nations through **the gospel of Christ**.

It is empty to imagine we belong to Christ if **his Spirit does not reign in our hearts by faith**.

Lord, you are exalted as **Prince and Savior** to grant repentance and forgiveness.

Establish your kingdom in us.

Bring every thought into obedience to Christ and turn all the powers and desires of redeemed souls toward holy love, reverence and joy in you.

May praise **with understanding** rise from every heart both now and forever.

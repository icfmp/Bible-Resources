# 2 Chronicles 2 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Solomon's message to Huram respecting the temple, His treaty with Huram.**

## Solomon sends to Huram concerning the temple

Solomon told Huram about the particular services that would be performed in God's temple.

Unlike the mysteries of pagan superstition, the truths and mysteries of genuine faith **do not need to be concealed**.

Solomon wanted Huram to form great and exalted thoughts of the God of Israel.

We likewise should not be fearful or ashamed of any opportunity to speak about God and impress others with the importance of his favor and service.

While Israel remained faithful to God's law and worship, neighboring nations were willing to learn true religion from the Israelites.

Earlier, when Israel had turned from the Lord, the people had willingly absorbed the superstition and idolatry of neighboring nations.

A wise and godly king is evidence of God's special love for his people.

How much greater was that love when God gave his **only Son** to believing people as their Prince and Savior!

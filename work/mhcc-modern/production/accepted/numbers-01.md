# Numbers 1 — Modern English

## Israel's tribes are counted (verses 1–43)

God commanded a census of Israel.

The count showed his faithfulness in causing Jacob's descendants to increase so greatly.

It also helped organize the people for the coming wars and conquest of Canaan and established their family divisions for the future distribution of land.

The census counted, from each tribe, those able to serve in war.

Israel had not yet encountered these enemies, but battles awaited them.

Christians should likewise prepare to withstand the enemies of their souls, even when everything appears peaceful.

## The total number of Israelites (verses 44–46)

The census gave a large total.

Consider how much food was needed to maintain such a crowd in the wilderness!

God supplied everyone's needs day by day.

When we recognize his faithfulness in fulfilling promises that once seemed impossible, we can take courage concerning the promises still awaiting fulfillment for his church.

## The Levites are counted separately (verses 47–54)

The tribe of Levi was set apart.

Its members had distinguished themselves during the crisis of the golden calf.

Those who perform extraordinary service for God may receive extraordinary honors from him.

The Levites were entrusted with the tabernacle and its sacred treasures, both when Israel was camped and when it moved.

It honored the holy things that only those called by God could see or handle them.

None of us is naturally fit or worthy to enjoy fellowship with God.

We must first be called by grace into fellowship with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

As the spiritual offspring of our great High Priest, Christians are made priests to God.

We should be especially careful to prevent sin, because preventing sin is also preventing the judgment it brings.

As a holy tribe, the Levites were not numbered with the other Israelites.

Those who minister in holy things should not allow worldly concerns to entangle them or become entangled themselves in worldly affairs.

Every believer should seek to do what the Lord has commanded.

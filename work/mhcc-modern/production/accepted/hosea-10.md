# Hosea 10 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The idolatry of Israel. | 1–8 |
| They are exhorted to repentance. | 9–15 |

## Israel is a vine without good fruit (verses 1–8)

A vine's value lies in **the fruit it produces**.

Israel, however, had brought none of its fruit to maturity.

The people's hearts were **divided**.

God is the rightful sovereign of the human heart: he requires **the whole heart**, not merely a share.

If our hearts were directed wholly toward God, devotion would flow strongly enough to overcome whatever lay in its path.

Israel's claims to be in covenant with God were **false**.

Even its administration of justice had become **as poisonous as hemlock**.

How sad that **the visible church** may still be a nearly empty vine!

Earthly prosperity is no more substantial than **bubbles or foam on water**, quickly broken and gone.

Sinners will one day seek shelter in vain from the very **Judge** they now despise when he is offered to them **as Savior**.

## Seek the Lord and sow righteousness (verses 9–15)

God does not delight in sinners' death and destruction.

For that reason, **his correction can be an act of mercy**.

Sinful habits remained deeply rooted in Israel.

Enemies would gather against the people.

It is just for those who live carelessly in comfort and indulgence to learn what suffering means.

They should cleanse their hearts of sinful loves and desires and become **brokenhearted and contrite**.

They should abound in **devotion toward God** and in **justice and generosity toward one another**.

This is what it means to **sow to the Spirit**.

Seeking the Lord is a duty for **every day**, though there are special occasions that call us to seek him with renewed urgency.

**Christ will come as the Lord our righteousness**, granting his righteousness abundantly.

Those who sow righteousness reap **according to mercy**.

Such a harvest is a reward **of grace, not a debt God owes us**.

Even the profits obtained through sin bring sinners no real satisfaction.

Both sinful comforts and confidence in wicked practices will eventually disappoint.

Come, seek the Lord: **hope placed in him will not deceive you**.

Consider the cruelty and devastation of war.

Whatever evil is done, **sin lies behind it**.

How much misery human wrongdoing brings even in this life!

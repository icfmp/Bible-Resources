# Psalm 124 — Modern English

| Original outline | Verses |

| The deliverance of the church. | 1–5 |

## The Lord saves his people (verses 1–5)

Sometimes God allows his people's enemies to advance so far that his **power is displayed more clearly when he rescues them**.

Happy are those whose God is **the Lord, the all-sufficient One**.

We can apply this psalm to the deliverances he gave in ancient times or to rescues experienced in our own day.

But above all, let us remember **the great redemption accomplished by Jesus Christ**, through which believers were delivered from **Satan's power**.

## All the glory belongs to God (verses 6–8)

God is **the author of all our deliverances** and deserves all the glory for them.

Enemies lay traps to draw his people into sin and trouble and then keep them there.

At times those plans appear to succeed.

Yet those who put their trust in the Lord will not ultimately be put to shame.

Believers credit their salvation entirely to **God's power, mercy and faithfulness**.

Looking back on his guidance, they respond with wonder and thanksgiving.

Let us rejoice that all our help for the future is in **the One who made heaven and earth**.

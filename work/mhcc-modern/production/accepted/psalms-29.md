# Psalm 29 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Exhortation to give glory to God.**

## The glory and power of the Lord

The powerful and honored people of this world are especially obligated to worship God and give him glory.

Yet few approach him **in the beauty of holiness**.

When we come to God through the Redeemer of sinners in **repentance, faith and love**, he accepts our imperfect service, pardons the sin still mixed with it and approves the holiness the Holy Spirit has enabled us to exercise.

This is the nature of true worship: **giving the Lord the glory due to his name**.

Every act of worship should be holy, devoted to God, his will and his glory.

Holiness is beautiful and gives beauty to everything done in worship.

David also described the Lord's power in the natural world.

Thunder, lightning and storms testify to his glory. We should respond by thinking highly of God's greatness and worshiping him with reverence.

The brilliance of lightning matches the terror of thunder.

These displays remind us both of **God's power** and of **human weakness**.

They also warn of how defenseless unrepentant sinners will be at the final judgment.

Yet the work of God's word upon human souls **through the Holy Spirit** is more extraordinary than any thunderstorm.

The proudest are humbled, stubborn hearts tremble, hidden thoughts are revealed, sinners are converted, and people once cruel, sensual and corrupt become gentle and pure.

If we have heard God's voice and fled to the hope he offers, we need not fear our Father's voice even when he speaks in judgment against his enemies.

Those who remain without shelter may tremble, but people who have entered **his appointed refuge** can thank him for their security.

They may look toward judgment without terror, as **Noah was secure in the ark**.

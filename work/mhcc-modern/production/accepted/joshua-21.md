# Joshua 21 — Modern English

## The Levites ask for their appointed cities (verses 1–8)

The Levites waited until the other tribes had received their portions before bringing their request to Joshua.

They based the request not on their own excellence or service but on **God's command**.

Support for ministers is not something a congregation may leave entirely to personal whim, as though it were free to let its ministers starve.

Those who preach the gospel should live from the gospel and should be able to live adequately.

## The Levites live among the tribes (verses 9–42)

The Levites were distributed among Israel's other tribes.

They knew that the whole nation was watching them.

They therefore needed to live carefully so that no one could justly criticize their ministry.

Every tribe received some cities for the Levites.

This was God's gracious way of preserving true religion and making knowledge of his word available throughout the land.

Blessed be God that, under the gospel, his word has been spread more widely among us.

## The Lord fulfills his promises (verses 43–45)

God had promised Abraham's descendants the land of Canaan.

Now they occupied it and settled there.

The promise of the heavenly Canaan is just as certain for all God's spiritual Israel, because it comes from the One who cannot lie.

No enemy could stand against them.

The Canaanites' later successes resulted from Israel's failure to act diligently and from its sinful willingness to tolerate the surrounding people's idolatry and wickedness.

Not one good thing God had promised Israel failed to come true.

At the proper time, all his promises will be fulfilled.

Then his people will acknowledge that the Lord has exceeded their greatest hopes, made them more than conquerors and brought them into the rest they desired.

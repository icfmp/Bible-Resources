# Leviticus 22 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Laws concerning the priests and sacrifices.**

## Reverence, sincerity and trust in Christ's atonement

This chapter contains several laws about priests and sacrifices. Together they guard the honor of God's sanctuary.

We should remember gratefully that nothing can prevent our great High Priest, Christ, from performing his office.

The Lord requires us to revere his name, his truth, his appointed acts of worship and his commands.

We must beware of hypocrisy.

Let us examine ourselves for the sinful stains in our hearts and lives, seeking cleansing through Christ's blood and the sanctifying work of his Spirit.

A person who tries to atone for personal sin or approaches God proudly, relying on personal righteousness, dishonors Christ.

Such a person offends him just as seriously as someone who comes to the Lord's Table while deliberately indulging sinful desires.

A minister who loves people's souls must not allow them to remain deceived in this dangerous way.

The minister should urge them to repent of their sins and abandon them.

But repentance alone is not the whole message.

People must place their full trust in Christ's sacrifice, receiving forgiveness and acceptance with God through faith in his name.

Only in that way will the Lord make them holy as his own people.

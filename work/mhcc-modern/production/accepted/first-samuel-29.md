# 1 Samuel 29 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| David objected to by the Philistines. | 1–5 |
| He is dismissed by Achish. | 6–11 |

## The Philistine commanders object to David (verses 1–5)

David waited with a secret hope that the Lord would rescue him from his difficult position.

Yet his agreement to accompany Achish seems to have been influenced too strongly by **fear of human beings**.

It is hard to come right to the edge of sin without falling into it.

God inclined the Philistine commanders to object to David's taking part in the battle.

Their hostility became a blessing to him.

It gave him a kind of help that even his closest friends could not have provided.

## Achish sends David away (verses 6–11)

David had rarely received a greater deliverance than being released from such a dangerous and morally compromising obligation.

God's people should behave in ways that, as far as possible, earn the respect of everyone with whom they deal.

And when others have acted honorably, it is right to speak well of them.

# Psalm 96 — Modern English

## All the earth called to worship (verses 1–9)

When Christ completed his earthly work and was received into heavenly glory, the church began to sing **a new song** to him and bless his name.

His apostles and evangelists proclaimed **his salvation among the nations** and his marvelous works among all peoples.

The whole earth is summoned to worship the Lord.

We should worship him **in the beauty of holiness**, as the God who in Christ reconciles the world to himself.

The glorious truths spoken about Christ are both reasons for praising him and the very substance of our praise.

## The coming reign and judgment of Christ (verses 10–13)

We should hope and pray for the time when **Christ will reign in righteousness over every nation**.

He will rule human hearts through the power of truth and the Spirit of righteousness.

His coming is drawing near.

The **King and Judge stands at the door**, but the appointed day has not yet arrived.

The Lord welcomes the praise of those who seek the advance of Christ's kingdom.

The sea can roar, but we do not know how trees show their rejoicing.

God searches hearts and knows the mind of the Spirit.

He understands even the weak, broken words of people who struggle to pray.

**Christ will come to judge the earth**, justly punishing his enemies and fully accomplishing his greatest promises to his people.

What will that day mean for us?

Would we welcome his coming?

If we are not prepared, now is the time to get ready to meet God through **forgiveness of our sins and renewal of our hearts in holiness**.

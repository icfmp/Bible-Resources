# Joshua 16 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The sons of Joseph.**

## Ephraim's inheritance

This chapter and the next should be read together. They describe the inheritances of **Ephraim and Manasseh**, Joseph's sons.

Next to Judah, these tribes were to receive a place of honor. They therefore received the first and best portions in **northern Canaan**, just as Judah received its portion in the south.

God's people continue, as in earlier times, to allow his enemies to remain.

Blessed Lord, when will all our enemies finally be subdued? The last enemy to be destroyed is death (1 Corinthians 15:26). Drive them all out; you alone can do it.

The boundaries of these inheritances remind us that our circumstances and provision during this life, as well as our inheritance to come, are appointed by the only wise and righteous God.

We should therefore be content with our portion. He knows what is best for us, and everything we have is more than we deserve.

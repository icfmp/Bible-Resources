# Proverbs 27 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — Do not presume on tomorrow

We do not know what tomorrow may bring. This does not forbid sensible preparation; it forbids **assuming tomorrow is guaranteed**. Do not postpone the one necessary work of conversion.

## Verse 2 — Avoid praising yourself

There may be times to defend your conduct, but **self-praise** is not fitting.

## Verses 3 and 4 — Uncontrolled passion is a heavy burden

People unable to govern their emotions are often crushed by the weight of those passions.

## Verses 5 and 6 — Faithful correction is love

A plain and sincere rebuke is better than hidden hatred or affection that praises a person **while allowing sin to destroy the soul**.

## Verse 7 — Hunger makes the gospel sweet

Poor people often appreciate their modest comforts more and thank God for them more sincerely. Likewise the self-satisfied disdain the gospel, while those **hungry for righteousness** receive comfort even from a simple book or sermon about Christ.

## Verse 8 — A proper place for duty

People have their own responsibilities and places in society where they can serve safely and contentedly.

## Verses 9 and 10 — The nearer and better Friend

Do not assume relatives can help merely because of kinship. Seek trustworthy help from those able to respond. Above all there is **a Friend closer than a brother**; place complete confidence in him.

## Verse 11 — Good conduct honors the gospel

Wise parents desire children whose prudent lives bring them joy. Likewise believers' consistent behavior offers the strongest answer to people who criticize **the gospel**.

## Verse 12 — Do not walk toward temptation

People who knowingly expose themselves to temptation may fall into sin and reap painful consequences.

## Verse 13 — Do not ruin yourself with rash debts

Someone may become poor while remaining honest. But it is not honest to **bring ruin upon oneself through reckless financial undertakings**.

## Verse 14 — Beware craving praise

Loving admiration for its own sake is foolish and exposes us to the temptation of pride.

## Verses 15 and 16 — Relentless quarreling

A neighbor's quarrel may be like a brief, sharp shower; perpetual disputes within marriage resemble **continuous rain**.

## Verse 17 — Make one another wiser

Be thoughtful about whom you keep company with. Let the aim of conversation be to help one another grow **wiser and better**.

## Verse 18 — Faithful service receives honor

Even a tiring or socially despised occupation can bring worthwhile results when performed faithfully. **God honors those who serve him loyally**.

## Verse 19 — Examine the heart

Corrupt hearts resemble one another, as do hearts sanctified by God: the first bear the earthly image, the second the heavenly. **Compare your own heart carefully with Scripture**.

## Verse 20 — Unsatisfied sin; satisfied faith

Death and sinful desire are never satisfied. The worldly heart always wants more profit or pleasure. Those looking toward the Lord, however, find their satisfaction **in him forever**.

## Verse 21 — Praise tests character

As silver and gold are tested in the furnace, a person's character is tested by **the way praise is received**.

## Verse 22 — Grace alone renews entrenched sin

Some people remain hardened even under severe correction. **Only God's recreating grace** can finally change a heart.

## Verses 23–27 — Diligence, Providence and lasting treasure

Everyone should pursue useful work rather than idleness or interfering in matters they do not understand. Work diligently and wisely. Yet nothing earthly can be permanently secured, so choose **a lasting inheritance**. Trust God's blessing upon honest labor to provide as much temporal prosperity as is genuinely good for us.

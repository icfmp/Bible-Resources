# Psalm 117 — Modern English

## Original heading

**All people called upon to praise God.**

## All nations invited to praise the Lord

This psalm solemnly calls **every nation** to praise the Lord and gives them reasons to do so.

We soon grow tired of doing good if we do not keep **devout and loving affection for God** alive within us.

The spiritual sacrifice of praise needs to be kindled and kept burning.

This is a **gospel psalm**.

The apostle Paul quotes it in **Romans 15:11**, showing that the gospel was intended to be preached among the nations and would be welcomed by them.

For many centuries, God was known and praised especially within Judah.

The nations outside Israel were not yet receiving this call in the same way.

But Christ's gospel has been commanded to be preached **to all nations**.

Through him, people who were once far away have been **brought near**.

We ourselves are among the people to whom the Holy Spirit speaks through this psalm, inviting us to join God's ancient people in praise.

Grace has therefore abounded toward **multitudes of sinners who were heading toward destruction**.

Let us receive God's gracious invitation and pray for the day when **every nation on earth** will proclaim his praise.

Bless the Lord for the **unsearchable riches of gospel grace**.

# Ezekiel 44 — Modern English

## Original source structure

**No separate authorial heading; one continuous commentary.**

## Christ enters by his own righteousness

This chapter contains instructions concerning the **true priests**.

Henry understands **the prince** to mean **Christ**.

The words of **Ezekiel 44:2** remind us that no one else can enter **heaven, the true sanctuary**, in the way Christ did.

He entered by virtue of his **own excellence**, **personal holiness**, **righteousness** and **strength**.

He is **the brightness of Jehovah's glory** and entered by his own holiness.

But that way of entering is closed to everyone else.

We must all come **as sinners**, by **faith in Christ's blood** and through **the power of his grace**.

## Sanballat plots to hinder Nehemiah (verses 1–9)

When worthless companions try to lure us into idle, amusing gatherings, we can answer: **We have work to do, and we must not neglect it.**

We must never allow repeated pressure to wear us down into doing something sinful or unwise. When the same temptation returns, we must resist with the same reasons and determination.

Malicious people often claim that everyone wants something when in reality only they want it.

Nehemiah understood their intention. He denied not only the charges against him but also the claim that such rumors were being circulated.

His reputation was too well established for the accusations to be credible.

We should never abandon a known duty for fear that others may misunderstand it. Instead, maintain a good conscience and entrust your reputation to God.

Even when God's people are widely insulted, their good name may be far stronger than their opponents suggest.

Nehemiah sent up a brief prayer to heaven.

Whenever Christians face a task or a spiritual conflict, they may pray: **God, strengthen my hands for the work and the temptation before me.**

Every attempt to distract us from our duty should make us more determined to perform it.

## False prophets attempt to frighten Nehemiah (verses 10–14)

The worst harm enemies can do to us is to frighten us away from good work or persuade us to sin.

**Never abandon a good work; never do a bad one.**

Test every piece of advice and reject whatever contradicts God's word.

All believers should pursue consistency with their calling.

Can I profess to be a Christian, a saint, God's child, a member of Christ and a temple of the Holy Spirit, yet still be greedy, sensual, proud, envious, impatient, discontented or angry?

Can I give in to laziness, unbelief or lack of mercy?

What influence will such behavior have on other people?

Everything God has done for us, given us or accomplished through us ought to make us vigilant, self-denying and diligent.

After sin's inherent evil, we should dread the **scandal our sin brings upon God's cause**.

## The wall is completed despite betrayal (verses 15–19)

The wall was completed in **fifty-two days**, even though the workers rested on the Sabbaths.

Much can be accomplished in little time when people undertake a task sincerely and keep at it.

The alliances created through marriages with foreigners became harmful here.

Once people became relatives of **Tobiah**, they soon bound themselves to his cause.

Sinful affection can lead to sinful alliances.

The enemy of souls uses many agents and many schemes to disgrace active servants of God or draw them away from their service.

But we should follow the One who **gave his life for the sheep**.

Those who remain simply and faithfully devoted to the Lord and his work will receive his support.

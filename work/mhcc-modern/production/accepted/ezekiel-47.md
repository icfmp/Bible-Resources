# Ezekiel 47 — Modern English

## Original source structure

**No separate authorial heading; one continuous commentary.**

## Living waters, the gospel and the Spirit

The waters in Ezekiel's vision signify **the gospel of Christ**.

It went out from Jerusalem into neighboring countries and spread still farther.

They also picture **the gifts and powers of the Holy Spirit** that accompanied the gospel and enabled it to spread and bear blessed fruit.

**Christ is the Temple and the Door**.

The living waters flow from him, **from his pierced side**.

The waters grow deeper as they flow.

Notice both **the gospel's progress in the world** and **grace's growth in the believer's heart**.

Pay attention to the Holy Spirit's work under divine guidance.

When we examine God's truth, some matters are **clear and easy to understand**, like water reaching the ankles.

Others call for deeper investigation, like water reaching the knees or the waist.

Still others lie **beyond our understanding**, where we can only worship God for the depths of his wisdom, as Paul did (**Romans 11**).

It is wise to begin with **what is most easily understood** before going on to difficult and obscure subjects.

Where the gospel is preached, the promises of Scripture and the privileges of believers are made abundant in their souls by the **life-giving Spirit**.

They nourish and delight the heart, and never wither, fade or run dry.

Even the **leaves** serve as medicine.

The warnings and rebukes of God's word may be less pleasant than its consolations, but they help **heal spiritual disease**.

Everyone who believes in Christ and is united to him through **his sanctifying Spirit** shares in the privileges belonging to Israel.

There is room **in the church and in heaven** for all who seek the blessings of **the new covenant**, whose Mediator is Christ.

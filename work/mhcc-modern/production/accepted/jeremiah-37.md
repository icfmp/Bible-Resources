# Jeremiah 37 — Modern English

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |

## The Chaldean army will return (verses 1–10)

Many people see the deadly consequences of someone else's sins, yet carelessly step into that person's place and follow the same destructive course.

When we are in trouble, we should seek the prayers of **ministers and Christian friends**.

But it is common for people to want others to pray for them while refusing to accept **their advice**.

A temporary pause in judgment may even make sinners **more stubborn**.

If God does not help us, no creature can truly save us.

Whatever instruments he has determined to use will fulfill his purpose, even if they appear unlikely to succeed.

## Jeremiah is falsely accused and imprisoned (verses 11–21)

At times the wise course for godly people is to withdraw, enter their rooms and shut their doors (**Isaiah 26:20**).

Jeremiah was arrested as if he were deserting to the enemy, and he was thrown into prison.

It is nothing new for those who most faithfully serve the church to be falsely accused of secretly helping its worst enemies.

When such charges are made, we may deny them and entrust our case to **the God who judges righteously**.

The Lord gave Jeremiah grace to remain faithful.

He would not purchase a human ruler's mercy by betraying either **God** or **his rightful duty toward the king**.

He told the king the entire truth.

When speaking **God's message**, Jeremiah was bold.

When presenting **his own request**, he was humble and respectful.

**A lion in God's cause should be a lamb in his own.**

God gave Jeremiah favor in the king's eyes.

The Lord can even turn **a prison cell into pasture** for his people and raise up friends to provide for them.

He can feed them even during famine.

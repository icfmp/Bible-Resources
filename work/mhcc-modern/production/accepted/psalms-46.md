## Confidence in God's presence (verses 1–5)

This psalm encourages hope and trust in God's power, Providence and gracious presence with his church, **even during the most difficult times**.

It also speaks to our conflicts with spiritual enemies and the promise that **through Christ we will overcome them**.

God is a help, a **present help**—someone we have already experienced as our helper.

He is always near and available. We cannot desire a better refuge or find an equal among created beings.

People whose confidence rests upon unstable foundations will panic when the waters rage.

But those led to the **Rock** can stand securely.

The psalm gives the church joy even amid sorrow.

Its river pictures **the Holy Spirit's graces and consolations**, flowing throughout the church through God's appointed ordinances and bringing joy to every believer.

God promises that his church will not finally be moved.

When he dwells in our hearts and his word lives richly within us, he will establish and help us.

**Let us trust him and refuse to be afraid**.

## Behold God's works (verses 6–11)

Look at the consequences of devastating judgments and stand in reverence before God.

At the same time, recognize the church's ultimate safety and the promise of lasting peace.

Pray that those glorious days will arrive soon.

Worship and trust **our almighty Sovereign** with humble submission.

Let all believers rejoice that **the Lord of hosts, the God of Jacob**, has been with us, is with us and will remain with us.

He is our refuge.

Take hold of this assurance and answer every fear with it:

**If God is for us, who can be against us?**

Let that confidence sustain us both in life and in death.

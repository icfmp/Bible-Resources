# Psalm 93 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The majesty, power, and holiness of Christ's kingdom.**

## The majesty and holiness of Christ's kingdom

God could have revealed only his **justice, holiness and fearful power** in dealing with fallen humanity.

Instead, he has chosen to reveal **the riches of his mercy** and the power of his **renewing grace**.

In this great work the Father has given all authority to his Son, **the Lord from heaven**, who has made atonement for our sins.

Christ is able not only **to pardon**, but also **to deliver and protect** everyone who trusts in him.

He has given his word, and all God's people may rely upon it.

Everything foretold about **the Messiah's kingdom** will be fulfilled at the appointed time.

His people ought to live in strict holiness.

The church is **God's own house**, set apart from sin and devoted to his service.

**Where purity is found, peace will follow**.

Let each person examine carefully whether this kingdom has been established **within the heart**.

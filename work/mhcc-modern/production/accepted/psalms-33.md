# Psalm 33 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| God to be praised. | 1–11 |
| His people encouraged by his power. | 12–22 |

## God deserves our praise (verses 1–11)

Holy joy is at the heart of genuine praise. Thankful singing gives voice to that joy and is a fitting way to worship the Lord.

Every ability God has given us should be used with skill and earnestness in his service.

His promises are wise and good. His word is right, and we are right only insofar as we agree with it. Everything he does is done in truth.

Because the Lord is righteous, he loves righteousness.

How sad that an earth so full of signs of God's goodness should be so lacking in praise! Multitudes depend upon his generosity, yet so few live for his glory.

Whatever God does, he does effectively and permanently.

He overrules the plans of human beings and makes them serve his own purposes, even when the result surprises us. **Nothing can prevent the fulfillment of his eternal counsel**.

## God's people trust his power (verses 12–22)

God knows every movement and intention of the human soul—even things hidden from all other people. He understands our hearts better than we do.

Our lives and hearts are in his hands; he formed the spirit of each person.

Every created power depends upon him. Without God it can accomplish nothing.

When we are secure in his favor, we need not fear what stands against us. Let us give him the glory for his particular grace.

Humanly devised methods to save our souls are useless. But the Lord's watchful eye rests on people who **fear his name because they hope in his mercy**.

He helps them in difficulty and preserves them from spiritual harm amid danger.

Whoever fears God and his judgment must also hope in his mercy. We cannot escape from him except by **fleeing to him**.

Lord, let your mercy be upon us. Give us its benefit and comfort—not according to what we deserve, but according to **your promise and the faith your Spirit has created within us**.

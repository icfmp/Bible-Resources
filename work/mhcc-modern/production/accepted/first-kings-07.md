# 1 Kings 7 — Modern English

## Solomon builds his other houses (verses 1–12)

All Solomon's buildings were beautiful, but they were also intended for practical use.

Solomon began with the **temple**.

He built for God before building his other houses.

The surest foundations of lasting prosperity are laid in early devotion to the Lord.

Solomon spent **thirteen years** building his palace but a little more than **seven years** building the temple.

That did not mean he cared less about the quality of his own house.

He simply gave greater urgency and effort to the Lord's work than to his own comfort.

We should likewise value **God's honor above our ease and satisfaction**.

## Furnishings for the temple (verses 13–47)

The two bronze pillars at the temple entrance may, Henry says, have taught worshipers to depend on God alone for stability and strength in every act of worship.

**Jachin** can be understood as, “God will establish this wandering heart.”

It is good for the heart to be established by grace.

**Boaz** points to strength found in God, who works within us both to will and to act.

Spiritual strength and stability are received at the door of God's temple as we wait upon his appointed means of grace.

Spiritual priests and spiritual sacrifices must be washed in the cleansing basin of **Christ's blood and regeneration**.

We must wash frequently because we become defiled by sin every day.

The Lord has provided everything necessary for cleansing.

If we finally remain among the unclean, we have only ourselves to blame for rejecting what he provided.

Let us praise God for the fountain opened by Christ's sacrifice for sin and impurity.

## The vessels of gold (verses 48–51)

Christ is now **both the Temple and its Builder**.

He is **the Altar and the Sacrifice**.

He is the **Light of our souls and the Bread of Life**.

He can meet every need of those who have come or will come to him.

No outward image can adequately represent his worth.

Words cannot express it, and the human mind cannot fully imagine his love.

Come to him.

Let his blood wash away your sins, and seek the cleansing grace of his Spirit.

Through his intercession, remain in fellowship with the Father.

Give yourself and everything you possess to his service.

Strengthened by him, you will become accepted, useful and happy.

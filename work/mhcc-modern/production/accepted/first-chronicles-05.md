# 1 Chronicles 5 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Genealogies.**

## The tribes east of the Jordan

This chapter gives an account of **the two and a half tribes** that settled on the east side of the Jordan.

They were later taken captive by the king of Assyria because they had abandoned the Lord.

Only **two events** are highlighted in Henry's summary of their history.

**First, they shared a victory.**

Happy are people who live in harmony and help one another resist the common enemies of their souls, trusting the Lord and calling on him together.

**Second, they shared captivity.**

They had sought the best land, without considering that its position made it especially exposed to enemy attack.

Strong desires for earthly possessions can draw us away from God's appointed means of worship and prepare us for destruction.

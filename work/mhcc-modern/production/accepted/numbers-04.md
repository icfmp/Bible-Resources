# Numbers 4 — Modern English

## Levites in their prime serve the sanctuary (verses 1–3)

Levites aged **thirty to fifty** were appointed for the main service of the tabernacle.

Serving God deserves the best of our strength and the finest part of our lives.

Our time cannot be better spent than honoring him who is first and best.

We should take up God's work while we are most energetic and capable.

Those who postpone repentance until old age have failed to understand this.

They leave the most important work to the least favorable time.

## The duties of Kohath's descendants (verses 4–20)

The Kohathites carried the tabernacle's most holy furnishings.

The sacred objects had to be covered, both to protect them and to treat them with reverence.

They were also kept from ordinary sight.

The covering pictured the mystery surrounding the spiritual truths represented by the sanctuary, and the limited light given under that earlier religious order.

Through Christ, our situation has changed.

We are now encouraged to approach the throne of grace boldly.

## The duties of Gershon and Merari (verses 21–33)

The other two Levite families were assigned work that seemed less honorable than Kohath's responsibilities.

Yet their duties were just as necessary and had to be carried out carefully.

Each object was assigned to their care by name.

This illustrated God's concern for his church and for every individual member.

Scripture compares the death of believers to dismantling a tent (2 Corinthians 5:1) or taking it off (2 Peter 1:14).

But our bodies will be raised on the great day.

They will be transformed to resemble Christ's glorious body, and we will remain with him forever.

## The number of Levites fit for service (verses 34–49)

God arranged the numbers in a surprising way.

Although the Merarites were the smallest family group, they had the greatest number of men capable of service.

God equips people for whatever work he assigns them.

He supplies strength suited to the task and sufficient grace to perform it.

Even the least numerous of Israel's other tribes had more able men than the Levites.

Similarly, more people devote themselves to the business of this world than to God's service.

May our souls be wholly given to serving him.

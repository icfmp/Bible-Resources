# Psalm 60 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| David prays for the deliverance of Israel from their enemies. | 1–5 |
| He entreats God to carry on and complete their victories. | 6–12 |

## Prayer for Israel's restoration (verses 1–5)

David recognized God's displeasure as the source of the hardship he had suffered.

When the Lord begins turning his hand toward us in favor, we should remember our earlier troubles.

Since the people's trials had begun through divine displeasure, their restoration needed to begin through **God's renewed favor**.

Human folly and corruption create divisions and breaches in society.

Only **God's wisdom and grace** can truly heal them, by pouring out a spirit of **love and peace**.

Such reconciliation is essential to saving a nation from ruin.

Henry describes human misery, private and public, as the consequence of **sin and God's righteous displeasure against it**.

The proper response is to return to God through **repentance, faith and prayer**, asking him to return to us in mercy.

**Christ, David's Son**, is the banner given to those who fear the Lord.

In him God's people are gathered into one and receive courage.

Through Christ's name and strength they engage in the spiritual struggle against **powers of darkness**.

## Confidence in the coming victories (verses 6–12)

If Christ belongs to us, everything will ultimately contribute to our **eternal good**.

Anyone made a new creation in Christ may rejoice in the precious promises God has spoken.

Present spiritual privileges and the Spirit's sanctifying influence are **certain pledges of heavenly glory**.

David rejoiced over victories against neighboring nations hostile to Israel.

God's spiritual Israel becomes **more than conquerors through Christ**.

At times believers may feel abandoned by the Lord, yet he will ultimately bring them into **the strong city**.

Faith in his promises assures us that the Father delights to give his people **the kingdom**.

But the victory is not complete yet.

No sincere believer will use these promises as an excuse for laziness or presumptuous self-confidence.

Hope in God gives genuine courage.

What do people need to fear if the Lord is on their side?

Every victory comes from him.

Those who submit willingly to **our anointed King** will share his glory, while every enemy will finally be put beneath his feet.

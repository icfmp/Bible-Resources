# Joshua 23 — Modern English

## Joshua urges faithfulness before his death (verses 1–10)

Joshua was old and near death. The people therefore had special reason to listen carefully to his words.

He reminded them of the great things God had done during his leadership.

He urged them to be very courageous.

They must take care to **keep** what Scripture says, diligently **do** it and sincerely **respect** it.

They must also work cautiously to remove every reminder of pagan idolatry, so that its worship would never return.

Henry laments how casually people who call themselves Christians use the names of pagan gods, making them familiar in everyday conversation.

Joshua called Israel to perseverance.

There may have been many faults among the people, but they had not altogether abandoned the Lord their God.

One way to help people become better is first to recognize whatever good is already present in them.

## Joshua warns Israel against idolatry (verses 11–16)

If we want to remain close to the Lord, we must stay watchful. Many souls are lost through sheer carelessness.

**Love God**, and you will not want to leave him.

He has remained faithful to you; do not be unfaithful to him.

The One who promised is faithful (Hebrews 10:23).

Every Christian's experience confirms this truth.

Struggles may be long and difficult, and trials may be numerous and severe.

Yet in the end the believer will acknowledge that goodness and mercy followed throughout life.

Joshua explained exactly how apostasy would progress.

**First**, the people would become friends with idolaters.

**Next**, they would intermarry with them.

**Finally**, they would worship their gods.

Sin's path runs downhill. People who deliberately share fellowship with sinners cannot avoid becoming involved in sin.

Joshua warned that such a course would destroy them.

The excellence of the heavenly Canaan, and God's freely given and unfailing promise of it, will only deepen the misery of people who remain excluded from it forever.

Their awareness of how happy they **might have been** will make them see more clearly how miserable they have become.

Let us watch and pray against temptation.

Let us trust God's faithfulness, love and power, appeal to his promises and cling to his commands.

Then we will be blessed in life, in death and forever.

# Esther 5 — Modern English

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Esther's application received. | 1–8 |

## The king receives Esther (verses 1–8)

Esther had prevailed with God, as Jacob did, and therefore found favor with a human ruler too.

Whoever is willing to lose life for God will save it, or receive a better life.

The king encouraged her.

Let that encourage us to pray continually and not give up.

Esther approached a proud, imperious man.

We approach **the God of love and grace**.

Esther was not invited, but **we are invited**: the Spirit and the bride say, “Come.”

She faced a law against her.

We have many promises in our favor: **Ask, and it will be given**.

She had no friend to accompany her or plead for her.

Indeed, the king's current favorite was her enemy.

But **we have an Advocate before the Father**, one in whom the Father delights.

Therefore, approach the throne of grace boldly.

God moved Esther to postpone her request for one more day.

She did not know what would happen that very night, but God did.

## Haman plans Mordecai's execution (verses 9–14)

Haman's behavior illustrates **Proverbs 21:24**.

People who admire and flatter themselves are actually deceiving themselves.

The higher Haman rose, the less patiently he endured even a small sign of disrespect.

Mordecai's conduct ruined his pleasure in everything else.

A slight insult that a humble person might scarcely notice can torment a proud person almost to madness.

Those determined to be unhappy will always find something to make them unhappy.

Proud people can possess nearly everything they desire, yet if they lack one thing, all the rest becomes worthless in their eyes.

Many admire the wealthy and powerful who surround themselves with splendor.

But this is a mistaken view of happiness.

Many poor families in small cottages have far less anxiety than wealthy people living amid supposed advantages.

**Whoever does not know Christ is poor, however much wealth that person possesses**, because the only true riches are missing.

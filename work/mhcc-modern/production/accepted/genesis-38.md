# Genesis 38 — Modern English

## Original chapter heading

**The profligate conduct of Judah and his family.**

## Judah and his family (whole-chapter exposition)

This chapter tells the story of Judah and his family. It is such a troubling account that we may wonder why our Lord should descend from Judah, of all Jacob's sons (Hebrews 7:14).

But God shows us that his choice rests on grace, not personal merit. Christ came into the world to save sinners, even the worst of them.

We also learn that Christ's worthiness comes from himself, not from the people among his ancestors.

How little reason the Jews, whose name came from Judah, had for the boasting described in John 8:41!

What fearful examples God gives, through his judgments, of his complete hatred of sin.

Let us seek his grace to avoid even the appearance of sin.

And let the humility of Jesus make him still dearer to us. When he came to put away sin by sacrificing himself, he submitted to being born from a family line that included people such as those described here.

# Psalm 54 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| David complains of the malice of his enemies. | 1–3 |
| Assurance of the Divine favour and protection. | 4–7 |

## David appeals to God's name (verses 1–3)

God is faithful even when human beings prove unreliable.

How good it is for us that his faithfulness does not depend on theirs!

David had no plea beyond **God's name**, and no strength to rely upon except **God's strength**.

He made these his refuge and confidence, assured that God could answer his prayer.

David was betrayed by people from Judah, and Jesus was betrayed by **one of his own apostles**.

Why should we expect anything but ingratitude, treachery or cruelty from people who refuse to set God before them?

If people abandon the fear of God, what human ties of friendship, family, gratitude or solemn agreement will restrain them?

**Selah: pay attention to this**.

Let us set God before us continually, because without that practice we are in danger of despair.

## Assurance of God's help (verses 4–7)

**God is my helper**.

If we belong to him, he is for us. And if he is for us, we need not fear.

Every creature can do for us only what God appoints it to do.

He will save his people in due time.

Meanwhile he sustains them so that the spirits he created do not fail.

God's threats are true just as surely as his promises. Sinners who refuse repentance will discover that at their own cost.

David's present deliverance gave assurance of further rescue.

He spoke about the final completion as though it had already happened, because God's promise made it certain.

The Lord would ultimately deliver him from all his troubles.

May he enable us to carry our crosses without resentment and finally share **Christ's victory and glory**.

The voice of praise and thanksgiving should never fall silent in the church of the redeemed.

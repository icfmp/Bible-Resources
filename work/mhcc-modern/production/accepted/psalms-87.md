# Psalm 87 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The glory of the church. | 1–3 |
| It is filled with the Divine blessing. | 4–7 |

## Christ, the church's foundation (verses 1–3)

**Christ himself is the foundation** that God has laid for his church.

**Holiness** gives the church strength and stability.

We should not be ashamed of Christ's church, even when its outward condition is humble, or of the people who belong to it.

God has spoken glorious things about it.

No one can lay another foundation than **Jesus Christ**.

The glorious promises about Zion, spoken by the Spirit, point toward **Christ and his work**, the different offices he fulfills, the gospel church and its privileges and members, and finally **heaven with its perfect happiness and glory**.

## The blessing of belonging to Zion (verses 4–7)

Christ's church is more glorious and excellent than any earthly nation.

The records of heaven include even the humblest person who has been **born again**.

When God judges everyone according to their works, he will take account of those who enjoyed the privileges of his sanctuary.

**Much is required of those to whom much has been given**.

Those who belong to Zion should remember their privileges and live consistently with their profession of faith.

The songs of Zion will be sung with joy and triumph.

Worldly-minded people look for happiness in **wealth and pleasure**.

But a soul transformed by grace finds its springs of joy in **God's word and prayer**.

Every grace and comfort flows from **Christ through his appointed ordinances** to believers' souls.

# Exodus 36 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The making of the tabernacle, The liberality of the people restrained.**

## The builders' faithfulness and the tabernacle's meaning

The builders began their work with eagerness and zeal.

They followed their instructions exactly.

And they were faithful enough to object when people brought more contributions than were needed.

Their example deserves to be followed.

We should serve God with that same readiness, care and honesty.

We should also serve those in authority over us faithfully in everything that is lawful.

Everyone entrusted with public responsibilities should hate dishonest gain and avoid every opportunity or temptation to become greedy.

Where do we see God's love toward us pictured so clearly—the love by which we live in him and he lives in us?

We see it in **Immanuel**, “God with us” (Matthew 1:23).

This is the heart of the ministry of reconciliation (2 Corinthians 5:18–19).

It was also the purpose of the **tabernacle of testimony**.

The sanctuary visibly declared God's love for the human race, despite the fall from humanity's original condition.

That love was displayed fully when Christ came to live on earth.

The Word became flesh (John 1:14).

As the original expression suggests, he **pitched his tabernacle among us**.

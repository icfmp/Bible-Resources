## Genealogies from Adam to Abraham (verses 1–27)

This chapter and many that follow bring together genealogies—lists of parents and descendants—already found elsewhere in Scripture, while adding other details.

When we compare these lists with other passages, we may notice differences.

But those differences should not cause us to lose confidence in God's word.

We should instead thank him that the truths necessary for salvation are sufficiently clear.

This record traces the Jewish nation's ancestry to **the first man created by God**.

That makes it different from the obscure, legendary or absurd claims about national origins that have circulated elsewhere.

People of different nations have since become so mixed with one another that no present-day nation—or even most of its population—can be said to descend exclusively from a single ancient line.

One thing remains certain: God made **all nations from one blood**.

We are all descended from **one Adam and one Noah**.

Do we not all have one Father?

Has not one God created us? (**Malachi 2:10**)

## Abraham's descendants (verses 28–54)

From this point onward, the genealogy concentrates on Abraham's descendants.

As we read so many names, we should think about the multitudes who have lived on earth, played their part in its history and then departed.

One generation passes, including generations of sinful people, and another comes after it (**Ecclesiastes 1:4; Numbers 32:14**).

This succession will continue as long as the earth remains.

Our own passage through time into eternity is very short.

May we be distinguished as people who truly belong to the Lord.

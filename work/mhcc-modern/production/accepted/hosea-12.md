# Hosea 12 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Judah and Israel reminded of the Divine favours. | 1–6 |
| The provocations of Israel. | 7–14 |

## Remember Jacob and return to the Lord (verses 1–6)

**Ephraim** nourished false hopes of receiving human help while living **in hostility toward God**.

The Jews imagined they could secure Egypt's support by offering gifts from the produce of their land.

**Judah**, too, was called to account.

God sees the sins of **his own people** and will judge them.

Israel was reminded both of **what Jacob did** and of **what God did for Jacob**.

When Jacob's faith in God's promise overcame his fears, he prevailed through **God's strength** in prayer.

God is **Jehovah**, the same yesterday, today and forever.

What was once a revelation to an individual is now a lasting memorial of God for **all generations**.

Those who have departed from God should return to him through **repentance and faith**, acknowledging him as their God.

Those already converted should **walk faithfully with him** in holy conduct.

Let us wrestle in prayer for the blessings he has promised, determined to continue seeking him rather than give up.

Let us seek him in **the ordinances of his worship**.

## Israel's dishonest prosperity (verses 7–14)

Ephraim became a **merchant**.

Henry notes that the word can also mean **a Canaanite**.

The people traded on **Canaanite principles**, pursuing profit through greed, fraud and deceit.

They became wealthy and imagined that their prosperity proved **God's favor**.

But shameful sins bring **shameful judgments**.

They should remember not only Jacob's power with God, but also **the years he served Laban**.

When God has given us the privilege of his word, disregarding it makes our sin and folly **all the more serious**.

It is better to labor under the hardest conditions **in honest poverty** than to gain riches through sin.

We should compare our conduct with that of faithful believers who faced similar circumstances in the past.

Those who despise God's message will perish.

May we all receive his word with **humble and obedient faith**.

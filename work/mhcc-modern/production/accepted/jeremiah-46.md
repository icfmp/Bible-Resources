## The Egyptians are defeated (verses 1–12)

The whole word of God speaks **against those who refuse to obey the gospel of Christ**.

But it speaks **for everyone who turns to him**, including those from the nations outside Israel.

The prophecy begins with **Egypt**.

Let the Egyptians strengthen themselves with every skill, ally and resource they possess: their efforts will be useless.

The wounds God inflicts upon his enemies cannot be healed by ordinary medicines.

In this ever-changing world, strength and prosperity soon pass from one person or nation to another.

## Egypt is judged, while Israel receives comfort (verses 13–28)

Those who seized what belonged to other people would now find their own land invaded.

Egypt resembled a beautiful heifer not accustomed to bearing the yoke of subjection.

But destruction would come from the north: **the Chaldeans were coming**.

At the same time, God announced **comfort and peace to the Israel of God**.

Those promises encouraged his people while judgments were falling upon surrounding nations.

He would remain with them and **correct them in measure**.

He would **not** subject them to everlasting destruction away from his presence.

# Jeremiah 45 — Modern English

## Original heading

**An encouragement sent to Baruch.**

## God's correction and encouragement for Baruch

**Baruch** had written Jeremiah's prophecies and read them aloud, as described in **Jeremiah 36**.

The king threatened him because of that service.

People who are just beginning to serve the Lord are often discouraged by difficulties that are small compared with what may come later.

These complaints and fears arise from **the corruption remaining in their hearts**.

Baruch had expected too much from earthly life, making the suffering he faced far harder to endure.

The world's hostility would not trouble us so much if we had not foolishly hoped for its approval and eagerly sought its favor.

How foolish to seek **great things for ourselves here**, where everything is small and nothing is secure!

The Lord understands the real reasons for our anxiety and discouragement **better than we understand them ourselves**.

Let us ask him to examine our hearts and restrain every disordered desire within them.

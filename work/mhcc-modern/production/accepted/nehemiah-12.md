# Nehemiah 12 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |

| The dedication of the wall. | 27–43 |
| The officers of the temple settled. | 44–47 |

## The priests and Levites who returned (verses 1–26)

We owe faithful ministers a debt of gratitude.

Remember the leaders who taught us God's word.

It is good to learn what our godly predecessors were like, so that we can learn what we ought to become.

## The wall is dedicated (verses 27–43)

All our towns and houses should bear the mark **“Holy to the Lord.”**

A believer should undertake no task without dedicating it to him.

When our hands are to be used in God's work, we should cleanse those hands and purify our hearts.

Those called to help others become holy must first consecrate themselves to God.

For people sanctified to him, the good gifts and comforts of creation also become holy in their use.

The people rejoiced greatly.

**Everyone who benefits from a public blessing should share in public thanksgiving.**

## Temple officers are appointed (verses 44–47)

A day of thanksgiving truly benefits both ministers and people when it leaves them more careful and joyful in fulfilling their duties.

Whatever we do must be cleansed **by the atoning blood** and through **the grace of the Holy Spirit**.

Otherwise our work cannot be acceptable to God.

# Psalm 85 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Prayers for the continuance of former mercies. | 1–7 |
| Trust in God's goodness. | 8–13 |

## Remembering mercy and seeking pardon (verses 1–7)

Present trouble should not make us forget **God's earlier acts of mercy**.

His favor is the source of happiness for nations and individuals alike.

When God forgives sin, he **covers it completely**.

Forgiveness is not a partial hiding of the transgressions of his people.

Christ, our **Intercessor**, stood on their behalf.

Because of his mediation, the Lord turned away his anger and showed compassion.

Only when we have been reconciled to God can we expect to enjoy **the comfort of being reconciled to him**.

Salvation flows from **mercy alone**.

When believers sin, they may experience severe and prolonged correction.

Yet when they humbly repent and pray, God can restore their joy in him.

## God speaks peace through Christ (verses 8–13)

Sooner or later the Lord speaks **peace to his people**.

He may not give outward peace, but his Spirit can speak inwardly to their hearts.

This peace is promised to those who **turn away from sin**.

Every sin is folly, and backsliding is especially foolish because it means returning to wrongdoing once rejected.

Whatever our difficulties, God's salvation remains near.

His glory is also involved, so that honor may dwell among his people.

The truthfulness of God's promises appears in his mercy in **sending the Redeemer**.

Divine justice has been satisfied through **the great atonement**.

Christ, the Way, the Truth and the Life, came from the earth when **he took our human nature**.

The Father looked upon him with satisfaction.

For Christ's sake God grants every good thing, and especially **the Holy Spirit**, to those who ask.

Through Christ, forgiven sinners become fruitful in **good works**.

By looking to and trusting **the Savior's righteousness**, they find their feet established on the path of duty.

Righteousness guides them both in approaching God and in following him.

# Psalm 11 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's struggle with, and triumph over a strong temptation to distrust God, and betake himself to indirect means for his own safety, in a time of danger.**

## Trusting the Lord rather than worldly advice

Everyone who sincerely fears and serves God may trust him with confidence.

Before David described his temptation to distrust the Lord, he stated his decision to **trust him in life and in death**.

Believers may not be frightened by their enemies, yet they can still be tempted by **the fears of their friends**.

Well-meaning companions may persuade them to abandon their position or neglect their assigned work.

These friends see the danger but fail to recognize the believer's safety in God.

Their advice comes from **worldly calculation rather than heavenly wisdom**.

The truths of faith are the foundation of Christian confidence and hope.

We must hold firmly to them against every temptation to unbelief.

Without God to turn to, God to trust and the hope of eternal happiness, believers would be ruined.

The prosperity of wicked people and the hardship endured by godly people had tested David's faith.

Yet we do not need to climb to heaven to bring down a God we can trust.

His word is close to us, and **God is present in his word**.

His Spirit dwells within his saints, who are **living temples**.

The Lord is that Spirit.

God governs the world.

We may know only how people appear.

God knows what they truly are, as a refiner knows gold after testing it.

He examines hearts with eyes that cannot be deceived.

When the Lord afflicts righteous people, he tests them for their ultimate good.

Persecutors and oppressors may prosper temporarily, but if they remain unrepentant they will perish.

God is holy and therefore hates evil.

He is a righteous Judge and therefore will punish it.

At death, the wicked are carried away in a dreadful storm.

Every person receives an appointed portion.

Sinner, consider your danger: judgment is approaching, and without repentance death leads toward eternal wrath.

**Hurry to the cross of Christ.**

Ask yourself: what is the relationship between God and my soul?

Is Christ my hope, my comfort and my protection?

Only through him can the soul safely pass through all its troubles and spiritual conflicts.

# 1 Chronicles 22 — Modern English

## David prepares to build the temple (verses 1–5)

After the terrible judgment upon Israel for David's sin, God showed him where the temple should be built.

David was therefore moved to make preparations for this great work.

Although David was not to build it himself, he would do everything he could.

Before his death, he made abundant preparations.

Whatever our hands can do for God, our souls and the people around us, let us do with all our strength while life remains. After death there is no more work or planning.

When the Lord does not employ us in the role we hoped for, we should not become discouraged or idle.

We should do all we can, even in a humbler sphere.

## David instructs Solomon (verses 6–16)

David gave Solomon **three reasons** for building the temple.

First, **God had appointed Solomon**. Nothing more strongly encourages service than knowing the Lord has assigned it to us.

Second, Solomon would have **the peace and opportunity** to complete it. When God gives rest, he expects us to use it for work.

Third, **God had promised to establish Solomon's kingdom**. God's gracious promises should strengthen and encourage our service.

David explained how much he had already prepared.

He was not boasting; he wanted Solomon to enter the work cheerfully.

Solomon should never imagine that constructing a temple would give him permission to sin.

On the contrary, the work would not be acceptable if he failed to obey God's commands.

In both spiritual service and spiritual conflict, we need courage and determination.

## Others are instructed to help (verses 17–19)

Anything done to make God's word more widely known and obeyed is like contributing a stone or a bar of gold to the temple's construction.

That should encourage people distressed by seeing so little fruit from their efforts.

Much good may appear **after our death** that we never expected.

Let us not grow weary of doing good.

The work belongs to **the Prince of Peace**.

He begins and completes it, and graciously employs us as his instruments.

Therefore, let us rise and work, encouraging and helping one another.

Let us follow his rule and example, depend upon his grace and remain assured that our work in the Lord will not be wasted.

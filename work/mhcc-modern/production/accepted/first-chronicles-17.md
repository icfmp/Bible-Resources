# 1 Chronicles 17 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's purposes; God's gracious promises.**

## God's promise to David

This chapter repeats the account in **2 Samuel 7**. The comments on that chapter also apply here.

There is, however, a notable difference in wording: what Samuel calls **“for your word's sake”** is described here as **“for your servant's sake”** (verse 19).

Jesus Christ is both **the Word of God** (Revelation 19:13) and **the Servant of God** (Isaiah 42:1).

God fulfills his promises to all believers for Christ's sake and because of his mediation. In him, every promise is **Yes and Amen**.

For his sake the promises are fulfilled and made known. We owe all this greatness to him and look to him for the great things still to come.

These are the unsearchable riches of Christ.

If through faith we recognize their worth, both in themselves and in the Lord Jesus, we cannot help praising them as the only true greatness and speaking honorably of them.

May we seek this blessedness through life's trials, when we feel death approaching, and for the children who come after us.

# Jeremiah 21 — Modern English

## Surrender is the only path to survival (verses 1–10)

After the siege began, **King Zedekiah** sent messengers to ask Jeremiah how the conflict would end.

During danger and distress, people often seek **the advice and prayers of those they normally despise and oppose**.

Yet often they want only to escape **the punishment of their sin**, without giving up the sin itself.

Those who profess religion but persist in disobedience must not suppose their **outward privileges** will protect them.

The Lord may grant success even to **open enemies** when they are sent in judgment against his disobedient people.

The king and his officials would not surrender, but the prophet urged **the people to surrender** in order to preserve their lives.

No sinner on earth who **truly wants a refuge** has been left without one.

But the road of life is **humbling**.

It requires **self-denial** and may expose the traveler to real difficulties.

## The household of David is urged to practice justice (verses 11–14)

The sin of the king and his family was especially serious because of their connection with **David**.

They were called to do **justice immediately**, before the Lord's anger burned beyond restraint.

**If God is for us, who can stand against us?**

But if **God is against us, who can possibly help us?**

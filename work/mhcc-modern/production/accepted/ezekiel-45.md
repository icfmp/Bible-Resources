# Ezekiel 45 — Modern English

## Original source structure

**No separate authorial heading; one continuous commentary.**

## Justice, worship and Christ our Passover

In the period foretold here, **God's worship and its ministers will be provided for**.

Rulers will govern **justly**, recognizing that their authority is held **under Christ**.

The people will enjoy **peace, ease and godliness**.

Henry says these promises **seem to be expressed in images drawn from the customs of Ezekiel's own time**.

**Christ is our Passover sacrifice**, offered for us.

We celebrate the remembrance of that sacrifice in **the Lord's Supper**, our Passover feast.

There we rejoice in deliverance from **the Egyptian bondage of sin** and preservation from **the destroying sword of God's justice**.

Indeed, the whole Christian life is—and must remain—a feast of **unleavened bread**, lived in **sincerity and truth**.

# Jeremiah 19 — Modern English

## Original source statement

**By the type of breaking an earthen vessel, Jeremiah is to predict the destruction of Judah.**

## The coming ruin of Jerusalem (verses 1–9)

Jeremiah had to announce the destruction approaching **Judah and Jerusalem**.

Rulers and ordinary people alike needed to hear this message.

Jerusalem's holiness had once made it **the joy of the whole earth**.

But sin had made the city **a place of shame and disgrace before the whole earth**.

There is only one way to escape God's justice:

**Flee to his mercy.**

## The shattered earthen vessel (verses 10–15)

Once a potter's earthen vessel has hardened and been broken into pieces, **no one can fit it together again as before**.

So Judah and Jerusalem would be shattered by **the Chaldeans**, just as Jeremiah broke the vessel.

No human hand could repair the destruction.

Yet if the people returned to the Lord, **he could heal them**.

The people had filled **Topheth** with people slaughtered in sacrifices to idols.

Therefore the Lord would fill the city with those killed as **a judgment upon their sin**.

Whatever human beings imagine, God will prove as terrible toward unrepentant wrongdoing as Scripture has said.

Unbelief cannot make either **his promises or his warnings** ineffective.

Sinners are responsible for their own stubbornness.

If they refuse to hear God's word, it is because they have deliberately **closed their ears**.

We must pray that God's grace will rescue us from **hardness of heart** and **contempt for his word and commandments**.

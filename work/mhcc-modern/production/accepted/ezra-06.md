# Ezra 6 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The decree for completing the temple. | 1–12 |
| The temple is finished. | 13–22 |

## The decree allows the work to resume (verses 1–12)

When God's appointed time comes to fulfill his gracious purposes for the church, he can raise up unexpected people to assist.

Zechariah directs our attention beyond Jerusalem's temple to a greater **spiritual building**.

Jesus Christ continues laying stone upon stone.

Let us assist his great work.

Difficulties may delay the construction, but opposition must not discourage us.

At the appointed time the work will be completed to Christ's praise.

He will set its finishing stone in place amid shouts of **“Grace! Grace to it!”**

## The temple is completed (verses 13–22)

The gospel church, God's spiritual temple, has been under construction for a long time.

Yet it will be finished when the whole mystical body is complete.

Every believer is also **a living temple**, growing in the most holy faith.

Satan and our own sinful desires oppose the work.

We may work carelessly and stop and start repeatedly.

But **the One who began the good work will bring it to completion**.

Then the spirits of righteous people will be made perfect.

The people of Israel were freed from the sting of their earlier suffering as their sins were taken away.

They worshiped joyfully.

Let us receive God's appointed worship gladly and serve the Lord with joy.

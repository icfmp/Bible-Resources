# Nehemiah 10 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The covenant, Those who signed it. | 1–31 |
| Their engagement to sacred rites. | 32–39 |

## The covenant is signed (verses 1–31)

Conversion means separating from the ways and habits of this world and devoting ourselves to the conduct commanded in God's word.

When we bind ourselves to obey God's commands, we undertake to obey **all of them**.

We also acknowledge that he is not only the Lord in a general sense, but **our Lord**.

## The people undertake renewed worship (verses 32–39)

Having made a covenant against the sins they had committed, the people now undertook the duties they had neglected.

We must do more than stop doing evil: **we must learn to do good**.

No community should expect God's blessing while allowing public worship to fall into neglect.

Families are more likely to prosper when God's house and its work are properly supported.

When everyone contributes, even if each individual gift is small, the combined contribution can be substantial.

We must do what we can in acts of devotion and charity.

Whatever our circumstances, we should joyfully carry out our duty to God. This is the surest path to freedom and contentment.

God's appointed ordinances nourish and strengthen believers' souls, so they should not resent the expense of supporting them.

Yet many people **let their souls go hungry**.

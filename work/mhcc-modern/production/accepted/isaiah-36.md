## Original source statement

## The Assyrian challenge to Jerusalem

For this chapter, Henry gives **no new verse-by-verse exposition** in his *Concise Commentary*.

He instead directs readers to **2 Kings 18:17–37** and the commentary already provided there.

That original cross-reference, rather than a rewritten copy of the earlier commentary, is the entire authorial content of this chapter.

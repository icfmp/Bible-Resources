## Christ, God's Wisdom, calls to humanity (verses 1–11)

God makes his will known through creation and human conscience, but **more clearly through Moses and the prophets**.

The main difficulty is persuading people to pay attention to the instruction they receive.

Yet when even the most ignorant person listens to **Christ's words**, that teaching can lead to a saving knowledge of the truth.

Where a person has an understanding heart and willingly receives truth with love, **wisdom becomes more precious than silver or gold**.

## The character and riches of Wisdom (verses 12–21)

Here **Wisdom is Christ**, in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.

He is **Christ in the word and Christ in the heart**: not only Christ revealed to us, but **Christ revealed within us**.

Every form of true prudence and ability comes from the Lord.

Through the redemption purchased by **Christ's precious blood**, the riches of grace overflow in wisdom and good judgment.

Human beings have invented many ways to destroy themselves. **God has provided one way to restore us**.

Christ hates pride, arrogance, evil conduct and dishonest speech.

These sins make people resistant to his humbling, awakening and holy instruction.

True religion offers the soundest counsel when we face difficult decisions, and helps us see our path clearly.

Christ's wisdom makes those who receive it through love for him genuinely happy.

**Seek him early, earnestly and before everything else.** Christ has never told anyone to seek him in vain.

People who love him have seen his loveliness and have experienced his love poured into their hearts.

They are blessed here, or in the incomparably better life to come.

Wealth obtained through vanity soon diminishes.

But possessions gained honestly can be put to good use, and what is rightly spent upon **godliness and charity** has a lasting benefit.

If God's people lack riches and honor in the present world, he will give them something infinitely better.

They enjoy **his grace** even now.

Through his Spirit, Christ guides them into truth and righteousness; they walk according to the Spirit.

They will also enjoy **God's glory** hereafter.

Wisdom's promises store up blessings not merely for days or years but **for eternity**.

That is why her fruit is better than gold.

## The eternal Son and the creation of the world (verses 22–31)

In Henry's exposition, **the Son of God declares that he was involved in creating the world**.

How perfectly suited the Creator of the world is to be **its Savior**!

Before the world began, the Son was appointed to that great saving work.

If he delights in saving miserable sinners, how could we fail to delight in **his salvation**?

## Hear Christ's voice (verses 32–36)

We should listen to Christ with the readiness of obedient children.

Let us not refuse such mercy.

Blessed are those who hear the Savior's voice and wait upon him through **daily Scripture reading, meditation and prayer**.

People devoted to the world make time for empty amusements while still attending to what they regard as essential.

When professing believers seek excuses to neglect **the means of grace**, do they not show contempt for Wisdom's instruction?

Christ is both **Wisdom and Life** to everyone who believes.

We cannot obtain God's favor unless we find Christ and are found in him.

Those who offend Christ **injure their own souls**.

Sinners are destroyed because they choose the way of destruction.

God therefore remains just when he judges them.

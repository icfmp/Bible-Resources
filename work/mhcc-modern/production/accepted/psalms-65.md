# Psalm 65 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| God is to be praised in the kingdom of grace. | 1–5 |
| In the kingdom of providence. | 6–13 |

## Praise in the kingdom of grace (verses 1–5)

All praise offered to God from earth comes from **Zion**, arising through **the Spirit of Christ** and becoming acceptable through him.

At times praise is silent because no words can express the greatness of God's goodness.

The Lord reveals himself at a **mercy seat**, ready to hear everyone who comes by faith in Jesus Christ.

Our sins prevail against us.

We cannot balance them with any personal righteousness.

Yet by **God's free mercy**, and through **a righteousness he himself supplies**, our transgressions will not bring us into condemnation.

Communion with God is a great blessing. It means enjoying fellowship with him as One we love and value, making devotion to him the ordinary business of our lives.

Such fellowship is possible **only because God freely chooses to welcome us**.

His house contains abundance enough to satisfy every soul—enough for everyone and enough for each, always ready and **without money or price**.

Wherever we live, faith and prayer allow us to maintain this fellowship and receive comfort.

But sinners receive these blessings only through **Jesus Christ, who approaches the Father as our Advocate and Surety**.

## Praise in the kingdom of Providence (verses 6–13)

The almighty strength that establishes mountains also supports the believer.

The word that calms stormy seas can silence the enemies of God's people.

Morning and evening bring different blessings: the watchman longs for morning, while the laborer welcomes evening's shade.

Some understand the psalm's language as a reference to **morning and evening sacrifices**.

Whatever the interpretation, let daily worship—**both private and with our families**—be a necessary duty and a cherished comfort.

The fruitful earth depends upon gifts from above, and every good and perfect gift comes from God.

He generously enriches a world full of human sin. Surely he has both **power and willingness to feed the souls of his people**.

These earthly provisions picture greater spiritual blessings.

**Christ, the Sun of Righteousness**, rises upon sinners, and **the Holy Spirit, the river of God full of living water**, brings salvation and life.

By that work, barren hearts become fruitful in good deeds, and whole nations are transformed more wonderfully than the ground changes through sunshine and rain.

Where the preached gospel goes forward accompanied by the Spirit, God's paths overflow with abundance.

The gospel will reach the wilderness and the nations. People will hear, rejoice and bear **fruits of righteousness through Jesus Christ, to the Father's glory**.

Lord, your works of creation and grace are many and marvelous. In loving-kindness you made them all.

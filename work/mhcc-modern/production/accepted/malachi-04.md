# Malachi 4 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The judgements on the wicked, and the happiness of the righteous. | 1–3 |
| Regard to be had to the law; John the Baptist promised as the forerunner of Messiah. | 4–6 |

## The Sun of Righteousness brings light and healing (verses 1–3)

The prophecy points to **both the first and the second coming of Christ**.

God has appointed the time for each.

People who persist in wickedness and disregard the Lord's anger will finally experience it.

The warning certainly applies to **the day of final judgment**, when Christ will be revealed in flaming fire to judge the proud and everyone who continues doing evil.

Yet Christ's coming brings **joyful light** to those who faithfully serve him.

The **Sun of Righteousness** is **Jesus Christ**.

Through him believers are **justified and sanctified**, and so come into the light.

His gracious work makes sinners holy, joyful and fruitful.

The promise also depicts the gifts and comforts of **the Holy Spirit**, whom Christ gives to his own as light and comfort in their hearts.

He is their **Sun and Shield**.

The same day that burns like an oven for unrepentant sinners will be bright as morning to the righteous.

They await it more eagerly than watchmen await the dawn.

Christ came as the Sun not only to illuminate a dark world but also **to heal a sick one**.

Believers grow in spiritual knowledge and strength.

Their growth is like **well-fed calves in a stall**, not like delicate flowers that quickly wither.

Every triumph of the saints depends upon **God's victory**, not their own independent strength.

Another day is coming, more dreadful for everyone who persists in wickedness than anything before it.

How great the believer's happiness will be on leaving the sorrow of this world to rejoice in the Lord forever!

## Remember God's law and prepare for the coming Messiah (verses 4–6)

This is the solemn conclusion not only of Malachi's prophecy but of **the Old Testament**.

Our consciences call us to remember God's law.

Even when no new prophets speak to us, **we have the Scriptures**, through which we can continue in fellowship with God.

Other people may boast about their supposedly enlightened reasoning.

But we should remain close to the holy word through which **the Sun of Righteousness shines into our hearts**.

The people were to wait with faith for the gospel of Christ and watch for its beginning.

**John the Baptist** would call for repentance and reform as **Elijah** had done.

Turning hearts toward God and obedience is the best preparation for **the great and terrible day of the Lord**.

John's message would reach people's hearts and produce a real inward change.

In this way he would prepare the way for the kingdom of heaven.

Through their sin, the Jews had exposed themselves to the law's curse.

God was ready to bring judgment, yet would once again call them to repentance and return through John's preaching.

Believers should patiently await their final release and look joyfully toward Christ's second coming, when their salvation will be completed.

Those who resist God's correcting rod must expect his judgment.

Nobody can escape the curse of God's law or enjoy the happiness of his redeemed people without **turning from sin and worldliness to Christ and holiness**.

**May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with us all. Amen.**

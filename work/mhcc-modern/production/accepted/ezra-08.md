# Ezra 8 — Modern English

## Ezra gathers companions (verses 1–20)

Ezra assembled people scattered from Israel and Judah.

God moved the hearts of a small remnant to go with him.

How unfortunate it is when good people fail to do worthy work simply because **no one invited or encouraged them**!

## Ezra prays for protection (verses 21–23)

Ezra recruited Levites to accompany him.

Yet what good would their help be if the Lord were not with him?

Those who sincerely seek God are safe under the shelter of his wings even in great danger.

Those who abandon him are always exposed.

When entering a new period of life, we should make sure we are not carrying unrepented sins from our former way of life into it.

Whenever danger arises, seek **peace with God** first.

Then nothing can truly harm us.

We should entrust our own welfare, our families and our possessions to God through prayer, leaving their care with him.

Sometimes we should decline even a lawful advantage if taking it could make other people stumble or dishonor God.

Ask him for the wisdom to know when to **use** and when to **refuse** a lawful opportunity.

We will not ultimately lose anything by taking risks, suffering or surrendering something for the Lord.

Their prayers were answered, as the outcome demonstrated.

No one who has sincerely sought God has done so in vain.

During trouble or danger, setting aside a time for private or shared prayer is among the best means available for finding relief.

## Treasures are entrusted to the priests (verses 24–30)

If we expect God to preserve through his providence the possessions he has entrusted to us, let us also preserve **the things that belong to him** through his grace.

Let his honor and interests be our concern; then we may trust him for our lives and comforts.

## Ezra reaches Jerusalem (verses 31–36)

Enemies waited to attack the travelers, but God protected them.

Even ordinary journeys should begin with prayer and end with thanksgiving.

How much greater will our gratitude be when the Lord guides us safely through the whole pilgrimage of life, through death's dark valley, beyond every enemy and into eternal joy!

Among the offerings, Israel brought **a sin offering**.

**Atonement** gives sweetness and security to every mercy received.

No mercy can bring lasting comfort unless sin is taken away and peace with God is made.

Then God's people could rest.

Their journey points toward sinners being brought out of spiritual slavery and led, under **God and Savior's care**, to the heavenly Jerusalem.

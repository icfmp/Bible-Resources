# Ezekiel 22 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The sins of Jerusalem. | 1–16 |
| Israel is condemned as dross. | 17–22 |
| As the corruption is general, so shall be the punishment. | 23–31 |

## Jerusalem is accused of many sins (verses 1–16)

The prophet was told to judge **the city of bloodshed**.

Jerusalem deserved that name because of its crimes.

The charges were exceedingly serious: **murder, idolatry, dishonoring parents, oppression, extortion, profaning the Sabbath and holy things, sexual immorality and adultery**.

At the root of these wrongs lay **forgetfulness of God**.

Sinners provoke him because they refuse to remember him.

Jerusalem had filled the measure of her sins.

People who surrender themselves to the rule of their sinful desires may justly be handed over to their consequences.

Those determined to be **their own masters** cannot expect more happiness than they can supply for themselves.

It will be a miserable portion.

## Israel is like worthless dross (verses 17–22)

Among surrounding nations, Israel had once been as **gold and silver** compared with inferior metals.

But now the people resembled **the worthless residue** separated from silver in the furnace.

In God's sight, persistent sinners—especially people who profess faith but turn back to sin—are without the usefulness they were meant to have.

When the Lord brings **his own people** into a furnace of affliction, he watches over them like **a refiner guarding his precious gold**.

He keeps them there **no longer than necessary**.

The worthless residue is separated and the good metal made pure.

If we experience severe pain or long illness and find even these temporary trials almost unbearable, we should be warned to flee **the far greater wrath to come**.

Unless the Holy Spirit uses our suffering to cleanse our **hearts and conduct**, much worse misery may await us.

## Every level of society is corrupt (verses 23–31)

People of every rank and occupation contributed to the nation's guilt.

Those who possessed authority **abused it**.

Even buyers and sellers found ways to **oppress one another**.

It is a fearful sign when judgment is beginning and **the spirit of prayer has weakened**.

Let everyone who fears God unite to uphold **his truth and righteousness**, while wicked people from every profession and class unite to oppose them.

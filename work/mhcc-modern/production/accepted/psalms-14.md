# Psalm 14 — Modern English

## Original heading

**A description of the depravity of human nature, and the deplorable corruption of a great part of mankind.**

## Sin, human corruption and salvation from Zion

The fool says within the heart, **“There is no God.”**

The sinner described rejects the existence of a Judge governing the world and a Providence ruling human affairs.

But the denial begins **within the heart**.

Such a person cannot truly establish that God does not exist.

Instead, the person wishes there were no God and takes pleasure in the mere possibility that no divine judgment will come.

This is foolishness.

The sinner's wicked and irreverent conduct is both evidence of that folly and a cause of it.

God's word reveals the thoughts and desires behind it.

No one willingly denies divine accountability in this way until sin has hardened the heart so deeply that having no Judge appears desirable.

The disease of **sin has infected all humanity**.

Everyone has turned aside.

No one does good as the holy law demands—not even one.

Whatever good exists in human beings or is done through them comes **from God's work within them**, not from their own independent goodness.

People have turned away from the path of obedience and happiness and entered paths leading to ruin.

We should grieve over human corruption and recognize how greatly we need **God's grace**.

It is therefore no surprise that Scripture insists we must **be born again**.

We should not rest in anything less than **union with Christ and a new creation through his Holy Spirit**, producing holiness.

The psalmist tried to warn sinners who imagined themselves wise, upright and secure.

Those who show no concern for God's poor and his people show that they do not truly love God.

People continue committing wrong because they do not seek God's grace through prayer.

How much good can we expect from a life **without prayer**?

People unwilling to fear God may nevertheless become terrified by something as small as a shaking leaf.

The knowledge of our common corruption should make **salvation coming from Zion** precious to us.

In heaven alone will all the redeemed rejoice completely and forever.

The world is deeply sinful—how we should long for the Messiah to come and transform it!

The victories of Zion's King will be the joy of Zion's children.

**Christ's second coming** will finally overthrow the rule of sin and Satan and complete salvation.

This hope should comfort God's people amid the sins committed by sinners and the suffering endured by saints.

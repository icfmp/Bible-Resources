# Nehemiah 7 — Modern English

## Jerusalem is entrusted to Hananiah (verses 1–4)

After completing the wall, Nehemiah returned to the Persian court.

He later came back to Jerusalem under a new commission.

Public safety depends in part upon every person's care to guard themselves and their households against sin.

## The list of those who first returned (verses 5–73)

Nehemiah knew that, under God, a city's safety depends **more on the character of its inhabitants than on the strength of its walls**.

Every good gift and good work comes from heaven.

God grants both knowledge and grace. Everything comes from him, so everything should be devoted to him.

When human foresight produces good results, we must acknowledge the direction of divine Providence.

How terrible for those who turn away from the Lord because they love the present world!

How happy those who dedicate themselves and their possessions to God's service and glory!

# Psalm 130 — Modern English

## Crying to the God who forgives (verses 1–4)

A soul entangled in sin can find true relief only **by turning to God**.

Many amusements distract us, and many supposed remedies offer help.

But the soul discovers that **the Lord alone can heal it**.

Until we recognize our guilt and abandon our self-reliance to come directly to him, we will look in vain for peace.

The Holy Spirit awakens people to a fresh understanding of their deep need and moves them to **earnest prayer in faith**.

For their souls' welfare and God's glory, they must not neglect this duty.

Why do some continue in uncertainty for so long?

Often it is because laziness or discouragement makes them settle for merely routine, customary prayers.

Let us take action and seek the Lord.

We can approach him safely.

We must humble ourselves as **guilty before God**.

We cannot justify ourselves or claim innocence.

But what an inexpressible comfort that **forgiveness is available with him**, exactly what we need!

**Jesus Christ is the great Ransom and our continual Advocate**.

Through him we hope to receive pardon.

There is forgiveness with God **not so that we can treat sin lightly**, but so that we will fear and reverence him.

The fear of God often describes the whole life of worshiping him.

For sinners, the great invitation and encouragement is simply this: **the Lord offers forgiveness**.

## Waiting for abundant redemption (verses 5–8)

My soul waits for **the Lord himself**, for the gifts of his grace and the work of his power.

We should hope only for what **God has promised in his word**.

A watchman longs for the morning light before day breaks.

In the same way, a godly soul waits even more eagerly for signs of the Lord's favor and renewed grace.

Everyone devoted to the Lord should remain confident in him.

This redemption delivers us **from all sin**.

**Jesus Christ saves his people from their sins**, removing both **sin's condemnation** and **sin's power to command**.

His redemption is abundant.

The Redeemer possesses a fullness sufficient **for everyone and for each believer individually**.

There is enough for me, too.

Freedom from sin includes freedom from every other evil that would finally destroy us.

Through **Jesus' atoning blood**, his people will be fully redeemed from all their sins.

Everyone who waits upon God for mercy and grace can look forward to peace.

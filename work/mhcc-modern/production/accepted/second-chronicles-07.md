# 2 Chronicles 7 — Modern English

## Original heading

**God's answer to Solomon's prayer.**

## God answers Solomon

God graciously answered Solomon's prayer.

His mercy toward sinners was shown in a way that impressed all who received it with **his majesty and holiness**.

The people worshiped and praised him.

When God reveals himself as **a consuming fire** to sinners, his people can rejoice in him as **their light**.

Indeed, they had good reason to say he was good in doing this.

It is because of God's mercy that **we ourselves are not consumed**. Instead, a sacrifice was offered on our behalf, for which we should be deeply thankful.

Anyone who truly looks in faith upon **the Savior suffering and dying for human sin** will experience deeper godly sorrow, greater hatred of sin, closer watchfulness and a holier life.

Solomon successfully completed his plans for beautifying God's house and his own.

People who begin by serving God are likely to prosper in their other responsibilities.

It was to Solomon's credit that he completed what he undertook, though it was **God's grace** that made his success possible.

Let us reverence God and not sin.

Let us fear his displeasure, hope in his mercy and walk according to his commands.

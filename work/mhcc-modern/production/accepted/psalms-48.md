# Psalm 48 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The glories of the church of Christ.**

## Original source divisions

| Source commentary | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| First section | 1–7 |
| Second section | 8–14 |

## The beauty of God's city (verses 1–7)

Jerusalem is the city of God.

Henry teaches that those belonging to **the spiritual Jerusalem** are the ones who truly honor him on earth.

Happy is any kingdom, city, family or individual heart where **God is great and God is everything**.

There he is known.

The more clearly we understand his greatness, the more praise should arise from us.

Sin has disfigured the earth.

Therefore the place adorned with divine holiness could rightly be called **the joy of the whole earth**—a reason for all nations to rejoice that God truly dwells with humanity.

The earthly kings feared it.

The destruction of an entire fleet in a storm provides a fitting picture of **paganism being overcome by the Spirit of the gospel**.

Both require the Lord's mighty power.

## The security and everlasting guide of the church (verses 8–14)

God's people should learn from every glorious act of his favor.

Let such deliverances strengthen faith in his word, encourage hope in the church's stability and fill our minds with worthy thoughts of him.

Every stream of mercy flowing toward us must be traced back to **the fountain of his loving-kindness**.

Give God the glory for everything great he has done.

Every member of the church should take comfort from what the Lord does for his people.

Consider the church's **beauty, strength and safety**.

It is founded upon **Christ the Rock**, fortified by divine power and guarded by the One who never slumbers or sleeps.

His appointed ordinances are like **palaces**, and his promises are like **defensive walls**.

Recognize these blessings, be encouraged to belong to his church and tell others about them.

The God who has done great things for us does not change in his love or care.

If he is our God, he will guide and preserve us to the end.

He will take us beyond death's reach, so that death cannot finally harm us, and bring us into a life where **death no longer exists**.

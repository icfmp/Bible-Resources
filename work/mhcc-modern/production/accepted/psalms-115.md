# Psalm 115 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Glory to be ascribed to God. | 1–8 |
| by trusting in him and praising him. | 9–18 |

## Glory belongs to God alone (verses 1–8)

We must never appeal to **our own merit** in our prayers or praises.

Any good we accomplish comes from **God's grace**, and every good thing we possess is a gift of **his free mercy**.

He therefore deserves all the praise.

When we seek mercy and wrestle in prayer, we should look for encouragement **in God alone**.

Lord, act for us—not so that we may take the credit or comfort for ourselves, but so that **your mercy and faithfulness will receive the glory**.

The gods of the nations are lifeless things, made by human hands.

A painter, a sculptor or a craftsman cannot give life to an image and therefore cannot give it sensation or knowledge.

The psalm shows the folly of those who worship such idols.

## Trust, praise and the living God (verses 9–18)

Trusting lifeless images is foolish.

Trusting **the living God** is wise, because he is **help and shield** to everyone who relies on him.

True reverence for God can be joined to cheerful confidence in him.

Those who respect his word may trust it.

He has always proved faithful.

The greatest people need his blessing.

He will not deny his blessing to the humblest person who fears him.

God's blessing brings growth, especially **spiritual growth**.

We should praise the Lord for his wide generosity in giving the earth to human beings for their use.

After their souls are released from the burdens of their mortal bodies, faithful believers continue praising him.

But **a dead body cannot praise God**.

Death ends our opportunity to glorify him through work and struggle **in this present world**.

Other people have died, bringing their earthly service to an end.

We should therefore strive to do more for God while we still live.

We should also encourage others to praise him, even after we ourselves are gone.

Lord, you alone deserve our **faith and love**.

Help us praise you while living and as we die.

May your name be first and last upon our lips, and may its sweetness refresh our souls forever.

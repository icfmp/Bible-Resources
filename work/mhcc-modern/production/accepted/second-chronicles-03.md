# 2 Chronicles 3 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The building of the temple.**

## The temple is constructed

The building of the temple is described more fully in **1 Kings 6**.

It had to be built on the ground David had prepared: not merely land he purchased, but the place he selected by God's direction.

Clear instructions allow us to begin work with confidence and continue it with peace.

Blessed be God, the Scriptures thoroughly equip his people for every good work.

Let us search the Bible daily, praying that the Lord will enable us to understand, believe and obey his word.

Then our path and work will become clear, and everything can be begun, continued and completed in him.

As we contemplate God through Jesus Christ, his **true Temple**, far more glorious than Solomon's, may we ourselves be formed into a **spiritual house**, a dwelling place of God through the Spirit.

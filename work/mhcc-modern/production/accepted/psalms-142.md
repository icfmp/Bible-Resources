# Psalm 142 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's comfort in prayer.**

## Bringing every trouble before God

No danger or sorrow is so severe that a believer cannot receive comfort by praying in faith.

We are often tempted to examine our troubles repeatedly in our own minds, only making them appear worse.

That accomplishes little.

How much better to **lay those troubles before the Lord**, casting our cares upon the One who cares for us!

We should never allow ourselves to complain inwardly or to other people in a way that **we would be ashamed to bring before God**.

When our spirits are overwhelmed and discouraged, when enemies have placed traps along the way we are trying to obey, we can remember that **the Lord knows our path**.

Those who sincerely receive the Lord as their God find him completely sufficient as **their Refuge and their Portion**.

Everything else is an unreliable refuge and an inheritance of little value.

David therefore prayed earnestly.

The psalm also describes the spiritual condition of believers struggling with **doubt and fear**.

They should ask the Lord to set their souls free so that they can **run eagerly in the way of his commandments**.

God delivered David from enemies stronger than himself and treated him generously.

He likewise raised **the crucified Redeemer** to the throne of glory and made him **Head over all things for the benefit of his church**.

A sinner awakened to guilt cries for help and is eventually brought to praise God among the redeemed.

All genuine believers will finally be delivered from **this evil world, sin and death**, and will praise their Savior forever.

## Those who joined David at Ziklag (verses 1–22)

This is an account of people who openly befriended and supported David while he was being persecuted.

Neither difficulty nor danger should keep a sinner from coming to the Savior or drive a believer away from the path of duty.

Those who press through such obstacles and overcome them will receive an abundant reward.

Amasai's words teach us how to express our love and loyalty to the Lord Jesus: we must belong entirely to him and be ready to stand and act on his side.

When the Spirit rules our hearts, we want to belong among Christ's people and declare ourselves on their side.

When faith and love move us to embrace Christ's cause, he receives us, employs us and advances us.

## Those who joined David at Hebron (verses 23–40)

When Christ's throne is established in a person's soul, there is—or ought to be—great joy.

There is also provision for that soul, not merely for the few days described here but for all of life and throughout eternity.

Happy are those who wisely recognize that submitting to Jesus Christ, the Son of David, is both their duty and their best interest.

They abandon whatever is incompatible with allegiance to him.

Their efforts to do good are guided by the wisdom God gives through his word, experience and observation.

If any of us lacks that wisdom, let us ask God. He gives generously to everyone without reproach, and the wisdom will be given.

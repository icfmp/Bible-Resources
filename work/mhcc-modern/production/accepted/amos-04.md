# Amos 4 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Israel is reproved. | 1–5 |
| Their impenitence shown. | 6–13 |

## Israel is reproved for exploitation and false worship (verses 1–5)

Wealth obtained through **extortion** is frequently spent on bodily pleasures and fulfilling sinful desires.

What has been acquired through oppression **cannot bring lasting satisfaction**.

How miserable are people who seek security in **religious practices that Scripture does not authorize**!

They show themselves ready to believe a lie.

We should carefully examine whether **our faith, hope and worship** have the support of **God's word**.

## Stubbornness despite repeated warnings (verses 6–13)

People whose hearts are governed by bodily desires wander from one created thing to another seeking satisfaction.

They labor for what never fulfills them.

Yet after repeated disappointments they still refuse to listen to **the One who can meet their deepest needs**.

**The preaching of the gospel is like rain**.

Where this rain is missing, everything withers.

People often search diligently for the food their bodies need.

How much better if they showed the same care for their souls and, where the gospel is absent, **went to hear it wherever it could be found**.

Israel had persisted in disobedience and idolatry.

The Lord was therefore coming against the people as **an adversary**.

Soon each of us must **meet God in judgment**.

If he examines us strictly according to our own works, we cannot stand before him.

To prepare to meet him with peace and confidence, **we must meet him now through Jesus Christ**, the Father's eternal Son, who came to save lost sinners.

Seek him while he may be found.

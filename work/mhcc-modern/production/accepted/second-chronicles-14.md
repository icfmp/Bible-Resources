# 2 Chronicles 14 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Asa's piety, He strengthens his kingdom.**

## Asa seeks God and strengthens Judah

Asa wanted to please God and sought his approval.

Happy are those who live by that rule—not doing what seems right merely in their own eyes or in the eyes of the world, but what is right **before God**.

Experience teaches us that seeking the Lord is good.

It brings **rest**, whereas chasing the world brings only distress and frustration.

Asa discussed with his people how to use the peace God had given them well.

Together they decided that peace was no excuse for laziness or careless self-confidence.

A formidable army of Ethiopians invaded his kingdom.

God permitted the trial so that Asa's faith would be tested.

His prayer was short, but expressed genuine **faith and confidence in God**.

When we go forward in God's name, we have reason to trust that he will work everything for the good of those whom he favors.

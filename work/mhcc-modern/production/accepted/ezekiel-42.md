# Ezekiel 42 — Modern English

## Original source structure

**No separate authorial heading; one continuous commentary.**

## The priests' rooms and private devotion

This chapter describes **the rooms for the priests**, their purpose, and the dimensions of the holy mountain on which the temple stood.

There were many rooms.

Jesus said that **in his Father's house are many dwelling places**.

Even in God's house on earth there are many: multitudes have found a home in his sanctuary **through faith**, and yet there is still room.

These rooms were private, but they stood near the temple.

Similarly, **our private acts of worship and prayer** should prepare us for **public worship** and help us benefit more fully from it as circumstances permit.

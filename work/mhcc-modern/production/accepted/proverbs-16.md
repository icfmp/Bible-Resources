# Proverbs 16 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — God prepares the heart

Only God's renewing grace prepares the heart for every good work. By ourselves, we lack even the ability to think or speak what is truly wise and good.

## Verse 2 — Our self-judgment is biased

Ignorance, pride and self-flattery make us partial judges of our own conduct.

## Verse 3 — Entrust your cares to God

Lay the burden of your concerns upon God, leaving it there in faith and dependence on him.

## Verse 4 — God overrules the wicked

God uses wicked people as instruments of righteous judgment upon one another; he will receive glory when final justice is done.

## Verse 5 — Pride cannot escape judgment

Even when sinners join forces to strengthen themselves, they cannot escape God's judgment.

## Verse 6 — Mercy and truth in Christ

Through God's mercy and truth revealed in **Jesus Christ**, believers' sins are removed and sin's power is broken.

## Verse 7 — God can reconcile enemies

The One who holds every human heart in his hand can move even someone's enemies to make peace.

## Verse 8 — Honest means are better

A small possession honestly acquired is more useful and blessed than a large fortune obtained by wrongdoing.

## Verse 9 — The Lord directs our steps

If we make God's glory our goal and his will our rule, he will guide our steps by his **Spirit and grace**. **The CCEL paragraph then continues with an inline “10.”**: rulers and judges on earth must act justly and govern in the fear of God.

## Verse 11 — Justice in commerce

Honesty and justice in dealings between people are appointed by God.

## Verse 12 — A ruler's true security

A ruler who exercises authority rightly finds justice to be the surest protection of that office.

## Verse 13 — Wise speech in positions of trust

Those appointed to authority should know how to speak what is appropriate and useful.

## Verses 14 and 15 — Value God's favor above royal favor

How foolish it is to seek the favor of an earthly ruler by forfeiting **the favor of God**.

## Verse 16 — The worth of wisdom

Only true wisdom provides deep joy and lasting satisfaction of spirit.

## Verse 17 — Keep far from evil

A sincerely godly person avoids even the appearance of wrongdoing. Blessed are those who walk in **Christ** and follow **his Spirit**.

## Verse 18 — Pride precedes judgment

When people defy God's judgments and think themselves beyond their reach, punishment may be close. Rather than fearing other people's pride, fear pride **within your own heart**.

## Verse 19 — Humility before God

Humility may be despised by the world, but it is infinitely better than pride that turns God into an enemy. Those who understand his word will find what is good.

## Verse 21 — True wisdom lives within

Someone whose wisdom has taken root in the heart is more genuinely prudent than people who possess dazzling natural talents alone.

## Verse 22 — Wisdom refreshes others

A wise person is to friends and neighbors like **water upon thirsty ground**.

## Verse 23 — Know yourself to speak well

A wise person's understanding of the heart helps that person discover what should be said to others.

## Verse 24 — God's word heals

The word of God is a remedy for **the diseases that weaken the soul**.

## Verse 25 — Do not deceive your soul

The warning calls everyone to take care against **self-deception regarding spiritual condition and salvation**.

## Verse 26 — Work for eternal food

We must seek the food that endures to everlasting life rather than laboring only for what perishes.

## Verses 27 and 28 — The labor of doing wrong

Ungodly people often work harder to cause harm than would be needed to do good. Those who spread malicious whispers separate close friends—an especially hateful yet common evil.

## Verses 29 and 30 — Violence blinds its servants

Some people work as much harm as they can through force and violence, while refusing to recognize where their actions lead.

## Verse 31 — Let old age be honorable

Older people especially should be found walking in the way of faith and obedience.

## Verse 32 — Self-control exceeds military strength

Governing our own passions requires steadier and more demanding discipline than winning a victory over a human enemy.

## Verse 33 — Submit to Providence

Whatever God appoints in the matters we have entrusted to him, we should receive as his wise determination. Blessed are those who submit to his will, knowing he understands what is truly good for them.

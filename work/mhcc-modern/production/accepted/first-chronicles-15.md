## Preparing to bring the ark (verses 1–24)

Even wise and godly people can make mistakes. When they become aware of them, they should correct them.

David did not try to justify what had been done wrongly or blame others.

He acknowledged that he, together with others, had failed to seek God in the **proper order**.

## The ark is brought to Jerusalem (verses 25–29)

We should recognize God's providential help even in activities that are naturally within our abilities.

Without his assistance, we could not take a single step.

Whenever we fulfill a religious duty rightly, even to a small degree, we must acknowledge that God helped us. Left to ourselves, we would have made serious errors.

Everything we undertake must be done in dependence on God's mercy **through the Redeemer's sacrifice**.

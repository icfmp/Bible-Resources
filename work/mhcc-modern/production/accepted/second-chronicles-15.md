# 2 Chronicles 15 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The people make a solemn covenant with God.**

## Asa and the people renew their covenant

Full religious reform appeared so difficult that Asa had not dared undertake it until he received assurance of **God's help and acceptance**.

He and the people offered sacrifices of **thanksgiving for past blessings** and **prayer for further mercy**.

Under the gospel, our spiritual sacrifices are **prayer and praise**.

The people voluntarily entered a covenant to seek the Lord, with each person doing so earnestly.

What is true religion if not **seeking God**—inquiring after him and turning to him in every circumstance?

Religion is worth nothing to us unless it engages our hearts.

God requires **the whole heart, or none of it**.

Our devotion to God our Savior should be acknowledged and demonstrated solemnly and openly.

Anything done in hypocrisy is merely burdensome, empty labor.

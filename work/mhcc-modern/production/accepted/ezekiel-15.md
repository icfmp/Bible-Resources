# Ezekiel 15 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Jerusalem like an unfruitful vine.**

## Jerusalem as an unfruitful vine

A vine is valuable when it produces **fruit**.

If it bears none, its wood is nearly useless and is fit only to be thrown into the fire.

In the same way, human beings are capable of producing **precious fruit by living for God**.

This is the true purpose for which we exist.

If people refuse that purpose completely, their lives produce none of the fruit they were made to bear and end in ruin.

How blind are those who live in total neglect of **God and genuine religion**!

The picture applies especially to **Jerusalem**.

Let us beware of an **outward profession of faith that bears no fruit**.

Instead, let us come to **Christ**, remain in him, and seek to have **his words remain in us**.

# Psalm 127 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The value of the Divine blessing.**

## The Lord builds, protects and provides

We must continually recognize **God's Providence**.

In every matter affecting a household, we depend upon his blessing.

Henry describes **three areas**.

**First, establishing a family.**

If we do not recognize God, we have no reason to expect his help.

Even the most carefully made plans fail unless he grants success.

**Second, protecting a family or city.**

Unless the Lord guards the city, watchmen keep watch in vain, even if they never sleep.

Danger may arise that no amount of early detection can prevent.

**Third, providing for a family's needs.**

Some people pursue money so eagerly that their continual worry makes every comfort bitter and turns life into a burden.

They labor to acquire wealth, yet all their efforts are vain unless God prospers them.

Those who love the Lord, act diligently in lawful work and **entrust their cares to him** may receive what is needed without being consumed by anxiety.

We should seek to remain in God's love and be content whether we possess little or much.

But this never excuses laziness: **we must use appropriate means diligently**.

Children are **gifts from God**, a heritage and reward.

They should be counted as blessings rather than burdens.

We can trust that the One who gives children can also provide for their needs.

Children may become a source of strength and defense for the household.

When they are young, parents can direct them like **arrows toward a target**—God's glory and the good of their generation.

Once children have gone out into the world, they are arrows released from the hand; their earlier direction can no longer be changed in the same way.

Yet arrows in the hand can also become **arrows in the heart**, bringing pain to godly parents.

When children are raised according to God's word, however, they generally offer valuable support later in life.

They remember what their parents have done for them and care for them in old age.

All earthly comforts remain uncertain.

Yet the Lord will not fail to care for those who serve him.

And those who labor for **the conversion of sinners** will find that their spiritual children are **their joy and crown at the day of Jesus Christ**.

# Psalm 122 — Modern English

## Rejoicing in the gathering of God's people (verses 1–5)

The pleasure and benefit we receive through God's **means of grace** should make us willing to endure inconvenience and tiredness in order to attend them.

We should encourage one another toward what is good.

When Christian friends undertake some worthwhile work, we should want them to invite us and include us in it.

How eagerly should we look forward to **the heavenly Jerusalem**!

How cheerfully should we bear our cross and face death when we hope to receive a **crown of glory**!

Jerusalem is called a beautiful city.

It pictured **the gospel church**, whose members are joined together through **holy love and Christian fellowship**, making one united community.

If all Christ's disciples shared one mind and maintained **the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace**, their enemies would lose many of their greatest advantages.

But Satan has always tried to **divide in order to conquer**, and too few Christians recognize his schemes.

## Praying for Jerusalem's peace (verses 6–9)

Even those who cannot do anything else for Jerusalem's peace can **pray**.

We should regard all who sincerely seek the Redeemer's glory as **brothers and sisters and fellow travelers**, without allowing differences that do not affect eternal salvation to divide us.

Blessed Spirit of peace and love, who dwelt in the soul of the holy Jesus, come upon his church and fill its people with your heavenly character.

Put an end to bitter disputes and make us united in heart.

Love for fellow believers and love for God should move us to imitate Jesus through **fervent prayer and tireless work** for human salvation and God's glory.

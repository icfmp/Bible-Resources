# 1 Chronicles 6 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Genealogies.**

## Levi's descendants

This chapter records the descendants of **Levi**.

More than any other Israelites, the priests and Levites needed to preserve and be able to prove their ancestry clearly.

The honors and privileges of their priestly service depended on that descent.

Under the gospel, however, **the Spirit of God calls ministers to service without limiting them to any particular family**.

At the same time, both then and now, ministers and believers may be very useful to the church, but **only our great High Priest, Jesus Christ, can make atonement for sin**.

No one can be accepted by God except through Christ's atoning sacrifice.

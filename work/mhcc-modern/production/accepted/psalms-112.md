# Psalm 112 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The blessedness of the righteous.**

## The blessing of trusting and fearing God

We should praise the Lord because there are people in the world who fear and serve him, and because **they are happy through his grace alone**.

Their fear is not the terror that love drives away.

It is **the reverence that love produces**: a desire not to offend the One they love.

This fear belongs together with trust.

When the Holy Spirit touches a heart, it turns quickly toward God, like **a compass needle touched by a magnet**.

It does so with loving reverence and awe.

God has stored up blessings for the faithful and their descendants.

He gives them genuine riches, together with as much earthly property as is truly beneficial to them.

Even during their darkest troubles, **hope and peace can dawn** within them, and timely help can turn grief into joy.

Their Lord teaches them to be compassionate, kind and fair in every relationship.

They use good judgment so that their generosity may **do the greatest good**.

Envy and slander may temporarily hide their true character, but they will be remembered with lasting honor.

They do not need to fear every alarming report.

Their hearts can become stable.

True believers seek to keep their thoughts fixed upon God, making their hearts calm.

He has promised both **reasons for confidence** and **grace to exercise it**.

Trusting him is the firmest foundation for the heart.

Human hearts cannot settle with complete satisfaction anywhere except in **the truth of God**.

Faith gives them a stable footing and patience to wait for the appointed outcome.

Compare this with the distress of the wicked.

They envy the happiness of God's people.

But their desires are entirely bound to worldly pleasure and the flesh.

When these perish, their joy perishes too.

The blessings of the gospel are **spiritual and everlasting**.

They belong to Christ's church through **Christ its Head**, who is the example of every righteous life and the giver of all grace.

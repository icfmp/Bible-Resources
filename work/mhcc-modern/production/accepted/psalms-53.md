# Psalm 53 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The corruption of man by nature.**

## Humanity's corruption and Christ's saving work

This psalm closely resembles **Psalm 14**.

Its purpose is to convince us of our sins.

Through David, God reveals the seriousness of human corruption and confirms it by **his perfect knowledge**.

He warns persecutors, among the worst sinners, and encourages his suffering people.

Why are human beings so wicked?

Because **they do not fear God**.

Bad behavior flows from corrupted convictions.

People may profess to know God while denying him through their **thoughts and conduct**.

Sin is foolishness.

Those who cherish such thoughts are fools in God's sight, and his judgment cannot be mistaken.

The results of sin show how far people can fall when their hearts are hardened by self-deception.

Yet the saints' faith offers hope of a cure.

**A Savior is coming**, bringing great salvation—the salvation of human beings **from sin**.

God will deliver his church from enemies and believers from their own sinful bondage, so they can never again be carried captive by sin.

That freedom will bring everlasting joy.

For this saving work the Redeemer received the name **Jesus**, because he would save his people **from their sins** (**Matthew 1:21**).

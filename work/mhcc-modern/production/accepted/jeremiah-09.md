# Jeremiah 9 — Modern English

| Original outline | Verses |

## Jeremiah mourns the people's corruption (verses 1–11)

Jeremiah wept much. Yet he wished he could weep even more, hoping to awaken the people to a proper awareness of **God's hand in their troubles**.

Even withdrawing into the desert cannot remove temptation or sin.

Without **communion with God through Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit's influence**, the wilderness would still be a place of evil.

With these blessings, however, we can live holy lives **even in crowded cities**.

The people had trained their tongues to lie. They had become so untrustworthy that even **a brother could not trust his brother**.

In trade and negotiation, they said whatever would serve their interests, even when they knew it was false.

**God noticed every falsehood.**

What good can be expected where there is **no knowledge of God**?

He has many ways of turning a fertile land into a barren place because of the wickedness of its inhabitants.

## Death reaches every place (verses 12–22)

When the people had remained near God, Zion had heard the sounds of **joy and praise**.

Now their sin had changed those sounds into **mourning**.

People whose hearts are not humbled may grieve deeply over **their suffering** while refusing to grieve over **the sin that caused it**.

No door can be closed so tightly that death cannot enter.

It reaches even the **palaces of rulers and great people**, no matter how beautifully built, strongly defended or carefully guarded.

And those outside their homes are no safer.

Death takes children in the open spaces and young men from the streets.

Listen to the Lord's word and mourn with **godly sorrow**.

That kind of sorrow alone brings true comfort.

Through it, even the heaviest suffering can become an instrument of **precious mercy**.

## True boasting is knowing the Lord (verses 23–26)

We live in a world filled with **sin and sorrow**, and earthly life will soon end in **death and judgment**.

How foolish it is to boast of our knowledge, health, strength or wealth when these things do not free us from **sin's dominion and God's judgment**!

Indeed, we will have to account for the way we used those advantages, and they may increase our guilt.

**The true Israel** consists of people who worship God **in the Spirit**, rejoice **in Christ Jesus** and put **no confidence in the flesh**.

Let us prize the distinction **given by God**, which endures forever.

And let us seek it diligently.

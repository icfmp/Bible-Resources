# 1 Chronicles 10 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The death of Saul.**

## Saul's death and the way opened for David

A major purpose of Chronicles appears to be **preserving the history of David's family**.

That is why the writer does not repeat Saul's whole reign.

He records only Saul's death, which opened the way for David to become king.

Henry draws **three lessons** from Saul's downfall.

**First**, sinners' wrongdoing will eventually catch up with them, whether sooner or later.

Saul died because of his transgression.

**Second**, no one's importance or social rank can exempt that person from God's judgments.

**Third**, disobedience brings death.

Saul died because he did not keep the Lord's word.

May we be delivered from unbelief, impatience and despair.

By waiting for the Lord, we will obtain a kingdom that cannot be shaken.

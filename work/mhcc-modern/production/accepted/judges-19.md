# Judges 19 — Modern English

## Original heading

**The wickedness of the men of Gibeah.**

## The wrongdoing at Gibeah

The final three chapters of Judges tell a deeply distressing story about the wickedness of the people of **Gibeah**, in the tribe of Benjamin.

The righteous Lord sometimes permits sinners to carry out his just judgments against one another.

If the history described here is horrifying, how much more dreadful will be the things revealed on the **day of judgment**!

Every one of us should therefore consider how to escape the coming wrath.

We should put to death the sins in our own hearts, resist Satan's temptations and keep away from the pollution of the world.

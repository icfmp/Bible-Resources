# 1 Chronicles 7 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Genealogies.**

## The missing records of Zebulun and Dan

This chapter contains no genealogical account of either **Zebulun or Dan**.

We cannot explain why only these tribes were omitted.

Yet it is a disgrace connected with Dan that idolatry began among its settlers in **Laish**, the city they renamed Dan (Judges 18).

Later, **Jeroboam** established one of his golden calves there.

Henry also notes that Dan is omitted from the tribal list in **Revelation 7**.

People become detestable when they abandon the worship of the true God in order to worship some created thing.

## David's military organization (verses 1–15)

In earthly kingdoms, being prepared for war helps preserve peace.

In the same way, **nothing encourages Satan's attacks more than spiritual carelessness**.

As long as we stand equipped with the **whole armor of God**, exercising faith and keeping our hearts ready for the struggle, we will certainly be secure and probably also enjoy peace within.

## The rulers and officers (verses 16–34)

David's officers were responsible for managing the fields, vineyards, herds and flocks that formed the wealth of kings in that part of the world.

The wisdom of rulers is often shown by their choice of ministers and officials.

Ordinary people show wisdom in choosing advisers as well.

Yet with all these counselors around him, **David preferred God's word above them all**.

“Your testimonies are my delight and my counselors.”

# Psalm 145 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| David extols the power, goodness, and mercy of the Lord. | 1–9 |
| The glory of God's kingdom, and his care of those that love him. | 10–21 |

## Thanksgiving for the Redeemer's goodness (verses 1–9)

Those who pray earnestly through troubles and temptations will eventually have abundant reason to thank God.

Grateful praise is the true language of **holy joy**.

Above all, we should celebrate **God's wonderful work of redemption** as we speak of his greatness.

Neither Israel's rescues nor the punishment of sinners reveals God's justice as clearly to an enlightened mind as **the cross of Jesus Christ**.

Everything the Lord Jesus says and does is marked by **goodness and grace**.

He is full of compassion.

That is why he came into this world to save sinners.

During his earthly life, he showed mercy to **both people's bodies and their souls**.

He healed bodily sickness and taught them heavenly wisdom.

**He is the merciful High Priest** through whom God shows mercy to sinners.

## The Lord's care and the glory of his kingdom (verses 10–21)

All God's works declare his praise.

He supplies the needs of every living creature.

Yet sinful human beings can be so dissatisfied that nothing God gives them seems enough.

The Lord does good to all humanity, while showing his people special care.

Many believers have come close to falling into sin or despair.

God has shown his goodness by **preventing their fall** or **quickly restoring them through grace and comfort**.

Everyone carrying the heavy burden of sin may come to **Christ in faith**.

He will relieve and raise them.

The Lord willingly hears and answers his people's prayers.

He is present everywhere, yet in a special way **near to those who call upon him sincerely**.

By faith he lives within their hearts, and they remain in him.

He comes near in every hour of need.

He has taught them to love his name and holy ways.

He will therefore deliver them from the destruction awaiting the unrepentant.

May we love his name, obey his ways and pray that **all humanity will praise his holy name forever**.

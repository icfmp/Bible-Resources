# Psalm 82 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| An exhortation to judges. | 1–5 |
| The doom of evil rulers. | 6–8 |

## Judges must govern for the public good (verses 1–5)

Magistrates have been entrusted with authority **for the public good**.

They serve God's Providence in maintaining **order and peace**, punishing wrongdoing and protecting people who act rightly.

God directs the decisions of rulers who desire justice and restrains those who intend evil.

We should acknowledge God's authority behind the offices and governors he permits to rule.

But when people responsible for justice twist it into wrongdoing, **no good can be expected**.

The wrong actions of public officials bring **harm upon the public**.

## Every ruler will answer to God (verses 6–8)

Human beings find it difficult to receive honor and authority without becoming proud.

Yet all earthly rulers will die.

Every title and earthly dignity will be brought down into the dust.

**God himself governs the world**.

There is a righteous God to whom we may turn and on whom we may depend.

Henry also sees these words as pointing toward **the Messiah's kingdom**.

When we look at the world's disorder, we have good reason to pray that **Jesus Christ** will soon rule openly over every nation, bringing truth, righteousness and peace.

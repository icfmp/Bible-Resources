# Jude 1 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The apostle exhorts to stedfastness in the faith. | 1–4 |
| The danger of being infected by false professors, and the dreadful punishment which shall be inflicted on them and their followers. | 5–7 |
| An awful description of these seducers and their deplorable end. | 8–16 |
| Believers cautioned against being surprised at such deceivers arising among them. | 17–23 |
| The epistle ends with an encouraging doxology, or words of praise. | 24, 25 |

## Called from sin and urged to contend for faith (verses 1–4)

Christians are called **out of the world**, away from its sinful attitudes, and **above the world**, toward heavenly and eternal realities.

They are called **from sin to Christ**, from emptiness to seriousness, and from uncleanness to holiness, according to God's purpose and grace.

If they are made holy and eventually glorified, **all credit and glory belong to God alone**.

The One who begins grace's work in the soul also carries it on and perfects it.

Do not rely on yourselves or on the amount of grace you imagine you have accumulated. **Trust God himself**.

His mercy is the source of everything good we possess or hope to receive.

Mercy helps not only miserable people but **guilty sinners**.

From this mercy comes **peace** through assurance of pardon; from peace grows **love**: Christ's love for us, our love for him and brotherly love for one another.

Jude desires not small portions of these blessings, but a rich abundance in believers' souls and fellowships.

The gospel invitation excludes no one except those who **stubbornly reject it**. Its blessings are enjoyed by believers, weak and strong alike.

Those who have received the message of common salvation must **contend earnestly, but not angrily or violently**, for its truth.

**Lying in support of truth is evil. Angry abuse does not help either**.

The apostles contended by **courageously and patiently suffering for the truth**, not by making people suffer for refusing religious opinions they regarded as important.

Oppose those who enter the church secretly, like snakes, to corrupt the faith.

Especially dangerous are those who turn the abundance of God's saving grace into **an excuse to sin more boldly**.

Grace aims to free people from sin and bring them back to God.

## Outward privilege cannot protect unrepentant sinners (verses 5–7)

Religious privileges, outward profession and apparent conversion cannot protect people who persist in unbelief and disobedience.

The Israelites who perished in the wilderness witnessed extraordinary miracles daily, yet were judged for unbelief.

The fallen angels rejected the places God assigned them; **pride** contributed fundamentally to their fall.

They await the great judgment.

If even glorious angels could not escape judgment, no human sinner should think social standing or religious distinction will provide safety.

**Sodom's destruction** warns people to flee desires that attack their souls (**1 Peter 2:11**).

God remains as pure, just and holy as ever.

**“Stand in awe, and do not sin”** (**Psalm 4:4**).

Only the Holy Spirit renewing the soul into God's likeness can preserve someone from destruction among God's enemies.

Even highly dignified creatures gain no exemption from judgment.

How then should sinful humans tremble, who **drink wrongdoing like water** (**Job 15:16**)!

## Corrupt teachers and their certain end (verses 8–16)

These false teachers are dreamers whose corrupt teaching wounds and defiles souls.

They are restless and rebellious, forgetting that lawful authorities are established under God's providence (**Romans 13:1**).

On the dispute concerning **Moses' body**, Henry **supposes** that Satan wanted the location of his grave revealed so the Israelites would be tempted to worship Moses; the plan was restrained and the devil resorted to blasphemy.

This is **Henry's conjecture**, not an independently stated fact of the biblical account.

Even during serious disagreements we must **not use slanderous or abusive accusations**.

We should defend those who belong to God.

People who reject Christianity often live in contradiction to basic principles of natural religion.

They may boast of wisdom while being compared to unreasoning animals, corrupting themselves through things that ought to be obvious.

Their chief problem is not a lack of intelligence but **corrupt desires and disordered wills**.

It is a disgrace when professing Christians deny their religion through their lives.

God will correct these evils **in his own way and time**, not through human attempts **to pull up wheat together with weeds**.

How grievous when people seem to begin in the Spirit and end in sinful flesh.

They are described as **twice dead**: first dead through fallen nature and later showing further evidence of spiritual death through hypocrisy.

They resemble barren trees ready to be burned.

Raging waves frighten people at sea but can no longer harm them once they reach harbor.

False teachers face judgment in the present world and the world to come.

They flash like meteors or falling stars, then sink into **the blackness of darkness forever**.

Jude quotes a prophecy connected with **Enoch**, a passage not recorded elsewhere in Scripture.

Henry emphasizes that **one clear biblical testimony suffices to establish a divine warning**.

The announced coming of the Lord for judgment is a solemn reality.

Notice how often **ungodly** is repeated.

Many now mock not only godliness but the very words used to distinguish it from evil.

Unjust harsh speech about others will be judged too.

People who live only to satisfy sinful appetites are seldom content; their desires and complaints become their own rule.

Those most devoted to sensual pleasure often surrender most readily to uncontrolled anger.

God's servants have long announced judgment upon this way of life.

**Follow other people only insofar as they follow Christ**.

## Keep the faith and rescue others with discernment (verses 17–23)

People governed by sensual desires separate themselves from **Christ, his church and the Holy Spirit's holiness**.

This is infinitely worse than leaving a particular visible church over secondary opinions or questions of outward government or worship.

Those lacking the Holy Spirit do **not belong to Christ**.

Saving faith is holy: it **works through love, cleanses the heart and overcomes the world**.

Such fruit distinguishes it from empty belief.

Pray **in the Holy Spirit**: under his influence and direction, according to Scripture, with faith, earnest affection and perseverance.

The hope of eternal life strengthens our resistance against sinful enticements.

Keep watch over one another, correct one another faithfully but prudently, and set good examples.

**Different people need different forms of care**.

Show tenderness and compassion toward those who are weak or confused.

Warn those who are willful and persistently reckless with the seriousness appropriate to their danger.

Every attempt to restore sinners must be united with **real hatred of sin** and a refusal to participate in works of darkness.

Avoid whatever leads into wrongdoing or even appears evil.

## God is able to keep his people and present them blameless (verses 24 and 25)

God is not only **able** to preserve believers from falling but also **willing** to do so.

He will present them without fault before his glory.

Not because they **never sinned**, but because without divine mercy and **Christ's sufferings and merits** they would rightly have been condemned.

All genuine believers were given by the Father to the Son.

**He has lost none and will lose none of them**.

Now remaining sin fills believers with fears, questions and grief.

But their Redeemer has committed himself to presenting them faultless.

Where sin no longer exists, sorrow will vanish too.

Where holiness is perfected, joy will be perfect.

Look often to the Lord who keeps us from falling, sustains and increases the work of grace begun in us, and will bring us blameless into his glorious presence.

Our joy then will exceed everything earth could give, and God's delight in his people will be complete.

**To the One who wisely planned and perfectly accomplishes our salvation belong glory, majesty, dominion and power, now and forever. Amen.**

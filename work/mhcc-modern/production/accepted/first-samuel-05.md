# 1 Samuel 5 — Modern English

| Original outline | Verses |

## Dagon falls before the ark (verses 1–5)

See how the ark of God triumphed over **Dagon**.

In the same way, Satan's kingdom will surely fall before Christ's kingdom.

Error will give way to truth, irreverence to godliness, and sinful corruption to grace within believers' hearts.

Even when true religion seems close to defeat, we can remain confident that its victory will come.

When Christ, the true Ark of the covenant, enters a fallen human heart, he enters what had been Satan's temple.

Then the idols fall.

Every attempt to restore them is vain.

Sin is abandoned, unjust gain restored and the Lord takes his rightful place upon the throne.

Yet pride, self-love and worldly desires, although overthrown and crucified, still remain within us like **Dagon's broken stump**.

We must watch and pray that they do not regain power.

Let us seek the more complete destruction of every lingering sin.

## God's judgment falls on the Philistines (verses 6–12)

The Lord's hand was heavy upon the Philistines.

He not only demonstrated the folly of their idol worship but severely corrected their insolence.

Still they would not abandon Dagon.

Rather than seek God's mercy, they tried to remove his ark.

People whose hearts are ruled by worldly desires may suffer under God's judgments and still prefer to push him away instead of entering fellowship with him and seeking his friendship.

But every plan they made to escape God's judgment only increased their suffering.

Anyone who insists on fighting against God will soon discover the cost.

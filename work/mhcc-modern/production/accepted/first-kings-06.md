# 1 Kings 6 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The building of Solomon's temple. | 1–10 |
| Promise given concerning the temple. | 11–14 |
| Particulars respecting the temple. | 15–38 |

## Solomon builds the temple (verses 1–10)

The building was called **the house of the Lord** because God himself directed its form and construction.

It was to be devoted to his service.

This made it beautiful with **the beauty of holiness**, a beauty greater than every outward ornament.

It was a temple for **the God of peace**.

Therefore no iron tool was to be heard during its construction.

Quietness and silence are fitting for religious devotion and help us carry it out.

God's work should be performed **carefully, but with little noise**.

Shouting, disorder and violence tend to obstruct rather than advance his work.

So too God's kingdom grows silently within the human heart.

Henry attaches the reference **Mark 5:27** to this image.

## God's promise and warning to Solomon (verses 11–14)

Whenever people work for God, his eye is upon them.

But God warned Solomon plainly that the great responsibility and expense of building the temple could not excuse disobedience to divine law.

Nor would building such a house protect him from judgment if he turned away from the Lord's commands.

## The spiritual meanings of the temple (verses 15–38)

Henry identifies **four realities pictured by this temple**.

**First, Christ himself is the true Temple.**

In him all the fullness of deity dwells.

All God's spiritual Israel meet in him.

Through him we approach God with confidence.

**Second, each believer is a living temple.**

The Spirit of God dwells within the believer (**1 Corinthians 3:16**).

This living sanctuary is built upon Christ as its foundation and will become perfect at the appointed time.

**Third, the gospel church is God's spiritual Temple.**

It grows into a holy temple in the Lord, enriched and beautified by the Spirit's gifts and graces.

It stands firmly upon the Rock.

**Fourth, heaven is the eternal Temple.**

There God's people will remain forever.

Everyone who will become part of that heavenly building must be prepared and fitted for it during the present life.

Let sinners come to Jesus, the living Foundation, so that they may be built upon him, made part of his spiritual house and devoted in body and soul to God's glory.

# Revelation 20 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Satan is bound for a thousand years. | 1–3 |
| The first resurrection; those are blessed that have part therein. | 4–6 |
| Satan loosed, Gog and Magog. | 7–10 |
| The last and general resurrection. | 11–15 |

## Satan is restrained (verses 1–3)

The vision uses a **figure** to show the restraint placed upon Satan himself.

Through his almighty power, **Christ will prevent the devil from misleading humanity as he has previously done**.

Christ always possesses both the power and the means to break Satan's influence.

He shuts the prison by his power and seals it by his authority.

The church will enjoy a time of peace and prosperity.

Yet **all her trials will not be over**.

## The first resurrection and the reign of the saints (verses 4–6)

The saints reign during the same period in which Satan is bound.

Those who suffer with Christ will reign with him in his **spiritual and heavenly kingdom**, reflecting his wisdom, righteousness and holiness.

Henry identifies this with **the first resurrection**, a blessing reserved for those who truly serve Christ and suffer for his sake.

Only holy people can be truly blessed, and everyone made holy will ultimately share that blessing.

We know something of **the first death**, and it is deeply frightening.

We have never directly experienced **the second death**, but it is more dreadful still: the soul's eternal separation from God.

May we never know it.

Those who share in a **spiritual resurrection** are delivered from its power.

Henry expects that after the destruction of **antichristian, idolatrous and persecuting powers**, a thousand years will come in which pure Christianity—right teaching, worship and holiness—will spread throughout the earth.

Through the almighty work of the Holy Spirit, fallen human beings will be made new.

Faith and holiness will then become as widespread as unbelief and wickedness appear to be now.

Imagine how many diseases, hardships and cruelties might disappear if all people became sincere and consistent Christians.

Public and private conflicts would cease, and happiness would increase in countless ways.

People would seek to relieve others' distress instead of increasing it.

We should **pray for those promised days** and do everything our public or private responsibilities allow to prepare the way for them.

## Satan is released and the last conflict comes (verses 7–10)

While the present world remains, Satan's power will not be altogether destroyed, even when his activity has been restrained.

As soon as he is released, he returns to deceiving the nations and stirring them to war against God's saints and servants.

Would that Christ's ministers and people were as energetic and persistent in doing good as the enemies of his kingdom are in doing evil.

But **God himself will fight the final and decisive battle** for his people.

The victory will be complete, and all its glory will belong to him.

## The final resurrection and judgment (verses 11–15)

After the events just foretold, the end approaches swiftly.

The vision mentions nothing else before **Christ appears to judge the world**.

On that great day, the Lord Jesus Christ will be seen in the majesty and terror of the Judge.

All the dead must stand before him: **small and great, young and old, low and high, poor and rich**.

No one is too insignificant to have gifts and duties to account for.

No one is important enough to escape the accounting.

The judgment includes not only those alive at Christ's return but **everyone who has died**.

A book of remembrance records good and evil.

The hidden book of each sinner's conscience will also be opened.

Actions long forgotten will be recalled.

Another book is **Scripture**, the rule by which human lives are judged.

The opening of the book of life also shows the Lord's knowledge of his people and his recognition of their **repentance, faith and good works**, revealing the blessings of the new covenant.

People will be judged **according to their works**, because their conduct reveals the principles that governed them.

Those whom the gospel has **justified and acquitted** will be recognized as justified and acquitted by the Judge.

They enter everlasting life without any further fear of death, hell or wicked enemies.

All these enemies are destroyed.

The second death is **the final separation of sinners from God**.

Our urgent concern should be to examine whether Scripture now confirms or condemns the way we live.

Christ will one day judge humanity's secrets according to the gospel.

Who could endure that judgment without his mercy?

## Editorial review — Revelation 20

**Complete original-English second witness directly read:** [CCEL, Revelation 20](https://ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhcc.lviii.xx.html). Published **MHCC 0.1.1** remains the authoritative native source pending exact keys, markup and independent readback.

- **1–3:** binding expressly **figurative**, **Christ's power and authority** restraining Satan, church peace **without ending all trials** — retained.
- **4–6:** saints' reign **spiritual/heavenly**, **first resurrection as spiritual blessing** in Henry's exposition, **second death as eternal separation**, predicted future **thousand years following antichristian powers' destruction**, Spirit's worldwide regenerating work, reduction of suffering, prayer and public/private labor — retained as Henry's eschatological reading.
- **7–10:** temporary restraint not total final destruction, Satan's renewed deception, **final victory God's alone** — retained.
- **11–15:** no intermediate events after final conflict, **all persons judged regardless of rank**, conscience and remembrance, **Scripture as rule**, repentance/faith/works **as evidence** and **gospel justification prior to final acquittal**, second death and sober appeal — retained.

**Secondary text anomaly:** CCEL reads “We know **something thing** of what the first death is.” The manuscript gives the coherent sense; the actual published **MHCC 0.1.1** must be checked before any textual correction is accepted. The original is unchanged.

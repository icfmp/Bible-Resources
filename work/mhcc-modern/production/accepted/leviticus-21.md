# Leviticus 21 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Laws concerning the priests.**

## Priestly holiness and the greater Priest

Israel's priests were appointed as pictures of Christ.

Ministers of the gospel should therefore imitate the Savior in their own lives, so that others may follow their example.

Christ was without blemish and separate from sinners.

He carried out his priestly work on earth in perfect holiness.

How serious, then, is the calling of anyone who ministers in his name!

Yet all Christians are spiritual priests.

Ministers are especially responsible for setting a good example, but the whole people of God should follow Christ.

Bodily weakness or disability, thank God, can no longer exclude anyone from serving him, enjoying his privileges or inheriting heavenly glory.

A healthy, beautiful soul may live within a physically weak or visibly impaired body.

And someone who is not suited to a particular form of ministry can still serve God joyfully through other responsibilities within the church.

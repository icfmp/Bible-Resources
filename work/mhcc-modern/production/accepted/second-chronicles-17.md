# 2 Chronicles 17 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Jehoshaphat promotes religion in Judah, His prosperity.**

## Jehoshaphat teaches the law in Judah

Jehoshaphat found widespread ignorance among his people and worked to ensure they were well taught.

In every age, the **public teaching of God's word** is the principal means of promoting true godliness.

Through it, people are taught to understand, while their consciences are awakened and directed.

This chapter also describes Jehoshaphat's prosperity.

His large army was not what stopped neighboring nations from attacking Judah.

They were restrained by **the fear of God**, which came upon them as Jehoshaphat reformed the land and established a teaching ministry.

God's appointed ordinances are a kingdom's greater strength and security than soldiers and weapons.

Scripture continually calls us to notice God's hand in every event, yet few people take that call seriously.

Let everyone make use of the abilities they possess, being faithful even in small matters.

Establish the worship of God in your homes.

Caring for a family is an important responsibility.

Why should we not teach our families from the book of the Lord's law, as Jehoshaphat taught his people?

But live consistently.

Do not recommend one thing while practicing another.

**Begin with yourselves**.

Seek the Lord God of Israel, and then invite children and servants to follow your example.

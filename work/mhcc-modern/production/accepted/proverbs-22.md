# Proverbs 22 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — Value a good reputation

We should be more careful to acquire and preserve **a good name** than to increase our wealth.

## Verse 2 — Rich and poor before God

Providence has ordered that some people are wealthy and others poor. Yet **all are sinners before God**, and the poor are as welcome as the rich at his throne of grace.

## Verse 3 — Find refuge before the storm

Faith sees the judgment approaching sinners and turns to **Jesus Christ as the sure refuge**.

## Verse 4 — Reverence produces humility

The fear of the Lord leads to humility, spiritual riches and ultimately **eternal life**.

## Verse 5 — Two very different paths

The way of sin is dangerous and distressing, while the way of duty is safe and good.

## Verse 6 — Train children toward the Savior

Raise children not along the path their fallen hearts naturally choose, but in the way they should follow. **Lead each child to know the Savior as early as possible**.

## Verse 7 — Beware unnecessary debts

The danger of debt makes it important to avoid careless borrowing and obligations. Though rich and poor differ in this life, remember that **the Lord governs the difference**.

## Verse 8 — Misused power fails

The strength and authority abused by wicked people will not last.

## Verse 9 — Compassion receives blessing

Those who seek to relieve other people's needs and suffering receive God's blessing.

## Verse 10 — Scoffing destroys peace

Profane mockers and slanderers disturb peaceful fellowship.

## Verse 11 — Sincerity and divine friendship

God becomes the Friend of those whose hearts are without deceit. This honor belongs to **all his faithful people**.

## Verse 12 — The defeat of treachery

God turns the clever plans of dishonest and treacherous people toward their own confusion.

## Verse 13 — Laziness invents a lion

The lazy person imagines a lion waiting outdoors while ignoring **the real danger of the devil—the roaring lion—and of idleness destroying the soul**.

## Verse 14 — Sexual impurity clouds judgment

Sexual wrongdoing can so darken the mind that recovery becomes exceedingly difficult.

## Verse 15 — Foolishness in the human heart

Sinful folly belongs to human nature from childhood and clings to the soul. We all need correction from **our heavenly Father**.

## Verse 16 — Responsible stewardship

We are stewards of what God entrusts to us and must use his gifts according to **his will**.

## Verses 17–21 — Listen and trust God's word

Bend the ear to God's teaching and apply the heart to it through **faith and love**. The foundation of practical religion is delighting in God and depending upon him. The sure way to understand the truth of God's word is to **live conscientiously according to it**.

## Verses 22 and 23 — Defend the poor

Those who exploit and oppress poor people do so at great danger to themselves. If others refuse to defend them, **God himself will take up their cause**.

## Verses 24 and 25 — Avoid contagious anger

Our fallen hearts contain so much dry tinder that close association with people continually throwing sparks of anger is dangerous.

## Verses 26 and 27 — Do not waste what families need

Everyone owes justice to themselves and their families. Carelessly guaranteeing debts or wasting needed possessions violates that duty.

## Verse 28 — Respect property and work diligently

Do not infringe on another person's rightful property. True diligence is rare and valuable: it can lift people to higher responsibility. Most importantly, **be diligent in God's work and in the duties of faith**.

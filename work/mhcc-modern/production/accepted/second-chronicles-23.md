# 2 Chronicles 23 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Joash crowned, and Athaliah slain.**

## Joash becomes king

Recognizing ourselves and one another as **the Lord's people** should make us earnest in carrying out our duties both toward God and toward other human beings.

In this way the change of government was accomplished successfully, and the people rejoiced.

When **the Son of David** is enthroned within a person's soul, peace and joy follow.

For the related account, see **2 Kings 11**.

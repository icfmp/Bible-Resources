# Proverbs 12 — Modern English

## Verse 1 — Love instruction

People who possess God's grace welcome instruction and correction. Those who suppress conviction behave **like unreasoning animals**.

## Verse 2 — Disguised evil remains evil

Someone who conceals selfish and corrupt plans under the outward appearance of **religion or friendship** will be condemned.

## Verse 3 — An enduring foundation

People may rise through dishonest methods, but they cannot become secure through them. Those who are rooted **in Christ by faith** are firmly established.

## Verse 4 — A wife who brings honor

A godly and prudent wife who manages her household well, performs her duties conscientiously and patiently endures hardship brings honor and comfort to her husband. A wife who does the opposite causes him distress and drains his strength.

## Verse 5 — God knows our thoughts

Thoughts are not beyond moral accountability. God knows them, and they are therefore subject to his commands. **Scheming and deceit** bring shame.

## Verse 6 — Words can wound or help

Wicked people use their words to injure neighbors. Yet even **one good word** can sometimes accomplish a valuable work.

## Verse 7 — The households of the righteous

God's blessing often continues within the families of godly people even when the wicked are brought down.

## Verse 8 — Wisdom in suffering disgrace

The apostles displayed wisdom by **rejoicing to suffer dishonor for Christ's name**.

## Verse 9 — Humble sufficiency

Someone who works for daily bread without servants is happier than a person boasting of noble ancestry or fine clothing while lacking life's necessities.

## Verse 10 — Kindness to animals

A godly person does not impose **unnecessary pain even on an animal**. Wicked people may describe as merciful treatment that they themselves could not bear for a single day.

## Verse 11 — Attend to honest work

It is wise to work diligently at an honest occupation. Neglecting ordinary responsibilities is folly; **God's grace teaches us to despise nothing except sin**.

## Verse 12 — Do not envy unlawful gains

When wicked people see others prospering through sin, they may wish to imitate them. But **God's grace rooted in the righteous heart** produces different desires and aims.

## Verse 13 — The costly misuse of speech

Many people have paid dearly, even during this life, for sins committed with their lips.

## Verse 14 — Speech that serves others

People who rightly use their words to instruct and comfort others enjoy **acceptance through Jesus Christ** and the conscience's encouragement that they have, in some measure, fulfilled their purpose.

## Verse 15 — The fool's own standard

In Scripture the fool is a wicked person who rejects wisdom from above. The rule of life becomes **whatever is right in that person's own eyes**.

## Verse 16 — Restrain anger

A fool becomes angry quickly and displays it recklessly, thereby inviting trouble. We treat ourselves kindly when we **overlook insults** rather than making the worst of them.

## Verse 17 — Be ruled by truth

Everyone should regard lying with abhorrence and be guided by honest speech.

## Verse 18 — A healing tongue

Whispered accusations and unfounded suspicions cut like swords, separating even close friends. But **the speech of wise people brings healing**.

## Verse 19 — Truth endures

Truth remains firm and valid even when people dislike hearing it.

## Verse 20 — Peace brings joy

Deceit and lies bring terror and confusion. Those who seek others' peace and happiness find **joy in their own hearts**.

## Verse 21 — The security of the righteous

God has promised protection to those who are genuinely righteous. But people who delight in causing harm will experience plenty of harm themselves.

## Verse 22 — Truthful words and conduct

Make honesty a matter of conscience **in actions as well as words**.

## Verse 23 — Folly announces itself

Foolish people publicly expose the emptiness and folly of their own thinking.

## Verse 24 — Diligence is honorable

People who avoid honest work and try to survive through tricks and dishonesty reduce themselves to a miserable condition.

## Verse 25 — Encouragement in anxiety

Worry, fear and sorrow weaken people's strength for duty and courage in suffering. **A good word from God**, received through faith, restores joy to the heart.

## Verse 26 — Real riches in the Spirit

Righteous people possess abundance even when they lack worldly wealth: **the Spirit's graces and comforts** are true riches. Wicked people falsely assure themselves their chosen way is harmless.

## Verse 27 — Use opportunities well

Lazy people waste the advantages Providence has given and receive no comfort from them. The diligent person's possessions, even if modest, bless that person and the family and are received gratefully as **God's answer to prayer**.

## Verse 28 — Life on the path

True religion leads along a straight and clearly marked way of righteousness. **Life is found not only at the end of the path but along it**, through genuine spiritual comfort.

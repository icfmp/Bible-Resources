# 2 Samuel 22 — Modern English

## Original heading

**David's psalm of thanksgiving.**

## Praise for deliverance

This chapter is a psalm of praise.

Almost the same song appears later as **Psalm 18**.

Those who trust the Lord while walking in the path of duty will find him ready to help them in their greatest dangers.

David experienced this personally.

When God preserves us in a remarkable way, we should remember those particular mercies and mention them specifically when praising him.

We will not be completely delivered from every enemy until we reach heaven.

But God will preserve all his people until then (**2 Timothy 4:18**).

People who have received great mercy from God should give the glory to him.

David sang this song **on the very day** when the Lord delivered him.

While the mercy is still fresh and we are deeply moved by it, we should bring our offering of thanks.

The warmth of our gratitude can kindle the offering.

David's hopes and joys finally centered upon the **great Redeemer**.

All our own hopes should end in him as well.

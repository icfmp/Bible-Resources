# Hosea 8 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| Destruction threatened for the impiety of Israel. | 1–4 |
| For their idolatry. | 5–10 |
| Further threatenings for the same sins. | 11–14 |

## Israel's empty claim to know God (verses 1–4)

When Israel faced disaster, the people would claim to know God and ask for his protection.

But **such words would be disregarded**.

What good is it to say **“My God, I know you”** if we cannot also say **“My God, I love you, serve you and cling to you alone”**?

## Idolatry produces a barren harvest (verses 5–10)

By worshiping idols, the people promised themselves **abundance, peace and military success**.

Those expectations would come to nothing.

They sowed seed that produced **no stalk or blade**.

Even if a shoot appeared, it would produce **no grain**.

The works of darkness are barren, and their final outcome is **death**.

The hopes of sinners will deceive them, while the gains they seek become **traps**.

In times of danger—and especially at the final judgment—every merely human or worldly strategy will fail.

The people chose to go their own way.

Like **a solitary wild donkey**, they became easier prey for a lion.

Human beings most resemble an untamed donkey when they seek from **created things** the help and fulfillment that come **only from God**.

Sinners may experience some sorrow.

But if it is not **godly sorrow leading to repentance**, it will end in lasting grief.

## Corrupt worship cannot make up for disobedience (verses 11–14)

To corrupt the worship of God is a serious sin, regardless of how reasonable the excuses may appear.

The Lord had caused his law to be written down for Israel.

Yet the people neither wanted to know it nor intended to obey.

They built temples as though they remembered their Creator, but **had actually forgotten him** because they no longer feared him.

No one has hardened the heart against God and ultimately prospered.

As long as people despise **the truths, commands and appointed worship of Scripture**, elaborate and costly ceremonies of their own invention only increase their guilt.

Only worship performed **according to God's word and through Jesus Christ** is acceptable to him.

# Ezekiel 41 — Modern English

## Original source structure

**No separate authorial heading; one short, continuous commentary.**

## From the courts into the temple

After Ezekiel had examined the **courts**, he was brought **inside the temple**.

When we pay careful attention to the **clearer parts of religion**, follow their instructions and benefit from them, we will be led into a deeper understanding of **the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven**.

# 2 Chronicles 18 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Jehoshaphat's alliance with Ahab.**

## Jehoshaphat's alliance and rescue

This history has already been recorded in **1 Kings 22**.

Great wealth and honor offer many opportunities to do good, but they also carry many traps and temptations.

Those who eagerly seek riches in the belief that wealth will enable them to accomplish good often fail to understand **Satan's schemes and the deceitfulness of their own hearts**.

Who can harm those whom God protects?

Who can shelter those whom God has decided to judge?

Jehoshaphat remained safe **in his royal robes**, while Ahab was killed **despite his armor**.

The race does not belong automatically to the swift, nor the battle to the strong.

We should beware of becoming entangled in the worldly projects of wicked people, and be even more careful never to join their sinful undertakings.

Nevertheless, God can deliver his faithful people even from dangers they brought upon themselves through sinful choices, when they cry to him.

All hearts are in his hand, and he can rescue them easily.

Blessed is the person who trusts in the Lord.

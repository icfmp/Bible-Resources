# 1 John 3 — Modern English

## Original chapter outline

| Original outline | Verses |
| --- | --- |
| The apostle admires the love of God in making believers his children. | 1, 2 |
| The purifying influence of the hope of seeing Christ, and the danger of pretending to this, and living in sin. | 3–10 |
| Love to the brethren is the character of real Christians. | 11–15 |
| That love described by its actings. | 16–21 |
| The advantage of faith, love, and obedience. | 22–24 |

## The astonishing privilege of being God's children (verses 1 and 2)

The world understands very little of the happiness of Christ's genuine followers.

It scarcely imagines that humble, poor and despised believers are **God's favored children** and will dwell with him in heaven.

Let Christians endure earthly hardships contentedly. They live in a land of strangers, where **their Lord was mistreated before them**.

God's children must **walk by faith and live through hope**, looking forward to the return of Jesus Christ.

At his appearing, they will be recognized by their resemblance to their Head.

**Seeing Christ will transform them into his likeness**.

## Hope in Christ requires abandoning sin (verses 3–10)

The Lord is too holy to allow anything unclean or wicked to dwell in his presence.

A hope of heaven that makes room for indulgence in sinful desires is **the hope of hypocrites, not of God's children**.

Those loved and adopted by God should express gratitude through humble and willing obedience.

Sin means rejecting the divine law.

**Christ had no sin whatsoever**.

He took the human weaknesses that expose us to **suffering and temptation**, but never our **moral corruption or inclination toward sin**.

Anyone who abides in Christ does not continue in a settled practice of sin.

Abandoning sinful behavior is a principal evidence of genuine union with Jesus and saving knowledge of him.

Be alert against self-deception.

Righteous conduct shows the work of righteousness within.

A person cannot deliberately act like the devil while genuinely living as a disciple of the Son who came **to destroy the devil's works**.

New birth is the **Holy Spirit's inward renewal**. True religion is not merely a skill acquired through training; it is **a new nature**.

The regenerated person cannot continue sinning in the same unrepentant manner as before.

The mind sees sin's evil. The heart develops an aversion to it. A new spiritual principle opposes sinful actions. When sin nevertheless occurs, **genuine repentance follows**.

Deliberate, settled sin becomes repugnant to the believer.

God's children and the devil's children reveal different characters.

Those rejecting true religion and hating God's people show their allegiance by these actions.

The person justified through faith is also **taught and inclined toward righteousness by the Spirit**.

Let everyone who professes Christianity examine themselves by these marks.

## Love for believers shows the passage from death to life (verses 11–15)

Love Jesus Christ, recognize the greatness of his love and therefore love **all believers who belong to him**.

Such love is a special fruit of faith and a reliable sign of new birth.

Human nature's corruption explains why ungodly people often despise and oppose God's children.

But love for our Christian family helps us recognize that we have **passed from death to life**.

This is not merely loyalty to a religious **party**, or affection toward people who share our outward label or opinions.

It is love arising from **the grace of God active in a renewed heart**.

That life of grace is the beginning of everlasting glory.

People who deliberately hate other believers show that this new life is missing.

## Christ's blood calls us to practical compassion (verses 16–21)

What astonishing love: **God redeemed the church with his own blood**.

Surely we should love the people whom he has loved so deeply.

The Holy Spirit is grieved by selfishness, and a selfish heart is left without true comfort, in darkness and terror.

How can a person claim to understand Christ's love for perishing sinners while valuing worldly goods above the suffering of a brother or sister?

Each act of selfishness weakens the evidence that a person has been converted.

Habitual and deliberate refusal to help others decisively contradicts the profession of a changed heart.

If conscience rightly condemns a known sin or neglected duty, God's judgment also condemns the wrong.

Therefore **teach conscience well, listen to it and take it seriously**.

## Confidence before God through faith and love (verses 22–24)

Through **the Spirit of adoption** and faith in our great High Priest, believers may approach **their reconciled Father** confidently.

They may ask freely, and God grants what is good for them.

Heaven proclaimed goodwill to humanity; likewise those approaching heaven must have goodwill toward people, especially other believers.

Anyone following Christ in this way abides in him as **a refuge, ark and resting place**, and through him abides in the Father.

Union between Christ and believers comes through **the Spirit he has given**.

Faith takes hold of God's promises and leads the mind to consider them.

The Spirit changes the heart, transferring a person from **Satan's power to God's**.

Do you long for peace with God more than worldly profit, pleasure or status?

Nothing should hold you back from following Christ.

Our confidence rests on **God's own testimony through the Spirit**.

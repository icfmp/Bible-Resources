# Leviticus 12 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Ceremonial purification.**

## Purification after childbirth

After the laws concerning clean and unclean food come laws concerning people who were considered ceremonially clean or unclean.

Henry understands these regulations in light of the fallen condition inherited by every child.

Human beings pass their corrupt nature on to their descendants.

Apart from the atonement of Christ and the sanctifying work of the Holy Spirit, even the original blessing to increase and multiply (Genesis 1:28) has become bound up with the transmission of sin and misery throughout the fallen human family.

Women who have received God's mercy in childbirth should respond with heartfelt gratitude for his goodness.

Such thanksgiving pleases him more than sacrifices.

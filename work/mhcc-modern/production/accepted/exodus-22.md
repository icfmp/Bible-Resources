# Exodus 22 — Modern English

## Original heading

**Judicial laws.**

## The spirit of these laws

God's people should always be ready to show gentleness and mercy, according to the spirit of the laws given here.

We must answer to God not only for the wrong we do deliberately, out of malice, but also for harm caused by our carelessness.

Therefore, whenever we have harmed our neighbor, we should make restitution, even when no human law forces us to do so.

Let these Scriptures remind us what God's grace does in those to whom it has truly appeared.

Grace teaches us and, by its holy power, enables us to turn away from ungodliness and worldly desires.

It teaches us to live with self-control, righteousness and devotion to God in this present world (Titus 2:12).

God's grace also teaches us that the Lord himself is our portion.

In him there is more than enough to satisfy every desire of our souls.

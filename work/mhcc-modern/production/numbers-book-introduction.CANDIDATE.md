# Numbers — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

This book is called *Numbers* because it records several censuses of the people of Israel.

Its account extends from the giving of the law at Sinai until Israel reached the plains beside the Jordan River.

It tells how the people complained and refused to believe, and how they were therefore sentenced to wander in the wilderness for nearly forty years.

The book also contains laws, some moral and others ceremonial.

The people's trials helped reveal the difference between wicked people and hypocrites, on the one hand, and God's true and faithful servants, who worshiped him sincerely, on the other.

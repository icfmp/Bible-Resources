# Isaiah — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Isaiah prophesied during the reigns of **Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah**.

He has rightly been called **the evangelical prophet**, because he speaks frequently and at length about the **Messiah**—his coming and character, his ministry and preaching, his suffering and death, and the extent and lasting nature of his kingdom.

Behind the promised deliverance from **Babylon**, Isaiah also reveals a far greater deliverance to be accomplished by the Messiah.

He seldom speaks of the nearer rescue without also pointing toward the greater one. Sometimes he becomes so absorbed in the later deliverance that he seems to leave the nearer event behind, speaking at length about the Messiah's **person, work, character and kingdom**.

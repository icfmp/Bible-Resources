# Lamentations — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Henry considers **Jeremiah** to be the clear author of the Lamentations traditionally associated with his name.

He dates the book **after the Chaldeans destroyed Jerusalem**.

May it teach us to recognize **sin as the cause of our calamities**, and amid trials to practice **submission, repentance, faith and prayer**, trusting in the promised deliverance that comes through God's mercy.

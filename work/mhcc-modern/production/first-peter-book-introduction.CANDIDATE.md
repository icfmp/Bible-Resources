# 1 Peter — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Peter's letter applies the same great doctrines found in Paul's letters to **the practical life of believers**.

It is especially marked by **gentleness, tender affection and humble love**.

Though brief, it provides a clear summary of the comfort and instruction Christians need on their journey toward heaven.

It directs their thoughts and desires toward eternal happiness, and strengthens them against **sinful corruption within and temptation and suffering from outside**.

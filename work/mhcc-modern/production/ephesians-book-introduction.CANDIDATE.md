# Ephesians — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Paul wrote this letter while **a prisoner in Rome**.

Its purpose appears to have been **to strengthen the Ephesians in their faith in Christ**.

He wanted them to recognize the greatness of **God's love** and the dignity and excellence of **Jesus Christ**, so they would not be shaken by the apparent disgrace of his cross.

Paul taught that they had been **saved by grace**.

Although they had once lived in a wretched condition, they now enjoyed **the same privileges as Jewish believers**.

He encouraged them to persevere in their Christian calling and live in a way that agreed with their profession.

That involved faithfully performing both **the common duties of Christian living** and the **particular duties arising from family and social relationships**.

# MHCC Modern English — Job 9–17 focused fidelity continuation

**Date:** 2026-11-19. **Status:** draft editorial proposals only, not independent original-native or release approval.

## Method

Read the complete online original-English [CCEL *Concise Commentary*](https://ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhcc.xix.html) units for Job 9–17, in their printed verse scopes, against the corresponding existing modern English manuscripts. Compare source claims, speaker attribution, judgment vocabulary, divine agency and historical referents. The published exact MHCC 0.1.1 native K source remains authoritative; CCEL is only a **second digital witness**.

| Chapter | Focused review result |
| --- | --- |
| Job 9 | No new substantive correction. |
| Job 10 | No new substantive correction. |
| Job 11 | No new substantive correction. |
| Job 12 | **Draft correction:** the earlier generic *human malice* erased Henry's explicit historical attribution concerning the crucifixion. The revision **quotes and attributes** his historical phrase instead of presenting collective culpability as independently verified. Explicit editorial decision on historical framing still required. |
| Job 13 | **Draft correction:** recover original **righteous vengeance** affecting both body and soul, previously rendered as the much broader *righteous sentence*. |
| Job 14 | **Draft correction:** retain Henry's active **God may send disease and pain**, rather than merely permitting them. |
| Job 15 | No new substantive correction. |
| Job 16 | No new substantive correction. |
| Job 17 | **Draft correction:** recover the exact threefold agency statement **from God, from enemies and from friends**, replacing the weaker *through God's permission*. |

All four amendments have dated, local editorial notes. The historical attribution in Job 12 is **preserved for source-fidelity review, not endorsed as a claim about every Jewish person**; its publication is expressly **not authorized** without an editorial decision.

## Native source follow-through

Four exact original compiled K scopes and their hashes for Job 12:12–25, Job 13:23–28, Job 14:16–22 and Job 17:1–9 are now added to [JOB_NATIVE_FIDELITY_SOURCE_ANCHORS_2026-11-19.json](JOB_NATIVE_FIDELITY_SOURCE_ANCHORS_2026-11-19.json). They use the successful full-source receipt's real K boundaries; short lexical anchors were independently read from the public CCEL pages. The existing source-neutral private native SWORD probe runs against **the released, SHA-pinned MHCC 0.1.1 ZIP**, not against modern manuscript self-assertions. It emits only metadata/boolean anchor results and does **not** confer passage-level semantic approval.

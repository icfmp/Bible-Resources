# Hosea — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Henry considers it **probable** that Hosea came from **the kingdom of Israel**.

Hosea lived and prophesied over **a long period**.

His prophecies sought to **expose, rebuke and convince** the Jewish people in general—and the Israelites in particular—of their many sins, especially **idolatry**.

He also drew attention to the **corruption of the kingdom**.

Yet Hosea invited the people to **repent**, promising mercy and announcing future gospel blessings.

Henry understands these promises to concern the restoration of **Israel and Judah** and their eventual **conversion to Christianity**.

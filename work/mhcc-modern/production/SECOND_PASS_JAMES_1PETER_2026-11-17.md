## Original introductions

Two distinct candidate prefaces are present and have been read alongside the complete authorial secondary witness:

- [James book introduction](james-book-introduction.CANDIDATE.md): the letter's practical duties and compatibility with Paul's teaching on justification, God's grace and good works are retained. [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhcc.li.html).
- [1 Peter book introduction](first-peter-book-introduction.CANDIDATE.md): Henry's tenderness, practical application of Paul's doctrines and encouragement for the heavenly journey are retained. [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhcc.lii.html).

**Both introductions remain unplaced candidates** until the exact original SWORD 0.1.1 book-level I/K slots are positively established. They are not silent additions to chapter 1.

## Meaning/provenance fidelity observations

- **Never hide important authorial interpretations** behind euphemisms merely because they are theologically stern or historically controversial. Where Henry interprets Noah's imprisoned spirits as damned with no redemption, carry that *as Henry's interpretation*, not as a newly established original biblical statement.
- **Do not insert independent editorial glosses directly into translated authorial prose.** The previous James 5 parenthetical explanation of Henry's six-thousand-year historical reckoning was removed from the translated body and recorded in editorial notes. The original author's figure remains unchanged.
- **Source-author distinctions matter:** James 5 historically attributes responsibility for the crucifixion in collective language. Preserve a faithfully attributed record of Henry's writing rather than silently deleting it or presenting inherited guilt as a doctrine accepted by the editorial team.

## Still required

Do not present the ten chapters as *fully editorially verified*: primary-source **exact native** prose and XML for every linked verse entry, separate book/chapter I bodies, cross-references, annotations, authorial paragraph structures, possible missing subsidiary clauses and independent editorial signoff remain unverified.

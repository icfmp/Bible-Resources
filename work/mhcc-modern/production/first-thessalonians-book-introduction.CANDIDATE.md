# 1 Thessalonians — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

This letter is **generally regarded as the earliest of Paul's epistles**.

Henry says it **appears** to have been written following encouraging news that the church in Thessalonica remained steadfast in faith in the gospel.

The letter is marked by **affection and confidence**.

Compared with some other Pauline letters, it offers more **comfort and practical instruction** and less extended doctrinal discussion.

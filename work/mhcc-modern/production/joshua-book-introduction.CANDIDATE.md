# Joshua — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

The book of Joshua tells how Israel entered Canaan, conquered the land and divided it among the tribes under Joshua's leadership.

It also records the people's history until Joshua's death.

The book wonderfully reveals God's power and faithfulness in fulfilling his promises to Israel.

It also describes his carrying out the judgments he had justly threatened against the Canaanites.

These events should teach us to take seriously the fearful warnings God's word gives to sinners who refuse to repent.

They should also encourage us to seek refuge in Jesus Christ.

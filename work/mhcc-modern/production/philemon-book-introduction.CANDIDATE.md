# Philemon — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Philemon lived in **Colossae**. Henry describes him as a man of some wealth and standing who had come to faith through Paul's ministry.

**Onesimus was enslaved to Philemon.** He had fled, reached **Rome** and there became a Christian through Paul's preaching.

Henry understands Paul to have kept Onesimus with him long enough to see evidence of genuine conversion.

Onesimus wanted to repair the harm done through his departure, yet feared the punishment that could follow. He therefore asked Paul to write on his behalf.

Henry praises Paul's letter as **particularly compelling and beautiful in its reasoning and appeals**.

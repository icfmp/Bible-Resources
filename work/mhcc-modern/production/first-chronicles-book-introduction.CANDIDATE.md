# 1 Chronicles — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

The two books of Chronicles repeat much of the history found in Samuel and Kings.

Yet they also contain excellent and useful details that are recorded nowhere else.

**The first book** traces the Jewish people from Adam and then recounts the reign of David.

**The second book** continues the history of Judah's kingdom through its progress and decline, and also records the return of the Jews from Babylonian captivity.

Jerome said that people who think they understand the Scriptures without knowing Chronicles deceive themselves.

These books preserve events passed over in other accounts, genealogical names and connections between passages.

They also help explain many questions concerning the gospel.

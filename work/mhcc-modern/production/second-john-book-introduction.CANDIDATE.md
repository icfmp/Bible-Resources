# 2 John — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

This short letter is much like **a condensed form of John's first letter**. It treats the same central truths in fewer words.

The apostle commends **the elect lady** for giving her children a virtuous and godly upbringing.

He urges her to remain in **Christ's doctrine**, persevere in the truth and carefully avoid false teachers.

Above all, he appeals to her to practice the great command of **Christian love and charity**.

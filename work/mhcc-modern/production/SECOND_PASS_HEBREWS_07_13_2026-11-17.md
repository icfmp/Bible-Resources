## Exact source-fidelity corrections, reversible in the manuscript's own Git history

1. **Hebrews 8:7–13:** restored the original statement of eternal misery for relying on worldly vanity or empty religion, not just the draft's generic loss of hope.
2. **Hebrews 9:23–28:** restored the original eternal punishment warning without adding an unsupported *deliberately* restriction.
3. **Hebrews 11:32–38:** reintroduced the otherwise omitted roaring lion as an example of the danger overcome through faith.
4. **Hebrews 11:39–40:** restored the *entire missing final application*, not merely the existence of an end section: greater obedience of faith, patience of hope and labor of love proportionate to gospel advantages; the former saints' testimony at the last judgment; prayer for stronger faith; perfected holiness/happiness and shining like the sun in the Father's kingdom.

These edits make **no changes to original MHCC 0.1.1**. Review notes in each corrected modern file identify the source witness. No artificially invented original K subdivisions were created.

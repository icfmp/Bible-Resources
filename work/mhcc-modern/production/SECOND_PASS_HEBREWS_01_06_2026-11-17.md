### Reversible changes, all in the unshipped modern derivative

1. **Hebrews 1:4–14:** restored the claim that the present world will no longer be **abused** when Christ folds it away; the already-present imagery of Christ's unchanging sovereignty remains.
2. **Hebrews 1:4–14:** restored Henry's explicit warning of **everlasting misery** for those setting their hopes on perishing treasures.
3. **Hebrews 2:10–13:** restored the shared **heavenly Father** wording, not merely membership in God's family.
4. **Hebrews 4:11–16:** restored the theological point that Christ's **purity entails his unwillingness to sin**, making him compassionate toward his people's deliverance.
5. **Hebrews 6:1–8:** restored the explicit **everlasting misery** statement in the author's caution, rather than the draft's vague endangerment.

Each correction is also noted in the affected manuscript's editorial review section, with direct secondary-witness source. No original SWORD text, canonical Scripture, verse key, introductory unit, citation or source-authorial paragraph division was changed.

### Book introduction

The separate [Hebrews introduction candidate](hebrews-book-introduction.CANDIDATE.md) was compared with the [full CCEL book introduction](https://ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhcc.l.html). Its key points remain: the ceremonies only pictured Christ; his true deity and sufficient sacrifice; the converted Hebrew audience and false attempt to return to Moses; the qualified principal aim of perseverance; and the harmony of both Testaments in Christ. The native book-intro placement and possible original annotations still require exact original SWORD verification.

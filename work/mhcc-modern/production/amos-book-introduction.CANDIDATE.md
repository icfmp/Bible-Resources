# Amos — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Amos was a herdsman and also worked in agriculture.

The **same Holy Spirit** guided Isaiah and Daniel in royal courts and Amos among the flocks, giving each person the ability and eloquence necessary for the assigned task.

Amos warned **the twelve tribes** that the nations around them would be destroyed. Since the Israelites themselves had become devoted to wickedness and idolatry, he rebuked the people severely.

Yet he also spoke of **the church's restoration through the Messiah**, extending into the latter days.

# 2 Peter — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

This letter is closely related to **Peter's first letter**.

Having described the blessings to which God calls Christians, the apostle urges those who have received these gifts to **grow in grace and virtue**.

He warns against **false teachers**, deceivers and people who mock the promised return of Christ.

He refutes their claims and explains why the great day of Christ's coming has been delayed.

The letter describes the solemn circumstances and consequences of that day, and calls believers to **diligence, steadfast faith and holy living**.

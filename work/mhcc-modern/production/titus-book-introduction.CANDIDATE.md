# Titus — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Paul's letter gives Titus instructions concerning **elders in the church** and the manner in which he should teach and exhort the congregation.

Later it directs him to encourage **obedience to civil authorities**, insist upon **good works**, avoid foolish disputes and reject heretical teaching.

The instructions are **clear and straightforward**. Christianity was not designed to serve worldly ambition or selfish purposes: it is **God's wisdom and God's power**.

### B. One *printed-source* reference discrepancy — distinguish from digital encoding

**Numbers 29:1, source K `Num.29.1`, original ordinal 1**. The historical concise-commentary text itself reads **“Le 22:24”** after its sentence about the institution of the Feast of Trumpets. The pinned original source digital target is **`Lev.22.24`**, so this is **not** a case where only the hyperlink target disagrees with the printed source. CCEL's independent Concise witness also prints Leviticus **22:24**. However, the relevant biblical command is in **Leviticus 23:24**, and Matthew Henry's **complete** commentary on Numbers 29 refers to the correct chapter **23**. See:

- [CCEL concise witness, Numbers 29](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhcc.v.xxix.html)
- [Full Commentary, Numbers 29](https://ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhc2/MHC04029.HTM)
- [Leviticus 23:24 biblical text](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+23%3A24)

This is a **proposed correction to a printed source-edition reference** (likely an inherited typographical mistake), **not** merely a broken digital hyperlink. Preserve the existing printed label/target; seek explicit editorial approval for a separately documented derivative-edition correction. Do not count it as a successfully linked modern citation.

**Editorial fidelity defect discovered:** In Matthew 3:7–12 the original source makes **two** distinct assertions about the Spirit: (1) extraordinary gifts granted to the apostles and (2) ongoing graces and comforts of the Spirit for those who ask. The current modern manuscript retains the **first** but omits the explicit **second** assertion, as well as the corresponding four source citations (Luke 11:13; John 7:38–39; Acts 11:16). The omitted original passage is independently supported by [Matthew Henry's complete commentary on Matthew 3](https://ccel.org/h/henry/mhc2/MHC40003.HTM) and the [Concise witness](https://reformed.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/MatthewHenrysBibleCommentary.pdf). **Do not manufacture links to a sentence that is absent from the modern manuscript.** Draft a faithful, source-audited modern restoration of the omitted meaning and citation block, with user editorial approval, before changing any accepted manuscript.

Other missing explicit citations may be restored as separately tracked source-reference annotations attached to precise modern paragraphs; do not invent Matthew Henry's original hyperlink span or silently change the translated prose.

1. Preserve the Son's creative agency as *made by the Son* instead of weakening original *by* to *through* (1–2).
2. Restore the precisely scoped **earth-from-waters** heading and the conditional “share in the Fountain” relationship (6–13).
3. Restore the **birds / little children** image (29–30).
4. Restore the author's exact choice of **bless** for the creation and human response (31).
5. In the separately owned **Genesis book introduction**, preserve the author's singular “most ancient surviving book” assertion (rather than plural “oldest books”), and distinguish his ancient **pagan** authors from a generic modern non-Jewish category. These are **Henry's claims**, not an independently established endorsement of the historical argument.

## Original edition headings and markup — separate incomplete work

Source commentary has an original **eight-row chapter outline** plus
original title/heading/table wrappers. Draft `##` headings are modernized
*editorial* renderings of the corresponding meanings; they must **not be
misattributed as Henry's verbatim published words**. The final structured
derivative must retain the original outline's role, source-to-new provenance,
and native key/range rather than blindly dropping the table or duplicating
it at each repeated verse-key address.

Native metadata of `Gen.1.1` includes 8 `table` and 8 `row` elements,
three `title` elements and 16 `cell` elements, representing this
original outline and associated wrappers. `Gen.1.3` and `Gen.1.26`
each have one native `reference` element. The source also contains
distinct `Gen` (book) and `Gen.1` (chapter) I keys, which must be
handled separately. The experimental OSIS preview currently declares
**original introductions and source references not preserved**; a
successful SWORD build alone cannot satisfy this gate.

# Philippians — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

The Philippian believers cared deeply about **the apostle Paul**.

Paul wrote to **strengthen their faith**, encourage them to live in a manner worthy of Christ's gospel, warn them against **teachers who wanted to impose Jewish practices**, and express gratitude for their **generosity and Christian kindness**.

Henry describes this as **the only one of Paul's letters containing no explicit or implied rebuke**.

Instead, every part displays Paul's **praise and confidence** in the Philippians.

He addresses them with a particular warmth and affection that any attentive reader can recognize.

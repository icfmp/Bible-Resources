# 1 Timothy — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

Henry understands the purpose of this letter to be Paul instructing **Timothy, whom he had left at Ephesus**, in the selection of suitable **church officers** and the faithful administration of an orderly **gospel ministry**.

Paul also warned about **false teachers**, whose clever distinctions and endless disputes corrupted the gospel's purity and simplicity.

He urged Timothy to devote himself to this responsibility with **diligence, faithfulness and zeal**.

These themes occupy **the first four chapters**.

**Chapter 5** gives directions concerning particular groups of people in the church.

The last chapter condemns **quarrelling and contentious speculation**, exposes the danger of **loving money** and calls wealthy people to **good works**.

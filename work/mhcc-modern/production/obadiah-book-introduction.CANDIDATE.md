# Obadiah — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

The first part of this prophecy announces **the destruction of Edom**, concentrating on the injuries that the Edomites had inflicted upon the Jewish people.

The second part predicts **the restoration of the Jews** and **the church's later glory**.

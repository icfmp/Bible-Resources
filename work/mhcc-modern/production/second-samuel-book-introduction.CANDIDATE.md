# 2 Samuel — Book introduction (Modern English production candidate)

This book records the reign of King David.

It describes his victories, Israel's increasing prosperity and his efforts to reform the nation's religious life.

It also records the serious sins David committed and the troubles that came upon his family and his nation as punishment.

Many events in this history deserve to be imitated, while others were written to warn us.

Scripture gives a truthful account of David's reign rather than concealing his failures.

Anyone who weighs his many virtues and excellent qualities fairly alongside his faults will recognize him as a great and godly man.

# MHCC Modern English — Job 33–42 secondary-witness fidelity continuation

**Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Branch:** `feature/mhcc-modern-english`; draft PR #272.  
**Scope:** ten modern manuscripts, Job 33–42, following [Job 18–32](JOB_18_32_FIDELITY_REVIEW_2026-11-19.md).

## Method and limitations

All ten full chapter expositions and their outlines/authorial sections were reread against the corresponding public [CCEL Matthew Henry *Concise Commentary*](https://ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhcc.xix.html) English text. Specific original-wording losses were repaired **only in draft modern chapters**, with the previous sentence and reason preserved in each file's Git history and its dated editorial review note. The published original `MHCC 0.1.1`, its pinned source identities, native K/I ranges, cross-reference targets and Bible app were not touched.

This is **not** a verified exact compiled SWORD source comparison; CCEL's text may have inherited digital corruptions (for example the dangling Job 33:29–33 clause and duplicated or truncated phrases in Job 36). Corrections remain **proposals**, not independent editorial approvals.

## Findings

| Chapter | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Job 33 | Restored explicit *hell* (14–18) and *God afflicts the body for the soul's good* rather than merely permits sickness (19–28). Original secondary-witness 29–33 lacuna remains pending. |
| Job 34 | Restored the statement that God **afflicts** Job without injustice rather than only **permits** the affliction (10–15). |
| Job 35 | Restored Henry's explicit conditional claim about **divine wrath in troubles** and Elihu's having been **directed by God** to humble Job (14–16), clearly attributed to Henry. |
| Job 36 | Restored original **sent** trial (1–4); burning furnace, dying without knowledge/grace, **heap up wrath** and **everlasting misery** (5–14). Existing secondary-witness duplication/truncation caveats remain. |
| Job 37 | Restored **wrath to come** (1–13) in place of generic judgment. |
| Job 38 | Reviewed; no new substantive amendment. |
| Job 39 | Restored explicit **wrath of God** in the warhorse analogy. No historical animal identification changed. |
| Job 40 | Restored **wrath** alongside the sword of divine justice (15–24). |
| Job 41 | Reviewed; no new substantive amendment. |
| Job 42 | Restored Henry's **self-abhorrence** language accompanying Job's repentance (1–6), carefully attributed to the historical commentator. |

**Eight** of these ten chapters contain new, individually documented draft restorations: Job 33, 34, 35, 36, 37, 39, 40 and 42.

## Combined 2026-11-19 checkpoint

- **25 consecutive chapters (Job 18–42)** received a focused fresh secondary-witness comparison.
- **14 distinct chapter manuscripts** received fidelity repairs (Job 24, 26, 27, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37, 39, 40, 42); the previous Job 21 correction was also confirmed without another manuscript modification.
- Recurring defect pattern: draft modernization sometimes replaces **hell / wrath / eternal punishment** with generalized judgment, or substitutes **God permits** for source claims that **God actively afflicts**. This finding calls for **a wider systematic semantic-risk audit**, not automatic rewrites or unreviewed blanket substitutions. It does not establish how many of the entire 1,189 chapters are affected.
- **No** new original-source cross-reference retargeting, verse key mapping, published SWORD module, catalogue, merge or release.

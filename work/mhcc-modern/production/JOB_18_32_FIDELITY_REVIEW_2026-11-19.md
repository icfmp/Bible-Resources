# MHCC Modern English — targeted Job 18–32 fidelity review

**Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Scope:** fifteen consecutive chapter manuscripts, Job 18–32, in draft PR #272.  
**Status:** **secondary-witness review and proposed corrections only**, **not** exact MHCC 0.1.1 native-source verification, independent editorial acceptance, source-license clearance, GUI acceptance or release.

## Method

- Reread each of the fifteen already-drafted modern chapter expositions against the chapter's full publicly accessible CCEL *Concise Commentary* English text, including original section divisions and distinctive theological arguments.
- Check particularly whether narrative qualifiers, explicit judgment language, Christological teaching, practical exhortations and source-printed scripture references remain represented.
- Preserve existing headings, chapter outlines, source identifiers and original verse scopes. Do not alter the original published SWORD MHCC 0.1.1 module.
- This is a **second digital witness** of the original English, not a verified identical printed edition. Shared online ancestry prevents this pass from being called independent source-native acceptance.

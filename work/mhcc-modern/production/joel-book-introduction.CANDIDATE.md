# Joel — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

The prophet **Joel** warned of destruction coming upon **Judah** through **locusts and other insects**.

He called the people to **repentance, fasting and prayer**.

He also spoke of **the blessings of the gospel** and **the final glorious state of the church**.

# Hebrews — Book introduction (Modern English candidate)

The letter presents **Christ as the purpose, foundation, substance and truth of everything pictured by the Mosaic law**. The legal ceremonies had no power, in themselves, to save the soul.

A central teaching is that **Jesus of Nazareth is truly God**.

Unconverted Jews tried to persuade Jewish Christians to leave the faith. They claimed that the law of Moses was superior to the Christian gospel and spoke against everything connected with the Savior.

The writer therefore explains the superiority of **Jesus Christ, the Son of God**, and the blessings flowing from his suffering and death as the sacrifice for sins. Christianity is fuller and more excellent than the Mosaic ceremonial system.

Henry understands the principal aim as encouraging believing Hebrews to advance in knowledge of the gospel, become established in Christian faith and resist **apostasy**, against which strong warnings are given.

Although the first audience was the Hebrew Christians of that time, the letter remains vital to the whole church.

**Knowledge of Jesus Christ is the heart of all Scripture.** The ceremonial law is full of Christ, and the gospel is full of Christ. The lines of both Testaments meet and harmonize beautifully in him; the letter seeks to show that unity.

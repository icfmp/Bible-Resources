# HMCC Modern 0.1.0 — editorial and rights record

Henry's Modern Concise Commentary is a distinct English modernization, not the original unchanged Matthew Henry edition. The primary witness is the published Bible Resources MHCC 0.1.1, curated from the CrossWire public-domain module. The approved derivative incorporates 15 separately audited reference corrections (28 source-derived navigation targets) and two restored Matthew Henry exposition sections, independently attributed to a secondary historical textual witness. The digitized CCEL PDF and its presentation are not redistributed. The source witness and modern manuscripts remain unchanged in this packaging step.

The six compiled SWORD zCom data files are byte-for-byte identical to the successfully compiled editorial QA module; the only packaging change is the addition of mandatory Encoding and TextSource fields to the final module .conf. The human owner authorized publication on 2026-11-20, with the remaining Xiphos editorial-note click interaction assigned to manual post-publication testing. This does not imply independent certification of every individual hyperlink.
